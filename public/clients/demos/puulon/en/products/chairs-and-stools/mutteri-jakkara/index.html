Mutteri stool

Mutteri ('nut') is a cheerful, playful stool that can be arranged into interesting groups. Four stools stack into a symmetrical pile that also works as a decorative element.

The plywood seat (Iki Formica Carnaval) has a durable, easy-to-clean laminate surface. The legs are turned birch. The wood parts are lacquered. The product is made at Puulon's factory and delivered assembled. It was designed by furniture designers Lauri Vartola and Risto Luoma. It suits public spaces: in project orders the colours can be chosen from the Formica Collection (120 solid colours).
