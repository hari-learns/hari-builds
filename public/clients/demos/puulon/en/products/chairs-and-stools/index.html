Chairs and stools

Chairs and stools made from high-quality materials suit homes as well as public spaces. Materials include oak, ash, birch and birch plywood. The timeless chairs combine easily with our dining tables for a harmonious dining set.

Our stools have beautiful, sanded details and a touch of play. Sturdy and durable, they are also well suited to public spaces.

Railo chair

1960 series chair

1960 series bar stool

Metallimiehet bench

Mutteri stool

Puulon 365 chair

Q chair
