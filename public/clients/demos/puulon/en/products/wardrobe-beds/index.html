Wardrobe beds

A wardrobe bed is an excellent choice for small spaces. It works for everyday use as well as a spare bed. Lifted up against the wall, the bed gives the room a tidier look and frees floor space.

Wardrobe beds suit small flats, summer cottages, children's rooms and guest rooms. We make both vertical and horizontal models, which can also be integrated with other storage furniture. The beds come in several widths with quality Finnish frame and spring mattresses. We are happy to help you choose the right one.

Lauri wardrobe bed, vertical, with fixed frame mattress

Vertical model with cabinet-style doors and a fixed Finnish frame mattress.

Mikko, vertical wardrobe bed

Vertical model with a solid birch mattress tray and slatted base fixed to the door.

Minttu, horizontal wardrobe bed

Horizontal model: the long side of the bed opens out, so it needs less floor space than a vertical model.

Will a wardrobe bed fit your room?

Enter the wall width, ceiling height and floor space. You see at once whether Lauri, Mikko and Minttu fit, and get an enquiry pre-filled with your numbers.
