Wardrobe beds

Mikko, vertical wardrobe bed

Mikko is a vertical wardrobe bed. A solid birch mattress tray with a slatted base is fixed to the door. A spring or foam mattress can be placed in the tray.

Bedding is held with quick-lock straps. Lifting the bed into the cabinet is light thanks to strong springs. Mikko is made for mattress widths of 80, 90 and 120 cm. The cabinet must be fixed securely to the wall.

Will a wardrobe bed fit?

Dimensions

  • Mattress 80 × 200 cm: 219.5 × 94.5 × 44 cm (235 cm when open)
  • Mattress 90 × 200 cm: 219.5 × 104.5 × 44 cm (235 cm when open)
  • Mattress 120 × 200 cm: 219.5 × 134.5 × 44 cm (235 cm when open)
  • Minimum room height 230 cm

Materials

  • Cabinet frame and doors in white melamine as standard, other colours to order.
  • Prices do not include the mattress.

Accessories

  • Finnish spring mattress and topper mattress, extra cabinets and alternative colours.
