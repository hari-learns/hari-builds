Wardrobe beds

Lauri wardrobe bed, vertical, with fixed frame mattress

A wardrobe bed suits small homes, holiday homes, home offices and guest rooms, and in professional use control rooms, hospitals, barracks and any space where sleeping is needed. Lauri frees up space for other uses without compromising sleeping comfort.

Lauri looks like a two-door wardrobe with a frame mattress inside. It is light to pull out for the night and back into the cabinet again, thanks to a clever mechanism and strong springs.

The doors open sideways, up to nearly 270 degrees, and a leg section that folds over the bed keeps the cabinet only about 46 cm deep. Bedding is held in place with quick-lock straps. A topper mattress (sijauspatja) is included in the delivery. The cabinet must be fixed securely to the wall.
