Coffee and side tables

Boom side table

The Boom side table is made of many small pieces of wood. The frame and solid wood top edge are solid ash and the top is real-wood-veneered furniture board. It is finished with the stain of your choice and a hard-wearing semi-gloss catalysed lacquer. The ash grain stays visible after staining. The table is delivered assembled and ready to use. It has furniture castors.
