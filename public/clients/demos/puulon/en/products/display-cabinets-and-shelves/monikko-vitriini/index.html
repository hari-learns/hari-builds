Display cabinets and shelves

Monikko display cabinet

The Monikko series grew from customers' need to furnish Finnish homes with Finnish carpentry. Puulon's own design has been one of the company's most popular series for years.

The Monikko cabinet has a solid birch frame, with doors and shelves in 5 mm safety glass. The top and bottom panels are birch-veneered MDF.
