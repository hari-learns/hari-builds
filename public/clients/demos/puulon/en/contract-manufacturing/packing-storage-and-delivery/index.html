Contract manufacturing

Packing, storage and delivery

Packing

We deliver products either as pallet shipments or in standard packaging. If you wish, we pack products straight into tailored packaging in your company's look. This keeps costs lower, saves you time and lets us act responsibly by avoiding unnecessary links in the supply chain.

Storage

All our products can also be stored on our premises. Your company does not need to invest in storage space or add resources to run storage. Our storage costs are based only on the space actually used. The supply chain becomes more flexible, faster and more cost-effective.

Delivery

Besides packing and storage, we deliver the products you order, even on a fast schedule, straight to the end customer or another agreed address. This saves time and keeps costs lower. We can also handle deliveries in packaging that carries your company's look.
