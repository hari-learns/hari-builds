Finishing and assembly

Surface treatment of wood and plywood

Surface treatment and finishing are among the most important stages of production. Treating wood and plywood gives the product its final colour and a protection that extends its life and durability. The finish can emphasise the wood grain or cover it.

We finish products in our modern paint shop to the customer's wishes. A sixty-place hanging line keeps finishing smooth and cost-effective. Besides catalysed paints and lacquers we also use water-thinned products. For waxed products we use Osmo Color oil-wax products.

Product assembly

To save your company time and resources we also take care of assembly if you wish. At assembly we do the final inspection and pack the products, either in standard packaging or in packaging tailored with your company's look.
