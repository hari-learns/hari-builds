Wood parts and components

Wood parts and components

We produce wood parts and components for the furniture industry and others. We make small single pieces as well as larger series of thousands of pieces. Tell us your idea and we will make it. We make wood parts and components quickly and cost-effectively for small entrepreneurs and international corporations alike.

CNC machining

Our modern 5-axis CNC machining centre makes complex jobs possible, dimensionally accurate and cost-effective. We can machine tabletops over 4 metres long and mill moulded seats to shape. The large work area allows both large and small pieces.

We accept finished design data as 3D files.

Moulding

We make beautiful, individual profiled mouldings with our versatile machinery. We start by edging raw timber into suitable blanks and planing them to exact size. With the eight-cutter moulder we can make even very complex profiles. Special shapes are always designed on a computer, and matching profile knives are made for them.

Turned parts

With our automatic lathes we turn round shapes for traditional and more modern products alike, from small lamp bodies to table legs. The automatic lathe is fast and economical, especially for large series. The main material is birch, but other species also work. All turned parts are sanded as part of turning.

Please note: the workpiece must be shorter than one metre and thinner than ten centimetres in diameter.

Laser marking

All our products can be personalised, that is, carry the customer's own brand. Laser marking is crisp and stylish. With our current equipment, the work area for a laser-marked product is 60 × 90 cm. Other common marking methods are a product sticker or a type plate designed by the customer.

Moulded parts

We also supply veneer- and laminate-faced moulded parts, made with selected partners. We design and make the moulds needed. Pressed parts are machined on the 5-axis CNC and finished to the desired shape. If needed, the products are finished to the customer's wishes.
