Product design and advice

Product design

Product design often starts from the customer's need. The customer's needs combined with innovative design create a beautiful and durable product together. Our aim is to bring out the properties of wood in the finished product in the best possible way. Careful product design and the right manufacturing method guarantee a durable result.

We take responsible production into account at the very start of design: with the right material choices we can influence not only the look of the product but also how much waste material is produced.

Advice

Our skilled staff gladly help with any question about product design, choice of materials and manufacturing. We know how wood behaves and the best way to work it. The right treatment and machining of wood has a big effect on a product's durability and usability.

Get in touch and tell us your problem, we will try to solve it.
