Contract manufacturing

Contract manufacturing

Puulon Oy is a respected maker of wood products and a reliable partner for many furniture brands. Our products are known for good quality, beautiful design, a responsible production chain and Finnish origin.

A customer-oriented and reliable partner

As a partner we work customer-first and offer a complete service, from product design to storage and shipping to the end customer. All our products can also be supplied with the customer's own name and brand.

Thanks to our modern machinery we can make and deliver even large lots on the agreed schedule. We have been praised for smoothly run projects such as the furnishing renewal of the Lieto church and our cooperation with the Holiday Club hotel chain.

We also give technical advice: we help with material choices and with how wood is machined and made.

What to include in an enquiry

  • A 3D file or drawing (we accept finished design data as 3D files)
  • Wood species and finish (stain, lacquer, paint or Osmo Color oil wax)
  • Quantity and delivery schedule
  • Whether you want assembly, your own packaging or storage

Investment support for Puulon Oy

Puulon Oy invested in a wide-belt sander and a 5-axis CNC machining centre to increase production capacity from the last quarter of 2021. The investment was financed with ELY investment support (20%) and private funding (80%). We work as a contract manufacturer for several companies, for the domestic market and for export.

We make wardrobe beds, dining tables, chairs and stools at our own factory in Padasjoki. We are also a contract manufacturer for other furniture brands.
