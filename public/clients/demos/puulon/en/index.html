Finnish Furniture 2020

Railo chair: all-wood birch, 5-axis CNC

Designed by Juho Pasila and made by Puulon, Railo won the Finnish Furniture design competition in 2020. The Finnish Forest Foundation gave it an honourable mention for using PEFC-certified Finnish birch.

Its durability has been tested to EN 16139 (2013) level L1, and it is a stackable small chair for public spaces.
