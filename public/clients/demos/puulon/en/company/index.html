Puulon Oy

Company

Puulon Oy has made Finnish furniture since 1978. Our company cherishes the nearly 100-year-old carpenter's craft, and thanks to solid expertise our products have spread widely around the world. Puulon's products show modern thinking combined with traditional carpentry.

Every product is carefully made, using the properties and possibilities of wood. Puulon Oy's furniture suits homes and public spaces as well as modular solutions.

A solid foothold in Finnish furniture production

As a subcontractor and reliable partner we are firmly part of Finnish furniture production. We offer customer-oriented, complete service from product design and advice to storage and shipping of the products.

History

  1. 1848

    Aarno sawmill is founded. The name Aarnon Puu comes from this sawmill.

  2. 1920

    J.K. Luukko founds Aarnon Saha ja Harjapuutehdas (sawmill and brush-wood factory).

  3. 2016

    Puulon Oy, operating in Kuhmoinen, buys the business of Aarnon Puu. The business expands into contract manufacturing, public furniture and brush-wood production.

  4. 2019

    Operations move into a new hall in Padasjoki, where the whole factory and the factory outlet are now located.

  5. 2020

    The Railo chair wins the Finnish Furniture competition.

  6. 2021

    The company's own paint shop is completed. Investment in a wide-belt sander and a 5-axis CNC machining centre.

The people

Jaakko Järvi

Managing director, carpenter

Jaakko has always known he wanted to be a carpenter. In 2013 he graduated as a wood artisan from the Ikaalinen College of Crafts and Design. In spring 2019 he passed the master carpenter qualification, the highest possible in carpentry.

In 2015 he took a shared tenth place at WorldSkills in Brazil and received the Medal for Excellence. In 2016 Jaakko won the European championship at EuroSkills. In his work he combines being managing director with a passion for making things by hand, and he also takes part in Puulon's production.

+358 400 292 043

Risto Luoma

Head of product development and designer cooperation

Risto has always been interested in design and trying new things. Over the years he has been involved in many kinds of projects, working with different materials and in various product development roles. He studied design at the Kuopio Academy of Design and later furniture design at the Lahti Institute of Design (UAS). Risto is responsible for Puulon's cooperation with designers.

Designers

Puulon's products are by Finnish designers.

  • Risto Halme

    Interior architect who graduated from Ateneum in 1959. Designed Puulon's 1960 series.

  • Jouni Leino

    Interior architect and designer. Designed Puulon's Pouta series and the Nelio shelves.

  • Juho Pasila

    Designer. His Railo chair won the Finnish Furniture competition in 2020.

  • Eero Aarnio

    Finnish designer behind the Ball Chair (1962), Bubble Chair (1968) and Pastil Chair (1968). Designed the Metallimiehet bench.

    Juhani Rauvala

    Designer whose 3Way products build ecology in from design to production.

    Eeva Kokkonen
