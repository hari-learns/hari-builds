Factory outlet

Puulon Oy's factory outlet is next to our factory in Padasjoki, at Valutie 9, 17500 Padasjoki, Finland.

At the outlet you can get to know our products and try them in practice. On display are our own tables, chairs, stools and wardrobe beds, Finnish Hilding Andersen mattresses, glass display cabinets and modular shelves, as well as Nukkuvilla beds and futon mattresses from Porin Villa ja Peite. The shop also has changing special lots and small products such as sauna brushes, chopping boards and gymnastic sticks.

At the outlet you can also see the furniture-making competition pieces of master carpenter Jaakko Järvi: from the European championships in Sweden (1st place) and the world championships in Brazil (shared 10th place).

What is on display

  • Wardrobe beds
