Wardrobe beds: common questions

How much floor space does a wardrobe bed need?

It depends on the model. Mikko needs 235 cm when open. The horizontal Minttu opens from its long side and needs 115 cm (80 cm mattress) to 175 cm (140 cm mattress). Lauri's open depth is not given on the product page, so ask the factory.

What room height is required?

For the vertical models Lauri and Mikko the room height must be at least 230 cm. The cabinet height is 219.5 cm.

Is the mattress included?

Lauri has a fixed Finnish frame mattress and a topper mattress is included. Prices for Mikko and Minttu do not include the mattress: a spring or foam mattress goes into the tray.

What can it be fixed to?

Lauri and Mikko are fixed securely to the wall. Minttu is fixed securely to the floor or wall.

Can I choose the colour?

In the basic model the frame and doors are white melamine. Other colours are available to order.

We make wardrobe beds, dining tables, chairs and stools at our own factory in Padasjoki. We are also a contract manufacturer for other furniture brands.
