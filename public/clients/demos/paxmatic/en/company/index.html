Swiss quality since 1890

As a highly specialised manufacturer of packaging machines we offer our customers innovative, customised processes and machines from handling to the packaging of parts of almost all kinds. Based on your requirements, product characteristics and process specifications we develop the appropriate solution.

Counting, weighing, packaging: that is the core of PAXMATIC®. More than 40 years of experience in film packaging and feeding systems form the basis for comprehensive concept development including software engineering. The pleasure of innovative, sometimes unusual technical applications and the personal support of our contacts at the customer are the foundation.

No PAXMATIC® solution is like another. At the same time our services are connected by unchanging principles: customer orientation, precision, quality.

Approximately 2,500 PAXMATIC® machines are in operation worldwide in the furniture, toy and automotive industries, at manufacturers of medical and electrical technology as well as in the plastics and metal processing industries. Founded in 1890, PAXMATIC® is one of the leading providers today. Experienced engineers and technicians vouch for solid constructions with long service lives in proverbial Swiss quality.
