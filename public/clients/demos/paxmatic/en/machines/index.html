Packaging machines, feeding systems and counters

PAXMATIC® packaging machines are available for bag and film packaging (PE, PP or composite films as tubular or flat film) with different bag widths, continuously adjustable bag length and a variable number of bags/min. Flat products can be packed with inserts, individually or in bag chains with individual sealing seam (also by ultrasonic process), security sealing and tear perforation.

Shorter product cycles change the requirements during the service life of machines. PAXMATIC® machines are therefore modular and their hardware can be adapted at any time. A further level of flexibility is controlled via the PAXMATIC® software, for example when choosing clock rate, bag dimensions, welding parameters or system language. Jolting, check weighing, counting of divergent pieces and further functions can be integrated into the packaging process at any time.
