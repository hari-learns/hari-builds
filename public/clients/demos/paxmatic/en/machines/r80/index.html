R80

Vertical bagging machine, back seam

Compact bagging machine with vertical filling tube and back-seam welding. Processes a wide range of weldable plastic flat films.

Technical data

Output
up to 30 bags/min
Bag width
up to 200 mm
Bag length
up to 200 mm (longer with split feed)
Passage height
70 mm
Film thickness
approx. 40 to 150 my
Supply
230 VAC / 50 Hz, min. 5 bar
Dimensions
approx. 1400 x 1300 x 1500 mm, approx. 225 kg

Features

  • Vertical bagging machine, air-cooled cross-sealing system
  • Processes flat film (PE, PP, composite)
  • Siemens S7 controller with colour touch panel
  • Graphic input screens and online help, fault messages in plain text
  • Automatic sealing-temperature control
  • Access rights structured by password levels
  • Flexible profile-frame construction, modular and adaptable
  • Synchronisation for additional machines available

Options

  • Manual workstation
  • Slide or flap in the filling tube
  • Bag clamp
  • Print-mark control
  • Air-hole punch
  • Recipe management
