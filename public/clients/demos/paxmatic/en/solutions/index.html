Complete solutions from material-flow planning to service

Swiss quality and efficiency from the highly specialised machine builder

Our services

  1. Development of the solution concept according to customer requirements and material conditions
  2. Coordination and approval by the customer
  3. Configuration of the packaging line and programming of the software
  4. Set-up, trial run and hand-over of the facility by our qualified employees

PAXMATIC® ensures optimal use of the machines with minimal downtime and a long service life thanks to a help desk, maintenance contracts, short call-off times for technicians and an express spare-parts service from the PAXMATIC parts warehouse.

Process

  1. Take-over of the articles
  2. Precise feeding, counting and weighing
  3. Careful packing in single, protective, picking, bag-chain or collective packs
  4. Sealing (for flat products bands are also possible)
  5. Marking the pack with barcode, best-before date or graphics
  6. Hand-over to the next station, e.g. for re-packing into cartons
