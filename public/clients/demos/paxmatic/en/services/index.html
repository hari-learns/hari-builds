Packaging as a Service

Would you like to pack your products safely and attractively but do not have your own packaging machines? No problem! We pack your non-food products in high-quality film bags or paper bags.

Packaging service, packing in paper and film, packaging service provider, contract packaging

Professional packaging

We have state-of-the-art packaging machines and experienced specialists to make sure your products are packed safely and attractively.

Diverse materials

Whether film or paper bags with back seam or side seam or special packaging for industrial products: we offer a wide range of packaging materials and options.

Tailor-made solutions

Every customer is unique and we adapt our packaging services to your individual requirements. From the size of the bags to the design. Of course every single bag can be individually coded/printed during the packaging process.

Efficiency and reliability

We understand the importance of punctuality in your business. Our fast and reliable packaging service makes sure your products are packed on time.

Cost savings

Instead of investing in expensive packaging machines you can benefit from our cost-efficient packaging services. As an alternative we also offer you to rent the suitable packaging machine from PAXMATIC.

At PAXMATIC we are proud to offer our customers high-quality packaging solutions and first-class service. Your satisfaction comes first.
