Your challenges are our incentive

Over 2,500 PAXMATIC® machines are in operation worldwide in plastics, wood and metal processing, in the automotive industry and in electrical and medical technology.

PAXMATIC® machines typically process bulk and piece goods such as screws, pins, fittings, toy bricks and other parts and components. We are also specialists in packaging flat products such as cards, envelopes, brochures, ring-binder inserts or CDs.
