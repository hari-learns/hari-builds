We design, make and extrude

We are a plastic profile manufacturer with many years of experience in designing, building tools and producing profiles by extrusion. We specialise in profiles from PVC, PE, PMMA, elastomers, ABS, PP, PS and PC: angles, bathroom profiles, expansion joints, roller shutter guides, small hoses, tubes, square profiles, LED and SE covers, open and closed profiles. In any colour, also with a wood-grain print.

Technology partner of the Silesian University of Technology, one of Poland's largest technical universities.

From idea to profile

  1. 1

    Pre-implementation analysis

    When starting cooperation, we first have a conversation with the customer in order to well understand their needs and establish mutually beneficial terms of cooperation.

  2. 2

    Tool design

    Using state-of-the-art CAD systems and computational fluid dynamics tools, we design a set of tools for extruding profiles. This guarantees high quality and, above all, a smooth process of its implementation into production.

  3. 3

    Making the tool

    Precision manufacturing of the tool in the workshop by our engineers makes it possible to obtain the ideal profile according to the customer's concept.

  4. 4

    Profile manufacturing

    After making a sample series approved by the customer, we proceed to extrusion of profiles.

Lead time: a standard profile on an existing tool 1 to 4 weeks, a new profile from tool design to serial production 8 to 16 weeks.

What we extrude

Eight plastic families. Two figures from the company's own property tables on each card.

  • PVC

    Polyvinyl chloride

    Durable, corrosion resistant, light and easy to process.

    • 41 MPatensile strength (0% plasticiser)
    • 90Shore A hardness (0% plasticiser)
  • PC

    Polycarbonate

    High impact resistance and good dimensional stability.

    • 55–75 MPatensile strength
    • 100–150 %elongation at break
  • PMMA

    Polymethyl methacrylate

    High transparency, hardness and weather resistance.

    • 48–76 MPatensile strength
    • 2–5 %elongation at break
  • PE

    Polyethylene

    High chemical resistance, low moisture absorption and excellent flexibility.

    • 20–37 MPatensile strength, HDPE (LDPE: 8–12 MPa)
    • 100–650 %elongation, LDPE (HDPE: 20–80 %)
  • ABS

    Acrylonitrile butadiene styrene

    Strength, stiffness and impact resistance.

    • 40 MPatensile strength
    • 3–75 %elongation at break
  • PP

    Polypropylene

    High chemical resistance, low density and good thermal stability.

    • 30–40 MPatensile strength
    • 200–600 %elongation at break
  • PS

    Polystyrene

    High transparency and stiffness.

    • 35–55 MPatensile strength
    • 3.0–3.5 GPaYoung's modulus
  • TPE

    Thermoplastic elastomers

    Exceptional flexibility and resistance to deformation.

    • 30–90Shore A hardness
    • −50 to +150 °Cworking temperature

3D design

Designing in a 3D environment can significantly reduce the cost and time of prototyping products. Work is underway to simulate plastic flow and heat flow in tools using the SolidWorks Flow Simulation module, including designing components for advanced CAM 3D machining.
