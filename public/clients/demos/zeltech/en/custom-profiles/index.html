Technical profiles extruded to order

We specialise in technical plastic profiles tailored to individual customer needs. Extrusion lets us make profiles of complex shapes to the customer's design.

We can also make standard technical profiles

  • PVC angles in any colour, also with a wood-grain print: L 10×10, L 15×15, L 20×20, L 20×10, L 25×25, L 30×30, L 40×40, L 50×50M
  • Universal PVC angles: NUB 20×20, NUB 25×25, NUD 20×20, NUD 25×25
  • Carpet angle
  • Bathroom finishing profiles for tiles
  • Expansion joint profiles
  • Roller shutter guides
  • Small hoses
  • Tubes
  • PC square tube profile – LED 30×30
  • LED cover – EX milky and transparent
  • SE cover – EX milky and transparent
  • Open and closed profiles

From idea to profile

  1. 1

    Pre-implementation analysis

    When starting cooperation, we first have a conversation with the customer in order to well understand their needs and establish mutually beneficial terms of cooperation.

  2. 2

    Tool design

    Using state-of-the-art CAD systems and computational fluid dynamics tools, we design a set of tools for extruding profiles. This guarantees high quality and, above all, a smooth process of its implementation into production.

  3. 3

    Making the tool

    Precision manufacturing of the tool in the workshop by our engineers makes it possible to obtain the ideal profile according to the customer's concept.

  4. 4

    Profile manufacturing

    After making a sample series approved by the customer, we proceed to extrusion of profiles.

Lead time: a standard profile on an existing tool 1 to 4 weeks, a new profile from tool design to serial production 8 to 16 weeks.

Tools for profile extrusion

We specialise in making tools for extruding technical profiles to individual order. Our engineers and technologists work with the customer at every stage, from concept and design to final implementation. We have our own design studio for moulds and dies and a tool room.

Frequently asked questions

What is the lead time for a profile?

It depends on several factors. For a standard product with an existing tool the time ranges from 1 to 4 weeks; if we introduce a new profile, the time from tool design through tool making, implementation and serial production is 8 to 16 weeks.

What plastics can the profile be made from?

Our profile production is based on various types of plastics. Mainly PVC, but we also extrude polyethylene (PE), polycarbonate (PC), polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA), acrylonitrile butadiene styrene (ABS) and polypropylene (PP).

Does the customer have to pay for the tool?

Implementing a new profile requires making a complete extrusion tool, which involves paying for the tool. The tool cost can be spread over the running metre of the profile.

Can profiles be wound on a spool?

Yes, depending on the shape and the material. Spools can be wound in lengths of 10 to 100 running metres.

What is the maximum length of the profiles?

Profiles can be made in lengths from 0.2 m to 7 m, or wound on a spool up to 100 m.

Do we make profiles with a wood-grain print?

Yes, a wood imitation can be printed on flat profile surfaces, among others: light beech, pink beech, dark beech, red oak, manor oak, natural oak, antique oak, rustic oak, mahogany, walnut, cherry, pine.

In which colours can profiles be made?

We work with renowned European makers of plastic colourants and can match the colour to the customer's wishes according to the RAL system.

Do we make transparent profiles?

Yes, from materials such as PC, PMMA and PVC, most often used for LED lighting and price strips for shop shelves.

What are the maximum parameters of a profile?

Our technical capabilities let us extrude profiles up to 1 kg per running metre and with a maximum side length of 100 mm.

Can plastic profiles replace aluminium profiles?

With aluminium prices rising and availability falling, customers turn to plastics that can replace it. We make plastic profiles with UV and UD additives that can replace aluminium profiles.

Are the profiles weather resistant?

Materials with UV additives allow the profiles to be used outdoors. They are resistant to solar radiation, temperature and moisture.
