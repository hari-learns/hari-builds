From idea to profile

  1. 1

    Pre-implementation analysis

    When starting cooperation, we first have a conversation with the customer in order to well understand their needs and establish mutually beneficial terms of cooperation.

  2. 2

    Tool design

    Using state-of-the-art CAD systems and computational fluid dynamics tools, we design a set of tools for extruding profiles. This guarantees high quality and, above all, a smooth process of its implementation into production.

  3. 3

    Making the tool

    Precision manufacturing of the tool in the workshop by our engineers makes it possible to obtain the ideal profile according to the customer's concept.

  4. 4

    Profile manufacturing

    After making a sample series approved by the customer, we proceed to extrusion of profiles.

Lead time: a standard profile on an existing tool 1 to 4 weeks, a new profile from tool design to serial production 8 to 16 weeks.
