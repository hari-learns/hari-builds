About the company

Proven quality, advice and professionalism are the qualities that guide us in working with our customers.

Mission

Żeltech's mission is to provide comprehensive services in the design, improvement and manufacture of plastic profiles by extrusion. Thanks to our individual approach to projects, cooperation with the customer and many years of experience, we create high-quality products.

History

We have been on the Polish market since 1989. Domestic, private capital and a highly qualified staff of specialists have allowed the company to build a strong foundation. Over the years Żeltech has established cooperation with several thousand companies in various industrial sectors.

Our products mostly go to the Polish market, although the volume of foreign customers from Germany, Switzerland, Sweden, the Czech Republic, Hungary, Ukraine and the USA is growing every year. Having our own mould and die design studio and tool room, we can realise our customers' original ideas.

  1. 1989

    Getting started

    Żeltech began business in 1989 as a limited liability company. From the beginning its goal has been to produce plastic extruded products. In the early years, construction profiles were the primary product line.

  2. 1998

    Machine park

    Expansion of the machine park with two new processing lines to increase the productivity and efficiency of the plant.

  3. 2010

    Structural profiles

    Since 2010 the production of structural profiles using a wide range of plastics for various industrial applications has been developed. Żeltech developed its own tooling department, which allowed it to respond quickly to customer needs. Żeltech has 7 processing lines with varying capabilities and a total production capacity of 50 tons/month.

  4. 2020

    New challenges

    Żeltech is moving into new hands: a Polish company with Polish capital. Investment in machinery, employees and plant infrastructure. New visions, new markets, new customer groups.

  5. 2021

    Silesian University of Technology

    Signing a cooperation agreement with the Silesian University of Technology. Under this agreement, researchers work alongside Żeltech employees to transfer knowledge and experience in designing and implementing the production of plastic profiles on tooling made by the company.

  6. 2022

    Professional training

    A series of professional training courses to improve employee competence in designing and implementing new plastic profiles into production based on innovative technologies and new materials.

  7. 2023

    Forbes Diamonds

    Żeltech was named a winner of the Forbes Diamonds 2023 ranking, a compilation of the most dynamically growing companies in Poland, compiled in consultation with the editors of Forbes and Dun & Bradstreet Poland.

Quality and innovation

Every day we excel in the extrusion of plastic profiles. We strive to make the quality of our services as high as possible, so we cooperate with the best, as evidenced by our cooperation with the Silesian University of Technology.

Quality certificate: URS ISO 9001:2015

The quality of our services is confirmed by ISO certification. By obtaining it, we have proven that Żeltech is a reliable and strong company that cares about the highest standards of its products.

3D design

Designing in a 3D environment can significantly reduce the cost and time of prototyping products. Work is underway to simulate plastic flow and heat flow in tools using the SolidWorks Flow Simulation module, including designing components for advanced CAM 3D machining.

Collaboration with the best

We signed a cooperation agreement with the Silesian University of Technology, one of the most important technical universities in Poland and internationally. Our company has access to the knowledge of its scientists and to its technical facilities, in the design and implementation of new technologies, testing and research, and employee training.
