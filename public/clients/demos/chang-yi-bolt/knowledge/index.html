Knowledge

What are the materials of screws and bolts?

Part 1

About the daily screw and bolt we use on our DIY crafts or constructional works, have you ever wondered what it is made of? You may know it is made of steel, but what kind of steel, and where does it come from? Here is the brief information for you.

This is not an international standards perspective; check ISO 898-1 for that.

R material

Rimmed quality material

Basically recycled steel put back into the furnace, with no refining process. A middle class material that can be used for cold forming screw production, but because it contains recycled material there may be some impurities, so it is not the best material for screw and bolt production.

K material

Killed quality material

Sounds like silicon-killed and aluminum-killed steel, which are relatively high quality steels, but the information available is unclear. The conclusion on the current site is that K material is the lowest class, recycled material of such quality that it is normally only used for hot forming bolts.

A material

Practical saying in the Taiwan fastener industry

Defined by experienced workers as the best level of screw and bolt producible material: newly made steel from iron ore, so it contains fewer impurities. Aluminum-killed steel and silicon-killed steel are A material.

Summarised from the knowledge article on the current Chang Yi Bolt site. The article says the A, K and R labels are a practical saying in the Taiwan fastener industry, not an international standard.

Need a specific grade?

Chang Yi lists ISO grade 4.8 and 8.8, ASTM A307 and A325, SAE J429 grade 2 and 5 on its larger bolts and lag screws.
