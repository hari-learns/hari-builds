How's QUALITY of CYB?

Quality is the essential of CYB offer

ISO is the International Organization for Standardization; 9001 is the number of the standard. The 9000 series represent standard provisions related to the quality management mode. ISO originated from the management system model of the industry. It aims to establish a set of operating procedures to run towards predetermined goals, so that the organization can produce high-quality products and provide ideal service quality.

ISO promulgated the international standards related to the ISO 9000 quality management system in 1987, which are suitable for all walks of life around the world to improve the quality of their products, projects or services, so as to reach the world-recognized level.

The ISO 9001 quality system stipulates the quality management system requirements. It includes the quality assurance model of development, design, production, installation and service, which is used by the organization to verify that it has the ability to meet customer requirements and applicable regulatory requirements.

ISO 9001 is actually a set of management system standards that can be widely used in many types of organizations. There are also many other international standards based on ISO 9001, such as ISO 13485 (medical equipment) and IATF 16949 (automobile industry). ISO 9001:2015 is the current version released in September 2015.

Chang Yi Bolt Co., Ltd (CYB) had been qualified for the ISO 9000 quality management system in 1997. We keep improving our quality level to benefit our customers. We are fixed with you.
