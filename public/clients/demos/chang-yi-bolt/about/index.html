About us

Chang Yi Bolt: fastener maker since 1987

Gangshan, Kaohsiung, Taiwan, the Kingdom of Fastener.

Chang Yi Bolt is a Taiwan-based fastener manufacturer, established in 1987 by Mr. Hsin Deng Chen and Ms. Bertha Lee. It is located in Gangshan, Kaohsiung, where the biggest scale of fastener valley is.

It was a small scale factory with few employees and now it becomes one of the most competitive screws and fastener manufacturers in the Gangshan area. In 2013, Citi TV selected eight of the most competitive fastener manufacturers and shot a series of video clips. Chang Yi was one of the eight.

Due to strict quality control and delivery-on-time policies, Chang Yi earns a good reputation in the markets. Our main export markets cover Europe, South America, North America, and Australia.

Chang Yi Bolt aims to focus on the customer's competitiveness. The mission of this company is to provide valuable solutions and effective services to enlarge customers' value.

What we make

Chang Yi Bolt is a fastener supplier. Our product range has over 5000 types of fasteners, including screws, bolts, and nuts. Besides standard products such as chipboard, self-tapping and wood screws, we specialised in customized screws. We are capable to design fasteners based on customers' demands. We can provide something beyond your imagination.

Where we make it

We have accounted for over 4,000 m² area of the factory. The official address is No. 99, Pen-Chou Road, Gangshan, Kaohsiung, Taiwan. We can offer our products to all over the world. We export over 8000 tons of fasteners annually. Chang Yi Bolt has over 30 years of experience in the fastener manufacturing industry.

Chang Yi Bolt's commitment to customer service, quality products, and on-time delivery earned our customers' respect and reputation.
