    How the matching works

    1. The substrate picks the product families that suit it, using the family names (wood screws, concrete screws, drywall screws, self-drilling screws and so on).
    2. Your diameter and length are checked against the size range printed on each product page, in millimetres. Inch values are converted.
    3. The finish filter uses the surface list of each product.
    4. What fits goes into the enquiry. What is close is shown separately, with the reason.
