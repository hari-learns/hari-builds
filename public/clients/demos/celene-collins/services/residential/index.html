New Builds | Renovations

Residential

Whether it’s a new build, your first family home or the home you’ve lived in for many years; Decorating your home can be an overwhelming and daunting task. For some people just thinking about furnishing their home is exhausting, that’s where we come in.

We work with clients across the Cork area to deliver thoughtfully designed homes tailored to how you live.

Whether you’re starting with a blank-canvas new build or undertaking a major renovation, we specialise in projects that require a fresh direction – from full transformations and layout changes to structural alterations.

If your home simply needs an update, we can help there too. As families grow or lifestyles change, your space should evolve with you. We design interiors that adapt to your needs, both now and in the future.

The Process

Every project begins with your brief. Whether you’re building a new home or reimagining a space you’ve lived in for years, making the right design decisions can feel overwhelming – that’s where we come in. We take the time to understand how you live, what you need and what you envision for your space. Once the design is approved, we guide you through the transformation with a personal, one-to-one service, supported by site visits and regular communication.

Interior Architecture

We help you reimagine your space to work better, look better and feel right for how you live. From floor plan optimisation and room layouts to lighting plans and kitchen or bathroom design, we focus on getting the most from your interior architecture. Every detail is carefully thought through, from doors and ironmongery to fireplaces and balustrades.

Bathroom Planning

Whether you’re working with a compact en-suite or a generous family bathroom, balancing layout, functionality and storage can be challenging. Working with trusted, experienced tradespeople, we coordinate flooring, lighting, plumbing and tiling to deliver beautifully finished bathrooms, efficiently and with care.

Colour Consultation

We help you make confident colour choices by looking at natural light, room orientation and how each space is used throughout the day. Our colour consultation service provides a cohesive palette that complements your interior and brings clarity to the decision-making process.

Layout Solutions

We create smart, effective layouts that make the best use of your space. Our 3D visuals allow you to clearly see how your space will look and function before work begins, helping you make confident decisions and move forward with clarity.

Room by Room Interior Design Ideas

If you’re not planning a full renovation but would like fresh ideas for a specific space, our room-by-room interior design service is an ideal solution. We can focus on any area of your home, from hallways and living spaces to bedrooms, dining rooms or nurseries.

Window Dressing and Window Treatments

From fabrics and textures to colours and patterns, window hardware to mounts and poles, window dressing presents a bewildering array of choices. Celene will guide you to dress your windows in a style that compliments and enhances the rest of your décor.
