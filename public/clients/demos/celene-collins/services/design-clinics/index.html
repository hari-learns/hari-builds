Interior Design Clinics

Interior design made easy. Decisions made simple. One-to-one design support at The Fifth Quarter on Paul Street, Cork.

Our Design Clinics are one-to-one interior design sessions held in our studio at Paul Street Shopping Centre, Cork. They’re designed to give you focused, practical advice and clear direction, without the commitment of a full design service.

Whether you’re building, renovating, remodelling or refreshing a room, a Design Clinic gives you access to professional expertise in a relaxed and supportive setting.

Who’s it for?

  • New builds
  • Renovations and remodelling
  • Room makeovers
  • Homes and commercial spaces at any stage of change
  1. 01

    Choose Your Session

    Select a Studio Design Clinic that suits your needs. All sessions are held in our studio at Paul Street Shopping Centre, Cork.

  2. 02

    Prepare for Your Visit

    You choose one or two rooms to focus on during the session. You’re welcome to bring along photos, plans, samples, or inspiration images – anything that helps explain your ideas. If you’re unsure where to start, that’s absolutely fine too.

  3. 03

    One-to-One Design Time

    Enjoy a private, one-to-one session with Celene, where we’ll work through layouts, colours, finishes, furniture ideas and practical next steps – all tailored to your style, space and budget.

  4. 04

    Leave with Clarity

    You’ll leave the session with clear direction, practical advice and the confidence to move forward with your project.
