About Celene Collins

Interior Architecture | Design

Our Creative Director, Celene Collins, is a highly respected interior designer with over 25 years’ experience working across residential, commercial and hospitality projects throughout Ireland.

Celene has collaborated on a wide range of projects, from private homes and renovations to hotels, restaurants, offices and show houses. Her work has been featured on RTÉ’s Room to Improve, as well as in leading publications including The Irish Times, The Sunday Times, VIP Magazine, Home Interiors & Living, and other national newspapers.

Celene’s design approach is rooted in the belief that every space should reflect the people who live or work within it, while supporting the practicalities of everyday life. This philosophy underpins the work of the entire studio.

Led by Celene, our team works closely with architects, engineers and trusted specialists to ensure each project is supported by expert insight at every stage. From bespoke joinery and hand-selected furniture to carefully sourced accessories and finishes, we bring a thoughtful, detail-led approach to every brief- delivering beautifully crafted interiors tailored to each client and project.

We offer a luxury, end-to-end interior design service, managing every stage of the process i.e. from early concepts through to installation. By carefully sourcing exceptional pieces, from furniture and lighting to fabrics and wallpapers, we create interiors that are both refined and practical, designed to stand the test of time.

By intentionally working on a limited number of projects at any one time, our team is able to focus fully on each brief – developing original ideas, curated concepts and bespoke designs tailored to every client.

Celene Collins

Creative Director & Interior Designer

With over 25 years’ experience in interior design, Celene Collins has built her career around a simple belief: a beautifully designed space should not only look exceptional, it should work beautifully for the people who use it.

As Creative Director of Celene Collins Interior Architecture & Design, Celene leads residential, commercial and hospitality projects ranging from family homes and renovations to restaurants, offices, hotels and show houses. Her approach combines creativity with practicality, taking time to understand how clients live, work and use their spaces before developing a design that feels distinctly their own.

Known for her ability to see the potential in a space and solve the practical challenges that come with transforming it, Celene works closely with clients, architects, engineers, craftspeople and suppliers throughout the design process. Her work has been featured on RTÉ’s Room to Improve and across national interiors and lifestyle media.

Today, Celene leads her growing team from the studio at The Fifth Quarter in Cork city, bringing the same personal, hands-on approach to every project -creating considered interiors that feel individual, timeless and made for real life.

Gaby Rodrigues

Interior Architect

Gaby is an Interior Architect with a passion for creating functional, beautiful and timeless spaces. With a background in architecture and interior design, she brings creativity, technical understanding and a strong attention to detail to each project.

Her experience in 3D visualisation allows her to bring concepts to life, helping clients clearly understand the potential of a space before it is realised. Gaby believes successful interiors should reflect the lifestyles, personalities and needs of the people who use them – creating spaces that are not only beautiful, but practical, comfortable and considered.

Estefany Carr

Junior Interior Designer

Estefany Carr is a Junior Interior Designer with a passion for creating spaces that feel as meaningful as they are beautiful. She approaches design with curiosity, creativity and attention to detail, looking beyond aesthetics to understand how people live, work and connect within a space.

With a particular interest in residential, hospitality and wellness design, Estefany is inspired by sustainable and biophilic approaches and the positive influence thoughtfully designed environments can have on everyday life. She believes the most successful interiors balance form and function while creating a genuine sense of comfort, connection and belonging.
