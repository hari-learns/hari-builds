Residential · Cork

Limeworth

The brief was to create a considered family home where functionality and flow were central to the design, while allowing carefully chosen materials and detailing to make an impact.

The entrance set the tone for the entire scheme. With six doors leading from the space, we introduced walnut as the primary material, creating a sense of continuity across the doors and new staircase. Classic black and white floor tiles complement the proportions of the hallway, while wall panelling adds texture and architectural interest. The walnut staircase was finished with a tailored runner and metal stair rods for a refined, timeless finish.

The space beneath the stairs was also put to work, transformed into a bespoke home bar featuring antique mirrored glass and a Dekton Laurent worktop – combining rich detailing with the durability needed for everyday family life.

In the open-plan living area, the challenge was to create a comfortable place for the family to watch television without compromising the flow of the space. The solution was to close off the existing double doors to the sitting room and introduce a bespoke partition wall designed to house a rotating television, allowing it to serve different areas as required.

The result is a timeless, carefully considered family home where thoughtful design enhances how the spaces are used every day, while allowing the home to adapt as the family grows.
