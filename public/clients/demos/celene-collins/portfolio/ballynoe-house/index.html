Holiday homes · Clonakilty, Cork

Ballynoe House

When people are searching for their dream holiday, they search for a beautiful space that provides everything they need for their break. Ballynoe House in Clonakilty was once three stables, but the client had a lifelong wish to transform these into self-catering holiday homes.

Working alongside the client, we came up with a design to maximise each space, using clever layouts, before finishing the rooms with the highest quality materials. Our in-house joinery team installed the kitchens, and we took great care in choosing interior finishes from wallpaper to fabrics and furniture that complimented the houses.

Overseeing installation of all the finishes, we also sourced the tradespeople from our extensive list of contacts, ensuring a smooth and efficient build.
