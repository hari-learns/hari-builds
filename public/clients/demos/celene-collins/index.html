About Celene Collins

Welcome to Celene Collins Interior Architecture & Design, a luxury Irish interior design studio creating beautifully considered residential and commercial spaces.

With over 25 years of industry experience, we specialise in interiors that balance elegant design with everyday functionality – from private homes and renovations to hotels, restaurants, offices and show houses.

We believe great design should reflect how you live, work and feel in a space. Our collaborative approach, working closely with architects and engineers, ensures every project is delivered seamlessly, professionally and to the highest standard.

A home is a feeling.

Celene Collins

Tranquility House, Dublin

A beautifully detailed Georgian property at 58 Grosvenor Road was carefully selected as the ideal setting. Over 100 years old and spanning four floors, the house offered both character and scale – providing the foundations for a welcoming “home from home” for patients and their families while attending treatment at St. James’s Hospital.
