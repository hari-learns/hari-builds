FAQs

Questions, answered

How experienced is Celene?

With over 20 years’ experience in Residential & Commercial Interior Design, Celene Collins is very well respected in her field and a leading Interior Designer in the Cork area.

What services do you offer?

We offer full service interior design. We can help you with Space Planning, Colour Consultation, Lighting Design, Fabrics & Upholstery, Window Treatments, Storage and lots more.

Do you work online?

Yes. Celene is also a virtual interior decorator working with clients from Ireland, the UK and a lot further! She will help you refresh or transform your space with her virtual interior design service.

Can I see my design before it is done?

You don’t need to wait to see your finished project. See it in advance; virtually. Our 3D Visualisation or 3d Interior Design uses the very latest in photo realistic technology.

What styles do you work in?

Contemporary or traditional, modern or period homes, we will help you achieve the look you love. Celene loves to mix the old with the new, up-cycling and has experience in all styles.

What about my budget?

We work with you to suit your budget, no matter how big or small, for a home or commercial space. You decide the budget; we stick to it.

Can you help with just one room?

Yes, absolutely. Whether it’s your living room, bathroom, hallway, playroom or master bedroom etc.; we will have a wealth of ideas to achieve a great makeover within your budget.

Do you offer gift vouchers?

Whether your loved one would like a 1 to 1 consultation, to get some practical advice or discuss a bigger project, our gift vouchers are a perfect way to begin. To purchase a voucher please send a Voucher Enquiry, or call Celene on (021) 7011128.
