Frequently asked questions
What kinds of moulds and parts does Soshin handle?
Soshin makes and sells ultra-precision injection moulds for optical equipment, camera parts, game accessories, medical equipment and in-vehicle parts. The order mix covers camera, precision equipment, automotive and medical divisions.
Can design through moulding be ordered from one company?
Yes: mould design, injection moulding, modification and mass-production moulding are all done in-house. Because quality control of the parts themselves is done in our own factory, the finished parts can also be delivered directly.
Where are the factories?
Soshin Co., Ltd. is at 3-12-15 Kashiwada-nishi, Higashi-Osaka (8 minutes on foot from Kinukasa-Kami-Kita station, JR Osaka Higashi Line). Wakayama Soshin Co., Ltd. is at 1079-10 Kitaseta, Kinokawa, Wakayama (1 minute from Kinokawa IC on the Kyonawa Expressway).
How can I contact you and when?
By phone at 06-6224-6060 (weekdays 9:00-17:30 JST) or by email at mold-on@soshin.com.
