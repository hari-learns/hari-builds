The craft of the master

Since our founding Soshin has kept to a small team of experts, whose experience-backed knowledge and know-how realise original product design, development and machining. However far mechanisation goes, without people who can draw out everything the hardware can do, a company cannot lead the industry.

Ultra-precision moulds from advanced technique and a commitment to monozukuri

A layered quality-assurance system built on accuracy and quality. Soshin makes moulds with micron-level accuracy and also moulds and quality-controls the plastic products themselves in its own factory.
