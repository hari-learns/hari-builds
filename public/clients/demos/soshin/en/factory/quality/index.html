Quality control and assembly

Product accuracy assured by measurement.

Draft English translation of the company's Japanese page, to be confirmed. Machine names are as published.

6 / 7

[Quality control] Mould parts made on site, electrodes and plastic products made by injection moulding are measured, and the data is managed and organised. Measuring tools include coordinate measuring machines, microscopes, surface roughness testers and image-processing machines. Quality is assured on this basis.

[Finishing and assembly] Parts made in other departments are finished with files and motor tools to raise the mould's accuracy. Assembly and final adjustment are also done here, and once all steps are finished the mould is run and the product checked.
