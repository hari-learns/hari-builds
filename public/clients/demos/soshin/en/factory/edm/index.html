EDM and wire EDM

Steel and carbide, with the electrode's shape transferred exactly.

Draft English translation of the company's Japanese page, to be confirmed. Machine names are as published.

5 / 7

[Die-sinking EDM] An electrode of a highly conductive material such as copper is used, and an arc discharge is generated in insulating machining fluid between the electrode and a conductive material such as steel or carbide. The workpiece surface melts at high temperature and the electrode's shape is transferred to it.

[Wire EDM] Wire EDM is a type of electrical discharge machining: a brass wire is used as the electrode and discharges against the workpiece, melting and cutting it like a thread saw.
