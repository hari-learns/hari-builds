Message from the president

Basic technical strength is decided by how each employee approaches the work.

Founded in 1980 as "Soshin Seiki", the company has reached its 40th anniversary thanks to the support of our customers.

To meet the accuracy that mould making demands, basic technical strength must come first, and it is no exaggeration to say that this strength is decided by each employee's attitude to the work.

With factories in Osaka and Wakayama, Soshin has earned the trust of Japan's top makers in optical-communication parts as well as mirrorless-camera and in-vehicle lens-barrel parts, and keeps putting the latest equipment into operation to reach even higher precision.

Representative Director and President: 小嶋 資久

"Perfect is still not enough." We build mutual trust with our customers.

Management philosophy and three company mottos

All employees take pride in being Japan's number one plastic mould maker, keep innovating, and contribute to society as our customers' best partner.

01

Public benefit

Serving customers sincerely on cost, quality and delivery so that they make a profit

02

Personal benefit

The happiness of all employees, in body and spirit

03

Company benefit

If the two above are achieved, the company has a prosperous future

Basic philosophy on quality and environment

We take pride in being a company that is kind to human health and the natural environment, meet the expectations of customers around the world, and do our utmost to supply products stably that exceed those expectations.

Quality and environment policy

To realise the basic philosophy, we have set the following policy for our business activities.

  1. Strive to provide products and services that meet customers' requests.
  2. Carry out business in consideration of its environmental impact, to contribute to society and the community.
  3. Maintain a good working environment for occupational safety and health.
  4. Set and continuously carry out quality and environment goals to meet requests and requirements.
  5. Keep good communication for smooth business operations.
  6. Comply with relevant laws, regulations, agreements and social ethics.

* This policy is reviewed and revised as appropriate.
