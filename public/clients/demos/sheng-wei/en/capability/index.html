FAQ

What is the minimum order?

100 pcs minimum.

How long does sampling take?

For a simple part, the first article can be ready in 10 days; a complicated part may take 3 weeks.

How soon can I get a quotation?

Within 3 days for a simple part with no secondary operation; 5 to 7 days for a complicated part with secondary operations and treatment.

What is the production lead time?

1.5 months for the first mass-production lot and 1 month for regular orders.

What are the payment terms?

Usually 50% T/T with order and 50% before shipment, or 100% T/T before shipment; negotiable by order amount and relationship.

How are goods shipped?

By sea, air or express, depending on the customer's request.

The English site's sampling, quotation and payment answers differ slightly from the Chinese site; treat Sheng Wei's reply as final.
