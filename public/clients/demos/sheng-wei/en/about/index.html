Sheng Wei

Founded in 1974, machining ever since.

Sheng Wei began in 1974 making motorcycle and auto parts, then kept renewing its machining technology and adding Japanese high-precision compound CNC equipment, moving into precision parts of every kind.

The company site says it is known for machining capability, fair prices, on-time delivery, good quality and efficient service.
