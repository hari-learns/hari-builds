Redrow

Stair balustrades for a Redrow scheme

We were recently appointed to a Redrow scheme, based in Chester, where the client required five sets of communal stair balustrades to five separate apartment blocks.

The client required us to copy a balustrade design already installed on a previous apartment block. Our Surveyors attended site to obtain the specification and details of the existing stair balustrades for Redrow so that we could produce our fabrication drawings to the same design.

Our balustrades were then manufactured in our workshop, by our experienced welder/fabricators, before being powder-coated in RAL 9005, black. Following this, the panels were then installed on site, and all handrail sections were completed with plastic capping, to provide a more comfortable handrail grip.

We’re looking forward to installing many more stair balustrades for our valued customers.
