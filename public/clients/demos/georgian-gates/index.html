Our process

Design. Manufacture. Install.

01

Design

With more than 30 years of metalwork experience, we love to use our knowledge to help design and refine our customers' projects.

How we do it

Here, at Georgian, we aim to bring our customer’s vision to life. We love to help you design your project, whether it be your new driveway gates or balustrades to your patio area. Once the design is finalised, our skilled metalworkers and craftsmen take over. Bending, cutting, welding and shaping metal with expert precision, they utilise a variety of techniques to bring the design to life. These techniques range from traditional blacksmithing methods to state-of-the-art laser cutting and CNC machining.

02

Manufacture

Our expert welders and fabricators manufacture all our products in our North Manchester unit, ensuring the highest quality throughout the process.

How we do it

Our journey begins in our North Manchester workshop. There, we manufacture all our products. We prioritise quality and performance from the very first step: selecting high-grade metal materials from trusted suppliers. Whether mild steel or aluminium, each piece undergoes a rigorous inspection to ensure quality and integrity before reaching the production floor. Our skilled fabricators wield a range of tools and machinery, from punches and saws to laser cutters and press brakes, achieving accurate cuts and bends with meticulous attention to detail. At Georgian, our certified welders take immense pride in their craft. They ensure every weld delivers both structural strength and aesthetic appeal.

03

Install

Our friendly and experienced team members carry out installation on site, bringing our projects to life.

How we do it

Once our projects have manufactured and passed through our quality inspection process, all components are carefully packaged and transported to site. All of our installation teams are equipped with the necessary tool and safety gear to carry out their work safely and efficiently. After the installation is complete, a thorough inspection is conducted to verify the work’s quality. Any final touches or paint touch-ups are then applied to ensure a perfect finish.
