Driveway & Garden Gates

Our garden and driveway gates are manufactured in a wide range of styles, including traditional and contemporary designs to suit the aesthetics of practically any property and its grounds.

Find your metalwork0161 763 4145

Bespoke gate designs

Our garden and driveway gate designs are fully customisable, giving you the freedom to choose from a variety of materials, including composite boards, aluminium and wrought iron. You can also add security features like high-quality locks to ensure your gates are not only beautiful but also secure.

For added peace of mind, our gates are built to last. All metal components are hot-dip galvanised to prevent rust and increase longevity. The gates are finished with a durable paint or powder coat in any RAL colour to suit your property’s aesthetic.

Expert installation & free consultation

With over three decades of experience, we guarantee safe and efficient installation. Our team will work with you from start to finish, ensuring your side gates, double gates, or driveway gates meet your exact requirements. We offer free site surveys and no-obligation quotes, giving you expert advice tailored to your project.
