Automated Gates

Here at Georgian Gates we specialise in custom-made automated steel gates and professional gate installation services. Our designs range from traditional wrought iron designs, to modern aluminium styles and anything in between.

Find your metalwork0161 763 4145

Tailor-made electric gates

Our entrance automated steel gates are crafted by experienced metalwork fabricators in our North Manchester workshop from high-quality materials. With minimal annual maintenance, they will provide decades of benefit to the homeowner, enhancing your home’s security, whilst adding value to your property.

Our automated gates are fully bespoke and designs are tailored to your requirements. We can offer traditional designs with steel infill bars. Alternatively, our gates can be designed to include composite or aluminium boards, finished in any colour or woodgrain effects.

We are proud to be an official Gate Safe Installer, meaning our Gate Technicians are fully trained in planning and installing high-quality systems, with the safety of the users at the forefront.
