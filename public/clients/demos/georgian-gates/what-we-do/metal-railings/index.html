Metal Railings

Our bespoke metal gates and railings, crafted from high-quality wrought iron, can transform the appearance of your property’s exterior, adding a touch of refinement and elegance while also offering seclusion and security.

Find your metalwork0161 763 4145

Custom-made railings

All our metal gates and railings are custom-made in our North Manchester workshop from high-grade solid steel, ensuring durability and style for your UK property. They can be produced in a variety of styles and are all custom-made to your exact requirements.

Our metal railings and gates are galvanised as standard and can be spray-painted or powder-coated in a wide range of colours, offering enhanced protection and a bespoke finish with added longevity.
