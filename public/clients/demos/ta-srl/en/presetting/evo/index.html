CNC tool presetting / EVO

Tool presetting EVO

Tool presetting precision machines. 7 sizes, from diameter 320 to 700 mm and height from 360 to 600 mm. C-MOS camera, 20,000 tools in memory.

Check your measurementsDownload the datasheet (PDF, Italian)

The EVO family presetters are high-precision, easy-to-use instruments, born from the tool control and measuring needs of those who work with CNC machines. All are equipped with linear guides, interchangeable spindle, high-precision optical scales, high-resolution camera and integrated PC with complete software.

Technical characteristics

Diameter / height
from 320 × 360 mm to 700 × 600 mm (7 models)
Tool load capacity
with support rollers 70 kg
Spindle
interchangeable rotating ISO / BT / HSK / VDI
Axis resolution
X = 0.001 mm · Z = 0.001 mm
Optics
telecentric lenses, field of view 7 × 7 mm, magnification 40×
Monitor
17" colour TFT
Memory
20,000 tools, 200 machine origins
Connections
7 USB outputs, 1 Ethernet-LAN output
Software
fixed axes and moving axes with automatic measurement of maximum diameter and height; tool inspection, shaped profiles, geometric functions, printing of lists and labels, update via internet
