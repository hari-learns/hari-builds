Our CNC tool presetting: 4 models for every need

Machines for measuring tools. The perfection of your products depends on the perfection of your instruments.

EVO

7 sizes, from diameter 320 to 700 mm and height from 360 to 600 mm. C-MOS camera, 20,000 tools in memory.

Datasheet and data

ASTRID

X axis 400 mm, Z axis 400 mm. Three versions: ASTRID, ASTRID VISUAL, ASTRID T-CAM.

Datasheet and data

GA 400

Measuring range X Ø 400 mm, Z 400 mm. TC 100 display, 200 tapers and 2000 tools in memory.

Datasheet and data

LOREL 320

T718 family

Versatile and easy to use. Solid and with unbeatable precision. Diameter from 250 to 600 mm, height from 300 to 600 mm, resolution 0.01 mm, accuracy 0.03 mm, repeatability 0.01 mm. Interchangeable taper holder (ISO, BT, VDI, HSK, shaft holder), battery powered.
