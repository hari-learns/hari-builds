The Company

Since 1980 a modern precision CNC mechanical workshop

In 1980 the Travetti Alberto mechanical workshop was founded, working in turning, milling and grinding with traditional machine tools. Today it is T.A. srl.

The idea was born from the concrete vision of the founder Alberto Travetti, who since boyhood dreamed of creating something that was only his, starting by drawing in his school notebooks the first sketches of the logo that would later represent him.

The logo, with the letters T and A at the centre of two different but overlapping circles, is a synthesis of the company policy: Technology and Avant-garde.

«I wish you always to have plenty of will and confidence, not to lose heart and to always be positive, because only in this way is every goal reached.»

Alberto Travetti to his son Lorenzo

The history, year by year

  1. 1980

    The Alberto Travetti mechanical workshop is founded, later transformed into T.A. srl. The decade between 1980 and 1990 is a period of great growth.

  2. 1993

    The premises move from Varese to Arcisate. From here comes the shift from the traditional machine to the precision CNC machine.

  3. 2000

    An era of great change, from the wish to create something new to the search for continuous innovation.

  4. 2007

    Lorenzo Travetti joins T.A. srl alongside his father.

  5. 2008

    The first version of CAD-CAM arrives in the company, up to the four-axis integration for machining three-dimensional objects.

  6. 2018

    The Citizen BNJ 51 joins the company, allowing a great leap in quality.

To the customer

The Company offers solutions for any mechanical need. Attention to the customer thus becomes attention to the quality of the work, to the materials and to meeting the agreed delivery dates.

The technical office

The technical office has always been inclined towards designing prototypes and mechanical parts for various sectors and towards carrying out the customer's drawing projects, characterised by a very high level of precision.

Management

With pragmatism and concision, management has adopted three simple cornerstones on which to base the business: technology, flexibility and quality.

Awards

Milestones

1999

Prealpina · Artigianato Lombardo

The company distinguished itself in Lombard craftsmanship thanks to quality, attention and continuous evolution. It thus won the «Artigianato Lombardo» mark, the Region's quality mark.

2006

ItaliaOggi · excellent companies

It was awarded as one of the national companies that, more than others, stood out for the management practices adopted and the results achieved, and therefore considered excellent.

In 2006 the «Officina Meccanica Travetti Alberto & C. di Arcisate (Va)» is among the 10 companies awarded by the comparison carried out by the University of Milan and the CNA on 2,869 companies nationwide (Mec-Spe, Parma).
