Yabby: an optical pulse compressor for Yb lasers

Lastek’s Yabby is an all-solid-state optical pulse compressor that reduces the pulse duration from common femtosecond ytterbium lasers operating around 1030 nm by factors of up to 6 with high efficiency.

Yabby is optimised for pulse energies in the tens of microjoule range and uses a novel multi-pass cell containing a nonlinear element to chirp the input pulse so that it can then be compressed using dispersion-compensating mirrors. By employing high efficiency reflective or anti-reflective coatings on all optical components, the overall transmission of the system is above 90%, with typically above 90% of that energy contained in the compressed pulse.

Yabby is designed to compress pulses from industrial Yb fibre lasers producing 275 to 400 fs pulses around 1030 nm.

  • 325 fs to 65 fstypical compression, a 5X ratio
  • >90%overall transmission
  • 5 to 6typical compression ratio

Features

  • Compression ratio typically 5 to 6
  • Designed specifically for pulses containing tens of µJ of energy
  • The Kerr nonlinearity of a glass plate contained in a novel multi-pass cell is used to chirp the input pulses
  • Separate chirped mirrors compress the pulse from the cell to near the time-bandwidth limit
  • No compressed gas required

Benefits

  • High transmission efficiency, above 90%
  • Typically above 90% of energy is contained in the compressed pulse
  • Multiple configurations possible to allow for different input pulse energy
  • Compact, maintenance free design
  • Maintains pointing stability of the input laser
