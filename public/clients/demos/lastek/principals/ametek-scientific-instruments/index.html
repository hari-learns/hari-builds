AMETEK Scientific Instruments

Pioneering High Precision Electrochemical and Battery Instrumentation

AMETEK Scientific Instruments are at the forefront of innovative design, manufacturing, and support for high precision electrochemical, battery, energy storage, corrosion, and materials analysis instrumentation. Our powerhouse brands, Princeton Applied Research and Solartron Analytical, are dedicated to delivering unparalleled solutions that perfectly balance performance and affordability.

Unveiling the Future of Precision Measurement :

With over a century of instrumental excellence, Princeton Applied Research and Solartron Analytical are revolutionizing both DC and AC measurements. Our cutting-edge technology is empowering researchers exploring energy devices, physical electrochemistry, corrosion, biomedical research and solid-state materials, driving scientific advancements that shape the world.

Experience the Impact:

Our product portfolio, which includes highly accurate and reliable battery cyclers, potentiostats , galvanostats, frequency response analyzers and more, stands as a testament to our dedication to excellence. As pioneers with 110 years of innovation, we take pride in contributing to the most-cited research worldwide. Join us in pushing the boundaries of knowledge and shaping the future of precision instrumentation.

Expanding Horizons:

Signal Recovery, pioneers of lock-in amplifier tech, now offer a wide array of related products: signal averagers, amplifiers, light choppers, multiplexers, and counters. This aids end-users and OEM partners, extracting subtle signals from noise for advancements in diverse fields.
