Revealer

Innovators in Optical Measurement Instrument and Systems

Revealer specializes in high-speed visual perception and measurement technology. Our core products include High Speed Cameras, sCMOS Cameras, Digital Image Correlation (DIC) Measurement System, and Particle Image Velocimetry (PIV) Measurement System. These advanced instruments are widely used in scientific research, aerospace, industrial, and biomedical fields for transient process analysis, strain field measurement, flow field analysis, motion tracking, and real-time fault detection.

  • High-quality Optical Measurement Instruments for Global Customers

High Speed Cameras: Max 25 000fps at 1.3M Pixel, BSI sensor,compact size, 320GB high-speed internal RAM, 10Gb Ethernet, perfectly match the application requirements in combustion, aerodynamic flows, Hopkinson bar, Vibration and Shock, etc.

sCMOS Cameras: 95% QE, 135fps at 2048×2048, 1.2e- readout noise, perfectly match the application requirements in cell biology, quantum physics, cold atoms, spectroscopy, etc.

Digital Image Correlation(DIC) Measurement System: Non-contact, full-field strain, deformation measurement, vibration analysis, combining thermal data with digital image correlation, perfectly match the application requirements in materials mechanics, biomechanical, automotive, etc.

Particle Image Velocimetry(PIV) Measurement System: Volumetric velocimetry, laser-induced fluorescence, high reconstruction accuracy - perfectly matched to the application requirements in fluid mechanics, aerospace, automotive, thermal science, etc.

Looking for high speed cameras, scientific cameras, DIC measurement system, PIV measurement system for research? The perfect choice is Revealer, your reliable and trustworthy manufacturer supplying quality and precise measurement instruments under the strictest quality assurance system certified by ISO. All instruments have been granted access permit certifications for major global markets including CE, CNAS, FC, etc.

Revealer is the proprietary brand of Agile Device Co.,Ltd., a scientific instrument company dedicated to the innovation of high-speed visual perception and measurement technology. Based on its core self-developed technologies, the company has developed a range of optoelectronic measurement instruments and analysis systems. These instruments and systems have been widely applied in various fields such as scientific research, aerospace, industrial manufacturing, and biomedical engineering.

Revealer's photoelectric measuring instruments and measuring systems developed based on independent innovative core technologies have accumulated rich application practices in transient process analysis, strain field measurement, flow field measurement, motion trajectory measurement, target tracking and identification, product performance test, real-time fault analysis, online quality inspection and other scenarios, which are widely used in scientific research, aerospace, industrial intellectual property, biomedical and other fields.
