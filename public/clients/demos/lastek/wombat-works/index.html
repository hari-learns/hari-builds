Wombat Works: custom optical engineering

Lastek Wombat Works is our custom optical engineering consultancy. We can design, test, prototype and build optical systems from the simplest to the most complex.

Give your R&D project a head start: talk to our optical design team about how we can assist you in your custom optical design and prototype development.

Our team includes dedicated optical design engineers, laser experts, spectroscopists and advanced microscopists. Together we have unrivalled experience in delivering custom optical design and engineering solutions.

Ask Wombat Works for a one-stop shop for your research projects, prototype development, and any job that requires design, manipulation and control with light, involving lasers, LEDs, arc lamps, optics, opto-mechanics, detectors and all.
