OpticsShop: US optics, one shipment to Australia

OpticsShop is Lastek’s own consolidated shipping service from the USA to Australia and New Zealand, offering savings for internet orders on optics, photonics and more. It’s fast, convenient and cost-effective.

How to order

  1. Visit the website of your US supplier of choice and select the products you need.
  2. Forward your shopping list to OpticsShop at Lastek by email.
  3. OpticsShop will order the contents of your shopping trolley for you at US list +10% plus $65 for delivery, plus GST, and arrange the consolidation. The $65 fee applies to an order for goods weighing less than 1 kg from one US supplier. For orders exceeding 1 kg an additional $20 per kg or part thereof will apply.
  4. As the goods arrive in Australia, they will be held in Adelaide until they are all in hand, and then delivered in one shipment to you. There are no extra charges no matter how many partial deliveries the supplier sends from the USA to Australia.
  5. If you would prefer part deliveries, these can be arranged on request at a charge of $15 per second or subsequent delivery from Adelaide.
  6. To order from another US supplier, start another order per the procedure above.
