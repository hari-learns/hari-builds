Services: design, safety, ordering and demonstrations

Beyond supplying and supporting more than seventy principals, Lastek designs, builds, audits and ships.

Wombat WorksLastek Wombat Works is our custom optical engineering consultancy. We can design, test, prototype and build optical systems from the simplest to the most complex.Laser Safety AustraliaLaser Safety Australia is a division of Lastek, offering a full range of laser safety equipment and expert advice. Our range includes laser safety eyewear, laser safety signs, enclosures, windows, blinds, shields, interlocks and more.OpticsShopOpticsShop is Lastek’s own consolidated shipping service from the USA to Australia and New Zealand, offering savings for internet orders on optics, photonics and more. It’s fast, convenient and cost-effective.Thorlabs via LastekAs the official Thorlabs Channel Partner for the Australia/NZ region, Lastek provides the most cost effective source for all Thorlabs products.Yabby pulse compressorLastek’s Yabby is an all-solid-state optical pulse compressor that reduces the pulse duration from common femtosecond ytterbium lasers operating around 1030 nm by factors of up to 6 with high efficiency.
