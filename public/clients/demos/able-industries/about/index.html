About

A family business, in its 51st year.

Established in 1975 as a small sheet metal fabricator in Melbourne, Able Industries Engineering is proud to be in its 51st year as a successful Australian family owned and operated company.

Starting with a team of just four staff, Bryan Martyn took ownership of the business in 1976 at its Spotswood site and during the 1990s moved to its current site in South Kingsville.

In 1999, Bryan’s son, Stephen Martyn, took on the role of Managing Director, and has overseen the expansion of the company, which now has a specialised facility in South Kingsville and a second factory on the site of the original business in Spotswood.

Our key to success over the past 51 years has been a focus on continual improvement in our products and looking after our customers. We invest significantly in research and development, best-practice systems and procedures, advanced engineering technologies and most importantly, our people and our clients.

Some of the business’s most iconic projects the public would recognise around Melbourne include Crown Casino and Federation Square, right down to smaller public infrastructure projects such as rubbish bins and planter boxes. The company also built the Lethal Weapon at Movie World on the Gold Coast and the Cyclone Rollercoaster at Dreamworld.

Able is a Defence Recognised Supplier, a member of the Australian Industry and Defence Network (AIDN) and a member of the Australian Industry Group (AIG).
