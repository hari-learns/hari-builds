Services

Concept design to production, and onto Through Life Support.

From the smallest metal components, right through to large architectural structures for buildings, Able Industries Engineering offers a complete design package.

  1. 1

    Concept design

    Engineering design and drafting in SolidWorks and Solid Edge, with CAE including linear and non-linear F.E.A.

  2. 2

    Production

    Fabrication, welding, assembly and finishing across two Melbourne factories.

  3. 3

    Through Life Support

    Support to completion and beyond.

Setting ourselves apart, Able has a three phase involvement in your project: concept design, production, and ‘Through Life Support’ (TLS), so you are supported to completion and beyond.

Our facilities, across two specialised factories in Melbourne, are fully equipped with the technology and machinery for your metal fabrication and project-based engineering needs.

Design and fabrication

  • Multiple qualified specialist project managers
  • Engineering design and drafting (SolidWorks & Solid Edge)
  • CAE capability including linear and non-linear F.E.A.
  • Rapid prototyping including portable 6 axis CMM & 3D laser scanning
  • Production manufacturing including 2D & 3D CAM
  • Highly specialised welding capability including ballistic armour, high nickel alloys, ferrous and non-ferrous metals
  • Niche manufacturing, assembly and integration
  • Military spec spot welding (0.5 mm to 12 mm): ferrous / non-ferrous
  • Customised jigging
  • Laser welding
  • Precision fabrication using industry-leading multi-axis robotic welding automation systems

Painting and blasting

  • Military spec blast and painting facility (APAS, DPP, CARC)
  • Full downdraught spray booth ovens certified to AS/NZS 4114.1 and 4114.2
  • Metal blasting to AS1627
  • 160 cubic metre blast booth and multiple spray booths
