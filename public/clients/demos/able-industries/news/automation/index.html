Enhanced Automation Capability

Able have always been ready, willing and able to take on a challenge, bring our expertise to solve your design and manufacturing challenges and work with our partners to ensure delivery of high quality outcomes, on time and on budget.

We are proud to announce this investment continues, having recently enhanced our capability with significant investment in machines and accreditations to meet your needs, including a new servo drive CNC 30T turret punch (2 m × 5 m × 6.35 mm), 3 kW IPG CNC tube fibre laser (10 m × 410 diameter × 12 mm), 6 kW IPG CNC flat bed fibre laser (4 m × 2 m × 25 mm), multiple CNC ABB and OTC robotic welding systems (10 m × 4200 diameter × 4T), a 160 cubic metre blast booth and multiple spray booths.
