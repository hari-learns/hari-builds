Able is Defence Industry Security Program accredited, and welds to the German military standard.

    51 years

    A family business that kept investing.

    1. 1975

      Established as a small sheet metal fabricator in Melbourne.

    2. 1976

      Bryan Martyn takes ownership at the Spotswood site, with a team of four.

    3. 1990s

      The business moves to its current site in South Kingsville.

    4. 1991

      Certified to ISO 9001, and has been ever since.

    5. 1999

      Stephen Martyn becomes Managing Director.

    6. 2020

      DIN 2303 Q2/BK1 German military welding standard, from GSI SLV: first in Victoria, third in Australia.

    7. 2022

      Joins Team Redback for the composite rubber track system; new laser, punch and robotic welding investment.

    8. 2024

      Defence Industry Security Program (DISP) membership and ISO 45001.
