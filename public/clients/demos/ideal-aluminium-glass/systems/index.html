High-performance glazing systems

To meet the demand for high-quality, high-performance facade systems that withstand the extreme climate of the region, Ideal partners with leading European system manufacturers. It can meet high-performance glazing requirements of local and international statutory bodies with:

  • Alu-K
  • Alumil
  • Reynaers
  • Vistawall International
  • Technal

Thermal break and non-thermal break

Ideal also uses several local and international commercial systems as the client requires, for thermal break and non-thermal break windows and doors, from:

  • Montana system by Gulf Extrusions
  • Balexco system
  • Al Jaber Extrusions
  • Arexal systems by Arabian Extrusions
  • Thermos 110 system by Elite Extrusions
  • Elegant 65 systems by Al Hamad Extrusions
  • Emirates Extrusions
  • White Aluminium
