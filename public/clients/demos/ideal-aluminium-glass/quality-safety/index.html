Quality and safety

Quality control from procurement to site installation.

The quality policy and the health, safety and environment policy as published by Ideal.

Quality, the cornerstone of continuing success

To ensure that all the materials used by Ideal Aluminium & Glass meet international quality standards and fulfil the design, finish and performance requirements of the projects in question, the company follows a strict Quality Control Policy at all stages of operation, from procurement of materials and work preparation through to site installation.

Management will:

  • Monitor client satisfaction and set objectives for continuous improvement.
  • Analyze the causes of any complaint and take appropriate action to prevent recurrence.
  • Ensure the availability and competence of the support resources for the core processes.
  • Provide its services to all clients equally, without discrimination.
  • Provide the necessary work environment to ensure the well-being of employees and visitors.
  • Encourage all employees to identify problems and make suggestions to improve working practices.
  • Ensure that all employees are aware of the Quality Policy and committed to the Quality Management System.
  • Comply with all necessary regulatory and legal requirements.

Health, safety and environment

Ideal Aluminium & Glass firmly believes that the company will install and develop a health and safety culture across the organization, preventing injuries to employees, sub-contractors and persons affected by its activities, and loss of property, plant and equipment.

To achieve this, Ideal will:

  • Implement an effective Health and Safety Management system, so operations follow local, international or customer-specific practice.
  • Ensure responsibility for health and safety at all levels through appropriate delegation of authority.
  • Provide effective training and development so employees can identify and manage the risks of the work.
  • Maintain credible and accurate records of incidents, used to measure continuous improvement.
  • Comply strictly with UAE health and safety legislation and the Technical Guidelines issued by the Environmental, Protection and Safety Section (EPSS).

Policy objectives:

  • Establish safe and environmentally acceptable working practices.
  • Provide a safe and healthy workplace and avoid damage to property and the environment.
  • Prevent all injuries at the workplace.
  • Develop a high degree of safety and environmental awareness.
  • Ensure contractors working on Ideal's behalf implement comparable standards.
  • Seek continual improvement in the efficient use of environmentally friendly products, minimising emissions and waste.
  • Assess, evaluate and monitor risk as an ongoing process.
