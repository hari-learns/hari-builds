How a package runs

From tender to after-sales, under one roof.

Ideal lists all of these as in-house, except structural calculation, which is in house or outsourced if required.

Residential tower under installation, from Ideal's gallery

  1. 01

    Tendering

    Ideal works with main contractors from the tender stage, giving competitive prices and technical submissions for their bids. The in-house estimation team prepares the proposal.

    In house
  2. 02

    Estimation and survey

    Quantity take-off and site survey are done in house.

    In house
  3. 03

    Design and engineering

    The technical and design team handle design and engineering to the project specification. Structural calculation is in house or outsourced if required.

    In house
  4. 04

    Shop drawings

    Detailed shop drawings and structural design calculations are prepared before production, for approval by the consultant and client.

    In house
  5. 05

    Procurement

    Materials available in the UAE are in Ideal's scope. Other materials are procured after the client approves the material technical submission.

    In house
  6. 06

    Fabrication in Al Quoz

    A 15,000 square foot building in Al Quoz Industrial Area 2 holds the fabrication machinery and a well-equipped warehouse.

    In house
  7. 07

    Delivery and logistics

    After inspection, materials are packed and delivered by Ideal's own transportation team.

    In house
  8. 08

    Site installation and supervision

    Installation, site supervision and project management by Ideal's own people.

    In house
  9. 09

    After-sales service

    Maintenance and repair services for the glass and aluminium Ideal installs.

    In house

Company

Founded in 2008, in Al Quoz, Dubai.

The Ideal Aluminium and Glass LLC was founded in 2008 in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, with a mission to meet the ever-growing demand for aluminium and glass products and services. Its commitment and its ability to understand the customer's needs and provide products tailored to the customer's budget without compromising quality have kept its graph growing upward, even when the UAE market was in recession.

Ideal undertakes turnkey architectural projects from villas to commercial complexes, schools and shopping malls, residential buildings, row houses and substations. It is the approved sub-contractor for DEWA and FEWA substations across the UAE.
