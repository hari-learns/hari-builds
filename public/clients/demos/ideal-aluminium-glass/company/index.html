Company

Ideal Aluminium and Glass LLC, founded in Dubai in 2008.

An architectural metal and glass contractor that designs, engineers, procures, manufactures, installs and maintains.

The Ideal Aluminium and Glass LLC was founded in 2008 in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, with a mission to meet the ever-growing demand for aluminium and glass products and services. Its commitment and its ability to understand the customer's needs and provide products tailored to the customer's budget without compromising quality have kept its graph growing upward, even when the UAE market was in recession.

Ideal undertakes turnkey architectural projects from villas to commercial complexes, schools and shopping malls, residential buildings, row houses and substations. It is the approved sub-contractor for DEWA and FEWA substations across the UAE.

Its strength and backbone lie in its people. The management team has many years of proven track record and experience in the Gulf market for architectural aluminium and glass, and most of the fabrication and site installation staff have years of experience in fabrication, production and installation.

Ideal Aluminium and Glass LLC specializes in all types of architectural metal and glass products. In addition to designing, engineering, procuring, manufacturing, installing and maintaining architectural glass and aluminium products, it offers maintenance and repair services: facades, curtain walls, spider glazing, skylights, aluminium composite panel cladding and other building envelope solutions.

Mission

Through continuous technology advancements and continuous improvement of its client interactions, Ideal strives to exceed clients' expectations in quality, timely delivery and cost-effectiveness, and to become the benchmark for excellence in the architectural aluminium and glass industry in the region.

Vision

To become one of the leading companies in architectural glass and aluminium works by inspiring employees to be the best they can be, and to be one of the most specified sub-contractors with the top consultants and clients in the region, known and remembered for quality of work and service.
