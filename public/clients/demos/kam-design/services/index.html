Home renovation & new build · Interior design & decoration · Kitchens & baths · Art · Styling · Construction management

Services

KAM DESIGN provides comprehensive whole home residential interior design solutions including renovations, new builds, kitchen and bath design, architectural detailing, and project/construction management. Interior Designer, Kim Annick Mitchell, creates client-inspired timeless interiors that express clients' design aspirations interpreted through their lifestyle, experiences, interests and aesthetic preferences.

From the first plan to the finished room

  2. 02Living room: custom millwork elevation

  3. 03Living room: custom millwork, final installation

  4. 04Living room: final implemented design

01

Programming

  • Define overall objectives, project scope, budget, schedule, and personal style via individualized interviews
  • Conduct a complete needs assessment
  • Establish functional goals and aesthetic design vision for each space
  • Conduct site survey and inventory of existing furnishings

02

Interior Design

  • Develop and implement innovative and comprehensive design concepts and solutions
  • Create space plans: Demolition/new construction floor plans
  • Generate architectural renderings (furniture plans, elevations, lighting plans)
  • Design and implementation of Kitchens and Baths
  • Design custom millwork (built-ins, vanities, etc.)
  • Employ AutoCAD, InDesign, and PhotoShop software for design representation and presentations
  • Research, recommend, design and specify material, finishes and furnishings
  • Manage fabrication and procurement: Furniture, Wall and Floor Coverings, Lighting, Cabinetry, Fixtures, Counter tops, Decorative Hardware, Window treatments
  • Review and design interior detailing, including architectural modifications
  • Conduct exterior and interior paint and color consultations
  • Craft home staging/styling to create a transformed interior style with existing or new furnishings, lighting, furnishing arrangements, decorative accessories and art

03

Project Leadership & Management

  • Lead overall project process with professionalism and attention to detail, while balancing goals, budget and schedules
  • Supervise bidding process
  • Coordinate, consult and project manage with architect and contractor
  • Review architectural plans and design details with project team
  • Maintain on-going clear and proactive communication with client and project team
  • Represent clients
  • Supervise on-site installations and construction
  • Generate punch list report of all work to be completed after on-site walk-through

Fees are determined post confirmation of project scope.
