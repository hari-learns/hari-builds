HGTV

The Property Brothers

Kim is thrilled to announce her work with HGTV's The Property Brothers as a Design Lead for Buying & Selling with The Property Brothers, Season 5 filmed in Westchester County, NY. Kim was also invited to be Design Lead for The Property Brothers, Season 10 that premiered on 2/15/2017.

  • Season 10, Episode 9: Unexpected Dream Home

    Juggling after-school activities and busy careers, Mark and Eugenie haven't had much time for house hunting. And while the couple has a luxurious list of must-haves, they'll need to compromise if they want to box up their rental situation for good. Encouraging the couple to keep an open mind, a creative range of options are given including a sprawling showstopper that might just deliver everything on their wish list. During demolition, the added repairs add up faster than anyone expected. But even with the budget stretched to the max, the couple rejects nearly every one of the money-saving suggestions.

  • Season 5, Episode 2: Get Us Out of the City

    Joe and Lana want the best for their three young kids, but city living is costing them a fortune and Joe's working seven days a week just to keep the family afloat. The couple is desperate for a new life in the suburbs, but they won't be going anywhere until their current home gets a major makeover. Meanwhile, the search is on for a sprawling suburban home.

  • Season 5, Episode 3: Making Room for Grandma

    With a teenage daughter and grandma just about to move in, Nick and Julie's tiny house is seriously cramping their style. And while they'd love to find a spacious property with room for everyone, their cluttered interiors and the home's over-the-top yellow façade are holding them back. A major makeover is needed but is going to cause sticker shock. An ambitious house hunt ensues where the couple's budget and wish list simply don't add up.

  • Season 5, Episode 8: Baby on the Way

    With a four-year-old and another baby on the way, Mike and Nicole need more room to grow. But while they're eager for an upgrade, their current property is holding them back. The couple's dark and dated décor is given an overhaul, transforming their lackluster interiors into a contemporary showstopper that proves more profitable than ever imagined. Meanwhile, the hunt is on for a dog-friendly home that ticks all the boxes.
