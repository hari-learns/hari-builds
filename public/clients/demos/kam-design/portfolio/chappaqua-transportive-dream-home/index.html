Chappaqua Transportive Dream Home

A highly personalized home inspired by heritage, enriched through global travel, and transformed by artistry and craftsmanship.

The most memorable homes are those that tell the story of the people who live within them. This whole-home transformation was designed for two busy professionals and their young family as a deeply personalized haven to inspire the feeling of elsewhere, without ever leaving home – each space feeling like its own destination. The designs create transportive moments of discovery inspired by their Indian heritage, lifelong passion for global travel, appreciation for artistry and craftsmanship, and their desire to create a meaningful forever home.

From our earliest conversations, the homeowners shared vivid memories of the architectural details, craftsmanship, and atmosphere of the home where they grew up in India. Rather than recreate those memories literally, our goal was to interpret their emotional resonance through original, timeless design—creating spaces that feel familiar in spirit yet entirely new in expression. Throughout the home, antiques, custom furnishings, artisan-made textiles, museum-quality lighting, and collected artwork create interiors that feel thoughtfully assembled over generations rather than decorated all at once.

Upon arrival, the home's dramatic eighteen-foot foyer became the first introduction of the family's story. While architecturally impressive, the volume lacked warmth and identity. Visitors are immediately welcomed by an extraordinary eleven-foot sculptural textile mural wallcovering depicting a mythical Babylonian landscape abundant with lush foliage and elephants—symbols of wisdom, prosperity, and protection that honor the family's heritage. Rendered with remarkable texture reminiscent of carved stone, the artwork establishes the home's artistic narrative from the moment one enters.

Traditional cream damask portière curtains with lagoon-blue tiebacks frame the dining room beyond, creating anticipation and inviting curiosity. Above, an awkward architectural void surrounding a second-story window was transformed into an intentional focal point through a custom upholstered cornice featuring an exquisite embroidered Italian silk border dating to the 1720s. Early twentieth-century Syrian mother-of-pearl folding chairs, discovered at the Paris flea markets, inspired the foyer's seating vignette, establishing the home's collected spirit before guests explore further.

Beyond the drawn curtain, the dining room unfolds as a richly layered destination celebrating heritage, travel, and craftsmanship. An originally hand-painted Iskel mural inspired by a fantastical pre-Raj Indian landscape transforms the walls into storytelling, while a shimmering shagreen ceiling, an Italian Fortuny glass light and sconces that look as if they are growing from the mural lend architectural drama.

A custom Alfonso Marina dining table anchors the room, surrounded by layered textiles, curated antiques, and a custom banquette designed to encourage lingering conversations. Handcrafted furnishings and globally sourced pieces from India, Italy, Portugal, and France create a room that feels collected through a lifetime of travel while remaining deeply personal to the family.

The living room was envisioned as an intimate escape where the adults could step away from the pace of daily life. Wrapped in a lustrous silk wallcovering with subtle texture, the room feels warm, enveloping, and quietly luxurious. Antique carved Turkish doors replace traditional French doors to be able to close the space off from the adjacent playroom, immediately creating the feeling of entering a private lounge. A reproduction of a water gilded mirror from 1710 helps expand the relatively small living space. Handblown glass pendant lighting, Chinoiserie-inspired sconces with fringe, and an intricately carved Indian Jali marble cocktail table celebrate artisan-made while recalling memories of the homeowners' first home in India.

Curated artwork, including Slow Down by Leslie Archer and The Mystics by Isabel Lemay, reinforces the room's dreamlike atmosphere. Tropical embroidered drapery, layered textures, and a refinished French vitrine showcasing treasured travel collections complete a space that feels worldly, welcoming, and timeless.

On the second floor, beyond a custom Moroccan plaster arched vestibule, the primary suite draws inspiration from boutique hotels around the world to create a quiet sanctuary. Custom stained wood screens with integrated LED lighting create a warm, ambient glow at night, while a Venetian reticello glass pendant in hyacinth becomes both sculpture and light.

Rich aubergine accents in the bedside tables and area rug create an atmosphere that feels luxurious. A Louis XV-inspired chair, writing desk and dramatic serpentine abalone shell mirror create an inspiring destination for work or makeup, while Gabe Brown's Solitude, and a whimsical Victorian swan chaise and swivel chair create moments for contemplation and reading. Upholstered cornices trimmed with velvet scallops and beading soften the seating area, completing a bedroom retreat that feels exotic, restorative, and intimate.

Designed to evolve alongside the homeowners' two young boys, the shared bedroom balances sophistication with imagination. A dramatic gunmetal feature wall anchors upholstered twin beds with channeled headboards while a single centered metal bedside unit unites them. Handcrafted hammered lighting, layered wool and sheepskin rugs and flexible open play space create a room that will grow with them over time. Travel-inspired artwork, elephant book basket, and embroidered leather poufs subtly connect the room to the home's larger narrative while encouraging curiosity, creativity, and discovery.

Throughout the residence, every architectural enhancement, material, and collected object was selected with intention—not to follow trends, but to tell the homeowners' story. The result is a timeless home where heritage, artistry, craftsmanship, and personal history converge to create spaces that feel enduring and unmistakably their own.
