Entry Hall

This striking Entry Hall sets the tone for a dynamic young family home. Bold architectural shapes include a custom faceted jewel chandelier, and lapis-inlaid mirror with Yves-Klein blue accents in the fabrics, planter and adjacent dining chairs.

Playful shapes and a mixture of materials make this space unique.
