Dining Spaces · Chappaqua, NY

A Transportive Dining Experience

As part of a comprehensive whole-home transformation, this dining room was conceived as an immersive destination—a space that would honor the homeowners’ Indian heritage, lifelong passion for global travel, appreciation for artistry, and their desire to establish meaningful roots within their new forever home. From our earliest conversations, they shared vivid memories of the details they loved in the home where they grew up in India. Our goal was not to replicate the past, but rather to interpret those emotions and cultural connections through a fresh, sophisticated lens—creating a space that felt both deeply familiar and entirely transformative.

The journey begins from the foyer, where the dining room was intentionally designed to evoke curiosity and a sense of discovery. Wide entry openings to both the foyer and adjoining butler’s pantry are framed with traditional portière curtains in a cream damask fabric, gathered with lagoon blue tiebacks. This architectural gesture creates a threshold between the everyday and a more enchanting world beyond.

Once inside, the room unfolds as a layered narrative of craftsmanship and imagination. The walls become a canvas for storytelling through an original hand-painted Iskel mural depicting a colorful fantasy landscape inspired by pre-Raj India, set above Sea Salt satin-finished wainscoting. Overhead, a shimmering shagreen-printed metallic wallcovering transforms the ceiling into a jewel-like fifth wall, enhancing the room’s dreamlike atmosphere.

A two-tier Italian Fortuny etched glass lantern, whose silhouette recalls an inverted pagoda and is adorned with engraved silver spheres and tassel details, anchors the space with an exotic elegance. Beside a four-door corduroy-textured buffet finished in washed teal and champagne luster with antique bronze star-ring pulls and ferrules, mouth-blown fluted tea-colored glass sconces with bronze arms emerge organically from the mural, further blurring the line between architecture and artistry.

At the center of the room, a custom Alfonso Marina wood dining table with a softened racetrack-shaped top encourages both intimate gatherings and graceful circulation. It rests atop a hand-loomed rug woven with subtle sheened threads, while carefully curated objects—including a maroon phoenix-motif bowl and antique brass candlesticks—lend a sense of collected history.

The seating experience was intentionally layered to encourage guests to linger. Dining chairs upholstered in complementary paisley textiles introduce pattern and richness, while a custom armless corner banquette with a tailored flat skirt and linen tassel trim transforms the room into a relaxed lounge-like retreat. Pillows crafted in green caned raffia over burlap by Portuguese fashion designer Alexandra Oliveira, along with custom Fortuny and Dedar textiles, add a bohemian spirit and tactile warmth.

A handmade fluted maple side table finished in a copper-red ombré with a Crema Marfil stone top by Indian design studio INDO introduces a contemporary counterpoint, beautifully juxtaposed with a vintage 1930 Daum Nancy French glass table lamp. An oval pedestal accent table with a sculptural knobbed stem and curved feet offers additional surfaces for food and drink, supporting the room’s purpose as a place where conversation continues long after the meal has ended.

The result is a dining room that transcends function—a richly layered, transportive environment where heritage, craftsmanship, global influences, and personal storytelling converge. The homeowners often describe the experience as feeling as though they have traveled somewhere far away when they enter the space—exactly the sense of wonder, escape, and emotional connection the design was intended to create.
