Sriperumbudur: buying a plot where the factories are

Car plants, electronics makers and logistics parks within a short drive. Why the town on the Bengaluru highway is one of the most watched plot markets.

Few towns near Chennai have as many large employers within a short drive as Sriperumbudur. The car plants, electronics makers and logistics parks around it have turned a historic temple town into one of the most watched plot markets on the Bengaluru highway.

Who works nearby

Merloam's Heritage page lists more than twenty employers around the layout, including Hyundai Motor India, Renault Nissan, Daimler India Commercial Vehicles, Royal Enfield's Oragadam plant, Saint-Gobain, Samsung, Foxconn, Asian Paints and CEAT.

What is coming

  • The proposed greenfield airport at Parandur
  • A proposed metro rail link towards Parandur
  • The Chennai Peripheral Ring Road
  • The Chennai–Bengaluru expressway and the existing national highway

As with any proposal, timelines move. The employers are already there, and that is what keeps demand for homes steady in the town.

Heritage, Sriperumbudur

A 5-acre layout in the heart of town: 125 plots of 587 to 2,689 sq ft, DTCP / RERA approved, from ₹15 lakh, with solar street lights, stormwater drainage, an entrance arch with gate and a park.
