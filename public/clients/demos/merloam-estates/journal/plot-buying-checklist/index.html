Before you book a plot: a nine-point checklist

There is no building to inspect, so everything you need to know is on paper and on the ground. Nine checks to cover before you pay an advance.

A plot is the simplest kind of property to own and one of the easiest to get wrong. There is no building to inspect, so everything you need to know is on paper and on the ground.

  1. 01

    Approval number

    DTCP or CMDA approval for the layout, and the RERA registration where it applies. Check that your plot number appears on the approved plan.

  2. 02

    Patta

    The revenue record that shows who owns the land. It should be in the seller's name, or the promoter's for a new layout.

  3. 03

    Encumbrance certificate

    The EC lists registered transactions on the land. Ask for one covering a long period (many buyers and banks ask for 30 years) and check that it is clear.

  4. 04

    Parent documents

    The chain of sale deeds that shows how the land reached its current owner.

  5. 05

    Plot details

    Dimensions, total area, facing and position on the layout. Corner and main-road plots are usually priced differently.

  6. 06

    Road width

    The road in front of your plot decides what you can build and how easy it is to reach. Merloam's layouts list 24 to 60 ft roads depending on the project.

  7. 07

    Water and drainage

    Ask about ground water and stormwater drains. If you can, visit once after heavy rain.

  8. 08

    Loan pre-approval

    If you need a loan, speak to the bank before paying an advance. Merloam lists SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, PNB and Tata Capital as banking partners.

  9. 09

    Visit twice

    Once on a weekday morning, once in the evening. Drive the route you would take to work.

None of this is unusual to ask. If a seller hesitates over any of it, that is your answer.
