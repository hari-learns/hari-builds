About the layout

Easton Enclave is a 3-acre secured community of residential plots located within the 85-acre SSM Nagar township in Perungalathur. Located just 3 km from the Perungalathur bus stand, this region is well connected to major roads and social infrastructure.

The location is serene and offers a pollution-free environment with pristine air.
