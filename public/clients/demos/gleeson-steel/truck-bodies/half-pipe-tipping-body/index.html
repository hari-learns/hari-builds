The Gleeson Half Pipe Tipper

Ireland’s most recognisable workhorse.

32T and 36T rigid chassisHardox® 500

Striking in design and unmatched in performance, the Gleeson Half Pipe Tipper is our signature body and the most popular choice for operators across Ireland. With its bold, curved profile and rugged build, it’s a familiar sight on roads, in quarries, and on construction sites nationwide.

Available for 32T and 36T rigid chassis, this body is crafted entirely from Hardox® 500, the industry’s toughest wear-resistant steel. The rounded design not only turns heads: it improves material flow, reduces sticking, and enhances tipping efficiency.

Every body is fully shotblasted, primed, and finished in 2-pack polyurethane paint, available in any colour. We use only trusted hydraulic systems from Binotto and Hyva, ensuring smooth, reliable tipping every time.
