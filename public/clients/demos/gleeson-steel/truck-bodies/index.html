Truck bodies built to the chassis, the load and the fleet

Gleeson Truck Bodies: design and fabrication of custom bodies, built for strength, functionality, and long-lasting performance.

Every body is shotblasted or sandblasted, primed and finished in 2-pack polyurethane paint, available in any colour to match your fleet. Whether you are outfitting a single vehicle or an entire fleet, the team works with each customer on every detail, from the first weld to the final finish.

  • Listed on the NSAI register of Approved Vehicle Body Builders (AVBB scheme).
  • Hydraulics from Binotto and Hyva on the half pipe tipper
  • Any colour, finished in 2-pack polyurethane paint
