Rear tipper bodies: versatile, durable and built to spec

From construction sites to waste management.

7.5T to 26T chassisFixed sides or dropsides

From construction sites to waste management, the Gleeson Rear Tipper Body is the ultimate all-rounder, designed to handle a wide variety of applications with strength, reliability, and complete customisation.

Available for trucks from 7.5T to 26T, each rear tipper is individually built to suit your chassis and operational needs. Whether you need fixed sides for added strength or dropsides for flexible loading, we tailor every build to your exact requirements.

Every body is fully shotblasted, primed, and finished to the highest industry standards, ensuring long-lasting protection and a professional finish.

Custom features

  • Access ladders and grab handles for safe, easy entry
  • Fall arrest systems for operator safety
  • Underbody or front-end tipping gear to suit your preference
  • Tailboard options including tar chutes, barn door configurations, and top, bottom, or dual-hinged setups
  • Full range of colours, finished in durable 2-pack polyurethane paint
