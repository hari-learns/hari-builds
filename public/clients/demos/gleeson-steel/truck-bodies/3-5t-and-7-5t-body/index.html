3.5T and 7.5T bodies: built for strength, designed for you

Rear tippers, three-way tippers and dropsides.

3.5 tonne and 7.5 tonneSteel, aluminium or hybrid

At Gleeson Truck Bodies, we specialise in crafting high-performance truck bodies that deliver durability, versatility, and style. Our 3.5 tonne and 7.5 tonne ranges are engineered to meet the toughest demands of modern transport and construction industries.

Whether you’re looking for rear tippers, three-way tippers, or dropside bodies, we offer fully customisable solutions tailored to your exact needs. Choose from robust steel, lightweight aluminium, or a hybrid construction that balances strength and efficiency.

Every body is finished in a fully powder-coated coating, available in any colour to match your fleet or brand identity.

Optional extras

  • Tail lifts and in body crane solutions for effortless loading and unloading
  • Fully customisable mesh extensions for sideboards and rear doors
  • Integrated toolboxes for secure storage
  • And much more: if you can imagine it, we can build it
