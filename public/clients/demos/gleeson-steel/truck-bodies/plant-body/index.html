Plant bodies built for heavy lifting, engineered for precision

Transporting plant and machinery.

Cheese wedge, flip toe and beavertail rampsKeruing, chequer plate or mesh floors

When it comes to transporting plant and machinery, Gleeson Plant Bodies deliver the strength, reliability, and flexibility your business demands. Designed with the same robust engineering as our flatbed range, our plant bodies are purpose-built to handle the toughest loads with ease.

Available in a wide range of lengths, widths, and configurations, each body is fully customisable to suit your specific truck chassis and operational needs. Whether you’re moving diggers, dumpers, or tracked equipment, we’ve got the perfect solution.

Rear ramp options

  • Cheese wedge ramps for smooth loading angles
  • Flip toe ramps for added clearance and versatility
  • Beavertail configurations for low-profile loading and stability
  • Each ramp can be finished in steel, hardwood, or mesh coverings

Floor options

  • 28mm Keruing hardwood for natural grip and resilience
  • Steel chequer plate for heavy-duty durability
  • Steel catwalk mesh for enhanced drainage and traction
  • And more, to suit your load type and working environment
