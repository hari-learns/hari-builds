Our steel kiosks are strong, secure and built for a long service life. They suit a wide range of uses, from roadside cabinets to small pump stations, and are designed for easy access.

Every kiosk can be tailored to your project, with options for colour, finish, U-value and more. We can also fit them out with full electrical and water services, so they arrive ready to install.

Alongside our standard sizes, we build to bespoke dimensions. Whatever size you need, we’d welcome the chance to quote.

Our kiosk services include

  • Complete electrical wiring and installations
  • Water supply and plumbing integration
  • Durable steel construction for weather resistance
  • Custom layouts designed to your specifications
  • Installation of lighting, outlets, and safety systems
  • Compliance with all safety and industry regulations

GRP kiosks

Our GRP (glass reinforced plastic) kiosks give durable, non-corrosive protection in demanding environments. They resist weather, chemicals and impact, making them ideal for outdoor, marine and industrial sites. They’re lightweight yet strong, so they’re easy to transport and install. We keep a range of standard sizes in stock for quick delivery, and we also build to bespoke dimensions.
