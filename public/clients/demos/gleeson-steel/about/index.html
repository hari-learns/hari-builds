Founded in 1968 by the Gleeson family, Gleeson Steel & Engineering Ltd. has grown into one of the leading steel fabricators in the Munster region. With over 40 years of experience, we have continuously evolved to meet the demands of a competitive steel industry by adopting the latest technology and maintaining exceptional standards in craftsmanship.

Our company’s journey is rooted in a commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction, which has earned us the trust of clients across agricultural and commercial sectors.

Our expertise

At Gleeson Steel & Engineering, we specialize in the design, fabrication, and installation of high-quality structural steel solutions and farm buildings. Our services include:

  • Structural steel frameworks
  • Custom farm buildings (dairy units, beef sheds, stables, poultry houses)
  • General fabrication projects
  • Shed fit-outs and kiosks

The plant

Our modern 24,600 sq. ft. manufacturing plant in Thurles, Co. Tipperary is equipped with advanced machinery, including:

  • CNC saw and drill line
  • CNC angle line
  • CNC plate line
  • Spray paint workshop
  • Powder coating plant

These tools enable us to deliver precise fabrication with consistent quality and fast turnaround times.

Quality and compliance

We pride ourselves on maintaining strict quality control and safety standards. All our farm buildings comply with Department of Agriculture grant specifications (S101), and our steel fabrication meets I.S. EN1090-1:2009+A1:2011 standards for CE marking up to Execution Class 2 (EXC 2).

Our Factory Production Control System includes rigorous inspections and testing, and we provide clients with full certification packages including:

  • Factory Production Control Certificate
  • Welding Certificate
  • Declaration of Performance

Safety and on-site expertise

Safety is a cornerstone of our operations. Our qualified steel erectors are trained extensively in on-site safety protocols. We regularly conduct health and safety inspections to comply with Irish regulations including the Safety, Health & Welfare at Work Act 2005 and Construction Regulations 2006.

Both staff and machinery adhere to the highest safety regulations. We recently achieved our Safe-T certification and we operate a health and safety management system in accordance with the requirements of the Safe-T Certification System.
