Founded in 1968 by the Gleeson family

A leading steel fabricator for the Munster region and beyond.

Gleeson Steel & Engineering Ltd., founded in 1968, is a leading steel fabricator based in Thurles, Co. Tipperary. We provide expert steel engineering solutions to both commercial and agricultural sectors across the Munster region and beyond.

Our dedicated team of designers, fabricators, and site erectors work closely with clients to ensure every project meets exact specifications and industry standards.

Our story
The plant

Cut, drilled, bent, welded and painted in one place.

Our state-of-the-art 24,600 sq. ft. facility is equipped with the latest CNC machinery, allowing us to design and manufacture precision structural steel products, from farm buildings and workshops to bespoke fabrication projects.
