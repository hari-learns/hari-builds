SMARTIA MF65 Thermal Insulated Folding Door

SMARTIA MF65 is a thermally insulated system for folding doors with minimal design and little visible aluminium. It offers excellent thermal insulation, and high watertightness performance and is ideal for large constructions, remaining flawless over time.

Advantages

  • Elevates the aesthetics of your place, thanks to its minimal design and the very thin profiles (88mm sightline).
  • Achieves top performance in terms of air permeability, water tightness & wind load resistance.
  • Increases energy efficiency and cost savings, thanks to the high thermal insulation (up to Uw=1.1 W/m2K)
  • Allows robust large structures (up to 1.3m width or up to 3m height)
  • Provides enhanced levels of burglary protection, with quadruple latches, 3-point locking, and a special anti-lift mechanism.
  • Offers a wide variety of solutions with an odd number of vents, double or triple glazing (24-44mm), and standard and low thresholds.
  • Ensures smooth and flawless operation, without any problems, even at the open position, thanks to its high-quality, heavy-duty roller hinges.
  • Easy fabrication and installation, with adjustable roller hinges & locking latches, same frame profile perimetrically, without any special machining required, etc.
