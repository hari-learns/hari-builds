ALUMIL SMARTIA M35

SMARTIA M35 is a cost-effective curtain wall system that excellently merges aesthetics with high performance. With a minimal design featuring a reduced sightline of 35mm—narrower than typical curtain wall systems—SMARTIA M35 is perfect for Transparent Architecture.

Advantages

  • Enhancement of natural light
  • Ideal for constructions needing a minimal look, such as shopfronts
  • Excellent energy efficiency
  • Certified performance in air, water tightness, and wind pressure resistance
  • Increased static robustness, even under extreme natural conditions
  • Supports various hinged typologies: projected, parallel projected, and tilt-and-turn windows
  • Stands out from the competition
  • Compatible with sliding typologies
