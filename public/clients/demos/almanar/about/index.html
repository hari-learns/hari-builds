About

About us

Since its inception in 1980, ALMANAR ALUMINIUM has been delivering superior quality aluminum fabrication services to the construction industry in the United Arab Emirates and the MENA region. Our extensive experience, technical expertise, and innovative approach enable us to consistently exceed our clients' expectations.

Our main office is located in Dubai, UAE, providing easy accessibility to the Middle East market. In 2017, we expanded our operations and added a modern fabrication facility in Alexandria, Egypt, to serve our clients in North Africa.

At ALMANAR ALUMINIUM, we specialize in facade works, curtain walls, doors, and windows, and glazing works. Our in-house team of engineers, designers, project managers, and skilled craftsmen ensure every project we undertake is completed with optimum accuracy, precision, and quality.

We work collaboratively with our clients to understand their design and functional requirements and offer custom solutions that meet their unique needs. Our commitment to quality and safety is unwavering, and we ensure that every project we deliver is safe, on-time, and within budget.

We take pride in our exceptional customer service, and our clients can expect prompt, reliable, and transparent communication with us at each stage of their project. Our goal is to build long-lasting relationships with our clients based on trust, mutual respect, and exceptional quality of work.

History

We are a leading aluminum fabrication company that has been serving the construction industry since 1980. Our main office is located in Dubai, UAE, with a fabrication facility in Alexandria, Egypt, since 2017, that enables us to serve our clients in the Middle East and North Africa region. We take pride in offering a wide range of high-quality aluminum fabrication services, including facade works, curtain walls, doors and windows, and glazing works. We work closely with our clients to ensure that their visions are brought to life through aesthetic and functional designs.

7 March 2026

Almanar Iftar Party

This year marks 35 years of excellence in the aluminium and façade industry. Founded in 1989 by Eng. Abdulsalam Mahmoud, ALMANAR was built on two core principles:

1. Commitment to our people – ensuring timely salary payments.

2. Commitment to our suppliers – maintaining trust through timely payments.

Under Eng. Ayman Abdulsalam's leadership, ALMANAR has strengthened its position by partnering with leading international aluminium system suppliers and building strong relationships with reputable developers across the UAE and MENA region, contributing to prestigious projects.

We proudly expanded our new 17,000 SQFT production facility in Saja Industrial Area, equipped with state-of-the-art CNC and fabrication technology.
