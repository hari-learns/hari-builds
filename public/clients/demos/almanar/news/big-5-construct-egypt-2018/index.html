Big 5 Construct Egypt 2018

We would like to express our gratitude to all those who visited #almanar aluminium at #TheBig5ConstructEgypt. The exhibition proved to be a tremendous success, providing us with an invaluable platform to showcase the latest advancements in construction, cutting-edge solutions, and best practices within the aluminum industry. Additionally, we had the opportunity to present the various applications of aluminum with special finishes specifically tailored for the construction sector.

We are thrilled by the significant turnout from diverse industries, including engineers, architects, consultants, project managers, suppliers, and university students. It was a pleasure to welcome you to our innovative and captivating stand, where we demonstrated real-life examples to unveil the latest trends, groundbreaking solutions, contemporary designs, and state-of-the-art technologies that establish us as a leading company in the aluminum industry.
