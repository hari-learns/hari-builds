Description

AL BAHAR HOTEL & RESORT, situated in Fujairah, offers guests a remarkable stay just 5.3 km away from Fujairah Mall. This exquisite establishment boasts a range of amenities, including a restaurant, free private parking, an inviting outdoor swimming pool, and a well-equipped fitness center. With a focus on both comfort and aesthetics, ALUMIL sliding and curtain wall systems are incorporated throughout the property, ensuring superior performance and enhancing the breathtaking sea view
