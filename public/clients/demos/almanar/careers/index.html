Project / Site EngineerFacade

As a Project/Site Engineer specializing in façade at ALMANAR, you will play a pivotal role in overseeing and managing façade projects from conception to completion. Your technical expertise, leadership, and project management skills will ensure the successful execution of projects, contributing to the continued growth and reputation of our company.

Responsibilities

  • Project Management: lead façade projects, ensuring they are completed on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards.
  • Technical Expertise: utilize your expertise in façade engineering to develop innovative solutions and resolve technical challenges.
  • Site Supervision: oversee on-site activities, coordinate with subcontractors, and ensure compliance with safety regulations.
  • Team Leadership: manage and mentor project teams, fostering collaboration and excellence in performance.
  • Client Interaction: maintain strong client relationships by providing regular updates, addressing concerns, and ensuring client satisfaction.
  • Quality Assurance: implement and maintain quality control measures to uphold the company's commitment to excellence.
  • Cost Management: monitor project budgets, identify cost-saving opportunities, and manage expenses effectively.
  • Documentation: prepare and maintain project documentation, including reports, drawings, and progress reports.
  • Health and Safety: promote a culture of safety and ensure that all work is conducted in accordance with safety guidelines and regulations.
  • Problem Solving: proactively identify and address project-related issues and risks to prevent delays and disruptions.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree or Diploma in Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field.
  • Minimum of 3-5 years of experience in façade engineering and project management.
  • Proven track record of successfully managing façade projects.
  • Strong technical knowledge of façade materials, systems, and construction methods.
  • Exceptional leadership and team management skills.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Proficiency in project management software and tools.
  • Knowledge of relevant safety regulations and standards.
For applying, send your resume to careers@almanar-aluminium.com
Site SupervisorFaçade Specialist

As a Site Supervisor specializing in façade works, you will play a pivotal role in ensuring the successful execution of our projects. Your leadership and expertise will contribute to the delivery of high-quality façade solutions, maintaining our reputation for excellence in the industry.

Responsibilities

  • Site Management: oversee all on-site activities, ensuring compliance with safety regulations, project specifications, and timelines.
  • Quality Assurance: implement and maintain rigorous quality control measures to uphold the highest standards of workmanship.
  • Team Leadership: lead and motivate site teams, including subcontractors, to achieve project objectives efficiently and safely.
  • Project Coordination: collaborate closely with project managers and engineers to ensure seamless project execution.
  • Safety Compliance: promote a culture of safety, conduct regular safety meetings, and ensure adherence to safety protocols.
  • Material Management: monitor and manage the procurement and usage of materials to minimize waste and control costs.
  • Client Interaction: serve as a point of contact for clients, addressing concerns and providing progress updates.
  • Problem Solving: proactively identify and address site-related challenges and issues to prevent delays and disruptions.
  • Documentation: maintain accurate records of site activities, including daily reports, inspection logs, and incident reports.
  • Training and Development: provide guidance and mentorship to site personnel, fostering their growth and development.

Requirements

  • Minimum of 3-5 years of experience in aluminum façade works, with a focus on site supervision.
  • Strong leadership and team management skills.
  • In-depth knowledge of façade construction and materials.
  • Commitment to quality assurance and control.
  • Familiarity with safety regulations and best practices.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced environment.
  • Problem-solving and decision-making abilities.
For applying, send your resume to careers@almanar-aluminium.com
Draughtsman / Quantity SurveyorFaçade works

As a Draughtsman/Quantity Surveyor specializing in façade works, you will be responsible for producing detailed drawings and Quantities Take-Off for our projects. Your expertise will ensure accurate project planning, cost control, and adherence to quality standards in our façade solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Technical Drawings: create precise technical drawings and plans for façade projects, including elevations, sections, and details.
  • Quantity Estimation: accurately estimate material quantities required for projects, considering specifications and architectural drawings.
  • Material Procurement: collaborate with procurement teams to source and evaluate materials, ensuring cost-effectiveness and quality.
  • Quality Assurance: assist in quality control processes to maintain high standards of workmanship and meet project requirements.
  • Documentation: maintain accurate project documentation, including drawings, estimates, and change orders.
  • Project Coordination: work closely with project managers, engineers, and site supervisors to ensure project success.
  • Compliance: ensure compliance with industry standards, codes, and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Minimum of 3-5 years of experience in aluminum façade works, with expertise in both draughtsmanship and quantity surveying.
  • Proficiency in AutoCAD or similar drafting software.
  • Strong knowledge of façade materials, systems, and construction methods.
  • Excellent quantitative and analytical skills.
  • Attention to detail and accuracy in estimating and drafting.
  • Effective communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Ability to work independently and meet project deadlines.
  • Familiarity with industry regulations and standards.
For applying, send your resume to careers@almanar-aluminium.com
