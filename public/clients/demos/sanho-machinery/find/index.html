Machine finder

Find the Sanho machine for your product

Say what you want to do, enter your product and label size, and see which machines cover it. Every answer is checked against the numbers on Sanho's own specification pages.

How the check works

  • Every range comes from the specification table on the machine's own page on sanho.co.kr: label size, item size, thickness, speed, print height.
  • Where a page does not publish a number (for example the item size of the ALU-130), the finder says so and does not guess.
  • Label sizes are accepted in either direction; carton corner sizes are matched in any order of the three sides.
  • Where Sanho's English and Korean pages disagree on speed, the lower number is used and the difference is shown on the machine's page.

Sanho groups its machines by container shape: round labelers for cylinders, top labelers for meat trays and boxes, sealing labelers for pharma and cosmetic cartons, carton and pouch labelers, real-time print labelers for QR codes, expiry dates and barcodes, and roll-label printers.

Tell us what you label. We will tell you which machine fits.

Sanho answers by phone and email from its Seoul office in Geumcheon-gu.
