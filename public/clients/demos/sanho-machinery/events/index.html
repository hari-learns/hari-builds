In the press

  1. Dec 2024

    Sanho news page

    Four decades of label technology, a witness to Korea's label industry

    On the eve of its 40th anniversary Sanho describes how the IMF crisis pushed it to build its own machines. The pouch print-and-apply labeler is only 700 mm long and uses a double motor and a separating device so that two pouches are never fed together; label accuracy is within 1.5 mm.

    Translated from Sanho's Korean page for this concept; to be confirmed by Sanho.

  2. 2022

    Money Today

    Sanho wins the 2022 Brand Power Grand Prize in product innovation

    Recognised for the SCR-200 one-touch cartoner. Every part is modular, so a finished machine can be delivered within two weeks of the order, and service continues after delivery.

    Translated from Sanho's Korean page for this concept; to be confirmed by Sanho.

  3. 2022

    Sanho news page

    One-touch cartoning at KOREA PACK 2022 and a space-saving rotary cartoner at SEOUL FOOD 2022

    Two trade-show reports on the cartoners that run in line with Sanho's labelers.

    Translated from Sanho's Korean page for this concept; to be confirmed by Sanho.

  4. Feb 2019

    Yakup Sinmun

    Kim Jong-cheol inaugurated as 13th Chairman of the Korea Packaging Machinery Association

  5. Apr 2019

    Sanho launches real-time printing and labelling systems at COPHEX 2019
