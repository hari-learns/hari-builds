About Sanho

Sanho Machinery was established in 1984, and its major products include labelers and inkjet and laser markers.

It supplies sticker labelers, from high-speed labelers of high precision for the pharmaceutical industry to semi-automatic labelers for foodstuffs, and real-time printing with validation and screening systems (Track and Trace) for pharmaceutical boxes.

The CO2 laser marker the company developed is one of the world's leading products, capable of marking 170 meters per minute.

We have acquired ISO 9001 and INNOBIZ certifications, including CE certification for the major products for export. Sanho Machinery will grow as a beloved business and contribute to the lives of the people by supplying products recognized as top-tier to markets worldwide through continuing R&D.

Founder and chairman Kim Jong-cheol started in 1984 importing stamps, type and ink. After the company was incorporated in 1994 it sold inkjet printers and pharmaceutical box stampers. During the IMF crisis he chose to build machines himself, starting with a label separator and the first semi-automatic labelers, and those labelers opened the US market.

Milestones

  1. 1984

    Sanho is founded

    Established in November 1984 in Seoul, handling stamp products from the USA (MARSH), stamp ink from Japan and Germany and stencil machines.

  2. 1990s

    Marking for pharma

    MORICO stamps supplied to 200 domestic pharmaceutical companies; ALPHA DOT and HP TIJ 2.5 inkjet brought to Korea. During the IMF crisis Sanho starts developing labelers and exports a semi-automatic labeler to Japan.

  3. 2001-02

    Own labelers abroad

    Semi-automatic labelers exported to France (2001) and the USA (2002); label separators, auto and semi-auto labelers launched.

  4. 2008

    Print & Apply

    After the financial crisis Sanho develops its print-and-apply labeler and widens the labeler range.

  5. 2010-11

    Ten export countries

    Exports reach 10 countries in 2010; laser marker supply starts in 2011.

  6. 2014

    Own laser, own R&D centre

    Ultra-fast CO2 laser markers assembled in-house, an R&D centre set up, CE certification for exported machines.

  7. 2015

    INNOBIZ and a brand prize

    INNOBIZ certification, the Money Today Brand Power award, and Ganghwa Plant 1 completed.

  8. 2017-19

    Digital printers

    Astronova colour printers join the range: digital label printers T3 and the QL series.

  9. 2020

    Rotary cartoner

    Supply of the rotary cartoner starts.

  10. 2022

    Brand Power Grand Prize

    Money Today's Brand Power Grand Prize in product innovation, and a ministerial commendation for Kim Jeong-su.

  11. 2024

    Family-friendly company

    Selected as a family-friendly certified company; exhibits at PACK EXPO Chicago, K-PRINT, KOREA PACK and SEOUL FOOD.

  12. 2026

    interpack Düsseldorf

    Exhibits at KOREA PACK (spring), interpack Düsseldorf (May, Hall 6) and K-PRINT (August).
