About Sanho

Sanho Machinery manufactures and supplies only world-class products.

Sanho Machinery was established in 1984, and its major products include labelers and inkjet and laser markers.

It supplies sticker labelers, from high-speed labelers of high precision for the pharmaceutical industry to semi-automatic labelers for foodstuffs, and real-time printing with validation and screening systems (Track and Trace) for pharmaceutical boxes.

Milestones

  1. 2008

    Print & Apply

    After the financial crisis Sanho develops its print-and-apply labeler and widens the labeler range.

  2. 2010-11

    Ten export countries

    Exports reach 10 countries in 2010; laser marker supply starts in 2011.

  3. 2014

    Own laser, own R&D centre

    Ultra-fast CO2 laser markers assembled in-house, an R&D centre set up, CE certification for exported machines.

  4. 2015

    INNOBIZ and a brand prize

    INNOBIZ certification, the Money Today Brand Power award, and Ganghwa Plant 1 completed.

  5. 2017-19

    Digital printers

    Astronova colour printers join the range: digital label printers T3 and the QL series.

  6. 2020

    Rotary cartoner

    Supply of the rotary cartoner starts.
