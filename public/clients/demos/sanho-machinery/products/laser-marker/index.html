What it does

  • Marking date of manufacture, lot number, 2D barcode, etc. with laser.
  • Marked characters and 2D barcodes ∙ Defect removal
  • Marking after stopping the product to ensure quality assurance than movable marking
  • Excellent clarity to realize bar code grade B or higher
  • Large loading method is used to load and pack one package at the same time
  • Zero balance as well as error-free marking compared to inkjet
