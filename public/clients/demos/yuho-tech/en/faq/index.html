Storage or instant heater: which suits an apartment?

The Jhao Fong “storage + fast-heat” heater suits small households that want efficiency, little space and low energy use.

Flexible placement: thin wall-mounted body for rear balconies and small bathrooms.

Fast: the cold/hot double tank gives hot water in 5–8 minutes.

Energy label grade 1 or 2 with good insulation.

Small tank, more hot water than its volume; Taiwanese and imported electronics; standard heating elements that are easy to source.

How many gallons for 2–3 people?

A conventional storage heater needs about 10 gallons per person, so 20–30 gallons for 2–3 people, and 20–30 minutes to heat; for baths choose 30 gallons or more.

Jhao Fong's storage-plus-fast-heat structure: one bathroom AH-830 (30-gallon output), two bathrooms AH-1035 (35-gallon output), heavy use AH-1245 (45-gallon output).

Control panel: on-unit (power, manual temperature, ATS) or split panel (plus three timers). Heat-up 5–8 minutes; wall-mounted.

Setting heating periods on the split panel

Set both on and off times to complete a period. Periods must not cross 00:00.

For 22:00 to 02:00 use two periods: 22:00 on, 23:59 off; 00:01 on, 02:00 off.

How to set all-day heating

Split panel: set one period, on 01:05, off 24:55, and a temperature; never 00:00 or across it.

On-unit touch panel: after powering on, without a timed shut-off it heats all day.

Boost heating

Press once to heat for 2 hours; press again to stop immediately.

While active, long-press the up key to set the boost temperature.
