Electric water heaters

As thin as an air-conditioner outdoor unit

The Jhao Fong electric heater uses a double inner tank and is only 29 cm deep, mounted upright or on its side. Mirror-finish stainless outer shell, 304 high-nickel low-carbon stainless inner tank.

A patented vortex tube spins incoming cold water in a small tank; once heated it passes through a guide tube to the second tank, so only a small volume is heated at a time.

  • 29 cmbody depth
  • 5–8 minheat-up time (their FAQ)
  • 3 levelsprotection: electronic and mechanical thermostat, over-temperature cut-off
  • ATSpatented sensing of outdoor temperature to set heating temperature

About Yuho

One factory, from design to service

Since its founding in 2000, Yuho has integrated R&D, design, manufacturing, sales and after-sales service, so it controls product quality and the pace of innovation.

  • Distinctive hot-water technologyHeat-pipe solar (German technology, the only one in Taiwan), own flat-plate collectors, thin low-energy electric heaters.
  • Smart controlDual-CPU outdoor-temperature control (the only one in Taiwan) adjusts hot-water temperature automatically.
