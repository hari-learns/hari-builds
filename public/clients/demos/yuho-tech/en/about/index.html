About Yuho Technology

Constant R&D, improvement and innovation, for products that give good value, last, and are human and technical.

Vertically integrated green-energy and home-safety specialist

Founded in May 2000, Yuho has grown steadily in Dounan, Yunlin. Its strength is full integration of R&D, design, manufacturing, sales and after-sales service, giving it control over quality and the speed of innovation.

  • Distinctive hot-water technologyHeat-pipe solar (German technology, the only one in Taiwan), own flat-plate collectors, thin low-energy electric heaters.
  • Smart controlDual-CPU outdoor-temperature control (the only one in Taiwan) adjusts hot-water temperature automatically.
  • Home-safety partsStainless corrugated and insulated pipe, motor stands, level controllers, basin brackets.

Culture: customer-centred and always refining

Yuho believes after-sales service is an extension of the company's vitality. Customer feedback goes directly into product development, so products keep improving.
