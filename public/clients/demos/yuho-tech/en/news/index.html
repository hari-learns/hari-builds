    Press feature: the vortex heating and thin design

    Taming the power-hungry heater: Jhao Fong's patented energy-saving design

    2020-07-31

    • Vortex heating: the single tank becomes two, and a vortex in the 4-gallon tank means only 2 gallons are heated at a time, so hot water is ready in 5–10 minutes.
    • ATS: senses outdoor temperature and sets the heating temperature, for example 65 °C in winter and about 45 °C in summer, instead of always heating to 65 °C.
    • Thin design: 29 cm deep and 60 cm tall, roughly half the volume of a conventional heater, mirror-finish stainless shell.
