Electric water heaters

Yuho designs and builds its electric water heaters entirely in Taiwan, with an emphasis on performance and saving space. The thin wall-mounted body suits small homes and tight layouts.

  • Curved lines and a mirror-finish stainless shell
  • Patented double inner tank for more hot water
  • ATS sensing saves energy and sets temperature automatically
  • Touch panel with timer and set temperature
  • Three layers of safety protection

Advantages (from Yuho)

  • Frost-proofNo water in the tubes, so they do not freeze and crack.
  • Fast startHeat gathered by the tubes reaches the tank quickly.
  • Pressure-ratedCopper-sealed tank connection runs on mains pressure; one broken tube does not stop the heater.
  • SeparableTank and collector can be separated, ideal for central hot-water systems.
  • No scaleNo water enters the tube, so efficiency does not fade; nano-coated surface.
  • Leak-proofMechanical copper sleeve joints, no rubber seals.
  • Island climateVacuum-tube insulation holds heat in cold winds.
  • Used inHomes, businesses, factory dormitories, hotels, boiler pre-heating.
