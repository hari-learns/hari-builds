Thirty-seven years, one shop floor

From one machine to 5-axis and AS9100D.

  1. 1989 – 1992

    Established as a one-man-one machine shop. Focused on supply of precision components for the electronics industry.

  2. 1992 – 1995

    Invested in our first CNC machine to focus on the supply of precision turned parts for the disk drive industry.

  3. 1995

    Privatized to incorporate additional capital for expansion.

  4. 2001

    Consolidated all our facilities and shifted to Kapo Factory Building.

  5. 2006 – 2012

    Marketing network expanded to oil & gas, aerospace, green technologies and biomedical industries.

  6. 2012

    Obtained ISO 9001-2008 certification. Shifted to new 4,760 sq ft production plant & office at Bendemeer Road.

  7. 2014 – 2015

    Acquired 4th Axis CNC Machining Capability.

  8. 2018

    Acquired Sheet Metal Fabrication Capability.

  9. 2019

    Acquired Cast Parts Capability.

  10. 2020

    Acquired 5 Axis CNC Machining Capability.

  11. 2021

    Acquired 3D Plastic & Metal Printing Capability.

  12. 2023

    Obtained Quality Management Systems for Aviation, Space, and Defense Organizations: AS9100D.

Quality

ISO 9001 and AS9100D.

We are a ISO 9001:2008 certified company with a tracking system for every batch of order placed by our customers. Our quality inspection center comprises of all essential QA instrument and equipment such as Carl Zeiss Vista CNC CMM for contact measurement and Mitutoyo PH-3500 for profile measurement.

First article, in-process, and final inspections are consistently conducted on all products to ensure quality in every batch produced by Sunyi Precision Engineering. Material certificates, Certificate of Conformance and Inspection Reports are available upon request at no additional charge.
