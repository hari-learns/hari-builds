Welding Services

Orbital and manual welding in Singapore

Orbital, laser, TIG and MIG welding of steel, stainless steel and aluminium, with orbital machines developed in house.

Manual and orbital welding.

We perform manual and automated rotary welding operations for our machined components. At Sunyi, we pride ourselves on our welding ability and knowledge of the materials being joined and have developed orbital welding machines in house, which enable us to complete circumferential welds on cylindrical parts and assemblies in the vertical or horizontal plane.

We specialise in Steel, Stainless steel and Aluminium custom welding.

Our comprehensive welding suite

  • Orbital welding
  • Laser welding
  • TIG welding
  • MIG welding
  • Welding lathes
  • Turn tables
