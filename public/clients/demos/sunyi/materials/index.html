Materials

Materials we work with

Aerospace alloys, medical and semiconductor plastics and metals, and oil and gas steels: the lists Sunyi publishes, grouped by industry.

Our core competency is in the machining of high-mix low-volume precision components at competitive prices. Our clients are key players in the aerospace, electronics, hard disk drives, marine, medical, optical and other manufacturing industries.

We have deep expertise with wide ranging materials for use across heavy industries. These materials can be used in our fastener customization or CNC auto lathe process. You can contact us to enquire the material required and we will propose a turnkey solution from sourcing to manufacturing.
