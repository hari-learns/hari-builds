Our legacy

Five and a half decades of trust, father to son

Malik Estate Agents is a name to reckon with in reputed real estate consulting, started in 1965 by the late Mr Madan Malik, whose own father, Lala Lajpat Rai Malik, was founder president of the Punjab Stock Exchange, Lahore.

1965

Malik Estate Agents is established by the late Mr Madan Malik (1927-1996) as a real estate brokerage in Connaught Place, New Delhi.

1980

His son Sanjay Malik finishes his studies at Delhi University and joins the family business. Real estate investments remain his core specialisation.

Today

Chaired by Sanjay Malik, the firm deals in construction, sale-purchase-exchange, leasing and collaboration of properties across Delhi and the NCR, with sister company Vandana Homes Pvt. Ltd. building luxury floors and villas.

Own an old house in South Delhi?

House collaboration, in five steps

Collaboration is a deal between the old house owner and a builder, where the builder constructs luxury builder floors on the owner’s plot at their own expense in exchange for a floor in the new home.

  1. 1

    Builder selection

    Malik Estate Agents provides the selection of best builders to the plot owner and showcases the recent construction quality of the builder.

  2. 2

    Proposal

    The plot owner’s requirements are discussed with the shortlisted builders, the division of floors is decided and a collaboration agreement is signed.

  3. 3

    Designing

    The builder’s team prepares the layout plan of all floors, interior specifications and exterior design according to the owner’s requirements.

  4. 4

    Construction

    The builder constructs the new house to the decided timeline, while the plot owner is provided with provisional accommodation.

  5. 5

    Welcome to the new home

    The plot owner receives possession of their share of the new house, while the builder sells their share of floors to suitable buyers.
