Series 27

Large openings on private or leisure vessels.

Multi-fold

The S27 Multi-fold door is a relatively new product to the Seaglaze range. Designed for large openings mainly on private or leisure vessels maximising space and light. The use of a aluminium track allows the doors to glide open and fold back creating an attractive aesthetic for any modern boat.

Made bespoke to your space with the number of doors, folding pattern, furnishings and glazing.

Variety of furnishings to choose from.

  • Through fix frame
  • Size of aperture affects number of panes required
  • Available in any RAL colour
  • Maximises space on deck

Options

  • Individual folding panes or Rolling Track
  • Two to six panes
