Seatite hatches

Flush, fully watertight deck access.

Watertight, flush

The Seatite hatch range has been designed to offer complete protection against water ingress into your vessel whilst maintaining a simple yet robust construction. The hatches have been fitted to vessels for over 30 years, most of which are still in service today and still as effective as the day they were fitted.

In the modern marine environment, the equipment used onboard vessels is becoming increasingly expensive and sensitive to water damage. The hatches and deck rings are cast in high quality LM6 aluminium to BS1490, with material traceability from ingot to hatch. The Seatite range is manufactured to the highest standards; accreditations and certificates are available on request.

  • Cast LM6 aluminium hatch and deck ring, traceable from ingot to hatch
  • 316 stainless steel centre bolt
  • Solid silicone sealing ring
  • Galvanised steel strongback

Sizes offered

  • 400, 500 and 600 mm round hatch
  • 560 mm square hatch
  • 675 mm square hatch
  • 650 x 500 mm oval hatch

Features

  • Flush fitting
  • Simple operation
  • Robust construction
  • Corrosion resistant materials
  • Anti-slip pattern
  • Secure locking mechanism
  • 100% watertight
  • Optional hinges (square and oval)
