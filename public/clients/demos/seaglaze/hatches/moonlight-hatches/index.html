Moonlight hatches

Offshore, powerboat, flush, military and portlight hatches.

Offshore · certified

All Moonlight hatches and portlights are certified in accordance with 94/25/EC and ISO 12216. For more than 30 years, Moonlight has held a position as the top brand in high quality deck hatches and portlights.

Moonlight portlights and hatches allow light into the living spaces on board a vessel whilst enhancing the external styling of the yacht. Available in different styles, shapes and sizes, the range is adaptable and spare parts are available to keep the hatch working over a long life.

Offshore hatches (Series 62 and 63)

Anodised aluminium frames with grey tinted acrylic and friction hinges that let the hatch open to any intermediate position up to 180 degrees. No stay arms or obstructions in the way, and no sharp edges for a spinnaker to foul on. Opens from inside and outside, lockable from inside.

Features

  • Marine grade aluminium, silver satin anodised
  • Grey tinted acrylic
  • Inside and outside opening handles
  • Friction hinges

Options

  • Clear or coloured acrylic
  • Powder coated or black anodised frame
  • Flyscreen
  • External key lock

Powerboat hatches (Series 55)

Anodised aluminium frames, grey tinted acrylic and adjustable stay arms that lock the hatch in variable open positions. A very low profile to the deck; opens from inside and outside and locks from inside.

Features

  • Marine grade aluminium
  • Grey tinted acrylic
  • Adjustable stay arms

Options

  • Clear or coloured acrylic
  • Powder coated or black anodised frame
  • Flyscreen

Compact flush hatch

Superyacht styling at affordable prices: the offshore hatch base with a dark grey tinted acrylic lid, designed to sit flush with surrounding teak decking, drained through channels under the teak.

Features

  • Dark grey tinted acrylic
  • Stainless steel gas strut
  • Inside and outside opening handles

Options

  • Flush handles openable from outside with a winch handle
  • Flyscreen

Military hatch

A lightweight 3 mm aluminium panel in place of the acrylic, with a raised external handle for easy opening by operatives wearing gloves; a heavy duty alternative to plastic console hatches.

Features

  • Marine grade aluminium
  • 3 mm black aluminium plate
  • Outside opening handles

Options

  • Polyester powder coated frame and panel

Portlight

Fixed or inward opening portlights manufactured to international standards for structures from 1 mm to 42 mm. Fitted with clamp frame technology, so no holes through your structure, with a fitted seal for a watertight fit.

Features

  • Low profile
  • Friction hinges
  • Grey tinted acrylic
  • Aluminium inner trim

Options

  • Clear acrylic
  • Polished silver or black anodised frame
