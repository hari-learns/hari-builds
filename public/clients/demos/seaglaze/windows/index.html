Marine windows

With a wide range of windows from the classic through-fix to sleek hidden frame there are products to suit any boat.

Through-fix windows

A through-fix window is where the superstructure or hull is drilled to allow a fixing to pass through the boat window frame, and then into or through whatever the window is being attached to. With all through-fixing frames, different trims are available as an optional extra, as are the fastenings.

Clamp-in windows

Clamp-in windows are fast and easy to fit. All of the fastenings are incorporated inside the body of the frame, so there are no external fastening holes and the common source of leaks has been eliminated. The clamp-in design makes the window robust and durable, and pleasing to the eye.

Glass-look windows

Glass-look windows are becoming ever more popular to create a sleek, single-panel look from ferries to superyachts whilst increasing the amount of clear light that gets through. With hidden fixings increasing the water integrity and a range of glazing options available, they are perfect for creating the look you want on any vessel.
