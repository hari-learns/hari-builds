A family business

Founded in 1970. Still family owned.

Seaglaze Marine Windows was founded in 1970 by two families before being passed across to the Clayton family. Richard Clayton took over in the 1990s before retiring and passing it on to his son, Alastair Clayton, in the 2000s. Richard is still involved and sits on the board of directors with his wife.

The business has stayed in the same place at the Rackheath Industrial Estate, close to the heart of the Broads National Park on the outskirts of Norwich, but has expanded and grown over the years.

Values

What Seaglaze stands for

Our mission

Innovate new products for the marine market, focusing on quality rather than price, whilst utilising advanced IT based support and control systems.

Our vision

To enhance the lives of the people with whom it is involved through manufacture of quality, design oriented closure products for the marine sector.

Our promise

To provide our customers with high quality bespoke products for their marine needs.
