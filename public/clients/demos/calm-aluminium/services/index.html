Aluminium cutting and fabrication in Sydney

Material supply, coupled with in-house processes and a network of local and global suppliers and contractors.

In-house capabilities

  • Precision plate saw cutting
  • Precision band saw cutting
  • Water jet cutting

Our precision plate saw from MetlSaw (USA) cuts up to 12 inches thick with very close tolerance cuts. It is the largest and fastest saw of its kind in Australia. The close tolerance cuts allow for less wastage and shorter machining times. With our precision combination plate and extrusion saws and band saws we cut material to exact sizes. There is no minimum order for stocked items, and we can cut to size while you wait.

Other capabilities, through our network

  • Router cutting
  • Precision plate machining
  • Folding and bending
  • Welding
  • Anodising
  • Powder coating
  • Partial or full fabrication

If there is something we cannot process in-house, we find the most efficient option: directing you to someone who can perform the service, or working with our network of suppliers. Our contractors have aerospace and defence quality standards in place. From raw material to the end product, Calm Aluminium has the resources to provide the complete solution.

Hard-to-find alloys

The team has many years of experience in the industry, so when it comes to sourcing hard-to-find metals, we are here to help. Coupled with a global network of mills and distributors, we can source the alloys that others cannot. We also have access to stainless steel, copper, brass, titanium and other specialty metals, and engineering plastics on request.

Delivery

We are based in Sydney and deliver all over Australia. In most cases, for stocked items, we can dispatch orders the same day for overnight delivery.

Tell us what you need, big or small.

One of our team members will happily discuss your requirements. If we don’t stock it, chances are we know someone who does, and if not, we will source it for you.
