ISO 9001 certified, fully traceable aluminium supply

Product traceability is a core focus at Calm Aluminium, from the mill through to the end user.

Quality management system

Calm Aluminium is certified to ISO 9001:2015 by Intertek SAI Global. The certificate covers the procurement, warehousing and sale of metals and plastics in semi-wrought and cut-to-size form, and the subcontract material processing to customer requirements.

Traceability

Mill certificates are available for all orders on request. Nearly all of the products we receive are line-marked with lot numbers, ensuring the correct material will always be supplied. Over and above that, each item received is allocated an internal batch number, ensuring full traceability from the mill right through to the end user. Batch traceability is built into our inventory management system.

Certified metal

The metal we sell is certified to various standards across the globe. Some specific standards, depending on the grade, include:

  • AMS 4025
  • AMS 4026
  • AMS 4027
  • AMS 4050
  • AMS-QQ-A-200/8
  • AMS-QQ-A-250/11
  • AMS-WW-T-700/6
  • ASME SB-209
  • ASME SB-221
  • ASTM B209
  • ASTM B221
  • ASTM B928
  • DNV Certified

Approved supplier to BAE Systems Australia

Calm Aluminium has full aircraft approved supplier status for BAE Systems Australia and is one of only a few certified material supply companies in Australia to have achieved this status for supply of raw materials, both ferrous and non-ferrous metals.
