What we supply

Aluminium for structural applications, from one Sydney base.

Calm Aluminium specialises in the sourcing and supply of aluminium for structural applications. Our core product base includes aluminium in grades 6061, 2024, 7050, 7075 and 5083. We also stock commercial and architectural grades such as 1100, 1370, 5005, 5251 and 6060. Many other grades are available on request.

    Precision cutting

    The largest and fastest plate saw of its kind in Australia.

    Our precision plate saw from MetlSaw (USA) cuts up to 12 inches thick with very close tolerance cuts. Close tolerances mean less wastage and shorter machining times. We can supply full size product or cut to the exact size you need, even while you wait.
