Aluminium supply for aerospace, defence and toolmaking since 2002

Established to market products and act as a technical adviser on the correct materials and alloys.

“Calm Aluminium was established in 2002 to market products and services to the Aerospace, Defence and Toolmaking industries in Australia and New Zealand and also to act as a technical adviser of correct materials and alloys for the related markets. From its inception, Calm Aluminium has achieved competitive prices and lead times for Aluminium and other products for the local industry.”

Laurie Sutton, Managing Director

“We have come a long way. Back in 2002 it was just a one man team operating out of a home office. After moving into our first office and factory it wasn't long before we out-grew that! Our current purpose built facility allows room for expansion. Even our systems and processes have come a long way, from paper based beginnings, to fully automated batch traceability built right into our Inventory Management System. We have a great team who know how to get things done.”

Michael Sutton, Director / Special Projects

“Calm Aluminium is now an authorised stockist of AMAG rolling GmbH for aluminium sheet and plate.”

George Weger, Managing Director, AMAG rolling GmbH

Projects, past and present

Over the years we have worked on projects big and small. Larger contracts with BAE Systems Australia, Thales Australia and Boeing Aerostructures Australia, as well as Tier-2 defence sub-contractors across Australia have included supplying metal and parts for:

  1. Armour plate upgrades to the M113 armoured personnel carrier
  2. ESSM (Raytheon Evolved Sea Sparrow Missile)
  3. Eurofighter Typhoon
  4. JSF (Joint Strike Fighter, Lockheed Martin F-35 Lightning II)
  5. NULKA Decoy Missile

Other examples of what we supply

  • Aluminium sheet in grades 2024, 6061 and 7075 for the recreational flying industry
  • Aluminium caul sheet and caul plate used in the manufacture of plywood
  • Aluminium tubes used as ski poles on jet boats
  • Anodised sheet used for architectural applications
  • High-performance metals used in drag bikes and drag cars

Sponsorship and community

We sponsor the Aussie Invader 5R, providing solid blocks of aluminium for the wheels, and sponsor Formula SAE teams. Formula SAE is an engineering design competition for undergraduate and graduate students, who design, build and test a single-seat race car. Teams we sponsor:

  • Monash Motorsport
  • Redback Racing
  • RMIT Electric Racing
  • Sydney Motorsport
  • UOW Motorsport
  • UTAS Motorsport
  • Sunswift (UNSW)

Calm Aluminium sponsors the UNSW solar car team Sunswift in the World Solar Challenge. UOW Motorsport came home with first place overall in the 2016 Formula SAE competition. We also joined the Unicorn Foundation’s 2017 Sydney Cocktail Party, held to fundraise for research into neuroendocrine tumours.
