Embossed bright tread plate

Embossed tread plate for superior anti-slip safety. Calm Aluminium is the only supplier in Australia to stock this product.

Grade
3003-H22, good welding and formability
Thickness
1.6 mm and 3.18 mm
Size
Up to 1219 mm wide × 4876 mm long

It is resistant to weathering, corrosion and contact with chemicals, and stays slip resistant when the surface is wet or oily. The diamond pattern has a unique modification to the top surface that improves grip, on a bright reflective surface.

The perfect choice for sport trailers, slippery areas, dock surfaces, stairways and more. No minimum order, and a free sample is available.
