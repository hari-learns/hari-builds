Aluminium caul plate

Lightweight but strong: aluminium has been the material of choice for caul plates in the manufacture of plywood, fibreboard, hardboard and particle board.

Grades
2024-T81, 6061-T6
Market
Plywood, fibreboard, hardboard, particle board, faced furniture

Caul plate and caul sheet are also used in plastic- and veneer-faced furniture. The caul alloys described are Arconic’s 2024-T81 and 6061-T6.

Five critical requirements of caul materials

  1. Strength Caul plates need compressive yield strength at temperatures up to 176 °C to resist buckling and edge waves. 2024-T81 is more forgiving of gaps between adjacent panels, allowing wider cauls.
  2. Heat transfer Rapid heat transfer from press plate to load shortens resin curing. Aluminium 2024 and 6061 conduct roughly 36% better than sheet steel.
  3. Corrosion resistance A natural oxide layer protects aluminium and can be thickened by anodising to extend the life of the caul plate.
  4. Defect-free surface Nicks, gouges, scratches and slivers transfer to the pressed product, so surface quality matters.
  5. Light weight Aluminium weighs about 35% as much as steel by volume, which makes plates easier to handle in and out of the press.
