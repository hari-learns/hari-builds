Sheet & plate

Sheet and plate from 0.41 mm up to 305 mm thick, supplied full size or cut to the size you need.

Calm Aluminium specialises in the sourcing and supply of aluminium for structural applications. Core grades are 6061, 2024, 7050, 7075 and 5083, with commercial and architectural grades 1100, 1370, 5005, 5251 and 6060 also stocked.

Calm Aluminium is now an authorised stockist of AMAG rolling GmbH for aluminium sheet and plate.

Our precision plate saw from MetlSaw (USA) cuts up to 12 inches thick with very close tolerances, the largest and fastest saw of its kind in Australia. Close-tolerance cuts mean less wastage and shorter machining times.
