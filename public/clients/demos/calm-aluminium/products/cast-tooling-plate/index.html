Precision cast tooling plate

Precision cast tooling plate for flatness and dimensional control. Calm Aluminium is the Australian distributor for Vista Metals and PCP Aluminum.

Thickness
ATP-5 6.35–101.6 mm; Duramold 51–965 mm
Lead time
Short lead times
Applications
Drill jigs, index tables, mould cooling and heating plates, vacuum chucks, base plates, moulds, thermoforming

Due to its versatility and consistent mechanical and dimensional characteristics, precision cast tooling plate is the product of choice where flatness and dimensional control are critical. Cast plate is available in short lead times, up to 965 mm thick.

ATP-5 has outstanding machinability, excellent high-speed cutting rates and exceptional feed rates, which reduce machining time. Both ATP-5 and Duramold are cast aluminium plate from Vista Metals.

ATP-5 specifications (5xxx series)

Surface condition
Precision machined 2 sides, 20 RMS max
Guaranteed flatness (before and after sawing)
Over 12.7 mm: 0.127 mm or better. 12.7 mm and under: 0.381 mm or better
Gauges
6.35 mm to 101.6 mm, tolerance ±0.127 mm
Widths
1232, 1537, 1842 mm (+6.35/−0 mm)
Lengths
Up to 3670 mm (+6.35/−0 mm)
Edges
Saw cut. Material supplied PVC two sides; 100% inspection for flatness and thickness
Typical properties
Tensile 283 MPa, yield 124 MPa, elongation 15%, hardness 70 HB, density 2660, thermal conductivity 140 W/mK

Duramold-2 (2xxx) and Duramold-5 (5xxx)

Gauges

Widths
Duramold-2: up to 2388 mm. Duramold-5: up to 1880 mm (51–534 mm gauges), up to 1626 mm (534–762 mm)
Lengths
Up to 3823 mm (gauges 51–762 mm); up to 2540 mm (gauges 787–965 mm). Longer on inquiry
Surface
Machined 2 sides (25 RMS), saw cut edges, ultrasonically inspected
