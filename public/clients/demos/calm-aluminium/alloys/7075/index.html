What it is used for

Aircraft and aerospace structures needing high strength with moderate toughness and corrosion resistance: alclad skin sheet and structural plate up to 4 in thick.

7xxx alloys are heat-treatable and reach the highest strengths of all aluminium alloys. They are not considered weldable by routine commercial processes and are usually used in riveted construction.

Series notes paraphrased from the Aluminum Association monograph “Aluminum Alloy Selection and Applications”, linked from Calm Aluminium’s site.
