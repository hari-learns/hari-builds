What it is used for

Welded structural members such as truck and marine frames, railroad cars and pipelines; tooling plate.

6xxx alloys are heat-treatable with moderately high strength and excellent corrosion resistance. They weld readily, and are the first choice for extruded structural members.

Series notes paraphrased from the Aluminum Association monograph “Aluminum Alloy Selection and Applications”, linked from Calm Aluminium’s site.
