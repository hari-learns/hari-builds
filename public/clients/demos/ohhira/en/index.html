Products

Products

Writing instruments (for domestic and overseas manufacturers)

We manufacture over 300 million pen parts annually, which are used in 87 countries around the world. We mainly produce "writing instrument parts" such as mechanical pencils. In order to meet the diverse needs of our customers, we carry out integrated production in-house, from mold manufacturing to press processing.

Sensor cases and electronic components

We manufacture temperature sensor components called "thermistors," electronic circuit components, and medical components. Our quality has been highly praised by major temperature sensor and electronic component manufacturers.

  • Chuck / ring (mechanical pencil lead chuck)
  • Large chuck (crayon, chalk)
  • Internal mechanism
  • Multi-pen mechanism
  • SUS pen-tip holder (fine liner)
  • Knock / eraser holder
  • Temperature sensor case (pressed)
  • Temperature sensor case (cut)
  • Hot-water sensor case
  • Cap for electronic components
  • Probe socket

Technology

Technology

We utilize the precision deep drawing and precision cutting techniques we have cultivated over many years to provide products that meet a wide range of needs. We have earned the trust of our customers by manufacturing high-quality parts in a wide range of fields, from writing instrument parts to ABS parts for automobiles.

  • PressTransfer-press deep drawing. Product size Φ1.0–6.0 mm, length about 30 mm.
  • CuttingNC automatic lathes. Best at φ0.5–φ8.0 mm, up to φ16; holes under φ0.3 mm.
  • Secondary processingSlitting, crimping and automated pencil-mechanism assembly, on machines designed in-house.
