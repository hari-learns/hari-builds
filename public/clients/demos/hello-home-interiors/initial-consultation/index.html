Initial Consultation & Proposal

Thanks for getting in touch with Hello Home Interiors. We’re able to provide a sit down conversation in your home, to discuss the scope and brief for your project. Understanding your needs, your wish list, your hopes and dreams, your investment and the requirements of your project are the first step in our process.

Once we’ve gathered the information we will form a design brief and scope of work document. We will also prepare a fee proposal for you to consider the timeframes and design costs of your project.

Committing some time and thought at the beginning of your project can save you a lot of time, stress and money later! And we’d love to help you with this!

  1. A sit down conversation in your homeYour needs, your wish list, your hopes and dreams, your investment.
  2. A design brief and scope of workFormed from what we gathered together.
  3. A fee proposalTimeframes and design costs of your project, for you to consider.

The cost of our initial consultation is $250 NZD
