About

Where you live matters—it impacts your life in such fundamental ways. We’re here to help make your home your haven.

One that’s tailor made to match you and your lifestyle.

Not a museum, but luxurious everyday living.

Joneen has nearly 20 years experience working with home owners, suppliers, builders, and architects on residential Interior Design.

A Platinum Designer of the NKBA (National Kitchen and Bathroom Association), Joneen has extensive knowledge, expertise and experience designing and delivering functional and beautiful kitchens and bathrooms all across the Wellington region.

Joneen Rodgers and Hannah Ferens are joint owners of Wellington-based interior design studio, Hello Home Interiors.

We craft spaces to match your lifestyle.

We offer professional interior designs throughout NZ with a personal touch tailored to our clients’ individual needs. We work with you to bring all of your design ideas together cohesively without the stress of endless searches for the right material, fixture, colour, pattern, furniture and price.

We develop the design alongside you, specify fixtures and fittings, and document your final design for construction.

Hello Home Interiors has experience in delivering high quality interior designs in NZ across a range of residential projects - entire home renovations, kitchen and bathroom design, space planning, custom joinery design, lighting design, and specifying furniture, fixtures and fittings, and soft furnishings.

We work alongside proven and trusted trade professionals whom we have worked with for many years, and we can assist you through the construction process to ensure delivery of all interior finishes are to the highest standard.
