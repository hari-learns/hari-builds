The drum is the drive

Inside a PulleyDrive there is no motor and gearbox pack and no oil bath.

Magnets on the shell turn around a fixed stator on the shaft: one transfer of energy, straight to belt pull. We call it Direct Drive Rotary Motion (DDRM).

  • No oil to change
  • No seals to replace
  • No gears to wear out
  • No scheduled rebuilds
  • Mounted on both ends
  • Constant torque across a wide speed range

Runs on the VFD you have.

Runs on the VFDs you already have: Allen-Bradley, Yaskawa, Lenze, Emerson. Always use a VFD: connecting a One Motion drive straight to main power will damage it and void the warranty.

Questions people ask.

How do I choose a size?

Start from your conveyor's pulley diameter, belt width and the belt pull it needs. Each size shows its range, and the configurator lists every build that fits. If you're not sure, send us the conveyor details and we'll size it.

Which belts does it drive?

PulleyDrive is built for plain, crowned, lagged and Square Drive shells, and cut shells for Intralox, Volta, Habasit, Uni and Lumsden Wiremation belts.

How fast can I get one?

In-stock builds ship within 10 days from our Rogers, Arkansas headquarters. The configurator shows which builds are in stock.

What if my machine needs something special?

We design drives around the machine too. Tell us the diameter, face width and load, and our engineers will build it as a custom MagDrive configuration.

What warranty applies?

Every new One Motion direct drive carries a 3-year limited warranty covering materials and mechanical components under normal operating conditions.
