Same mount. No gearbox. No oil.

SideDrive takes the place of the whole motor and gearbox on the frame you already run. The drip tray goes, and so does the shaft mount bearing.

Running a servo and gearbox instead? SideDrive replaces those too, without an encoder or servo drive.

Where it fits.

Start, stop, reverse

Synchronous motion with 120 Nm of peak torque on tap.

Indexing and timing

Precise start and stop with no encoder and no servo drive.

Hygienic washdown

True IP69K in 316 stainless, with no oil to leak into the product zone.

Fewer spares

One drive covers gearbox ratios from 6:1 to 30:1 and up.

Three steps on the line.

No new frame, no new controls. If you can change a gearmotor, you can fit a SideDrive.

  1. 1

    Unbolt the motor and gearbox.

    Take off the gearmotor and its drip tray. The frame and the shaft stay where they are.

  2. 2

    Bolt SideDrive to the 56C pattern.

    The 1.25" hollow core takes the shaft directly. Four 3/8-16 holes on a 5.875" bolt circle, with a torque arm to the frame.

  3. 3

    Wire it to your VFD.

    Any VFD with a PM mode or sensorless vector control. On a PowerFlex 525, load our parameter file and run.

Questions people ask.

Will it fit my motor mount?

If the gearmotor mounts to a NEMA 56C face, yes. SideDrive bolts to the same four 3/8-16 holes on a 5.875" bolt circle and takes a 1.25" shaft in its hollow core. Send us your current motor and gearbox and we'll confirm the fit.

Does it replace a servo?

Yes. SideDrive gives synchronous, precise start/stop motion from a standard VFD, so you don't need an encoder or a servo drive.

Which VFD do I need?

Any VFD with a permanent magnet (PM) mode or sensorless vector control, from 110 V single-phase to 460 V three-phase. We recommend an Allen-Bradley PowerFlex 525, 460 V, 3 HP or comparable for most applications.

Can I connect it straight to the mains?

No. SideDrive always runs from a VFD. Connecting it to main power without one will cause severe damage and void the warranty.

What maintenance does it need?

No routine maintenance. There's no gearbox, no oil and no seals or bearings to service on a schedule.

What is the warranty?

Three years. SideDrive's direct drive technology has a proven service life of more than six years in washdown production.
