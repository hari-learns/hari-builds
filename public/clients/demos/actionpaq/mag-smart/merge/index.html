Mag-Smart Merge

Powered by One Motion™ Mag-Drive

Mag-Smart Merge systems are designed for multi-lane singulation or single lane aggregation. With servo like timing, gearbox free design, and the bare minimum moving parts this system offers the best in reliability and efficiency for your application needs.

Space saving Mag-Drive technology allows for a smaller footprint, less harborage areas, reduced mechanical pinch points and a simplified sanitation cycle. These systems meet IP69k washdown standards and require minimal maintenance.

These systems can be customized to suit your unique application's requirements.
