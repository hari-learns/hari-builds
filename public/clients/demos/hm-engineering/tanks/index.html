Non-pressurised, food grade or general industry

Stainless steel tanks made in Hamilton, New Zealand

Our standard non-pressurised stainless steel tanks, with normal capacities of up to 20,000 litres, are manufactured to either a food grade standard for mixing and storage of water, milk or food products intended for human consumption, or designed and manufactured for various other liquids to be stored for general industry use.

Capacity
up to 20,000 L
Duty
Food grade or general industry
Design life
at least 25 years
Documentation
Level 2 quality handover

The tank and its associated mounting, normally legs, may be designed and constructed for at least a 25 year original life in the intended duty.

Design, supply and installation of ancillary equipment may include

  • CIP systems for the vessels, including selection and supply of removable spray balls or cleaning devices
  • Mixers, agitators and motors
  • Manway access
  • Heating/cooling jackets with insulation and sheathing
  • Access platforms and ladders
  • Stainless steel conduit for agitators and instruments

How you can buy it

Tanks can be offered either on an ex works basis, delivered to site, or delivered and installed on site including seismically rated hold down bolts.

Documentation

Level 2 Quality Handover documentation is supplied on completion.
