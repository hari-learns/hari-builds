Quality control

HME has developed a comprehensive in-house quality system based on ISO 9002 and operates three levels of quality control to suit customers’ requirements:

  • Quality Level One: where documentation requirements are low (smaller items).
  • Quality Level Two: where formal documentation and a summarised quality/handover package is required.
  • Quality Level Three: where quality procedures need to be customised to suit a specific job with an inspection test plan (ITP) produced, possibly together with third party inspection and NDT requirements (e.g. coded vessels).
