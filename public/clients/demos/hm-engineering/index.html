  1. 1976

    Ron Hendl founds the company as a sole trader, after 17 years as a maintenance engineer with the NZ Co-operative Dairy Company.

  2. 1980

    Hendl & Murray Engineering is formed when Jim Murray joins. The first workshop opens on King Street, Frankton, Hamilton.

  3. 1984

    Noel Hendl joins the company, followed by Neil Edwards in 1985.

  4. 1987

    HME moves to larger premises it bought on Bandon Street, Frankton.

  5. 1992

    Outgrowing Bandon Street, the company moves to its current home at Tawa Street, Melville, Hamilton.

  6. 2024

    Neil Edwards retires after 38 years. HME is now led by Directors Noel Hendl and Greg Baker.

The workshop

Plant, cranes and people under one roof.

Our fabrication workshop has a total floor space of 3,500 m² under the hook of two 6 tonne gantry cranes (12 tonne tandem lift) and one 3 tonne gantry crane. Welding and forming of stainless steel plate and sheet metal is the company’s core business.
