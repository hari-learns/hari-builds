Fonterra Wynyard tanker reception

Vessels and equipment for the new Fonterra tanker reception at Wynyard, Tasmania: managed from New Zealand, built in Hamilton, shipped and installed on time.

Delivered on time

Hendl & Murray Engineering was engaged by its client to supply and install the vessels and equipment required for the new Fonterra, Wynyard, tanker reception.

The project was managed from our New Zealand base, with all tanks fabricated in our Hamilton workshop prior to being shipped to Tasmania. This was achieved through HME's vast experience exporting large vessels to Tasmania, which comes with its own unique challenges when compared with the Australian mainland.

The site crew was also dispatched from New Zealand, although the HME team were extremely familiar with the local environment having previously, on an annual basis, worked on the Wynyard site.

We are extremely proud to have been able to deliver this project on time, along with meeting the exacting quality standards of both Fonterra and our client, meaning our brand promise is once again upheld.
