Stainless steel pressure vessels to AS 1210 and ASME VIII

HME is able to design, supply and manufacture stainless steel pressure vessels under either the AS 1210 or ASME VIII codes. These may be to a food grade standard for the mixing and storage of products for human consumption, or for liquids or products to be processed for general industry use.

Codes
AS 1210 or ASME VIII
Duty
Food grade or general industry
Documentation
O&M manual + Level 3 quality handover
Supply
Ex works, delivered or installed

Design, supply and installation of ancillary equipment may include

  • CIP systems for the vessels, including selection and supply of removable spray balls or cleaning devices
  • Mixers, agitators and motors
  • Manway access
  • Heating/cooling jackets with insulation and sheathing
  • Access platforms and ladders
  • Stainless steel conduit for agitators and instruments

How you can buy it

Pressure vessels can be offered on an ex works basis, delivered to site, or delivered and installed on site including seismically rated hold down bolts.

Documentation

An Operation and Maintenance Manual and Level 3 Quality Handover documentation are supplied on completion.
