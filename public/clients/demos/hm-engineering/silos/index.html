Food grade or general industry, free standing on skirt

Stainless steel silos from 20,000 to 500,000 litres

Our standard non-pressurised stainless steel silos are manufactured either to a food grade standard for storage of grain, dairy and other products intended for human consumption, or designed and manufactured for various liquids to be stored for general industry use. HME silos range in capacity from 20,000 litres up to 500,000 litres.

Capacity
20,000 L up to 500,000 L
Duty
Grain, dairy and other food products, or general industry liquids
Mounting
Free standing on skirt, at least 25 year design life
Documentation
Quality handover on delivery

The silo and its associated mounting, free standing on skirt, may be designed and constructed for at least a 25 year original life in the intended duty.

Design, supply and installation of ancillary equipment may include

  • CIP systems for the silos, including selection and supply of removable spray balls or cleaning devices
  • Stainless steel alcoves for silos with redline vents
  • Agitators and motors
  • Manway access
  • Vent/overflow lines including tundishes within a silo gallery
  • Access platforms, stairways and/or caged ladders
  • Stainless steel conduit for agitators and instruments

How you can buy it

Silos can be offered either on an ex works basis, delivered to site, or delivered and erected on site including seismically rated hold down bolts and grouting (free draining under silo).

Documentation

Quality handover documentation is supplied upon delivery.
