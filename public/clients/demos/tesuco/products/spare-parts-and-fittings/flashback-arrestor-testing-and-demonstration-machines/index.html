Flashback Arrestor Testing and Demonstration Machines

The IBEDA Flashback Arrestor Testing Machine is supplied complete with a range of testing adaptors to enable the operator to correctly test the comprehensive range of IBEDA flashback arrestors available. Tesuco offers each of the test adaptors and other spare parts as individual items, as well as offering the entire spare parts collection kit. Spare parts and consumables are also available for the Demonstration Machine as detailed below.
