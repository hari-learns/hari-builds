DEMAX/SIMAX Adaptors

Manifold arrestors are made up of individual standard flow or high flow flashback arrestors manifolded into a single unit. Each of these individual units must be tested, along with the leak tightness of the unit as a whole. Detailed below are the spare parts commonly used in ensuring these units are repaired to manufacturer's specifications.
