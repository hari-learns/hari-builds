Blowpipes, Mixers and Cutting Attachments

Detailed below is Tesuco's range of blowpipes, cutting attachments and mixers, suitable for both oxygen / acetylene and oxygen / LPG applications.

6 product codes in this range

Blowpipe

The Tesuco® blowpipe can be used with the cutting attachment and tips, as well as the range of mixers and tips for welding / brazing and the barrels and tips for heating. The valves have double "O" ring seals and the blowpipe is marked according to AS 4839.

  • GWWH01
    BlowpipeShell Packed

Mixers

All mixers feature hand tightening for ease of attachment to the blowpipe. The GWMI10 and GWMI13 mixers are suitable for both oxygen / acetylene and oxygen / LPG (natural gas) applications. The GWMI13LN is an injector mixer suitable for super heating oxygen / LPG tips GWHTL3 and GWHTL4 and also for the range of heavy duty heating tips GWHTLH1 - GWHTLH5. Mixers are marked according to AS 4839.

  • GWMI10
    Mixer 10mmType 550
  • GWMI13
    Mixer 13mmType 570
  • GWMI13LN
    Mixer 13mm LongType 574Oxygen / LPG - Natural Gas

Cutting Attachments

Available in both a 90° and 180° head design, the Tesuco® cutting torches with multi mixer, is suitable for both Type 41 (oxygen / acetylene) and Type 44 (oxygen / LPG) cutting nozzles. They feature hand tightening for ease of attachment to the blowpipe and are marked according to AS 4839.

  • GWCA01
    Cutting AttachmentShell Packed
  • GWCA02
    Cutting Attachment180 degree Shell Packed
