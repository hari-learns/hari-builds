Cutting and Gouging Nozzles

It is important to select the correct nozzle for the materials being used and the work being done. Tesuco has a wide range of cutting nozzles to ensure you can select the correct nozzle for the job.

PDFBrochure

23 product codes in this range

Sheet Metal Nozzle Oxygen / Acetylene - Type 41

Sheet metal cutting nozzles are specifically designed for cutting very thin mild steel sheet metal. The size and position of the pre-heat and cutting oxygen holes ensures minimal distortion to the metal.

  • GWSMNA6
    Sheet Metal Nozzle Size 6Oxygen / Acetylene

Cutting Nozzles Oxygen / Acetylene - Type 41 (Copper)

Copper oxygen and acetylene cutting nozzles perform the same as traditional brass ones but have the advantage of better heat dissipation and less slag adherence. Nozzles are marked according to the requirements in AS 4839.

Cutting Nozzles Oxygen / Acetylene - Type 41 (Brass)

Gouging Nozzles Bent Oxygen / Acetylene - Type 41

Gouging nozzles for use with oxygen and acetylene are designed to gouge out metal to the right dimensions when removing faulty welding. They are bent on an angle for ease of use in horizontal positions.

  • GWGNA32
    Gouging NozzleOxygen AcetyleneSize 32 Bent
  • GWGNA48

Gouging Nozzles Straight Oxygen / Acetylene - Type 41

As with other gouging nozzles, this straight type is designed to gouge out metal to the right dimensions when removing faulty welding, in a vertical position. This model is for use with oxygen and acetylene.

  • GWGNA32S
    Gouging NozzleOxygen AcetyleneSize 32 Straight

Cutting Nozzles Oxygen / LPG - Type 44

Oxygen and LPG cutting nozzles perform extremely well for certain applications. Due to less carbon in LPG, there is less slag adherence to the bottom of the cut. Nozzles are marked according to the requirements in AS 4839.
