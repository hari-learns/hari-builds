Heating Tips and Barrels

Detailed below is Tesuco's range of heating tips, barrel attachments and adaptors.

PDFSelection Guide

16 product codes in this range

Heating Tips

The one piece oxygen and acetylene tip can be used for multi-purpose general workshop heating applications. The tip is marked according to AS 4839.

  • GWHT812
    Superheating Nozzle Size 8x12Oxygen / Acetylene

Heating Barrels

Heating barrels are made from tubular stainless steel and come in three lengths. Two bent types and one straight. Threads are to match the larger 13 mm mixer at one end and the superheating tips at the other end.

  • GWHBS450
    Heating BarrelStainless Steel Bent450mm
  • GWHBS700
    Heating BarrelStainless Steel Bent700mm
  • GWHBS735
    Heating BarrelStainless Steel Straight735mm

Superheating Tips Oxygen / Acetylene

Tesuco ranges two sizes of oxygen / acetylene heating tips. These screw directly onto the stainless steel heating barrels. Tips are marked according to the requirements in AS 4839.

  • GWHTA1
    Superheating Nozzle Size 8x12Oxygen / AcetyleneFor Use With Heating Barrels
  • GWHTA2
    Superheating Nozzle Size 12x12Oxygen / AcetyleneFor Use With Heating Barrels

Superheating Tips Oxygen / LPG

Tesuco has a range of four oxygen / LPG super heating tips to suit various heating applications. These tips screw directly onto the stainless steel heating barrels. Tips are marked according to the requirements in AS 4839.

  • GWHTL1
    Superheating Nozzle Size HTL118x12 - Oxygen / LPG
  • GWHTL2
    Superheating Nozzle Size HTL236x12 - Oxygen / LPG
  • GWHTL3
    Superheating Nozzle Size HTL348x12 - Oxygen / LPG
  • GWHTL4
    Superheating Nozzle Size HTL448x15 - Oxygen / LPG

Heavy Duty Heating Tips Oxygen / LPG

Tesuco has a range of five heavy duty oxygen /LPG heating tips to suit various very large heating applications. These tips require the GWHBA adaptor to screw onto the stainless steel heating barrels. Tips are marked according to the requirements in AS 4839.
