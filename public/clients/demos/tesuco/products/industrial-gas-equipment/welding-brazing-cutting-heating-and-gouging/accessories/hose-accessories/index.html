Hose Accessories

13 product codes in this range

Crimping Tool

The crimping tool is specifically designed for use with the GWC5 clips to secure the hose barb once inserted into the ends of the hose. The tool is robust and easy to use and makes this a very simple fixing method.

  • A2100
    Crimping Tool

Hose Clip

This hose clip is designed for use with 5 mm hose nut and tail. They are available as a spare part for hose repairs.

  • GWC5
    Clip 5mm ID Hose
  • GWC5D
    Clip 5mm ID Hose (Pack of 10)

Hose Joiner

These male threaded hose joiners are designed for the temporary joining of two hose sets when longer lengths are required. They are available in left and right hand for oxygen and fuel gas hoses.

  • GWHC34
    Hose JoinerType WB34 Right Hand5/8-18UNF RH

Hose Nut and Tail with Clip 5 mm

This type of nut and hose tail assembly is designed to be fitted into 5 mm hose, then secured with a clip using the special tool. They are available in left and right hand for oxygen and fuel gas hoses.

Hose Nut and Tail with Clip 10 mm

  • GWHCI10

Hose Connections / Hose Joiners 5 mm

This range of hose connection and joiner use a shroud and hose tail that screw into the 5 mm hose forming a very secure end fitting. A special tool should be used to fit them correctly. The connections are available in left and right hand for oxygen and fuel gas hoses. The joiner is suitable for either hose when a repair is required in the length of the hose.

    Hose Connection Brass5/8-18 UNF RH / 5mm HoseType LP112
  • GWLP240LH
