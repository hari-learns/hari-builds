10 mm ID Hoses

Detailed below is Tesuco's range of 10 mm ID twin hoses.

PDFFlyer

6 product codes in this range

Bulk Hose Rolls (50 Metre Lengths)

Twin hose with an internal diameter of 10 mm for oxygen / acetylene and oxygen / LPG is available in 50 metre rolls. This allows for customised lengths to be cut from the roll when required. By fitting approved hose connections, like those supplied by Tesuco, a complete hose set can be made.

  • GWTW1OA
    Twin Hose Oxygen / Acetylene10mm ID Roll (50 metres)
  • GWTW1OL
    Twin Hose Oxygen / LpG10mm ID Roll (50 Metres)

Twin Hose Oxygen / Acetylene 10 mm Hose Sets

Tesuco has a range of hose sets available with an 10 mm internal diameter for oxygen / acetylene applications. These are manufactured to AS 1335 including the strength tests for the end fittings. Hose fittings are marked with the assembly company as required by the standard.

  • GWTW1OA10
    Twin Hose Oxygen / Acetylene10mm ID, 10 Metre Coil
  • GWTW1OA15
    Twin Hose Oxygen / Acetylene10mm ID, 15 Metre Coil

Twin Hose Oxygen / LPG 10 mm Hose Sets

Tesuco has a range of hose sets available with 10 mm internal diameter for oxygen / LPG applications. These are manufactured to AS 1335 including the strength tests for the end fittings. Hose fittings are marked with the assembly company as required by the standard.

  • GWTW1OL10
    Twin Hose Oxygen / LpG10mm ID, 10 Metre Coil
  • GWTW1OL15
