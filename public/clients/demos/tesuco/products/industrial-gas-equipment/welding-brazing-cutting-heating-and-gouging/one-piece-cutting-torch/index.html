One Piece Cutting Torch

Tesuco has introduced a range of one piece cutting torches. They are extremely solid and robust and have been designed to use the standard Type 41 or Type 44 cutting nozzles, with a cutting capacity of 300 mm. They have a three barrel design, come in two standard lengths, have 90°and 180° head configurations and are available for either oxygen / acetylene or oxygen / LPG service. Spare tubes and supports are also stocked, so custom lengths can be made to order.
