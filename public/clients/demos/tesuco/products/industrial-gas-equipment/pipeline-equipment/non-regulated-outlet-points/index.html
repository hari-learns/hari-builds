Non-regulated Outlet Points

Tesuco has a range of single, twin and triple outlet points for use with conventional style bottom entry outlet point regulators or special outlet point regulators that are rated for high inlet pressure as required in AS 4289.

22 product codes in this range

Single

The single low pressure outlet points include a regulator mounting block, colour coded lever style isolation valve, 1/2" pipe compression fitting and warning label all mounted on a stainless steel bracket. They are available for maximum working pressures up to 4,200 kPa.

The single high pressure outlet points include a high pressure rated cylinder isolation valve, 1/2" solder pipe connection and warning label all mounted on a stainless steel bracket. They are available for maximum working pressures up to 20,000 kPa.

Twin

The twin low pressure outlet points provide a complete mounting system for either oxygen and acetylene or oxygen and LPG. They include regulator mounting blocks, colour coded lever style isolation valves, 1/2" pipe compression fittings and warning labels all mounted on a double stainless steel bracket. They are available for maximum working pressures up to 4,200 kPa.

The twin high pressure outlet points provide a complete mounting system for either oxygen and acetylene or oxygen and LPG. They include a high pressure rated cylinder isolation valve, 1/2" solder pipe connection and warning label all mounted on a double stainless steel bracket. They are available for maximum working pressures up to 20,000 kPa.

  • GOPOA
    Point Valve TwinOxygen T10, Acetylene T201/2" Comp Fitting, 4,200 kPa
  • GOPOL
    Point Valve TwinOxygen T10, LPG Type 201/2" Comp Fitting, 4,200 kPa
  • GOPOAH
    Point Valve TwinOxygen T10, Acetylene T201/2" Pipe Solder, 20,000 kPa
  • GOPOLH
    Point Valve TwinOxygen T10, LPG Type 201/2" Pipe Solder, 20,000 kPa

Triple

The triple low pressure outlet points provide a complete mounting system for either oxygen and acetylene or oxygen and LPG and allow for an inert gas regulator for welding or purging applications. They include regulator mounting blocks, colour coded lever style isolation valves, 1/2" pipe compression fittings and warning labels all mounted on a triple stainless steel bracket. They are available for maximum working pressures up to 4,200 kPa.

The triple high pressure outlet points provide a complete mounting system for either oxygen and acetylene or oxygen and LPG and allow for an inert gas regulator for welding or purging applications. They include a high pressure rated cylinder isolation valve, 1/2" solder pipe connection and warning label all mounted on a double stainless steel bracket. They are available for maximum working pressures up to 20,000 kPa.
