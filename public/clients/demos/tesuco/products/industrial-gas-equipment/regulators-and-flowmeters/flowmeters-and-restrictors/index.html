Flowmeters and Restrictors

Detailed below is Tesuco's range of flow control options for MIG, TIG and GMA welding, including flowmeters, flow restriction valves and flow rate testing units.

7 product codes in this range

Flowmeters

Flowmeters are used for precise flow control of the shielding gas to the MIG gun nozzle in welding applications. Tesuco has two in the range. One is 0-25 L/min for TIG and smaller flow MIG applications and the other is a 0-40 L/min for the larger flow MIG applications.

  • RCFL25
    Flowmeter0 - 25 L/min
  • RCFL40
    Flowmeter0 - 40 L/min
  • RTFL25
    Flowmeter0 - 25 L/min
  • RTFL40
    Flowmeter0 - 40 L/min

Flow Restrictors

In some applications for shielding gas where the work is repetitious and the shielding gas flow does not need to change, flow restrictors can be the best option. These are units that attach to the outlet of the set pressure regulator and have a pre-set flow rate. They have a quarter turn on / off knob and have no flow adjustment. Tesuco can supply these in three different flow settings.

  • RAFR08
    Flow RestrictorAr/CO2, 5/8-18UNF RH8 L/Min with On/Off Valve
  • RAFR15
    Flow RestrictorAr/CO2, 5/8-18UNF RH15 L/Min with On/Off Valve
  • RAFR20
    Flow RestrictorAr/CO2, 5/8-18UNF RH20 L/Min with On/Off Valve
