Temporary Manifolds

For many applications a single gas cylinder will not provide the flow rate required for the process. Temporary manifolds provide an affordable solution when a permanent manifold system is not required. Tesuco offer a range of temporary manifolds that allow for two or more cylinders to be joined via a brass t-piece and a flexible lead.
