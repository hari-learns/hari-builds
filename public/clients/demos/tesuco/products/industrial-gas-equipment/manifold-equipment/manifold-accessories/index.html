Cylinder Restraints

All cylinders must be protected from being knocked over, falling and/or impact damage to comply with AS4332-2004. This is regardless of whether the cylinder is full or empty and regardless of whether the cylinder is in or out of operation. The Tesuco range is designed for a number of applications.

Gas Pre-Heaters

Gas Heaters are used with industrial gases, particularly CO 2 and CO 2 mixtures on the high pressure side of the manifold to warm the gas prior to the regulator, improving the flow rate through the regulator and preventing the gas from freezing.

High Pressure Gas Filters

Gas Filters are used with industrial gases on pipeline installations. They are located on the high pressure side of the regulator on a cylinder manifold system and it is used where pressure regulators and line fittings must be protected from contamination. They can also be used on bulk liquid vessel installations, as the flow rate is high even at relatively low pressure. It is a requirement of the medical oxygen standard, that high pressure filters are fitted.

Safety Relief Valves

Tesuco Safety Relief Valves (SRV) have been specifically designed to meet the requirements in Australian Standards. The requirements in AS 1271, AS 4289 and AS 5034 have all been met for protecting low pressure equipment from excess pressure. There are a number of units designed to operate at various pressures as listed below to protect pipework and downstream equipment. Other models are available upon request. All of our ARV models are manufactured in Australia.

Blanking Plugs

When manifold inlet connections or unregulated outlet points are not in use, it is good practice to seal them with blanking plugs. This can save gas leakage, keep dust and other particles out and act as a safety device where there is a high pressure connection.
