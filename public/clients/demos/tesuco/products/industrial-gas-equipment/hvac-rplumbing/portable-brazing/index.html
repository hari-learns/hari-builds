Portable Brazing

Tesuco® offers portable kits with or without disposable cylinders as part of its industrial equipment range which can be used for various applications such as plumbing, refrigeration and air conditioning.

Kits

Tesuco offers the OxyTurbo Set 200 for various professional welding applications such as, plumbing, refrigeration, and air conditioning. Since 2010, the Tesuco Oxyturbo OTS200 has been the market leader.

Unique features include:

  • Independently certified by Apragaz.

GBTAK Air-Acetylene Kit

Tesuco® offers the Flame Pro™ Air Acetylene heating and brazing kit for fast soldering and brazing applications in the HVAC-R & Plumbing industries.

Designed to be used with acetylene cylinders the handle has a quick connect to fit the swirl flame auto ignite tip attachment eliminating the need for a spark lighter. Push button ignition saves time and fuel cost. Swirl flame tips produce a very hot efficient flame by mixing acetylene and air completely through a helical blade.
