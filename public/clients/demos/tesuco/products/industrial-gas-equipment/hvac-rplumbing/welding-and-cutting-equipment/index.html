Welding and Cutting Equipment

Tesuco offers a range of oxygen / acetylene and oxygen / LPG kits to suit the industries they are designed for. This includes complete cutting and welding kits and kits for the plumbing industry where cutting equipment is not required.

For our full range of Welding, Brazing, Cutting and Heating equipment and accessories, see here .

4 product codes in this range

Bottom Entry Kits

The Tesuco oxygen / acetylene and oxygen /LPG gas kits have been designed to include the equipment needed for the majority of welding, brazing and cutting work. The equipment fully meets the requirements and other referenced standards of AS 4839.

Plumbers Kits

The Tesuco plumbers' kit contains all the necessary items for welding, brazing and silver soldering applications. It does not include items not required by this industry, making this kit very cost effective. The equipment fully meets the requirements and other referenced standards of AS 4839.
