High Pressure Flexible Hoses

High pressure flexible hoses are used to connect the gas supply to a regulating device such as a semi-automatic manifold or supply panel. They are available with a PTFE lined inner hose, or in full stainless steel.

5 product codes in this range

GENTEC PTFE Lined

Detailed below is the Gentec range of PTFE lined high pressure, high purity gas supply hoses. They are suitable for use with high purity gases up to 6.0 (99.9999%) purity.

  • GHF09WP
    Flexible Lead Stainless SteelPTFE Lined 900mm1/4" NPT
  • GHF20WP
    Flexible Lead Stainless SteelPTFE Lined 2,000mm1/4" NPT

GENTEC Full Stainless Steel

Detailed below is the Gentec range of full stainless high pressure, high purity gas supply hoses. They are suitable for use with high purity gases up to 6.0 (99.9999%) purity. These hoses are also suitable for toxic and corrosive gases and gas mixtures.
