Ball Valves

GENTEC Ball valves are designed and manufactured for high purity applications with Stainless steel ball and PTE bushing. All valves are Nitrogen pressure leak tested to maximum operating pressures. Valves are available in 2, 3 or 5 way options. Valves area available for panel mounting.
