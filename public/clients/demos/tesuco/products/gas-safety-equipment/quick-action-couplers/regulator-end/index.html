Regulator End

Regulator end quick action coupling units are designed to be fitted to the outlet of a regulator or attached safety device, the coupling pin is attached to the hose. This allows for connection and disconnection of the hose from the regulator without the use of spanners. The coupling unit and pin meet EN 561 and the double 'O' ring design ensures no leakage. It is important to note that the quick action coupling units offer no protection against flashbacks. Flashback arrestors must be fitted in oxygen/fuel gas applications.
