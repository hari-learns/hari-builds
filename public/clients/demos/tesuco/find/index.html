    What to have ready when you order a regulator

    Tesuco asks for these to make sure the correct regulator is supplied:

    • Type of gas
    • Purity of the gas supply
    • Required inlet and outlet pressure
    • Single or dual stage pressure regulation
    • Required flow rate, if available

    Put them in the note on your enquiry. Any other installed equipment or required specification helps.
