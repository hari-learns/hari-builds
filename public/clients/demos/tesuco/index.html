Gas safety first

Fit flashback arrestors at the regulator and at the torch

Optimum protection is achieved when flashback arrestors are fitted to both oxygen and fuel gas hoses at the regulator outlet and the inlet of the blowpipe. In fact, this configuration is a requirement of AS4839 and legislation in some states.

Regulators

Order the right regulator the first time

Tesuco regulators have a fully machined brass body, are made with gas compatible materials and meet AS4267. To supply the correct one Tesuco needs:

  • Type of gas
  • Purity of the gas supply
  • Required inlet and outlet pressure
  • Single or dual stage pressure regulation
  • Required flow rate, if available
