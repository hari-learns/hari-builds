Paediatrics Therapy Regulator

10 October 2018

Tesuco has expanded the range of GENTEC Therapy Regulator to include a low flow Adult Regulator (8 L/min) and a Paediatric Regulator (4 L/min). Products features include:

  • Chrome-plated brass body
  • Easy to read flow rate window
  • 3000 psi maximum inlet pressure
  • Piston design regulator
  • Internal relief valve
  • Sintered bronze inlet filter for longer service life
  • Hose barb outlet (286MB-25LY2A includes 2 x 400 kPa SIS outlets)
  • Complies to all relevant Australian Standards
  • Listed with the Therapeutic Goods Administration Australia.
  • Meets ignition testing for oxygen service

The therapy range includes:

286MB-4LYA Regulator Click Style Medical Oxygen Paediatric 4L/min, 1 x hosebarb Flow selection settings: 0, 1/32, 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 0.50, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4 L/min

286MB-8LYA Regulator Click Style Medical Oxygen 8L/min, 1 x hosebarb Flow selection settings: 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 L/min

286MB-15LYA Regulator Click Style Medical Oxygen 15L/min, 1 x hosebarb Flow selection settings: 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 15 L/min

286MB-25LYA Regulator Click Style Medical Oxygen 25L/min, 1 x hosebarb Flow selection settings: 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 15, 25 L/min

286MB-25LY2A Regulator Click Style Medical Oxygen 8L/min, 1 x hosebarb, 2 x 400 kPa SIS outlet Flow selection settings: 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 15, 25 L/min
