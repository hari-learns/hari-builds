Power-Vac Medical Gas Driven Suction Device

20 September 2018

The Power-Vac is a venturi actuated suction device which features two power take-off connectors for connecting oxygen sources. It can be used in hospital treatments, life support, and any circumstances where patients require the assistance of oxygen and/or suction therapy. The device can be connected to a central oxygen pipeline outlet or cylinder regulator with a pre-set outlet pressure and can be used repeatedly.

Accessories include a 1 litre reusable collection vessel and console mounting slide for the collection vessel.
