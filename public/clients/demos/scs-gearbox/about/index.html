Humble beginnings

Listening to customers since 1979.

Since 1979, SCS Gearbox, Inc. has been listening to its customers’ needs and demands, and that is what has kept us going over the years. When we first started manufacturing, it was strictly to meet the needs of truck and tractor pullers. The goal was to provide specialized gearing that was easily interchangeable to meet track conditions; this goal was met, and well exceeded.

Specialized needs

We know that many of our customers have specialized needs when it comes to their racing vehicles. With a strong understanding that no two racing vehicles are the same, we are willing to work with you and do our best to get you exactly what you need without fail. It doesn’t matter if it is a replacement axle, transfer case, or a specialized gear set, we will do what we can to get you what you need. We accept any challenge you bring us. We work to make your ideas become reality.

Our mission

Without our customers we wouldn’t have much of a business, and if we didn’t listen to the customer’s needs we never would have had a start as a company. We take the time to talk to, listen to, and get to know our customers and their specific needs for current and future problems they may run into. We focus on the concept of happy customers.

Consistent quality

We believe in not only providing a high quality product but making that high quality product every time. We still make everything right where it all began in our one single shop in Bellevue, Ohio, with a staff that has been working with the same products for years.

The details

What sets us apart

Machining processes

Our attention to detail sets us apart in every stage of the machining process. Each component that leaves our shop reflects our commitment to precision from initial design through final inspection. We employ advanced machining techniques and rigorous quality control to ensure that every gear, axle, and transfer case meets exacting standards. Our experienced machinists understand that racing demands perfection, and we treat each project with the meticulous care it deserves.

Friendly staff

We go beyond the parts we make and strive to make every part of your purchasing experience quick, easy, and pleasant. We know what it is like to call a company and talk to someone that has no clue what they are dealing with or get a recording with unending confusion and frustration, and we don’t want to be like that. When you contact us, we will make sure that you speak with someone that can answer all of your challenging questions and is happy to speak with you.

Always improving

As a company, if we didn’t keep up with the demand and the ever changing elements of the racing world, we would never stay alive in such a competitive line of business. We are already working to stay ahead of the game and be ready for what tomorrow’s race world may throw at us. And if it is a situation we haven’t seen before, we will always do everything we can to get you the product that you need.

Since 1979

Family owned and operated.

Founded in 1979 in Bellevue, Ohio, SCS Gearbox began by manufacturing interchangeable gearing solutions for truck and tractor pullers, helping competitors quickly adapt their equipment to changing track conditions. Over the decades the company has grown alongside the racing industry, expanding its products to include quick-change gears, transfer cases, transmissions, axles and custom-machined components for applications from tractor pulling and circle track racing to monster trucks, off-road vehicles and marine racing.
