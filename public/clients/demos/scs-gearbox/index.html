How these numbers are worked out

Drop transfer case: lower ratio × upper ratio (underdrive), or lower ÷ upper (overdrive, only for upper sets up to 1.420). 10.66 / straight thru: lower and quick change each used as listed or inverted. Quick change rear end: low = ring & pinion ÷ gear ratio, high = ring & pinion × gear ratio, as printed on the SCS charts. Speed = RPM × tire ÷ (overall ratio × 336).

Indicative only. Speed is engine RPM × tire diameter ÷ (overall ratio × 336) and ignores tire growth, slip and drivetrain losses. Gear sets and tooth counts come from the SCS charts and calculators; some sets have limited availability. Confirm compatibility with SCS Gearbox before ordering.

About SCS Gearbox

Since 1979

The history, in their own photos.

Founded in 1979 in Bellevue, Ohio, SCS Gearbox began by manufacturing interchangeable gearing solutions for truck and tractor pullers, helping competitors quickly adapt their equipment to changing track conditions. Over the decades the company has grown alongside the racing industry, expanding its products to include quick-change gears, transfer cases, transmissions, axles and custom-machined components for applications from tractor pulling and circle track racing to monster trucks, off-road vehicles and marine racing.

  • 1979

    Mike Sage, Chuck Sage and Steve Callahan founded SCS Gearbox.

  • 1980

    Mike and Chuck were approached by Dan Utz to build a multi-engine modified tractor.

  • 1999

    SCS built the highly successful gearbox for the Dirtslinger tractor.
