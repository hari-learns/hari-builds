Drop transfer cases

12.66 Drop Transfer Case

The 12.66” drop transfer case uses SCS lower gear sets (bull gear) of 1.000, 1.640, 2.421 or 2.824 and quick-change upper gear sets from the SCS gear chart. The combined ratio is the lower ratio times the upper ratio, or the lower ratio divided by the upper ratio when the upper set is inverted for overdrive.
