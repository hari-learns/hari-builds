Monster Truck Transfer Cases

SCS Gearbox Monster Truck Transfer Cases are engineered for exceptional strength, precision, and durability in demanding high-horsepower monster truck applications. Designed to distribute power between the front and rear axles, our transfer cases provide dependable torque transfer and consistent drivetrain performance under extreme operating conditions. From aggressive acceleration and heavy landings to professional racing and freestyle competitions, SCS Gearbox delivers robust transfer case solutions built to withstand the rigorous demands of competitive monster truck applications.
