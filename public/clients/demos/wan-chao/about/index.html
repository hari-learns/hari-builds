Wan Chao Life Technology Co., Ltd.

Established in 1993.

Wan Chao is a leading supplier and exporter of premium stainless steel vacuum drinkware: thermal flasks, coffee mugs, tumblers, food jars, sports bottles and outdoor drinkware.

Our products are designed in Taiwan and manufactured in China, with a new factory in Vietnam launching production in December 2025, further strengthening our global supply chain.

As a reliable OEM and ODM drinkware supplier, we provide customized solutions for global distributors, retailers, and corporate gift companies.

Our brand: RICOCAFE

Alongside our manufacturing business, Wan Chao proudly owns and operates its lifestyle brand: RICOCAFE.

Inspired by the Spanish word rico, meaning abundance and richness in life, RICOCAFE embraces the spirit of slow living. Each product is thoughtfully designed using safe, eco-friendly stainless steel materials, reflecting our belief in creating meaningful, everyday essentials that elevate the quality of life.

Through RICOCAFE, we aim to help people rediscover the beauty in daily moments, one cup, one ritual at a time.

Sustainable materials. Smart designs.

Sustainability and innovation are at the heart of our product development. We utilize RCS-certified recycled materials and FSC-certified components to support environmentally responsible manufacturing.

Automation, quality, and compliance

Our factories feature automated production lines to ensure high efficiency and stable quality across large volumes. All Wan Chao products meet global safety and quality certifications, including:

  • FDA
  • LFGB
  • REACH
  • California Proposition 65
  • CPSIA
  • Drop and durability testing

Global reach, local strength

With facilities in China and Vietnam, and design based in Taiwan, Wan Chao delivers competitive pricing, flexible lead times, and responsive service to customers across Europe, Asia, North America, Latin America, and Oceania.
