FAQ

Their own answers, kept word for word.

What is recycled stainless steel?

First, you have to know what is RCS (Recycled Claim Standard). It is an international voluntary standard that sets requirements for third-party verification of recycled inputs and chain of custody. It applies to any product that contains 5% or more recycled content. RCS aims to increase use of recycled materials.

Our company meets the RCS standard and has the certificate (License Number: TE-00121378) to produce the products with 91% recycled post-consumer stainless steel.

What is FSC? Should I need to choose FSC-labelled products?

Have you seen such FSC label more and more often in recent years? And you're curious what this means ?

FSC (Forest Stewardship Council ) is an independent non-governmental organization (NGO). It applies to all forest industry-related services and products, including the manufacture, processing and sale of wood and paper products. There are three main types of FSC labels.

1. FSC 100% : All materials used come from responsibly managed, FSC-certified forests.

2. FSC Mix : The product is made with a mixture of materials from FSC-certified forests, recycled materials, and/or FSC-controlled wood.

3. FSC Recycled : The product is made from 100% recycled materials.

When you purchase FSC-labelled products, you are supporting "zero deforestation", "fair wages and working conditions", "species protection" and "local community rights".

Let’s do our part for environmental protection !

Why should you choose ceramic coating inside?

1. Enjoy the flavor - no metallic and bitter taste.

2. Ceramic coating prevent Staining.

3. Unbreakable - stainless steel mug with ceramic inner coating is more durable than pure ceramic mug and is convenient to bring it to anywhere.

How to use my new bottle/mug?

. Wash your new vacuum mug out with warm water before use.

. Hot liquid will stay hotter if you warm the mug with hot water before use.

. Cold liquid will stay cooler & longer if you add ice cubes before use.

. When filling the mug, leave a gap of approximately 1 cm between the liquid and stopper for better insulation effect.

Do I need to clean the gasket?

Yes, periodically take apart and clean the top gasket of cap to ensure that no the mildew is present, just be sure to put back the same position to prevent the leak.

How to clean the spot or water stains?

You may find the spots or water stains inside of bottle/mug caused by impurities in the water. To remove these spots or stains, frequently fill the bottle/mug with hot water and 10% vinegar and let stay for 30 minutes~1 hour and then wash thorough the interior.

Can I freeze my vacuum bottle/mug?

Do not put your vacuum bottle/mug in the freezer! Because liquids will expand during the freezing process and damage your bottle/mug even if it only is filled with the half liquids or leave the cap off. Filling the bottle/mug with ice cubes you need instead and let it slowly melt to become the ice cold water.

Can I put my vacuum bottle/mug into the boiling hot water for sterilizing?

Do not put your vacuum bottle/mug in the boiling hot water !

It will cause your bottle/mug with the situation of deformation, water leakage or paint peeling off.

Why my bottle has the rattle?

There is a small metal disk placed between the double wall of your bottle for completing the vacuum insulation process. Sometimes the disk will fall off and create a small noise. But, don’t worry, it will not affect the performance of insulation or structure of your bottle.

Is my bottle/mug BPA-free?

Yes, our all products are made of food-grade stainless steel and plastic, does not contain BPA.

What is BPA and is it safe?

BPA stands for bisphenol A. BPA is an industrial chemical that is commonly used in a variety of non-food applications: medical, paints, inks, thermal paper, electronic equipment, etc. BPA is also used to make epoxy resins, which is spread on the inner lining of canned food containers to keep the metal from corroding and breaking. People became worried about BPA safety because of animal studies that showed a link between high levels of the chemical and infertility , diabetes, obesity, heart disease, and high blood pressure.

Can I put hot liquid in the stainless steel single wall bottle/mug?

We strongly recommend to avoid putting the hot liquid into the single wall bottle and mug. Because the single wall is not vacuum structure.

You might get hurt when you take the bottle with hot liquid.

The vacuum mug and flask are more suitable for hot drink.

What is the difference between S/S 18/10 & 18/8?

The difference between S/S 18/10 & 18/8 is on the proportion of “ Nickel “ element. The proportion of Nickel is more, it will be better to defend the corrosion. . 18/10 : 18% of Chromium + 10% Nickel . 18/8 : 18% of Chromium + 8 % Nickel

Why my new bottle/mug has the plastic smell?

The smell feeling is usually more subjective, we suggest that you can take apart the plastic cap and silicone gasket in the ventilated place for letting the smell disperse or to soak the cap and gasket in a ratio of 1:10 vinegar and water, the smell will gradually fade away.
