Casement windows

We design and manufacture casement bays and windows to original specifications or to new designs to suit all property types. Our casement windows are made to order to match the design of your existing windows or to give your new build a traditional feel. Our expert craftsmen use their skills, with the best equipment and hardware, to ensure high security, efficient insulation and lengthy durability.

Stormproof casement

Stormproof windows are widely used modern windows. These casement windows feature an outwards rebate in which the casement overlaps the front face of the window frame, giving the window added weather protection. Stormproof casements offer enhanced protection from the weather and hold exceptional insulation qualities, while still maintaining a traditional style for a heritage look. In some circumstances where properties are highly exposed to adverse weather conditions, using Accoya timber will reduce the requirements for more frequent maintenance on hardwood timber framed windows.
