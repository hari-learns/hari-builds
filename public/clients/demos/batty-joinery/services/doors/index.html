Batty Joinery

Bespoke door and frame joinery

We can supply our clients with a wide range of door specifications. Batty Joinery are licensed to supply modified fire doors with apertures to match all clients' specifications. We have manufactured and supplied all types of internal timber doors, front doors, garage doors, gateways, sliding doors, trapdoors and partitions.

Meeting regulations and standards

From basic primed flush doors through to the highest quality inlaid veneer or panelled doors, Batty Joinery has the solution to meet most commercial or residential applications. Due to our bespoke nature, we will manufacture a range of fire, acoustic and security doors. Other performance doors are also tailored to suit individual customer requirements on all types of construction projects.

Pre-hung door sets

In our Heritage and Grand Design development projects particularly, individually tailored doors are increasingly being requested as a pre-hung door set. We are finding that our customers prefer to have matching door frames for their new, stylish doors instead of using the old, existing frame work. Clearly, major savings in time and money can be achieved by specifying and using our bespoke pre-hung door sets.

Facings, finishes and glazing

Doors are available with a comprehensive range of facings, finishes, glazing details and enhanced performance options. Batty Joinery is able to manufacture interior flush doors with a different wood finish on either side to match differing room décors. Interior and exterior panelled doors can be manufactured in styles and finishes to suit the widest range of aesthetic and functional requirements.
