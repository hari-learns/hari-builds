Batty Joinery

Custom grand design joinery

We have been involved with several new build developments and exciting major refurbishments across the UK, where we have supplied various aspects of joinery of the highest calibre.

Exceed client expectations

Some joinery projects have been passed to Batty Joinery because of the general complexity and intricacy of the joinery product at hand. A range of projects have included some spectacular designs that have given our joiners valuable experience in order to carry out the manufacture of further exciting joinery projects.

An example of our Grand Designs work has included a number of highly complex curve-on-curve hardwood timber windows and doors. These windows and doors incorporated custom made double glazing units, all for a private development in the UK.

The design and planning for this project was complex and needed great commitment from our experienced estimators and designers to calculate the detailed geometry involved in double curvature work.

Best quality timber

Batty Joinery has manufactured and installed many grand feature staircases, bespoke cabinets and fitted furniture in countless prestigious grand build projects. What makes our Grand Design joinery distinct is the use of the best quality timber materials, available from well-managed and responsible sources and manufactured with great respect and skill by our award-winning and qualified joiners.

Batty Joinery ensures great care and attention to detail from the start of the project to the finishing. We always do our utmost to exceed our clients' expectations and deliver an excellent product with a quality service.
