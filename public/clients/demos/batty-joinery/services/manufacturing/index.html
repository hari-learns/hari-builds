Batty Joinery

Custom manufacturing joinery

Batty Joinery manufacture bespoke joinery products that are custom built for each individual client. We believe this personal touch gives our service a unique quality that is hard to come across, but refreshingly important. Our range of joinery products stems from the usual to the elaborate.

Custom built

Some examples of our bespoke joinery work include complex, highly skilled curve-on-curve projects and straight and geometric oak staircases.

We have also worked on countless traditional and bespoke timber windows, fire doors, frames and screens, period roof trusses, bespoke fitted cabinets for domestic clients, and commercial internal fittings including numerous bars and reception counters.

From estimate to fitting

Our joiners and estimators are highly proficient in all aspects of joinery. We have a proven track record of delivering all types of bespoke joinery projects, from estimating and design development, through to production, and then final installation and fitting.

The windows manufactured for our client by Batty Joinery are a testament to their workmanship and experience.
