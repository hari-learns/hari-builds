Wire mesh

Welded reinforcing mesh, wire 4 to 10 mm, openings 10 to 30 cm, galvanized on request.

Welded reinforced wire mesh. Both the line wire and cross wire are fed from pre-cut wire. Steel wire mesh can be produced in all standard sizes, with the ability to produce special sizes.
