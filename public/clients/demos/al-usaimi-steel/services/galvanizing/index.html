Services

Galvanizing Services

Al Usaimi Steel Co. are committed to provide our customers ideal solutions to tackle the corrosion and rusting problems inherent in all steel products. Galvanizing is a process in which the metal is immersed into molten zinc to form a protective layer which covers steel surface completely, metallurgically sealing it to protect it from corrosive actions of the environment.

Galvanizing brochure (PDF, 16 pages)

The brochure is issued by Modern Steel Coating Co. (MSCC) and is the document linked from the Galvanizing Services tile on the current site.

13 mKettle length (brochure p. 9; p. 3 says 14 m)
1.6 mKettle width
3 mKettle depth, vertical dipping up to 2.9 m
20 mLongest item with a double dip

What is offered

  • Collection and delivery services on request
  • Fast track delivery in 24 hours on request
  • Customer service and technical assistance
  • Spin galvanizing for small tools (fasteners, bolts, nuts)
  • Double dip up to 20 meters (with 14 m long kettle)
  • Vertical dipping up to 2.9 meter (with 3 m depth kettle)
  • ISO 9001 approved

What makes it special

  • Ability to galvanize bolts and nuts using a high speed spinning machine, which rids the steel of any excess zinc coating
  • A high velocity system for even heat transfer to all sides of the kettle, for uniform coating thickness
  • A continuous filter system from the flux tank to keep its properties constant
  • A monorail system for dipping in the kettle, which allows more dipping per hour
