Scope of services

Upholding the highest standards at every phase, we offer comprehensive services ranging from design to installation.

Design

Continuous enhancement, adapting to evolving steel structure designs with highly skilled engineers, tailored to each project's specifications.

  • Full structural design
  • Full structural re-design
  • Shop drawings
  • Design verification

Steel works

Steel manufactured according to project designs and specifications, with efficient use of raw materials and energy.

  • Supply
  • Miscellaneous steel
  • Truss systems
  • Hot rolled sections (HRS)
  • Built up sections (PEB)
  • Fabrication: light, medium and heavy weight

Protection

Surface cleaning, hot dip galvanizing, paint systems and fireproofing, executed by skilled experts.

  • Surface cleaning
  • Hot dip galvanizing
  • Paint system
  • Fireproofing

Logistics and installation

Transportation and installation, with resources used efficiently.

  • Transportation fleet
  • Erection and installation
