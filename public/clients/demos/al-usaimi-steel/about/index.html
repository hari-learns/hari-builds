About

Fifty years of steel for the Kingdom and the Gulf

Since its inception 50 years ago, Othman Abdul Rahman Al-Usaimi & Partners Trade Co. pledged to provide to its customers with all its steel product needs in Saudi Arabia and neighboring Gulf countries.

Pursuing this vision, the company has created more than 50 branches to cover the needs and requirements of its customers in the northern, western, southern, eastern, and central regions in KSA. The company has also, in recent years, developed and expanded its presence in the Arabian Gulf by establishing commercial branches in Bahrain, Qatar, Kuwait, and Oman.

These expansions were followed by the launching of Al-Usaimi Transport Co. to manage the internal and external fleet serving all regional branches and customer requests on time.
