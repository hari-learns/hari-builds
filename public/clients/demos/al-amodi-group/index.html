From one store to Qatar’s supply chain

Based in Doha, Qatar, Al Amodi Group is the pioneer of direct supply and distribution of branded international building materials from all over the world. Family-owned since 1957, the parent company Al Amodi Store is an innovative company rooted in a time-honored tradition.

QAPROFIL - Qatar Profiles and False Ceilings Factory. Established in 2003 under the pavilion of the leader company in the field of building materials, Al Amodi Group for Trading and General Contracting. QAPROFIL serves in the production field of slitting utilities, indoor false ceilings, dry wall partitions accessories, light gauge steel buildings construction and steel buildings roof sheets and accessories.
