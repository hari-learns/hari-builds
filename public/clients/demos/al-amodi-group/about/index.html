Six decades of being part of building Qatar

Family-owned since 1957. Parent company Al Amodi Store; Al Amodi Group for Trading & General Contracting.

Based in Doha, Qatar, Al Amodi Group is the pioneer of direct supply and distribution of branded international building materials from all over the world. Family-owned since 1957, the parent company Al Amodi Store is an innovative company rooted in a time-honored tradition.

Over its 60 plus-year history, Al Amodi Group has become known as a trusted lifestyle brand offering quality building materials, sanitaryware, sanitary fittings, floorings and sophisticated home decor products.

From ceramic tiles, sanitaryware and fittings to the essentials of drywall and ceilings, Al Amodi Group has left its mark in the history of Qatar. We have been supplying renowned hotels, royal palaces, custom luxury villas and public projects such as schools and hospitals for Ashghal and the FIFA 2022 stadiums, with a high reputation in logistics and customer satisfaction.

With the ever-increasing requirement for quality assurance and control in supply, Al Amodi Group has executed projects on proven systems and procedures as per the requirements of its clients.
