FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What fit-out services does Fitout Bureau Interiors offer in Dubai?

We offer a full range of fit-out services including interior design, residential and villa fit-outs, commercial and office fit-outs, retail fit-outs, hospitality fit-outs, bespoke joinery, MEP (Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing) works, space planning, custom furniture production, and authority approvals. We are a true one-stop-shop for all your interior needs across the UAE.

How long does a typical fit-out project take?

Project timelines vary depending on the scope and size of the job. A small retail or office fit-out can take 4–8 weeks, while larger residential villas or hospitality projects may take 3–6 months. From day one, our dedicated project managers provide a clear timeline and ensure on-time delivery without compromising quality.

Do you handle authority approvals and permits in Dubai?

Yes, absolutely. Navigating Dubai's regulatory landscape can be complex, but we handle all government and authority approvals on your behalf — including Dubai Municipality, Trakhees, and developer NOCs. Our experienced team ensures full compliance so your project moves forward without delays or complications.

Can I see a 3D visualization of my space before construction begins?

Absolutely. We use cutting-edge 3D visualization and rendering tools so you can see exactly how your space will look before a single nail is hammered. This ensures full alignment with your vision and gives you the opportunity to make adjustments before construction starts, saving time and cost.

Do you work on both residential and commercial projects?

Yes. We have extensive experience across all project types — from luxurious private villas and apartments to high-end retail stores, restaurants, hotels, and corporate offices. With over 10 years of experience and 1,000+ projects delivered across the UAE, we bring the same level of passion and precision to every project regardless of size.

How do I get started with Fitout Bureau Interiors?

Getting started is simple. Reach out to us via our website or call us at +971 554393355 to schedule a free consultation. We'll sit down with you — over coffee if you like — to understand your vision, requirements, and budget, and then walk you through our process from design concept to handover.

What makes Fitout Bureau Interiors different from other fit-out companies in Dubai?

We combine award-winning design expertise with over a decade of on-the-ground experience in Dubai's competitive fit-out market. Our clients — including global brands like Atlantis, DAMAC, Emaar, Majid Al Futtaim, and Burj Al Arab — trust us for our attention to detail, transparent communication, on-time delivery, and ability to handle every aspect of a project under one roof. We don't just build spaces — we craft experiences.
