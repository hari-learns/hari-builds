What we make for you

Inkonox processes stainless steel and aluminium. Laser cutting, welding, CNC bending, rolling, laser welding, bead blasting, powder coating, drilling and many other finishing operations are part of the daily work in our workshop.

Laser cutting

A fully automatic flatbed laser cutting machine for single pieces and series, from a permanent stock of stainless steel and aluminium. Stainless up to 15 mm, steel up to 20 mm, aluminium up to 10 mm, sheet up to 3000 × 1500 mm.

Bending and rolling

CNC bending and rolling of cylinders and cones. Millimetres matter: measure, bend, measure again.

Welding

Experienced TIG and MIG welders: TIG, MIG/MAG, spot welding, laser welding.
