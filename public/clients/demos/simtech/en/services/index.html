Services

Industrial automation services

Draft translation based on Simtech's own English pages. To be confirmed by the company.

Industrial robot programming

We have many years of experience in programming robotic stations, both online and offline. Over the past few years, we have completed dozens of projects worldwide.

Our engineers have the knowledge and skills to work with the most renowned manufacturers such as KUKA, Fanuc and ABB.

  • Programming industrial robots
  • Commissioning robotic production lines
  • Designing robotic production lines
  • Optimization of robotic production lines
  • Technical consulting in robotics

PLC programming

Our engineers work with solutions from many renowned suppliers. We program PLC controllers from Siemens, Allen-Bradley, Pilz PSS, Mitsubishi, Phoenix, Beckhoff, Omron and Schneider Modicon, and design and commission industrial networks: Profibus, Interbus, DeviceNet, ControlNet, Modbus Plus, AS-i Bus, CANopen.

  • PLC programming
  • Industrial communication networks
  • Control system design (PMA KS98)

HMI and SCADA visualization

We design and implement SCADA and HMI systems with the tools of leading suppliers: Zenon (COPA-DATA), InTouch (Wonderware), WinCC and ProTool (Siemens), FactoryTalk and RSView (Rockwell Automation), VisAM.

  • Zenon
  • InTouch
  • WinCC
  • FactoryTalk

Comprehensive implementation of projects

We execute turnkey investments. Our offer includes comprehensive implementation of the project, from the design phase to commissioning: selection, supply and installation of components, commissioning, technical documentation and staff training.

  • Design
  • Supply and installation
  • Commissioning
  • Documentation
  • Training

Prefabrication of control cabinets

Our company specializes in the prefabrication of control and power cabinets, using our own materials or those provided by clients. Own 750 m2 hall in Gliwice, E-PLAN documentation, factory acceptance tests (FAT), delivery and commissioning support.

  • 750 m2 own hall
  • E-PLAN
  • FAT
  • IEC 61439
  • UL 508a
