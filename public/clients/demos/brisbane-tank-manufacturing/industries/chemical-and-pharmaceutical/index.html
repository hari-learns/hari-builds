Industry

Chemical and pharmaceutical tanks

Our business is complex and involves strong chemical blends, and the requirements for high quality tanks, pipework and installation works have always been very important to us. Ecolab Australia says BTM has become an excellent one-stop-shop for its tank related installations.

What BTM builds

  • A 10,000 litre 316 stainless pressure vessel with reactor, mixer, heating and cooling for the chemical industry.
  • Two 10,000 litre mixing tanks to AS1210 for Bayer, rated 3 bar and -1 bar vacuum, with dimple and water jackets.
  • A 2,000 litre 316 extraction tank with an in-house dimple plate and a 50 micron filtration basket.
  • A 400 litre insulated reverse osmosis tank in duplex 2205 for a pharmaceutical sterilisation department.
