Industry

Brewery and beverage tanks

We design and build storage tanks for breweries and we also design and build the fermenting tanks too. Our client base includes Carlton United Breweries, and BTM has made chilled ethanol tanks for the Castlemaine XXXX brewery at Milton.

What BTM builds

  • Balance tanks for brewing storage, with side or centre pipe manifolds and closed or open tops.
  • Fermenting tanks, made to work well under pressure, and hot and cold liquor tanks.
  • Two 30,000 litre stainless ethanol tanks designed to AS1210 and AS1692 that run the refrigeration at the XXXX Milton brewery.
  • "Within hours of calling them I had a competitive quote and a time frame the work would be completed." Harley Goodacre, All Inn Brewing Co.
