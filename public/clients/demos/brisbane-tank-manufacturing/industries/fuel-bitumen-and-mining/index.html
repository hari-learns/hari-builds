Industry

Fuel, bitumen and mining tanks

We've worked around Australia on some pretty massive projects from mining to breweries. Fuel and bitumen tanks are built in carbon or mild steel as well as stainless, documented to AS1692, AS1210 and API650.

What BTM builds

  • Fuel storage tanks from 1 litre to 150,000 litres, including self bunded, fire rated and trailer mounted tanks.
  • Bitumen mixing and storage tanks from a 5,000 L tank with four heating elements to a 65,000 L mild steel storage tank.
  • A vertical diesel tank for Fulton Hogan's Gladstone asphalt plant, built in 10 weeks, and four bunded 6,135 L tanks for BP.
  • Towed frac tanks that drain fully without sloping, blasted and coated or rubber lined.
