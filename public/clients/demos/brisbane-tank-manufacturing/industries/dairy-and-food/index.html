Industry

Stainless steel tanks for dairy and food

Brisbane Tank Manufacturing specialises in the fabrication of stainless steel products for industries as diverse as food, dairy, chemical, mining, commercial, brewing and beverage. Our client base includes industry experts such as Parmalat and Dairy Farmers.

What BTM builds

  • Storage tanks suitable for dairy, food and beverage, with CIP/CIL suitability, single skin or insulated, vertical or horizontal, up to 100,000 litres.
  • Balance tanks with steam injection for CIP, built to hit the stringent food grade standards.
  • Mixing tanks for food and dairy processing, with dimple plate, baffles and CIP systems.
  • Food grade 316 stainless steel, ground smooth, as in the three 15,000 L olive oil tanks that BTM manufactured and installed.
