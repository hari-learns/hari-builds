Frac tanks

Frac Tanks are normally used for fracing or hydraulic fracturing of wells in the gas and energy sectors. These high-quality storage tanks can also be used to store any liquids like run-off water and liquid waste products.

At a glance

Drainage
All the fluid drains out without sloping the tank
Finish
Blasted and coated, or rubber lined
Testing
Every tank water tested before leaving
Fittings
Safety rails, access ladders, hatches, manways, non slip decks

Our engineered designed custom built frac tanks are well fabricated and equipped with features that benefit our clients. Our tanks are extremely efficient and are specifically designed so all the fluid can be drained out from the tank without the need to slope the tank for complete evacuation. The floor of these tanks is custom-made to get rid of the buildup of material; our tanks have smooth lined inner walls which helps for easier drainage.

The tanks can even be used for large clean up operations or simply for temporary storage of water or other liquids.

To avoid any field modifications, our expert control team water tests every tank prior to leaving.

We can also customise and manufacture our frac tanks for inlets, outlets, drains and vents to the customer's specifications which can also include hoses, valves, pumps, and filters as required.

Features of our frac tanks

  • Multiple valves for easy access for various pumping operations
  • Convenient built in stairs to aid a safer working environment
  • 2 manway hatches, 1 at the front and the other on the side
  • Inspection hatch on the top and a fold down ladder at the rear
  • 4 by 4 inch valves at the front of the tank
