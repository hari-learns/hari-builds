Tanks

Mixing tanks

Brisbane Tank Manufacturing is a professional manufacturer engaged in engineering, designing, developing, manufacturing, selling, installation and after-sales service of mixing tanks. Our customer centric focus across all levels of the business means your investment in us pays off.

At a glance

Sizes
Stainless tanks and vessels supplied up to 50,000 litres
Materials
Carbon steel or stainless steel
Standards
ASME code or non-code, ASME pressure vessel code manufacture
Paperwork
Test certificates available with every tank

We have the ability to manufacture mixing tanks with a variety of steels. We offer both carbon and stainless steel tank options. These tanks are available in a wide range of sizes. Over the years we have supplied stainless steel tanks and vessels up to 50,000 litres, with a variety of specifications.

Measurement and assembly of all the tanks are done according to our customer's requirements, including the preferred rate of discharge and concentration of the solution, as well as desired volume of the tank.

Whether the mixing tanks are used for any big or small application, they are all custom built to order specifically to your design requirements.

All our tanks meet strict quality standards and protocols and all test certificates are available with every tank to guarantee our product meets industry specifications and needs.

Types of mixing tanks we make

  • Large diameter tanks
  • ASME code or non-code
  • Jacketed tanks
  • Carbon steel or stainless steel
  • Quality control inspection and testing
