Top entry agitator mixers

Brisbane Tank Manufacturing's Top Entry Agitator Mixers are designed to handle some of the industry's most demanding mixing applications. Built with a robust modular concept, these mixers offer versatile tank mixing solutions for open, closed, and fully sealed vessels.

At a glance

Drives
Electric, 0.37 kW to 40 kW
Shafts
19 mm to 100 mm diameter
Vessels
Open, closed and fully sealed
Options
Flameproof drives, mechanical seals for pressure and vacuum vessels

Options for flameproof drives, mechanical seals to accommodate pressure and vacuum vessels, and compatibility with corrosive or high-temperature products ensure adaptability across diverse environments. Custom options such as duplex alloy shafts, keyed or fully welded impellor hubs, a variety of gear drives, and varied impeller designs provide flexibility to suit any mixing requirements. Each design undergoes rigorous testing to optimise fluid flow and performance.

Ideal for

  • Blending solids
  • Wastewater and sewerage treatment
  • Wine processing
  • Chemical and cosmetic mixing
  • Water treatment
  • Food and beverage blending
  • Liquid fertiliser
  • Mineral processing
  • Product re-suspension
  • Vessel cleaning
