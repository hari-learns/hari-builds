Process

How an enquiry becomes a tank

1

You send an enquiry

It goes to the Estimating Manager, who gathers as much information as possible around the exact requirements for the tank.

2

Design

That information goes to the engineer, who uses a CAD system to create the drawings that act as the blueprint for the fabricators, including material thickness and shape.

3

Fabrication and testing

The fabrication team makes the tank. Every single tank is tested for leaks before it leaves, using dye penetrant, and test certificates are available.

4

Delivery and installation

BTM works with reputable transport and logistics companies to truck, unload and crane in, and installs the tank on site.
