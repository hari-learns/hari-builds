What we build

Six kinds of tank, one workshop

Brisbane Tank Manufacturing designs, manufactures, supplies and installs steel tanks that cater to all types of industries for a variety of storage requirements.

Storage tanks

Small and large stainless steel storage tanks for dairy, food, beverage, brewery, mining, pharmaceutical and chemical work.

See the range

Mixing tanks

Jacketed, dimple plate and agitated mixing tanks in carbon or stainless steel, ASME code or non-code.

See the range

Balance tanks

Side or centre pipe, closed or open top, with float assemblies, steam injection for CIP and vortex breakers.

See the range

Fuel storage tanks

Diesel, petrol and bunded fuel tanks from 1 litre to 150,000 litres, documented to AS1210, AS1692 and API.

See the range

Pressure vessels

Stainless and carbon steel pressure vessels and shell and tube heat exchangers, to AS1210, AS1692, ASME and API.

See the range

Frac tanks

Towed frac tanks that drain fully without sloping, blasted and coated or rubber lined, water tested before they leave.

See the range

Since 1999

Committed to quality, committed to you

Established in 1999, Tony Robertson had a vision to build a storage tank manufacturing and fabrication business based on quality and commitment; hence why our reputation as industry leaders is something we're all proud of.

Our products play a huge part in keeping major operations running efficiently, as well as safely, and everything designed and manufactured meets strict industry standards. We provide a full solution package which not only includes design and build but installation and maintenance too.

As Tony says, "you have to have passion and purpose" in your business and at Brisbane Tank Manufacturing we have this in tank loads.

Our dedicated team of professionals is made up of qualified engineers, designers, welders, fabricators, installers and support staff working together over every step of the process.
