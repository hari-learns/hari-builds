Installation

Tank installation

Depend on Brisbane Tank Manufacturing as the specialist in the expert installation of your stainless steel storage tanks. We've worked around Australia on some pretty massive projects from mining to breweries and our reputation is second to none.

Our awesome crew bring years of experience and expertise and it goes without saying all tanks are installed in compliance with national and local standards.

From design consultations to tank manufacturing, field installations to final tests; Brisbane Tank Manufacturing has the facilities, proven capabilities, expertise and high-tech resources to deliver the best solutions at the best possible prices. Over the years we have scores of repeat customers with relationships that are growing stronger and stronger.

As your installers and project managers quality control and execution is our number one focus. We know the correct installation of an industrial tank is critical as the life blood of your operation and we're only too aware of the fact that the topmost reason for tank failure is due to improper installation.
