About us

Lead modernly for generations

The name ENGSTLER stands for a flexible family enterprise which has been lead modernly for generations. By linking tradition and innovative technology we are able to present not only mechanical cuckoo clocks, but also the latest development of quartz cuckoo clocks.

The first cuckoo clocks were manufactured in the Black Forest during the 18th century.
