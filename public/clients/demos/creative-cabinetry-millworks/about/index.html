About Creative Cabinetry & Millworks

Established in 2016, Creative Cabinetry and Millworks, nestled in the heart of Anaheim, California, was founded on the principle of delivering luxury residential and commercial cabinetry and other millwork products to its community. With its own local manufacturing operation, we are able to deliver quality product in an expedient manner.

Our process

Four steps.

To bring you the best service and quality products, we follow these 4 simple steps to make your dream into a reality.

  1. Free consultation

    Our knowledgeable staff will set up an on-site consultation to discuss your project.

  2. Design & construction estimate

    We will take precise measurements and build a design based on your specification in order to provide a detailed quotation.

  3. Fabrication

    Upon approval of the project, we will begin fabrication immediately in our own factory mill in Anaheim, CA.

  4. Installation

    When the fabrication is completed, the installation will start. Then voila!

Our manufacturer

Overseas capacity, local service.

Creative Cabinetry is partnered with a manufacturer overseas. Our manufacturer overseas allows us to take on large projects, while our shop in Anaheim, California provides local after-sale service. We understand that damaged or missing parts can cause a delay in project completion, which is why we offer an after-sale service that immediately replaces any parts within a week, unlike normal suppliers who can take up to 3–6 months.

We take pride in delivering premium quality millwork products at a competitive price. Our team of experienced professionals is dedicated to providing excellent customer service and delivering products that meet your exact specifications and exceed your expectations.
