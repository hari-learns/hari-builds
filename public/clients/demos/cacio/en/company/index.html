About Cácio

A family company producing furniture for over 70 years

Cácio is a family-owned company that has been producing furniture for over 70 years. The oak furniture pieces, from rustic to modern, form multiple collections of living and dining rooms, which already have more than 200 catalogued products.

Mission

With a continuous investment in design and commitment to the highest quality standards, Cácio aims to promote happiness and customer comfort.

Vision

The aim of the company is to continue to be a reference in the international markets; for that matter, Cácio presents its clients the highest quality furniture, service excellence throughout the process and satisfaction of their desires and needs.

Values

The values of Cácio have begun with its founders, and those remain to this day. Ethics, respect for employees, customers and environment are the guidelines, amongst honesty and transparency. The continuous improvement and investment on innovation and quality are also essential values for the company that has always, as it is for more than 50 years, the customer in the spotlight.

The history

One family, one factory in Paredes

  1. 1960s

    To overcome the strong wave of emigration of the 1960s and improve their family's living conditions, Acácio Alberto Ferreira de Barros and Maria Barros da Silva build a factory and spread the art they learned with love.

  2. 1970

    With the help of their son Domingos Barros, the couple create a new unit in Santa Luzia, Rebordosa, Paredes, where the company expands until the end of 2005.

  3. 1987

    The youngest son, Elias Acácio da Silva Barros, joins. Management is shared by the two brothers, Alfredo Barros and Elias Barros.

  4. 1990s

    Internationalisation begins, first to Spain and France.

  5. 2000

    The internationalisation strategy is stepped up: the company starts selling to the rest of Europe.

  6. 2001

    The quality of the products leads to good acceptance in the European market and Cácio starts exporting 100% of its production.

  7. 2006

    The new facilities are inaugurated, equipped with the sector's cutting-edge technology.

  8. 2019

    Elias Barros takes on new projects and leaves the company. Management remains in the family, led by Alfredo Barros, Sara Barros and Alfredo Gonzaga Barros.

Export

The internationalisation process began in the 1990s, for Spain and France. In 2000 the company started to sell to the rest of Europe and, in 2001, to export 100% of its production.

Management today

A family company, today led by Alfredo Barros, Sara Barros and Alfredo Gonzaga Barros.
