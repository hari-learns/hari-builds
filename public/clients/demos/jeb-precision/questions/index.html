Straight answers

Questions buyers ask

Eight questions a buyer asks before sending a drawing. Every answer is taken from J.E.B.'s own website or its January 2024 plant list, with the page it came from.

Do you machine in 5 axis?

Yes. J.E.B. offers simultaneous 5 axis machining on a Deckel Maho Gildermeister DMU 40 Evo, and a DMG DMU 50 3rd Generation that offers full 5 axis simultaneous machining. Additional 5 axis positional capability on the Mikron Vario and Bridgeport 5AX machines offers single set up manufacturing. Integrated pallet loading systems enable lights out manufacturing. Their January 2024 plant list names five 5 axis machining centres.

Source on their site:CNC Turning & MillingDMU 50 newsPlant list, Jan 2024

CNC milling

What bar sizes can you turn?

J.E.B. has 9 CNC lathes (turning centres) with bar fed capacity of up to 50 mm diameter, including live tooling. The Nakamura AS200LMYS has 65 mm bar capacity with sub spindle and full Y axis on both spindles. Larger CNC turning is offered on the robust CMZ lathes, with a maximum billet size of 12 inch diameter.

Source on their site:CNC Turning & MillingNakamura news

CNC turning

Do you offer wire EDM?

Yes. J.E.B. offers wire EDM machining with Agie Cut 20 wire erosion technology, alongside its CNC turning and milling. The January 2024 plant list shows two Agie Charmilles Cut 20 wire eroders.

Source on their site:Wire ErosionPlant list, Jan 2024

Components are produced in a broad range of materials including stainless steels, non ferrous metals and engineering polymers.

About us

Which certification do you hold, and how is quality recorded?

J.E.B. Precision operates a Quality Management System approved to ISO 9001:2015. Each batch has a production routing that defines the manufacturing operations and inspection criteria, and records the inspection results and, where material traceability is required, the material certification. The documents are archived on site. There is a fully equipped inspection department with co-ordinate measuring machines, and a UKAS accredited laboratory is retained for calibrations beyond their own facility.

Source on their site:Quality Assurance

Do you export?

Yes. J.E.B.'s customer base stretches throughout the UK, exporting to Europe, Japan and the USA.

What quantities do you make?

J.E.B. produces components in low to medium quantities, with larger quantities scheduled monthly to meet customer requirements. Their machining and clean room assembly facilities are set up for regular monthly call offs or to Kanban (JIT) requirement.

Do you also assemble, weld and leak test?

Yes. J.E.B. cleans and assembles components with bought out or free issue parts to the customer's Bill of Material in a clean environment, and has TIG welding and helium vacuum leak checking on site. They describe it as a one stop shop for the scientific instrument and vacuum science industries.
