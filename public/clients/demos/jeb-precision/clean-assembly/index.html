Clean assembly: common questions

How are the parts cleaned?

Components are cleaned in a 7 stage aqueous ultrasonic system. On their January 2024 plant list it is an Everest 7 Stage Ultrasonic Aqueous Degreasing System. Where required, components are also electropolished and cleaned ready for immediate integration into the customer's product.

Can J.E.B. deliver assemblies regularly?

Yes. Their machining and clean room assembly facilities are set up for delivering regular monthly call offs or to Kanban (JIT) requirement.

Which quality system applies?

J.E.B. Precision operates a Quality Management System approved to ISO 9001:2015. A production routing defines the sequence of manufacturing operations and inspection criteria for each batch, and is archived on site with the inspection results.

Sources: their Assembly and Quality Assurance pages, and the January 2024 plant list.
