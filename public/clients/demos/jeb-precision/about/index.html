About us

Formed in January 1974, J.E.B. Precision Limited has achieved sustained growth which is founded upon a no compromise commitment to product quality combined with a 'right first time' culture which pervades throughout the company.

As a contract manufacturer we produce components in low to medium quantities, with larger quantities scheduled monthly to meet customer requirements. Components are produced in a broad range of materials including, stainless steels, non ferrous metals and engineering polymers.

Our manufactured components are cleaned and assembled with bought out parts, giving customers a single source of supply, thus eliminating problems associated with multi sourcing.
