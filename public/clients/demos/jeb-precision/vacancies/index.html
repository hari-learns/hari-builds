Roles J.E.B. has advertised on its own site

Role descriptions as on J.E.B.'s own vacancies page.

Inspector

Inspection (first off and final inspection) of machined components using conventional metrology equipment and coordinate measuring machines.

Preparation of components for dispatch to customers. This may involve manual finishing (de-burring/polishing) so that components are presented in the right condition to be incorporated in to customers' product.

Maintaining records and following procedures as per the Company's ISO9001 quality system.

Requirements

  • Fully experienced in the use of conventional metrology equipment and manual coordinate measuring machines.
  • Thorough understanding of engineering drawings including geometrical tolerancing.
  • Experience of working within an ISO9001 or AS9100 environment and of calibration systems would be advantageous.

Hrs Mon – Thurs 08:00 – 13:00, 13:30 – 17:00. Fri 08:00 – 13:00. Salary negotiable depending on experience.

CNC Turner, Setter/Operator

Setting and operating of CNC lathes equipped with Fanuc controls manufacturing short and medium batch high precision components in a wide variety of materials. You will be driven by a passion for quality and will be experienced in using a variety of inspection equipment to monitor product quality throughout a batch. We offer an excellent environment with varied and interesting work.

Requirements

  • Time served, fully skilled machinist.
  • Experience in the manufacture of low volume high quality instrument and or aerospace type components.
  • Experience with Fanuc controls.
  • Diligent flexible approach and a fine eye for detail.
  • Good understanding of geometrical and dimensional tolerancing.

Hrs Mon – Thurs 08:00 – 13:00, 13:30 – 17:00. Fri 08:00 – 13:00.

Instrument Assembler

Assembly of small intricate precision electro-mechanical units for scientific instrument applications. Operating as part of our assembly team, you will have a diligent aptitude, excellent dexterity and a fine eye for detail. You will build complex electro-mechanical assemblies from detailed engineering drawings in a timely manner to meet production schedule requirements.

Requirements

  • A sound mechanical engineering background, extremely organised, able to prioritise workload to ensure customer schedules are met.
  • Experience in one or more of: UHV System build, Clean Room Assembly Protocol, Electrical Soldering, Basic Wire Loom Manufacture, Vacuum Leak Checking and or TIG welding for UHV applications would be a distinct advantage.

Hrs Mon – Thurs 08:00 – 13:00, 13:30 – 17:00. Fri 08:00 – 13:00. Salary negotiable depending on experience.

Highly Skilled Manual Machinist

A highly skilled manual machinist, potentially from a tool making background: a multi skilled machinist with experience of producing high precision components, both in small batches and as 1 offs for jig and fixture production, experienced in turning, milling, cylindrical & surface grinding and jig boring.

Requirements

  • Time served, fully skilled machinist.
  • Experience of the manufacture of low volume high quality instrument type parts or tool making.
  • Diligent flexible approach and a fine eye for detail.
  • Ability to work from written operation instruction or direct from component drawing with a good understanding of geometrical and dimensional tolerancing.

Hrs Mon – Thurs 08:00 – 13:00, 13:30 – 17:00. Fri 08:00 – 13:00. Salary negotiable.
