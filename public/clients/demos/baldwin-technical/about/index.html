About us

About Baldwin Technical Services

We work closely with our clients designing and developing solutions to satisfy their specific requirements.

Who we are

Baldwin Technical Services are dedicated to Health and Safety in all aspects of the business. We pride ourselves on our commitment to Health and Safety and continuously strive to improve and maintain our high standards through education and training.

Baldwin Technical Services have many years of experience within the food industry, and employ specialist engineers in electrical, software and mechanical design.

We design, build, install and commission systems on a European wide basis. We believe that our impressive customer list reflects our ability.

Your project

What we can bring to your project

We are fully experienced with all major PLCs, HMIs, inverters and servo drives; our design and commissioning engineers are people with great in-depth skill and understanding of the electrical and process engineering industry.

We offer our customers a fully comprehensive technical support service and are always happy to help with in-house or on-site training.

We have a modern well-equipped workshop and offices. Customers are welcome to visit us in order to discuss their projects generally and to inspect their equipment on build or for pre-installation training.

History

Our history

Baldwin Technical Services Ltd has built an enviable international client base that includes some of the UK and global brands from within the food and beverage industry, such as Warburtons, Hovis, Bakkavor, Tip Top, Harry Brot and many more.

Baldwin Technical Services Ltd continues to invest significantly in its people and technologies, which has allowed us to expand and grow outside of the food and beverage industry.

  1. 1995

    Established

    Baldwin Technical has grown from a small team of core engineers working predominantly on control systems in the UK food industry to an enterprise that operates globally and can assist with all aspects of automation, electrical and software requirements.

  2. 2014

    Partnered with Sugden Ltd

    Baldwin Technical Services partnered with Sugden Ltd, world leaders in the design and manufacture of hotplate plants. Sugden specialise in the production of English muffin, crumpet, pancake and potato cake lines.

  3. 2016

    CFE partnership

    Sugden and Baldwin Technical partnered up with CFE Lighting, a company that specialises in manufacturing energy efficient LED lighting, providing customers with long-term solutions with short-term payback.

  4. 2019

    New facility

    Sugden open their new purpose-built production facility.

  5. 2020

    Storage facility

    Baldwin Technical acquire a new building to accommodate our expanding panel and bulk storage services.
