We work closely with our clients designing and developing unique Engineering Solutions

Baldwin Technical Services are dedicated to Health and Safety in all aspects of the business. We pride ourselves on our commitment to Health and Safety and continuously strive to improve and maintain our high standards through education and training.

Baldwin Technical Services have many years of experience within the food industry, and employs specialist engineers in electrical, software and mechanical design.

We are fully experienced with all major PLC's, HMI's, Inverters and Servo Drives; our design and commissioning engineers are people with great in-depth skill and understanding of the electrical and process engineering industry.

    History

    Since 1995

    Baldwin Technical Services Ltd has built an enviable international client base that includes some of the UK and global brands from within the food and beverage industry, such as Warburtons, Hovis, Bakkavor, Tip Top, Harry Brot and many more.

    1. 1995

      Established

      Baldwin Technical has grown from a small team of core engineers working predominantly on control systems in the UK food industry to an enterprise that operates globally and can assist with all aspects of automation, electrical and software requirements.

    2. 2014

      Partnered with Sugden Ltd

      Baldwin Technical Services partnered with Sugden Ltd, world leaders in the design and manufacture of hotplate plants. Sugden specialise in the production of English muffin, crumpet, pancake and potato cake lines.

    3. 2016

      CFE partnership

      Sugden and Baldwin Technical partnered up with CFE Lighting, a company that specialises in manufacturing energy efficient LED lighting, providing customers with long-term solutions with short-term payback.

    4. 2019

      New facility

      Sugden open their new purpose-built production facility.

    5. 2020

      Storage facility

      Baldwin Technical acquire a new building to accommodate our expanding panel and bulk storage services.
