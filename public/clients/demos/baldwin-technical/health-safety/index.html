CCNSG

The CCNSG safety passport covers

  • Health and Safety law and acts, Health and Safety at Work Act 1974
  • Safety behaviour and a safe work environment
  • Risk assessments
  • Accidents and reporting, RIDDOR
  • Hazards and risks, control procedures
  • First aid procedures
  • Fire prevention, precautions and procedures
  • PPE: personal protective equipment
  • COSHH
  • Manual handling
  • PUWER
  • HAVS: hand-arm vibration at work
  • LOLER
  • Noise safety
  • Safe working at height
  • Confined spaces
  • Electrical safety
  • Environmental awareness
