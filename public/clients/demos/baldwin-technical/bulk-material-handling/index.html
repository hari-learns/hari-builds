Process

Design, supply, installation

01

Design

The Baldwin design team have over 40 years of experience in dry bulk material handling systems, using both positive and negative pneumatic conveying solutions.

02

Supply

Baldwin supply the highest standard of aluminium silos. Silos are built to Baldwin's own special designs which are compliant with the latest UKCA and food safe standards. All silo equipment is designed and optimised for low duty operation, which extends the operational life and reliability of all equipment.

03

Installation

Our CompEx trained engineers have many years of experience working to the high standards required for the food process industry. Our specialist team will fully manage the installation and delivery of the bulk silo handling equipment.

04

Commission

Baldwin's in-house electrical and mechanical engineers will fully commission the equipment at the customer's site. The customer is supported throughout dry and wet trials along with handover, operator training and supporting documentation.

05

Servicing and calibration

Our engineers are trained and competent in bulk dry handling systems and offer servicing and annual servicing contracts, including a full calibration service for BRC compliance and certification.

06

DSEAR assessments

Baldwin offer a range of specialist reporting services associated with health and safety and explosive atmospheric regulations. Reports can include zone classification and ignition risk assessments.
