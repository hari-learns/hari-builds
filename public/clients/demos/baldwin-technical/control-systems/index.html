Control systems

We can design, manufacture, install and commission new control systems

Baldwin Technical Services Ltd are able to design, build, install, commission and support their customers in all their requirements for a new control system.

Control panel build

We have over 25 years experience in building control panels for the food and beverage industry and have specialist engineers who understand the demands that this industry can bring.

After ascertaining the customer's requirements, an assessment and proposal is carried out to create a bespoke solution and control system that meets all the latest safety standards and regulations (EN ISO 13849-1, BS EN 60204, UL508A).

Aftercare: an optional support and maintenance contract for your system.

New control systems and updates to existing ones

Baldwin Technical Services install new control systems and update existing control systems within the food and beverage, manufacturing and process industries.

Upgrades

Panel upgrades

Baldwin Technical Services Ltd are able to offer control panel upgrades to a wide range of machines that are still working mechanically but may lack the technology and software to run at their safest and most efficient.

After ascertaining the customer's requirements, a bespoke proposal will be produced in accordance with current regulations and latest technologies.

We work on a broad range of control systems from small PLC upgrades to large scale automation projects. A single installation can include mixing, proofing, baking, cooling and conveyor systems. Our team of expert design and software engineers allow us to take on large scale projects where multiple design engineers and software engineers are required. We can also accommodate a large number of small scale projects such as installation of new equipment or re-wiring and upgrading of existing equipment.

Panel shop

A dedicated panel shop in Lancashire

Our dedicated panel shop allows us to build and test everything on-site, whether you need UL approved panels or bespoke safety systems. This means faster turnaround times, consistent quality control, and direct communication with the engineers building your panel.

Our control panels are built in compliance with BS EN 61439, UK health and safety regulations, and UL certification requirements.

Customers are welcome to visit us to discuss their projects and to inspect their equipment on build or for pre-installation training.
