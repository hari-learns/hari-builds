LV Distribution and External Sockets

New LV distribution and 150 external 32A TP&N switched socket outlets with integral RCD protection around a distribution centre yard in the South West of England.

Scope

Baldwin Technical Services Ltd was asked to install new LV distribution and 150 external 32A TP&N switched socket outlets with integral RCD protection around the yard of a large distribution centre in the South West of England.

Installation

Each supply originated from strategically placed distribution boards around the yard, contained within stainless steel IP65 lockable feeder pillars. New sub mains cables and final sub circuits were installed on new HD ladder rack mounted around the perimeter of the site.

Design and documentation

Baldwin Technical Services Ltd was responsible for the detailed design with cable calculations for new electrical supplies and sub mains cables. On completion, full NICEIC inspection and testing documentation and design documents were issued.
