PUWER

Panel Health Checks

Whether it is one of our own systems or a third party installation, our comprehensive and thorough services will ensure you meet your PUWER regulation requirements.

Panel health checks are carried out with guidance from current safety standards such as BS EN 60204-1:2018, safety of machinery: electrical equipment of machines.

Audits can be fully customised to your requirements depending on scope or budget. A corrective action list and recommendations are produced with each audit, and corrective actions can be rectified as we audit if required.

  • Panels can be risk assessed and labelled red, amber or green to give an immediate risk status to engineers before opening the panel (implement a safe system of work for panel entry)
  • Panel location can be marked on a site drawing with individual panel identification numbers for easy traceability
  • Embedded pictures in the audits allow periodic monitoring of the panel condition
  • Corrective actions can be rectified as we audit: fitting shrouds or covers over exposed terminals, fitting warning labels, replacing trunking lids or blanking holes

Audit benefits

  • Identify risks to plant, people and production
  • Repeatable and reproducible
  • Prioritise improvements based on risk
  • Justify improvements and spend

Surveys

Surveys

Obsolescence survey

An obsolescence survey can be carried out independently or alongside a panel health check to highlight panel components that may no longer be available, supported or easily sourced. We can produce a comprehensive list of high risk devices such as PLCs, inverters, servos and HMIs to check against our obsolescence database. If an item is no longer supported or available, we can offer an alternative solution. During this survey we can also back up any software or parameters.

Thermographic survey

A non-intrusive check of an electrical control panel or distribution board that can determine such problems as loose terminals, circuit overload or damage which could lead to component failure or even fire. A report is generated with findings that identifies problems, potential causes and a remedial action plan.

Scope
