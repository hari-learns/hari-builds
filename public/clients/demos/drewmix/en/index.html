From beech to chair

With attention to detail

01

Wood raw material

The supplier of wood raw material for our furniture is the State Forests, which are PEFC-certified and care about sustainable forest management.

02

Many processes, handwork

For over 30 years we have made wooden furniture that goes through many woodworking processes and is finished by hand. For a chair and a table to reach the right quality, all production departments must work in harmony.

03

From board to chair

Once the wood has dried, it is sanded, stained, varnished, upholstered and packed. We love wooden furniture for its timelessness, durability and variety of forms and finishes.

04

Fabrics and colours

We make furniture from customer-supplied fabrics, so a home can be fully personalised. All materials can be seen and touched at our premises or at our trade partners.

See the material swatches
05

Water-based stains and solar energy

To stain and varnish chairs and table bases we use only eco-friendly stains and water-based varnishes, 100% free of volatile organic compounds. We use the energy from our photovoltaic farm entirely for our own needs.
