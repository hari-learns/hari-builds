Warranty

The DREWMIX furniture factory gives a 24-month warranty on the goods you buy, counted from the date of sale.

The essentials

  • 24 months warranty from the date of sale
  • Free repair during the warranty period
  • Report a complaint within 7 days of discovering the defect
  • Inspection no later than the 14th day after the report
  • Conditions of use: 15–30 °C, relative humidity 40–70%, at least 1 m from radiators

Furniture Care Instructions

Before using the product, the user is required to read the entire manual available at. This manual is provided to inform you about the proper use of the product. Please remember that following the guidelines in this manual will extend the product’s lifespan and appearance, and ensure its safe use. WOOD: Solid wood furniture should not be placed in rooms with excessively high air humidity, nor near radiators or other direct sources of heat. Care should also be taken not to expose varnished surfaces to mechanical damage, as the resulting scratches and dents leave the wood unprotected against external factors (moisture, sunlight). Wood should be dusted regularly with a clean, soft cloth. The cloth may be dry or lightly dampened with water.

Rules for Using Furniture

1. Furniture should be used in accordance with its design and intended purpose.

2. Furniture should be used in dry, enclosed rooms that are protected from harmful weather conditions and direct sunlight. Normal climatic conditions for furniture use:

– relative humidity 40–70% – temperature 15–30 °C

3. Do not place furniture closer than 1 m from active radiators.

4. Protect varnished furniture surfaces from scratches and dents.

5. Avoid prolonged exposure of lacquered surfaces to high temperatures and solvents. These factors may also damage the lacquer finish, resulting in discoloration and dullness.

6. Wooden components must not be cleaned with aggressive cleaning agents.

7. Tables and chairs should be used on level surfaces.

Warranty Terms

1. “DREWMIX Furniture Factory” provides a 24-month warranty on the merchandise you have purchased, effective from the date of sale at.

2. The seller warrants that the goods are of good quality and free from defects, and that they fulfill their intended functions, provided they are used as intended under conditions not deviating from typical use, with normal wear and tear, and with proper care and maintenance (the conditions for proper use are specified in the user manual)

3. The warranty period begins on the date the product is delivered to the Customer.

4. The warranty covers free repair of the product during the warranty period. The method of remedying the defect is determined by the seller.

5. If the buyer prevents the inspection or repair of the furniture on two occasions—in particular, if refuses to release the furniture for factory repair—the buyer is deemed to have waived their warranty rights.

6. The warranty period is extended by the duration of the repair.

Complaint Procedure – Complaint Report

1. A warranty repair will be performed only if the Customer submits a justified complaint that complies with the following requirements as specified at. The complaint must be submitted via the complaint acceptance form along with the original proof of purchase (contract) and warranty card at the showroom where the order was placed, no later than 7 days from the date the defect was discovered. Pickup or replacement of the furniture subject to the complaint takes place at the showroom where the customer placed the order.

2. After a complaint is filed, the person handling complaints will immediately schedule a visit with the Buyer to inspect the furniture subject to the complaint. The date for inspecting the furniture will be set no later than the 14th day following the filing of the complaint. In the case of minor defects that can be remedied immediately, the person handling the complaint will perform the necessary repairs on site after the inspection.

3. Within 14 days of the inspection, the Warranty Provider will:

– issue a letter rejecting the complaint – accept the complaint submitted at, setting a date by which the furniture will be picked up and the repair completed.

4. If it is determined that there are no grounds for the complaint regarding the furniture, all costs, including transportation and repair costs, shall be borne by the Buyer.

Warranty Exclusions

1. Warranty coverage is void in the following cases:

– using the furniture in temperatures below 15 °C or above 30 °C,

– using the furniture in air humidity levels below 30% or above 80%,

– spilling water or other liquids on the furniture,

– using the furniture for purposes other than those for which it is intended,

– soiling of the products – improper cleaning and maintenance procedures – attempts at repair or alterations by unauthorized persons – damage and defects caused by animals – damage resulting from improper transportation of the furniture by Customers, e.g., during a move Improper transport is defined as a failure to exercise due care in securing the transported items,

– damage and defects visible at the time of purchase, as a result of which the product’s price was reduced.

– mechanical damage,

2. The following shall not be considered defects:

– differences in the firmness of the seats of individual components of an upholstered product resulting from their varying sizes and determined by the structural solutions used – acceptable differences in the color and shade of the wood. As a natural material, wood is varied and unique, and minor differences are not a defect in the product,

– changes that occur naturally during normal use of the furniture – differences and indentations in the seat and backrest components resulting from improper use. All seats in a set of chairs should be used evenly, as frequent use of a single seat will cause it to wrinkle and become deformed,

– properties resulting from the type of material used – acceptable variations in the shade of wooden elements and natural veneer. As a natural material, wood has varying shades depending on its natural growing conditions:

– visible growth rings in the wood as part of its anatomical structure,

– mismatches in furniture patterns and fabric shades when adding to a furniture set
