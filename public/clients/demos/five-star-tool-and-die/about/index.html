About us

A permanent fixture in Wallaceburg since 1965.

Five Star Tool and Die Ltd. has been a permanent fixture in Wallaceburg, Ontario since 1965.

For the last 60 years we have been providing the industry machining services, metal stamping and have specialized in the manufacturing of Magnesium & Aluminum Cast Dies, Trim Dies, Fixtures and components.

From concept through finished product, Five Star Tool & Die Ltd. has the equipment and capabilities to produce and deliver quality products on-time at a competitive price.

We are fully capable of producing large diecast tooling for up to 4500 ton DCM.

We service our tools from start to finish and are renowned for turning around repairs within days.
