Our location

Five Star Tool and Die Limited is located in Wallaceburg, Ontario. We have close access to the Canada/US Border at both the Bluewater Bridge in Sarnia, Ontario / Port Huron, Michigan and also the Ambassador Bridge in Windsor, Ontario / Detroit Michigan.

Wallaceburg-based, ready to take care of all of your die casting needs. From 3D part Development, R&D, Cast Dies, Replacement Inserts, Trim Tools, General Die Maintenance and Rapid Repairs our experienced staff is ready to tackle your die casting project.
