Engineering & Design

Our on-site engineering department is capable of completing 3D cast and trim tool designs. Through experience our engineering team have the ability to analyze part data in correspondence with GD&T part prints.

Five Star Tool & Die Limited operates with an experienced, on site engineering department, capable of complete 3D cast and trim tool designs. Our staff is knowledgeable in multiple areas including: solid modeling, surfacing, drafting, part analysis, thermal design, and research & development. Through many years of experience, our engineering staff has the ability to analyze part data in correspondence with GD&T part prints.

Our engineers are able to create a design from square one and follow through with a practical layout that is taken to the manufacturing stage. In the engineering department, die cast designs are continually worked on during the tool's life. Our team is committed to making any required engineering changes work within the existing design by utilizing problem solving skills and quick thinking.

Our team possesses various important skills involving communication, time management and decision making that are used daily to contribute to a complete practical design.

What is covered

  • Solid modeling
  • Surfacing
  • Drafting
  • Part analysis
  • Thermal design
  • Research & development
  • GD&T part prints
