Repair Services

If you have a problem with tooling, let Five Star be the solution! Trust our expertise to keep your operations running smoothly and get your tooling back into production in record time.

At Five Star Tool and Die Ltd. we specialize in providing high-quality repair services for magnesium and aluminum die cast and trim tooling in rapid time. With years of experience in the industry, our team understands the unique challenges of working with lightweight metal tooling and is equipped to handle a wide range of repairs, from intricate die modifications to complete refurbishments. Our precision repair processes extend the life of your tools, reduce downtime, and enhance production efficiency, delivery cost-effective solutions for your business.

Trust our expertise to keep your operations running smoothly and get your tooling back into production in record time.

What is covered

  • Rapid repairs
  • Die modifications
  • Complete refurbishments
  • Magnesium & aluminum tooling
  • Cast and trim tools
