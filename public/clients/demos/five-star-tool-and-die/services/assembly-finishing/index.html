Assembly & Finishing

Using milling machines, surface grinders, drill presses and spotting presses, our assembly and finishing team are highly skilled and knowledgeable when it comes to fabricating cast and trim dies.

Our assembly team is very skilled, dedicated, and knowledgeable when it comes to fabricating cast and trim dies. Through many years of experience, our assembly staff can produce a functioning quality product with a high level of detail. Our assembly team is capable of constructing new projects, as well as performing maintenance or repair work to established jobs. Our assembly team makes small components for the jobs, while ensuring the main items such as inserts, and holders blocks are prepared for the assembly build process.

The assembly department includes equipment such as: milling machines, surface grinder, drill presses, and spotting presses. Within the assembly department, a polishing section operates specializing in finishing part cavity by achieving a smooth clean surface. A complete stockroom is available at anytime to our shopfloor team. The stockroom utilizes an internal system to ensure quantity is replenished as items such as: Bolts, fittings, and electrical supplies are used.

Five Star Tool & Die Limited is prepared for all cast and trim die builds, as well as repair.

What is covered

  • Milling machines
  • Surface grinder
  • Drill presses
  • Spotting presses
  • Cavity polishing section
  • Full stockroom
