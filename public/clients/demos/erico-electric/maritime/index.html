Maritime

Marine electrician for commercial and military ships

Our electricians keep control systems and machinery on military ships in working order.

Scope your job(906) 483-4906

Erico works with the maritime industry to service maritime electrical controls. We specialize in diagnosing and repairing electrical controls for HVAC systems, cranes, and winches on Navy ships across the world.

We use the term electrical maintenance to refer to routine and emergency repairs that are needed to keep your electrical system operating at its best. When it comes to military ships, every electrical component must be in good operating condition at all times.

In the event of an electrical issue that can't be resolved by the Electrician's Mate, Erico can provide support to the ship's crew for efficient diagnosis and repair.

We also provide routine maintenance to keep marine vessels operating at peak performance. Contact us today to schedule one of our electricians that specialize in maritime electric services.
