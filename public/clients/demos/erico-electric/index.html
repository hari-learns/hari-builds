About Erico Electric

Opened by Rory Erickson in 2003, now over twenty electricians

Erico Electric is based in the beautiful Copper Country and has been providing the western Upper Peninsula with expert electrical services since it was formed by Rory Erickson. Since then, we've grown to employ over twenty dedicated electricians as the company has expanded its commercial and automation panel services.

All of the apprentices at Erico are registered in the state-approved Penn-Foster apprenticeship program.
