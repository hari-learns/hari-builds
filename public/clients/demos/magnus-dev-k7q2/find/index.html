What the finder uses
Cartoning: the carton length, width and height ranges and the maximum speed Magnus publishes for the MPC-120, MPC-200, MPC-240, MVC-120 and MVC-R40. The MPC-120 also has a basic carton table (70-250 x 25-85 x 14-60 mm); sizes outside it but inside the wider range show as extended range.
Tube filling: the tubes per minute and tube material Magnus publishes for the MPTR 40/25 and the MPTF 60, 100, 120, 200 and 400.
Nothing is estimated or invented: where Magnus has not published a figure, the finder says so.
Filling bottles instead?
The MFC-100 fills and caps bottles. Magnus has not published an output or size range for it, so ask for a quote.
