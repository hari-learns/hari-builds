Compact rotary cartoner

MVC-R40 compact rotary cartoning machine

A compact cartoner with up to 12 operating stations and manual or automatic loading, for up to 40 cartons per minute.

40cartons per minute, up to
Request a quoteCheck my carton
Photo: Magnus compact cartoner

Cartons are erected into buckets and held in place by suction cups. The closing is hot melt or fold and tuck, airplane or reverse.

Stations are available for leaflet insertion, a printer, a barcode reader, open flap detection, missing product detection and automatic reject.
