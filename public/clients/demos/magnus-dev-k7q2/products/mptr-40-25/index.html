MPTR 40/25 rotary tube filling machine

A user-friendly machine for filling, sealing, folding and crimping laminated, plastic and metal tubes.

25-40tubes per minute
Request a quoteCheck my tube output
Photo: Magnus rotary tube filler

Toothpastes, shaving creams, cold creams, ointments, colours, paints, adhesives, food pastes and syrups are some of the typical products for which this machine has proved very successful.

It fills ointments, pastes, creams and liquids of different viscosities for the food, cosmetics and pharmaceutical industries.
