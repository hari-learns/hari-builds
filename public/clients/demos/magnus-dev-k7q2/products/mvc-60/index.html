MVC-60 vertical cartoning machine, 50-70 cartons per minute

The MVC-60 is the smaller vertical cartoning machine in the Magnus range, with an output of up to 50-70 cartons per minute depending on product and carton size.

50-70cartons per minute
Request a quoteCheck my carton
Photo: Magnus vertical cartoner (the MVC-60 and MVC-120 share one photo on the Magnus site)

It shares the features of the MVC-120: continuous motion variable speed, glue sealing (option) or tuck style closure, left hand load and straight front discharge.

Specification

Output
Up to 50-70 cartons per minute, depending on product and carton size
Motion
Continuous motion, variable speed
Closure
Glue sealing (option) or tuck style
Loading
Left hand load
Discharge
Straight front

Carton size range, weights and power are not published for the MVC-60. Ask Magnus.

Features

  • Continuous motion variable speed
  • Up to 50-70 cartons per minute (depends on product and carton size)
  • Accommodates glue sealing (option) or tuck style closure
  • Left hand load
  • Straight front discharge
