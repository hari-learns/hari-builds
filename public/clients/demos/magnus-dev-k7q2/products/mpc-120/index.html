MPC-120 horizontal cartoning machine, 100-120 cartons per minute

Magnus Packaging introduces an automatic horizontal continuous motion cartoning machine, the MPC 100-120, with an output of 100-120 cartons per minute.

100-120cartons per minute
Request a quoteCheck my carton
Photo: Magnus horizontal cartoner

The MPC 100-120 is a new model, based on integrated design from domestic and foreign technology. This high-tech product incorporates unique mechanical, electrical, electronic and pneumatic components to meet the Indian requirements.

It delivers high quality performance and the best efficiency in its class. Its functioning assures impeccable steadiness and reliability at rated speeds.

The MPC 100-120 is an optimum choice for packing blister packs, alu strips, ampoule and vial trays, eye-drop plastic vials, tubes, soaps and pouches into cartons. The standard machine can handle reverse type tuck-in cartons. With additional changes and a gluing device it can handle glued cartons. The machine can also be linked with upstream and downstream machines for line automation.

Outstanding features

  • Continuous motion variable speed
  • Up to 100-120 cartons per minute
  • Accommodates glue sealing (option) or tuck style closure
  • Straight front discharge
  • No product, no carton
  • Separate vacuum pumps for carton pick-up
  • Internationally accepted brands of electronic and electrical components
  • Simple assembly, flexible size and speed variation
  • All stations are laid out one after the other, which makes setting easy and running trouble free

Optional accessories

  • Leaflet inserter
  • Tube, bottle, pouch or multi-product transfer device
  • Batch coding device or integration to ink jet printer
  • Display for trouble shooting system (PLC touch screen, optional)
  • Automatic counting of speed and finished packs
