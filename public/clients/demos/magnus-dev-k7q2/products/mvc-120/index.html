MVC-120 vertical cartoning machine, up to 140 cartons per minute

Magnus Packaging introduces the vertical cartoning machine MVC-120 (called MVC 140 in the brochure) with an output of up to 140 cartons per minute.

How it works

  1. End load carton is removed from a supply magazine via a reciprocating vacuum system
  2. Carton is erected and placed down on a continuously moving chain
  3. Product loading into the carton can be integrated with your pick and place
  4. Product can be comfortably loaded in the open carton, as sufficient space is provided for 2 people on either side to load the products
  5. Loaded carton is transported through the machine and is either tucked closed or pressed between end pressing belts
  6. Completed cartons are then automatically delivered from the front of the machine at a convenient working height on an exit conveyor

Features

  • Continuous motion variable speed (intermittent motion also listed in the brochure)
  • Speed adjustable from 60 up to 140 cartons per minute, depending on product and carton size
  • Accommodates glue sealing (option) or tuck style closure
  • Left hand load
  • Straight front discharge

Standard features

  • Separate vacuum pumps for carton pick-up
  • All guards in SS sheet
  • All openable doors made of polycarbonate
  • Safety as per the industrial guidelines

Optional accessories

  • Batch coding device
  • Integration for ink jet printer
  • Auto product feeding
  • Integration with various other machines in the same line
