MFC-100 bottle filling and capping machine

Magnus Packaging introduces its latest filling and capping machine, the MFC-100, perfectly made for all your filling and capping requirements.

Request a quote
Magnus has not published a photo of the MFC-100: this is its general layout drawing

The filling system is integrated by Nordson and the capping system is driven by Oriental Motors from Japan and a Mayr torque controller from Germany.

Bottle filling, capping, labeling and packaging machines for craft beer, wine, water, oil, alcohol, sauces or chemicals.
