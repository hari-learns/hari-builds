Tooling

The saving is in the tool.

We have developed a unique unit tooling design which results in significantly reduced tooling costs, plus a reduction in time to mouldings.

  1. Made on site

    We specialise in low cost tooling made on site in our fully equipped tool room.

  2. Unit tooling design

    We have a unique unit tooling design which allows us to complete tools quickly and keep the cost to customers down.

  3. Samples without the wait

    By making our own tools we can avoid time delays and often get T1 samples the day the tool is complete.

  4. Guaranteed, no hidden costs

    We will guarantee your tool for the life of the project or to a set amount of shots, avoiding any hidden costs to you. We can work in fully hardened tool steel, pre-toughened steel or aluminium to get the right tool for you.
