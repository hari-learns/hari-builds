Injection moulding

Overmoulding

Extensive experience in overmoulding, on vertical injection machines.

We are ideally suited to medium or small batch work with a focus on time sensitive projects.

Overmoulding adds a moulded layer over a part you already have, such as the strain relief on a cable.

Post mould assembly is available too, and we can supply jigs and fixtures for assembly and finishing.
