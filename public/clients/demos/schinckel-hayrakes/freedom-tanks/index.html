Freedom fibreglass cartage tanks

Schinckel Hayrakes is the agent for South Australia and western Victoria. Fibreglass is the ideal cartage tank material.

Fibreglass is a composite of polyester resin reinforced with glass fibre. It does not corrode and, unlike ordinary plastics, has high tensile strength, so it stands up to the stretching, tearing and shock forces of liquid moving in a tank. It is lighter and stronger than low tensile materials that need extra mass to make up for it.

Freedom Tanks have developed and refined high performance cartage tank designs over many years, used across government, industry and agriculture. New super strength tanks are proven to handle densities 50 per cent greater than water, suited to the bulk liquid fertiliser systems now popular across Australia.

Cartage tanks for water and chemicals
