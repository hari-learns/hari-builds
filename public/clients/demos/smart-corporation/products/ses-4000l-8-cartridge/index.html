Which sealer fits my container?

This product is a product which roll-type silver foil (sealing paper) executes sealing after punching when container is supplied without cap instead of traditional way of sealing which execute sealing by putting silver foil into cap, and sealing status can be checked 100% and the cost to put silver foil into cap can be saved, therefore, it is a new future packaging technology device.

In addition, sealing can be done up to 15-100 per minute depending on the number of head. This is a product applied to mass production product and it can be used for the product for long-term storage purpose such as food packaging, cosmetic, grain, pesticide, agricultural and fishery product, medicine, bio product, etc. and it also can be used for the sealing and prevention of water leakage from leaking for the variety of products such as Plastic container, glass container + iron lid, capless container, paper cup, etc.
