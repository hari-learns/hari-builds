Used forCosmetics

English text on this page is a draft translation of the company's Korean page, to be confirmed with the company.

Designed for liquid and semi-liquid cosmetics such as skin-care toner and lotion. When the consumer opens the container cap, the foil sealed onto the cosmetic inner cap can be peeled off by hand, which solves the sealing problems of the old plastic inner stopper.

Process: inner caps fed and aligned by bowl feeder, sealing foil punched from a roll and induction-sealed, vacuum inspection of every seal, discharge.

Features

  • Up to 20 units per minute, stable in mass production
  • Different products can be set up according to size
  • Complete seal by induction, longer shelf life
  • Compact build for small spaces
  • Hygienic structure for cleanroom use
  • Capless automatic sealing with touch-screen monitoring and production records
  • Seal deformation and bonding checkable by eye
  • Gas flushing, capping and labelling can be added
