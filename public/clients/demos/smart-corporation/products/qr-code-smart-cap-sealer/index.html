Which sealer fits my container?

Used forFood and beverage

This is Korea’s first sealing and QR code marking system designed specifically for label-free containers.

Unlike existing “label-free” bottled water that still relies on plastic film wrapping around the cap for product information, new regulations from the Ministry of Environment will require full elimination of such film packaging by 2026 and mandate the use of QR codes for product labeling.

Developed by our company, the QR CODE SMART CAP SEALER – SES-4000L-12 is an eco-friendly packaging machine that performs high-frequency sealing and precise QR code marking directly on smart caps—the popular straw-insertable caps often seen in celebrity-endorsed water bottles.

This future-oriented solution enables a full one-stop automation process—from cap feeding and sealing to QR code marking—allowing high-speed, large-scale production with a single unit.

Production Flow :

Cap Feeding - Cap Alignment & Inspection - Sealing Film Punching & High-Frequency Sealing - QR Code Marking - Product Discharge Real-market application of our QR Code Smart Cap Sealer – eco-friendly packaging for label-free beverages (Bogoitsu brand).

Production Flow Overview – QR CODE SMART CAP SEALER

Features

  • Production Process: Cap feeding → Cap alignment & inspection → Sealing film punching → High-frequency sealing → QR code marking → Product discharge
  • Proprietary Technology: Applied our patented “Sealing foil cutting assembly and high-frequency cap-less sealing system”
  • Selectable Operation Modes: Independent operation of selected functions (e.g., high-frequency sealing and/or QR code marking) available
  • QR Code Marking on Pre-Sealed Caps: Compatible with already-sealed SMART CAPS → Process: Cap feeding & alignment → QR code marking → Discharge
  • 100% Seal Inspection: Optional features include moisture, vision, and vacuum-based inspection system
  • User Convenience: Simple operation with intuitive interface and easy sealing data management
  • Production Capacity: 100–120 units per minute

Capless sealing features

  • A new high-quality sealing method that performs inspection before capping
  • Achieves up to 93% electricity savings compared to electric heater bonding
  • When using an electric heater: 15kW load reduced to 3kW single-phase
  • Precise centering and contamination prevention via automatic punching of sealing film
  • Capable of producing multiple container types with a single machine
  • Parts can be replaced within 10 minutes
  • Compatible with special caps and square containers
  • Additional Options
  • Embossed logo placement possible at center of aluminum sealing film
  • Optional installation of sealing film surface sterilizer
  • Capper and filler integration available
