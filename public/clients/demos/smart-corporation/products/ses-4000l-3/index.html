Used forCosmeticsOverseas distributors

This equipment is a one-stop automated system that handles all the core stages of cosmetic production—container feeding, filling, inner lid insertion, spoon placement, Induction sealing, and capping—within a single line. By integrating these complex processes, it maximizes production efficiency while simultaneously reducing labor costs and ensuring consistent quality.

With a high-speed design capable of producing up to 30 units per minute, the system delivers outstanding performance for both small-to-medium manufacturers and large-scale OEM/ODM operations. Its precise volumetric filling system and advanced high-frequency sealing technology enhance product hygiene and preservation, while the flexible design accommodates a wide range of container and cap specifications.

The inclusion of automatic spoon placement and inner lid insertion ensures both product completeness and consumer convenience.

Its compact structure optimizes space utilization, and the hygienic design—compliant with GMP standards—enables stable operation even in cleanroom environments.

Process Sequence :

Container Feeding & Alignment ▶ Filling ▶ Inner Lid Insertion ▶ Spoon Placement ▶ Induction Sealing ▶ Capping ▶ Discharge

Process and highlights

  • Container Feeding & Alignment : Automatically feed empty containers and align them in a consistent direction.
  • Filling : Accurately fill using a metering pump, while reliably handling even high-viscosity creams.
  • Inner Lid Insertion : Automatically insert the inner lid to protect the contents and enhance sealing strength.
  • Spoon Placement : Accurately place the spoon on the lid to enhance consumer convenience.
  • Induction Sealing : Completely seal the lid and container using a high-frequency method to improve preservation and extend shelf life.
  • Capping : Automatically fasten the cap, with adjustable torque to accommodate various cap types.
  • Discharge : Automatically discharge the finished products after capping to the outside of the line.
