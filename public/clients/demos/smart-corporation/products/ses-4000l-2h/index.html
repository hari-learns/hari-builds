Used forPharmaceutical

Capless Sealing is an innovative next-generation packaging technology that seals containers without the use of a cap.The newly developed SES-4000L-2H, based on the Capless Sealing method, enables a seamless one-stop process—from container supply, washing, liquid filling, and induction sealing, to inner cap application, final capping, and labeling.

In the final product, when the consumer fully tightens the cap, the induction-sealed film ruptures, allowing the internal liquid to flow into the lower chamber and mix with existing contents.

To enable this "push-and-activate" mechanism, both the inner and outer caps are preset to close only 50% during the initial capping process.

Process Flow: :

Container Supply (Alignment) ▶ Washing ▶ Liquid Filling ▶ Sealing Film Punching Container Supply ▶ Washing ▶ Liquid Filling ▶ Induction Sealing ▶ Inner Cap Capping ▶ Final Capping ▶ Labeling This is the container cleaning station. The containers are cleaned from below using air jets and then lifted upward.

From left to right: liquid filling area, punching unit for sealing film, and induction sealing zone.

From the left side, the stations are arranged as follows: liquid filling, sealing film punching, and induction sealing.

This is the labeling station. After final capping, the container proceeds to the labeling process.
