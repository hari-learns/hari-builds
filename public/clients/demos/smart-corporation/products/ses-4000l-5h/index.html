The Capless Sealing method, which enables sealing without a cap, has been applied.The SES-4000L-5H model is custom-built according to customer requirements, allowing for various specifications even within the same model.

This unit is designed to process five containers simultaneously.

It is specifically configured to seal and inspect silicone containers.

Uniquely, sealing is performed inside the container rather than on the exterior, allowing users to pierce the inner sealing film before use.

Process Flow: Container Supply ▶ Sealing Film Punching ▶ Internal High-Frequency Sealing ▶ Sealing Inspection (Air) ▶ Discharge The process is strategically designed and managed to optimize both operational efficiency and product quality
