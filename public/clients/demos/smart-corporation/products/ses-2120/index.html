Standard type

  • [ A: Ø25 ~ Ø80 , B : 80~120 ]

Custom type

  • [ Suitable for 1:1 customization ]
  • Sealed products must be transported using a method that applies pressure with a cover.
  • Keep the cap in place during refrigerated storage, as it may become detached.
  • When sealing high-temperature products, ensure that moisture is removed from the adhesive surface.
  • [ The tunnel-type cap is suitable for tall containers ] ・ For products with cone caps, press caps, or dual caps—where sealing from above with high-frequency waves is difficult—a tunnel-type sealing method is used, which applies high-frequency energy from the side.
