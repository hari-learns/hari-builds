Which sealer fits my container?

Used forFood and beveragePharmaceuticalDiagnostic kitsOverseas distributors

This product uses an induction capless sealing method, and when a container filled with contents is supplied, This product is designed so that you can take a sheet of sealing paper filled at the top and cap it after sealing.

The method of capping after sealing allows for 100% sealing confirmation, and capping is possible after sterilization and drying.

In addition, it is an automatic product that can be used on containers without caps, glass bottles + iron lids, etc.

*External size may change.*
