English text on this page is a draft translation of the company's Korean page, to be confirmed with the company.

With the cap closed, the sealed product passes through the machine, which inspects in real time the foil responding to the high-frequency field. Non-conforming products are sorted out by lamp, buzzer or rejector. Faults covered: under-heated seal, over-heated seal, partial seal, folded foil, missing foil, torn foil.

Process: capping, induction sealing, cap-seal inspection, reject sorting by lamp, buzzer or rejector.

Features

  • One machine inspects many container and cap types (multi-model program)
  • Easy, accurate automatic inspection from one setup
  • Lamp, buzzer and rejector sorting
  • AI deep-learning vision inspection platform
  • Image and inspection-result storage
  • Data analysis and statistical report output for line maintenance
  • Reduces recall and return risk, protects your brand
  • Recommended for pharmaceutical, healthcare, agrochemical and food industries
