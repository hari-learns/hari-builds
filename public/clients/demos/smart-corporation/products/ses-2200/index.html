Which sealer fits my container?

Used forPharmaceutical

This manual high-frequency sealing system is specifically designed to deliver uniform and high-quality sealing for square plastic containers.

By utilizing high-frequency induction heating technology and precise thermal control, it ensures stable and consistent sealing performance even at the corners—a key advantage that sets it apart from conventional thermal plate systems.

Features

  • Consistent and Strong Sealing Performance: Ensures complete sealing along the edges of square containers
  • Material Versatility: Compatible with various materials including PP, PET, PE
  • Hygienic Non-Contact Process: Ideal for food and medical packaging applications
  • Main Applications
  • Product Research & Development (R&D)
  • Prototyping and Quality Validation
  • Pilot Production and Small-Scale Manufacturing

Capless sealing features

  • Seals a Wide Range of Containers : Compatible with special-shaped, compact-sized, oval, and square containers—ideal for flexible packaging needs.
  • Seals Cap-less Containers : Supports sealing of open containers without caps, adapting to various operational environments.
  • Ergonomic Pneumatic Operation : Pneumatic-assisted mechanism reduces wrist strain and enhances operator comfort and safety.
  • Adjustable Adhesion Settings : Users can fine-tune sealing parameters to achieve the desired level of bonding strength.
  • Expandable with Optional Equipment : A manual punching unit can be added before the SES-2200 model, allowing convenient one-person operation.
  • Ideal for Laboratory Use : Optimized for small-scale sample testing, making it perfectly suited for research and development settings.
