Which sealer fits my container?

English text on this page is a draft translation of the company's Korean page, to be confirmed with the company.

An automatic induction capless sealing system. A filled container is fed in, a sealing sheet loaded above is placed on it one sheet at a time and sealed, and the container can then be capped.

Unlike sealing a liner already inside the cap, the seal is made first without the cap, so 100% of seals can be checked by eye and quality control is easy. Sterilising and drying can follow sealing before capping, for a more hygienic process. It suits ordinary plastic containers, cap-less containers, glass jars and products with metal caps, and is preferred on cosmetic toner-pad lines for its sealing reliability.

Features

  • Capless automatic induction sealing system
  • Seal quality visible to the eye on 100% of containers
  • Container feed, sealing-sheet feed, sealing, capping
  • Suited to retort sterilisation (sterilise, then cap)
  • Sterilising and drying can follow sealing
  • Plastic, glass and metal-cap containers
  • Cosmetics, food and pharmaceutical use
  • Optimised for toner-pad production lines
  • Seal inspection before capping by eye, vision or vacuum (selectable)
  • Clean seal even when container heights vary
  • Up to 93% electricity saving against electric-heater bonding (15 kW reduced to single-phase 3 kW)
  • No preheating or heat control; instant on/off
  • Automatic punching of the sealing paper for precise centring
  • One machine for several container types, parts changed within 10 minutes
  • Special caps and square containers; no insert machine needed
