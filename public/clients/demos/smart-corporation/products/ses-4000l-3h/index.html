Which sealer fits my container?

The SES-4000L-3H, which uses a capless sealing method that seals products without a cap, can be customized according to customer requirements.

This product is designed to process three items simultaneously, sealing the star-shaped section commonly used in mayonnaise caps—rather than the container itself—to protect the contents from external contamination.

Process Flow:

> Container Supply ▶ Container Alignment ▶ Sealing Film Punching & Sealing ▶ Vacuum Inspection ▶ Production Completion This process is systematically carried out with a focus on both product efficiency and quality.

Process and highlights

  • Star caps stored in the hopper are supplied via the elevator feeder, followed by precise alignment.
  • After the aligned star caps are fed, the roll-type sealing film is punched and positioned on top of each cap, followed by a pressure-based sealing process.
  • After sealing, a vacuum check is performed to verify proper sealing, and the production process is completed.
