Which sealer fits my container?

Used forFood and beverageOverseas distributors

The product is a sealing device by irradiating high-frequency when plastic container comes in through conveyer belt in caped status. This utilizes the principal of irradiating high frequency to fasten the spiral part of the cap without using other pressure and the degree of precision of the cap is very good and it is suitable for mass production.

Features

  • This is a patent registered product with the help of research and development.
  • Adopted multi-function, multi-coil by PWM control
  • Structure of Integrated power unit and coil unit
  • Safety - eight types
  • Constant output can be made by applying PFC
  • It can be used continuously with minimum power, and does not need consumables
  • It utilizes air cooling system and boasts for its minimum external dimension and weight
  • It utilizes stand made from stainless material, therefore, it is suitable for diary plant, food processing plant which need a lot of water.
  • Characteristic of Digital Type
  • Control everything about the product with touch screen.
  • Set administrator to avoid others using it.
  • Set production quantity goal.
  • Check production quantity, defects
  • (Liner detection, Cap defect detection, Container fall detection, etc.)
  • Save data to USB.
