Which sealer fits my container?

The 2-Head Insert Machine, equipped with the latest automation technology, is a high-precision system designed to sequentially insert Bucking and Sealing Paper through two independent heads.

By performing both processes consecutively within a single machine, it maximizes production efficiency, minimizes operator intervention, and ensures consistent product quality.

Furthermore, the machine offers flexible head utilization depending on production requirements.

Both heads can be configured to insert Bucking for parallel processing of identical operations, or one head can insert Bucking while the other applies Induction Sealing Paper for combined processes. Conversely, both heads can be set to insert Induction Sealing Paper, enabling high-speed and high-precision sealing operations.

Notably, the machine is equipped with an adjustable jig system, allowing size adjustments within the range of 60 to 120.

This feature enables compatibility with various product specifications, enhancing flexibility and expanding the scope of applications across different production lines.

The 2-Head Insert Machine is more than just an automation device—it is a comprehensive production solution that delivers flexibility, precision, and reliability to meet diverse process conditions and product sizes.
