Which sealer fits my container?

The Capless Sealing method—sealing without a cap—has been applied. The SES-4000L-12H (HIV_buffer_tube) is an all-in-one system that handles everything from tube feeding to laser marking within a single machine.

Once containers are supplied, they are aligned and undergo a primary defect screening, followed by high-precision liquid filling.

Roll-type sealing film is then automatically cut and high-frequency sealed to prevent contamination and extend product shelf life.

Laser marking can be performed in user-defined designs.

The system is capable of producing up to 6,000 units per hour, contributing to labor cost and production cost savings.

Process Flow :

Container Supply ▶ Air Cleaning & Alignment ▶ Content Filling ▶ Sealing Film Punching ▶ High-Frequency Sealing ▶ Laser Marking
