How induction sealing works

The company's own explanation, from three pages that are pictures on the current site. English is a draft translation of their Korean pages, to confirm.

Why induction sealing

As sales channels multiply, from large retail stores to open markets, tamper-evident sealing that stops leaks and gives consumers confidence has become a necessity. It also keeps out foreign matter, extends shelf life, and shows by its absence that a container was opened, which protects trust in the product and its image. Induction sealing is used for pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, food, ink and chemicals.

What is electromagnetic induction heating

When a conductor is placed close to a coil carrying current, the magnetic field passes through the metal and drives eddy currents in it. The electrical resistance of the material turns these currents into Joule heat, quickly raising the metal to the temperature needed. The Smart Easy Sealer is built on this principle.

Choosing the seal

The company groups sealing paper in four kinds by product, contents and purpose.

  • Aluminium seal: lowest cost, strong adhesion; the most widely used; normally chosen for ceramic and glass. The thinnest seal, so it can fold at capping and may tear when opened. For PET, PS, PP, HDPE, ceramic, glass, paper cups and others.
  • Safety seal: seals cleanly, peels cleanly and adheres well; thicker than the aluminium seal so it does not fold at capping; seals as cleanly as the white seal; common for food and medicine; costs more than aluminium and cannot be used on ceramic or glass. For PET, PS, PP, HDPE.
  • White seal: seals cleanly, peels cleanly and adheres well; thicker than the aluminium seal so it does not fold at capping; common for food and medicine; costs more than aluminium and cannot be used on ceramic or glass. For PET, PS, PP, HDPE.
  • Two-piece seal (paper + aluminium): for products that must not open easily, with the strongest adhesion; pierce it with a pointed tool to use the product. Paper and foil are one piece before sealing and separate once the cap is opened after sealing. Often used for agrochemicals; the highest cost, and the sealed mouth does not come away cleanly. For PET, PS, PP, HDPE.

How the liner is built

A liner protects the contents from outside (tamper evidence), prevents leaks and keeps oxygen and moisture out, which extends storage life. It is a composite of a top layer, aluminium and a seal layer, punched to size and set inside the cap. The one-piece liner stays in one piece after sealing; the two-piece liner separates into two after sealing.

How to measure your container

Open the cap and take out the white pad. Fold it in half and measure the fold line: 8.9 cm means 89 mm. With ceramic, glass, metal-cap containers or caps without a pad, measure the widest part of the container mouth. Send a sample container so the company can confirm the fit.
