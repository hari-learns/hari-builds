About Smart Corporation

Smart Corporation is a partner for people and the future, providing comprehensive solutions in environmentally friendly induction sealing (Induction Technology Solutions).

Choi Ki-seok
CEO

Our heartfelt thanks to every customer who supports us with care and interest. This is Choi Ki-seok, CEO of Smart Corporation.

To respond to a fast-changing market we developed SMART EASY WELDER, an environmentally friendly induction bonding machine that anyone can use to join and separate parts, now recognised as a new technology.

SMART EASY SEALER, our aluminium-foil sealer that seals containers by induction heating, has won many patents in induction sealing, the first CE certification in the industry, a Commissioner of the Patent Office prize and the government prize for new future-packaging technology.
