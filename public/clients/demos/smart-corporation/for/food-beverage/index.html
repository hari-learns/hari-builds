Who it's for · Food and beverage

Seal food and beverage containers at the speed your line runs

Hand-fed benches for many container types, a stainless conveyor head for wet plants, and automatic lines up to 200 containers a minute.

Find your machineRequest a quotation

What matters to food and beverage makers

  • 1

    Many containers, one machine

    The SES-2120 seals Ø15 to 120 mm in PET, HDPE, PS, PP, acrylic, glass and ceramic. The company notes that food makers with many container types use it most.

  • 2

    Built for wet plants

    The SES-3400U conveyor head stands on a stainless-steel stand, which the company says suits dairy and food-processing plants that use a lot of water.

  • 3

    From a bench to 200 a minute

    Automatic lines seal 15 to 200 containers a minute depending on the number of heads, for food packaging, grains, seafood and more, in plastic, glass with metal cap, capless or paper-cup containers.
