Our next Exhibitions for 2025

Our next mid-2025 trade shows will be in Europe, Central America, and Asia.

As every year, from 7 to 9 May we will be at MACFRUT in Rimini, the important meeting for Italian and foreign manufacturers of the fruit and vegetable market. This year our stand will host the Automatic Banana Peeler CERERE 6000, patented by Tropical Food Machinery, already active in Asian and South American plants.

From 10 to 12 June, we will participate in EXPO PACK in Guadalajara, Mexico, where we will show our multi-fruit systems dedicated to tropical fruits and new technologies for aseptic or partially-frozen filling.

During the same days, from 11 to 14 June, you will find us in Bangkok, Thailand, to participate in the 2025 edition of PROPAK ASIA, an event where our focus will be our processing plants for bananas, pineapples, and mangoes.
