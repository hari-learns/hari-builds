Multifruit extraction line

TFM is among the major international players in the design and construction of multifruit processing lines with different production capacities for tropical or temperate climate fruits , capable of producing wholemeal pulp, natural juices or concentrates while preserving the quality and aroma of the raw material used.

Line capacityfrom 2.000 Kg/h up to 15.000 Kg/h of fresh fruit.
Ask for information+39 0524 91311

The Multifruit Line combines TFM extraction machines in a single plant to process a vast range of products: thick-skinned fruits (Pineapple or Citrus), stone fruits (Mango, Peaches, Apricots) or fruits with seeds (Passion fruit, apples, pears, etc.).

The juices produced are top quality and can subsequently be transformed into concentrate thanks to the Evaporator ZEUS , the final part of the extraction line includes a Product Sterilization and Filling Plant in an aseptic environment.

It is possible to develop a multifruit Line by adapting it to specific production capacities: thanks to its great versatility , the line can be used in every season of the year, reducing production stops which generate high costs for the processing industries.

The products obtained with the TFM Lines meet all the requirements of the international production standards and they can be exported to the main world markets.

All our machines are available in different dimensions depending on the required work capacity.

Production process

  1. 01

    Fruit pre-treatment

    • Washing Machine
    • Brushing Machine
    • Sorting Belt
    • Blanching Tunnel
  2. 02

    Pulp Extraction

    • Automatic Peeler ATENA
    • Extractor with Beaters ARTEMIDE with Shredder
    • Preheater EBE
    • Press Refiner AFRODITE
    • Turbo Refiner HERA
    • Decanter (optional)
  3. 03

    Concentration (for concentrated juice)

    • Evaporator by forced circulation ZEUS
  4. 04

    Sterilization

    • Sterilization plant TCMA with Tank for collection/deaeration, Homogenizer and Sterilizer
  5. 05

    Aseptic Filling

    • TFM Aseptic Fillers for 220 l drums, 1000 l bins or goodpaks
