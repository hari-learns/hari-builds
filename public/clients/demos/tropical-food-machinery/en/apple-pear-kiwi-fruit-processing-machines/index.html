Apple, pear and kiwi extraction line

The temperate climate multifruit extraction line for fruits with seeds such as apples, pears, and kiwis, can produces pulp, refined, clarified, or concentrated juices with a high yield and preserving the qualities and aroma of the raw material thanks to the cold extraction in an inert atmosphere.

Line capacityfrom 2.000 Kg/h up to 15.000 Kg/h of fresh fruit.
Ask for information+39 0524 91311

The fruits are pre-treated by washing, brushing, sorting, and blanching; subsequently the combined action of the Extractor with Beaters ARTEMIDE , the Preheater EBE and the Turbo Refiner HERA guarantee the complete separation of skins and residual seeds, producing a top-quality juice with very high yield and without incorporating oxygen.

The juice can subsequently be transformed into concentrate thanks to the Evaporator ZEUS , the final part of the extraction line includes a Product Sterilization and Filling Plant in an aseptic environment.

The products obtained with the TFM Lines meet all the requirements of the international production standards and they can be exported to the main world markets.

All our machines are available in different dimensions depending on the required work capacity.

Production process

  1. 01

    Fruit pre-treatment

    • Washing Machine
    • Brushing Machine
    • Sorting Belt
    • Blanching Tunnel
  2. 02

    Pulp Extraction

    • Extractor with Beaters ARTEMIDE with Shredder
    • Preheater EBE
    • Turbo Refiner HERA
    • Decanter (optional)
  3. 03

    Concentration (for concentrated juice)

    • Evaporator by forced circulation ZEUS
  4. 04

    Sterilization

    • Sterilization plant TCMA with Tank for collection/deaeration, Homogenizer and Sterilizer
  5. 05

    Aseptic Filling

    • TFM Aseptic Fillers for 220 l drums, 1000 l bins or goodpaks
