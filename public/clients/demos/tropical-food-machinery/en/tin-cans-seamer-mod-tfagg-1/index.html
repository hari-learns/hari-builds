Tin can seamer · TFAGG-1

Semi-automatic can seamer

Semi-automatic pneumatic seamer used for packaging cans of various sizes (52 – 73 – 83 – 99 – 153 mm). The can centering plate is suitable for all processed formats, the rollers and centering spindle are supplied depending on the can diameter.

Ask for information+39 0524 91311

The machine is composed by: – can centering plate with pneumatic lifting and lowering system; – pneumatically controlled rotating spindle; – manually controlled seaming rollers (first and second operation); – protective casing in transparent polycarbonate; – pneumatic control panel.

Length1000 mm
