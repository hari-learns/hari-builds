Room Studio

See your room in our designs

Upload a photo of your own room, pick a style and tell us what you want. In about 30 seconds you see your room redesigned, and you can send it straight to Studio A3 with your wishes.

No photo at hand? Try one of the before photos from our own projects (Mölndal and Marbella).

How it works

  1. 01

    Upload

    A photo of your room, or a floor plan or sketch. The room type is detected automatically, and you can change it.

  2. 02

    Choose

    Pick a style palette and write what you want: keep the sofa, warmer light, more storage. The chips help.

  3. 03

    See

    Your own room redesigned, with a before and after slider.

  4. 04

    Send

    Pass it on to Studio A3 with your wishes. Your first consultation is on us.
