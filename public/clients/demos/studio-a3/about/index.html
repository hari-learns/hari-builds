Studio A3

About us

Quality, dedication, and creativity

Studio A3 is an experienced interior design firm with a tight-knit team driven by creating unique, personal spaces. Quality, dedication, and creativity guide everything we do.

With over 20 years of experience, we create beautiful, functional, and comfortable interiors tailored to each client’s needs and wishes. We work in a wide range of styles and partner with many different suppliers, which lets us tailor every project with the right feel, materials, and details.

Most of our clients want a complete solution, with help all the way from idea and design to purchasing, delivery, and a finished interior.

Studio A3 + A3 Byggprojekt

Design and build, under one roof

One of our strengths is our long, close partnership with A3 Byggprojekt, a construction company we have worked with since we started in 2011. We share an office and run most of our projects together, which makes for a smooth process where interior design and renovation are connected from the first idea to the last detail. Together, we take full responsibility for each project and deliver a professional result with high quality and a strong eye for detail.

The team

The people behind your project

Linda S. Österbom

Owner, interior designer

Linda S. Österbom is the owner of Studio A3. She has worked in interior design since 2006, when she started her first interior design business in Varberg. In 2008 she moved to Gothenburg, and in 2011 she founded Studio A3. She designs private homes, offices, and restaurants. She finds most of her inspiration while traveling and has a soft spot for 1960s design with an international touch, Italy, and dogs.

linda@studioa3.se

Lina Wolgast

Interior designer

Lina met Linda through work in 2010, after Linda had moved to Gothenburg, and joined her when Studio A3 was founded the following year. She designs private homes, offices, and restaurants. Lina loves historic interiors and dreams of owning a large manor house, ideally in France. Even with her fondness for period furniture, she enjoys eclectic spaces that mix design from different eras.

lina@studioa3.se

Albin Kvarnslätt

3D renderings and orders

Albin has worked at Studio A3 since 2018. He keeps a lot of things running at the office and creates most of the 3D renderings for our projects. He’s also in charge of order management and makes sure everything runs smoothly from order to delivery and installation. When it comes to style, Albin loves midcentury modern.

albin@studioa3.se

Daniel Wolgast

Owner, A3 Byggprojekt

Daniel is the owner of A3 Byggprojekt, founded in 2009. He has a background in business and economics and many years of experience managing construction projects.

daniel@a3byggprojekt.se
