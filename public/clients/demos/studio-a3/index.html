Studio A3 is an experienced interior design firm based in Gothenburg, Sweden. We offer complete interior design solutions for homes, offices, restaurants, and hotels.

We handle the entire process, from floor plans, materials, furniture, and lighting to the finished interior on site.

We offer a full-service approach where experience meets creativity, with close attention to detail. We create the right design for the right space.

  • 2011Studio A3 founded in Gothenburg
  • 300 m²design showroom and material library
  • 150+suppliers of furniture, lighting and textiles
  • 20+years of experience in interior design

From moodboard to finished room

Every project, the whole way

Villa Östra Göteborg, 230 sqm. The same kitchen, four times: as an idea, as a plan, as a 3D rendering and as the finished room.

  1. 01

    Material selection

    We choose materials that suit your home, your style, and the way you live.

  2. 02

    Design concept, layout, and space planning

    We create solutions tailored to your home’s architecture, your style, your budget, and your stage of life.

  3. 03

    3D renderings

    You get a clear picture of the finished result, which guides decisions on materials and design.

  4. 04

    Purchasing and installation

    We handle all furniture and décor purchasing, plus delivery, carrying, unpacking, assembly, installation, and final setup on site. You can come home to a finished home.
