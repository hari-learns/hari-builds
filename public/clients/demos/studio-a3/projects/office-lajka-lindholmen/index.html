Office | Lindholmen | Lajka

Lajka Office

We had the privilege of helping the client with their office interior for the first time in 2023, in smaller premises at the time. When the company took the next step in 2026 and moved to larger premises to support its continued growth, we were once again trusted to create their new office.

The starting point was an overall interior concept inspired by exclusive hotel lounges and a clear sense of quality. The company has grown strongly in recent years and is marked by a strong drive forward, something we wanted the new interior to reflect.

As we reused some of their existing furniture, where the palette was mainly shades of green and burgundy, we complemented it with dark wood, earthy tones, brass details and black accents. The result is a timeless and elegant environment with a clear new identity.

The layout of the premises brought some challenges, which placed high demands on both planning and function. To make the most of the space we created, among other things, a generous made-to-measure lounge seating area using the modular sofa Bob from Blå Station, which naturally uses the long corridor, ties the social areas together and adds to the desired “hotel lounge feel”.

Our brief covered the whole process, from concept development and interior planning to furniture purchasing, order handling, delivery coordination and final installation on site. By being responsible for every part of the project we could make sure the vision was carried out in full and that the result met both the client’s needs and our own high standards of quality.

The finished office now offers an inspiring and representative workplace that supports the company’s continued development and creates a welcoming experience for staff and visitors alike.
