Restaurant | Johanneberg | Kizuna

Restaurant Kizuna

We had previously helped the client with another sushi restaurant, and we were trusted again when it was time to open a new restaurant in Gothenburg. The time frame was short and the budget tight, so we had to think a little extra to create a pleasant atmosphere and a big change with relatively small means.

The concept became salmon pink in different tones, a nod to salmon and tuna sushi, and we used lots of rattan and bamboo to connect the interior to Asia. Walls, benches and ceiling were painted in different pink tones. The bar was rebuilt and clad in a pink tile from Toniton. We designed shelves behind the bar and a bar shelf above the counter. We created a room divider of bamboo poles, and the wall lights, ceiling lights and roller blinds are in rattan. A geisha wallpaper sets the tone of the room, the inner part of the restaurant got a ceiling of climbing plants, and the back wall is clad in antiqued mirror glass with wall lights to create more space. The tables are a mix of painted existing tabletops and new ones in terrazzo.
