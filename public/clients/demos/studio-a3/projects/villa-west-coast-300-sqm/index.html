Villa | West Coast | 300 sqm

Architect-Designed House on the West Coast

In this lovely interior design project we were trusted to follow the clients all the way, from material selection and lighting design to fixed interiors, furniture, textiles, accessories, plants and outdoor spaces.

Together with Andersson & Keller in Varberg, the clients had prepared the building permit documents and then wanted help to create a well-crafted and cohesive whole.

The project included several buildings: a guest house, a main house and a pool house, where the patios tie the houses together and create a natural transition between inside and outside. The architecture is modern, strict and pared back, and to add warmth we worked with materials such as concrete, black and light wood, leather and textiles with lots of texture.

In the guest house we created a light Scandinavian feel with oak, concrete, wool and stone as the main materials. The main house was given a more strict and modern look, with a warm grey colour scale and darker elements that create depth and elegance.

We designed most of the fixed interiors, including the kitchen and bathrooms, in close collaboration with Himlekök. In addition, we chose and supplied furniture, lighting, textiles and details to create a home where every part fits together.

A fantastic project to be part of, where close collaboration between us, the clients, the architect, the building contractor and everyone else involved resulted in a warm, exclusive and carefully crafted home with a strong identity.
