Turn-of-the-Century Villa | Bohuslän | 280 sqm

Villa on the West Coast

In this villa we were responsible for the complete interior, with a focus on a classic and timeless style. The starting point was a light colour scale with soft white and beige tones that enhance the natural light and create a harmonious base. The warm tone of the wooden floors adds warmth and a soft contrast to the light walls.

The furniture was chosen in classic shapes that last over time and give the whole house a sober feel. Textiles in light shades add to the airy and welcoming atmosphere. As part of the overall approach we also designed all the headboards, which gave the bedrooms a personal and cohesive character.

The result is a complete interior that conveys light, elegance and timelessness, where every detail has been carefully worked through to create a unified whole.
