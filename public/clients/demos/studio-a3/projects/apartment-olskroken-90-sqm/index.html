Apartment | Olskroken | 90 sqm

A Colourful Apartment

In this project we were asked to develop a new overall approach for the apartment, focusing on surfaces, colour scheme, wallpaper and a new design for the kitchen and bathroom. All surfaces, including the existing wooden floor, were renovated and refreshed to create a more considered and personal whole.

The clients love colour and wanted a home with warmth, character and anything but white walls. We therefore worked with a palette of muted, powdery pastel tones that flow softly from room to room and tie the home together in a harmonious way.

In the kitchen we chose blue fronts, details from Toniton and a terrazzo worktop that ties the colour scale together. The bathroom was given a warm and personal look with pink/peach tiles, colour-matched details and a pink WC that adds to the playful feel.

The bedroom was given a calm, muted grey scale on walls and ceiling, while the living room is in a powdery pink tone in fine contrast to the Gradient wallpaper from Belarte by the dining area. In the guest room/office, a blue-green linen wallpaper was combined with Belarte’s Artistic Petals, giving the room both depth and personality.

Just like the clients, we love working with colour and are always a little extra inspired when we get to create homes where colours, patterns and materials really get to shine.
