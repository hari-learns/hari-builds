Office | Järntorget | Allié

Allié Law Office

We had previously helped this company update its lunch room with new furniture. When they were moving into completely new premises at Brick Studios, we were involved from the start of the project.

We helped them with all material selections, including floors, wall colours and the design of the kitchen and bathrooms. We also helped with the room layout and the interior planning of every room. We brought along the existing furniture that suited the new premises, handled all purchasing of new furniture, and delivered and installed everything on site.

The open lounge was to connect with the dining area as well as the view. Here we created a generous seating area with many possibilities using the flexibly configurable sofa Bob from Blå Station.

The kitchen’s terrazzo worktop ties together the colour scale, which is mainly different shades of green with light pink and rust-red accents. Together with dark woods and brass, this gives a sober, timeless interior with modern touches.
