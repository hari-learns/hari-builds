Villa | Östra Göteborg | 230 sqm

Complete Villa Renovation

When we took on this project, the goal was to create a home with a clear identity where architecture, materials and interior design work together in every detail. The whole house was completely renovated inside and rebuilt from the ground up with a new layout, new installations and an entirely new interior adapted to the family’s needs.

The common thread through the project is a warm and timeless material palette where dark-stained oak meets soft beige tones, natural stone and microcement. Arched openings, built-in furniture and custom-designed joinery create a home with a calm and exclusive feel where every detail feels considered.

The kitchen has been designed as the natural gathering place of the home, with generous work surfaces, custom-designed cabinetry and carefully chosen materials. In the bathrooms we worked with large-format Dekton combined with microcement to create a clean and cohesive look. All the vanities were custom designed for the project and made to harmonise with the rest of the house’s joinery. Kitchen, bathroom vanities and laundry room were supplied by Himlekök.

Built-in solutions recur throughout the home, from storage and niches to furniture and made-to-measure details, which gives the home function, character and a tailored look.

Studio A3 was responsible for the entire interior design concept and the architectural design, while A3 Byggprojekt handled project management and carried out the complete renovation. The result is a modern and exclusive home where materials, light and craftsmanship come together in a harmonious whole.
