Interior Design for Private Homes

Invest in Your Home

What does it take to create a home that’s comfortable, beautiful, and functional?

It takes knowledge and experience of how a space is actually used day to day, along with a feel for materials, colors, furniture, and lighting. It also takes time, which a lot of people don’t have these days.

We combine well-planned layouts with a sense for form, materials, and detail to create spaces that reflect the people who live in them.

We have many years of experience helping clients find the right style and materials for their home, their daily life, and their personal taste. Many of our clients come back to us, and we see that as the best proof that our work holds up in real life and not just on paper.

What a design proposal includes

Custom Interior Design Services for Your Home

Every home is different, so we tailor our services to your needs. A design proposal includes:

  1. 01

    Design concept, layout, and space planning

    We create solutions tailored to your home’s architecture, your style, your budget, and your stage of life.

  2. 02

    3D renderings

    You get a clear picture of the finished result, which guides decisions on materials and design.

  3. 03

    Material selection

    We choose materials that suit your home, your style, and the way you live.

  4. 04

    Furniture and décor

    We recommend furniture, textiles, lighting, and finishing touches that fit your home.

  5. 05

    Purchasing and installation

    We handle all furniture and décor purchasing, plus delivery, carrying, unpacking, assembly, installation, and final setup on site.

You can come home to a finished home.

Our interior design services

Full-Service Interior Design

Custom quote

A complete interior design process, led by a professional from start to finish.

This service is for you if you’re renovating and want thorough, hands-on help with specific recommendations for your floor plan, materials, and colors. It’s also a good fit if you want to update your existing furniture with new pieces, lighting, and textiles, or furnish your entire home from scratch.

We always start with a no-obligation consultation to go over what you need help with. After that, we send you a quote for our design fee to create a proposal based on your needs.

Included in the service

  • Initial consultation
  • Needs assessment
  • Site visit and measurements
  • Kickoff meeting
  • Design development
  • Floor plans and layout
  • 3D renderings
  • Product images and descriptions
  • Design review meeting
  • Final design package
  • Furniture quote
  • Final presentation
