Sustainability

Environmental sustainability on the factory floor

At Cunneen Signs, environmental sustainability is not a side initiative—it’s a core part of how we operate. We are proudly Australian made, and with that comes a responsibility to protect the environment we work and live in.

From our factory floor to our supplier relationships, we are committed to reducing our impact, managing risk, and improving our environmental performance in measurable ways.

What We’re Actively Doing

Recycling at Scale

We recycle significant volumes of acrylic, aluminium, and cardboard waste from our production line. Offcuts and scrap are sorted and collected in dedicated waste streams to ensure nothing reusable goes to landfill.

Reusing Foundry Materials

In our foundry, we reclaim and reuse leftover bronze from pours, reducing raw material demand and waste. This closed-loop approach ensures we make the most of every cast.

Waste Minimisation

We continuously look for ways to reduce material consumption, from efficient sheet nesting in our CNC processes to limiting overproduction and packaging waste.

Sustainable Procurement

We work with local and responsible suppliers who align with our sustainability values, helping us lower our carbon footprint while supporting Australian industry.

Pollution Prevention

We actively monitor and reduce potential pollutants in our operations, striving to eliminate harmful outputs where possible and responsibly manage any unavoidable waste.

Staff-Led Improvement

Environmental goals are part of our regular team meetings, where staff contribute ideas, feedback, and help refine our practices. Our sustainability culture is driven from within.

Compliance & Continual Improvement

We comply with all applicable environmental regulations and regularly review our systems to raise our standards.

Environmental responsibility isn’t a box to tick; it’s part of building a better business and a better future.
