Casting and foundry

Using both traditional and contemporary production methods, Cunneen Signs specialises in the creation of high-quality identification, interpretative, and commemorative plaques and cast solutions. Working across bronze, brass and aluminium, these plaques can be meticulously cast in each material offering distinct characteristics suited to a range of applications.

Bronze, known for its durability and timeless aesthetic, is particularly ideal for outdoor uses offering excellent corrosion resistance and a rich, warm appearance that deepens with age. Aluminium, on the other hand, is lightweight, versatile, and highly resistant to the elements, making it perfect for modern designs or more economical solutions. Brass, with its golden hue, offers a sophisticated finish and is often selected for more prestigious projects.

A bespoke feature of Cunneen Signs is we are the only sign company in Australia with our own in-house foundry. This means that we have complete control over the entire production process, from initial design to final casting. By managing every step in house we ensure craftmanship to the highest standards while maintaining tight timelines and budget.

Fabrication

Sign fabrication is the construction of custom signage using a wide range of materials. Cunneen Signs has no limitations in fabrication; our team work with timber, steel, aluminium, plastics and a number of new recycled and environmentally friendly materials. When building large bespoke signs our team are experts in understanding structural and engineer approved drawings ensuring your sign has a strong and long lifespan. Our services include all types of welding, folding, bending, rolling and all types of connection details. We also provide finishing services including galvanising, epoxy coating, anodising, and a variety of timber finishes.

Our fabrication capabilities cover a broad spectrum of signage – from pylon signs, wayfinding and directional signs to gateway and landmarks signs – council and park signs and all bespoke one-of-a kind custom pieces

Spray painting

At Cunneen Signs, all spray painting is carried out in-house using our professional spray booth, dedicated preparation areas, and temperature-controlled curing ovens. These facilities allow us to maintain the highest quality standards and consistent finishes across every project.

Spray painting provides a smooth, even coating with superior coverage — ideal for signage and fabricated elements where both appearance and durability are essential. Our controlled environment ensures optimal paint application and curing conditions, delivering long-lasting, flawless results.

We specialise in 2-pack polyurethane coatings, a high-performance system combining a base paint and hardener to create a durable, chemical- and UV-resistant finish. Available in gloss, satin, or matte, this coating enhances colour depth and protects against wear, moisture, and environmental exposure. We also apply a range of specialised finishes, including primers, sealants, lacquers, and anti-graffiti coatings, tailored to the demands of each project.

By managing every stage of the process in-house, we ensure efficiency, quality control, and a superior end product — every time.

Engraving and CNC routing

Cunneen Signs uses some of the most advanced computer controlled machinery, including CNC routers, engraving machines, and laser cutters, to produce precision cut-out letters, architectural designs, engraved door signs, plaques, and corporate logos. Our expertise spans a wide array of materials, including aluminium, brass, stainless steel, timber, and acrylic, allowing us to deliver high-quality results across diverse projects.

From intricate, custom-perforated or cut-through designs to straightforward laser-marked statutory door signs, our engraving and cutting department is equipped to handle both complex and simple requests.

Our state-of-the-art CNC cutting technology enables us to create highly detailed and accurate shapes, while laser cutting offers fine, clean edges with minimal heat distortion. Combined with our engraving services, which offer precision etching and marking, we bring a full spectrum of possibilities to life, whether it’s for interior design, corporate branding, or specialised signage solutions.

Digital print and graphics

Cunneen Signs has a well-established reputation for delivering eye-catching digital print signs, ranging from intricate multi-color prints for illuminated faces to large-format banners, retail displays, statutory signs, and interpretive messaging. Whether we are producing small, custom decals in limited quantities or crafting large-scale visual branding solutions, Cunneen Signs brings expert knowledge and advanced technology to every project.

One of our specialties is direct-to-substrate printing, a precise technique that allows us to print directly onto a variety of materials, such as acrylic, metal, glass, wood, and even textiles along with roll-to-roll vinyls. This eliminates the need for traditional vinyl applications, creating sharper, more vibrant prints that maintain their quality even in demanding environments. The versatility of direct-to-substrate printing enables us to achieve exceptional detail, which is perfect for producing high-quality, durable signage that needs to stand out in both indoor and outdoor settings.

Our wide-format printing capabilities also set us apart in the industry. With the latest in wide-format digital print technology, we can produce large-scale signage with incredible precision and vibrant colours, all in a single print pass. This is ideal for producing oversized banners, murals, and building wraps, as well as large display graphics for exhibitions and trade shows. The benefit of wide-format printing is that it offers a smooth, seamless result that traditional methods simply cannot match.

At Cunneen Signs, our policy of continuous training and reinvestment in state-of-the-art production equipment and software ensures that we stay at the forefront of the digital printing industry. We consistently strive to deliver the highest quality digital print signs at prices that offer exceptional value. Our skilled team is ready to help you bring your signage ideas to life, whether it’s a bold, brand-defining display or a simple yet effective informational sign.

Installation

Signage installation is the process of securely mounting and positioning signs in strategic locations to ensure maximum visibility and effectiveness. This can involve installing various types of signs such as wall-mounted, freestanding, illuminated, or digital displays. Proper installation requires precision, appropriate tools, and knowledge of safety regulations to ensure that the signage is stable, weather-resistant, and aligned with branding standards. Professional installation not only enhances the visual appeal but also ensures the sign remains durable and functional over time….

The importance of signage installation goes beyond aesthetics—it plays a crucial role in communication, safety, and brand identity. Well-placed signage guides customers, provides essential information, and helps businesses stand out in competitive environments. In commercial spaces, it enhances customer experience and reinforces brand recognition, while in public areas, it improves wayfinding and ensures safety compliance. A properly installed sign reflects professionalism, attracts attention, and contributes significantly to an organisation’s overall image and operational efficiency.

Our services, go beyond “just installing a sign”, we can provide local authority approvals, engineering, civil works, environment controls and electrical connections, as a complete turn key solution.
