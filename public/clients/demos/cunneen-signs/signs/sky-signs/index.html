Sky signs for Sydney skylines

Sky signs are among the most impactful forms of corporate branding, delivering maximum visibility and creating a lasting impression across a city skyline. At Cunneen Signs, we have the experience and technical expertise to design, fabricate and install sky signs on some of Australia’s most prominent buildings.

Brief this sign type 02 9637 9400

Our portfolio includes high-profile projects for QBE, Red Hat, DHM, UNSW, Sanofi Aventis, Wyeth, Spherion and many others. Each installation is unique, requiring careful consideration of structural, aesthetic and lighting requirements to achieve a result that performs as well in daylight as it does at night.

We handle the entire process in-house from engineering and prototyping through to fabrication, lighting design and installation, ensuring precision and quality at every stage. Our capabilities range from large-format illuminated lettering to advanced RGBW systems that can be programmed to change colour for events, brand campaigns or seasonal displays.

Working at height demands absolute attention to safety and detail. We have teams trained and experienced in rope access, crane lifts and other specialised installation methods, allowing us to tackle complex sites with confidence.

When you partner with Cunneen Signs, you are choosing a team that understands how to combine branding impact with architectural integration, delivering sky signs that stand out day and night.
