Versatile solution

Pylon and plinth signs, built in-house

At Cunneen Signs, we design pylons and plinths that combine impact, flexibility, and functionality. From marking building and park entrances to showcasing tenants, events, or wayfinding information, these freestanding signs are a highly visible and versatile solution for a wide range of sites.

Brief this sign type 02 9637 9400

Our installations can include interchangeable graphics, digital screens, illuminated elements or multi-sided designs, crafted from materials such as steel, aluminium, concrete, timber, brass and other non-traditional finishes. This combination of versatility and materiality makes them ideal for locations that require regular updates or seasonal promotions. Examples range from corporate office entry signage and educational campus wayfinding to retail centre pylons and large-scale logistics park markers.

All design, fabrication, and installation are managed in-house, ensuring quality, durability, and consistency from concept to completion. The result is freestanding signage that is visually striking, functional, and tailored to your site and audience.
