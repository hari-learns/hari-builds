Ambitious designs

Bespoke and architectural signage

At Cunneen Signs, we specialise in transforming ambitious design concepts into finely crafted, custom-built signage. Our bespoke and architectural projects often extend beyond traditional signage — encompassing public art, interactive installations, and distinctive material combinations that reflect the creativity of the designers and architects we collaborate with.

Brief this sign type 02 9637 9400

From timber and concrete inlays to precision-fabricated metalwork, illuminated features, and intricate sculptural forms, we combine technical expertise with a deep appreciation for design intent. Each project is approached as a true partnership — working closely with architects, designers, and builders to realise bold ideas in a practical, durable, and visually compelling way.

Whether it’s a public artwork, a statement feature, or a finely detailed architectural element, our team has the experience, innovation, and craftsmanship to bring even the most imaginative concepts to life. Whether it’s a single entry sign or a complete wayfinding system, all design, fabrication, and installation are handled in-house, ensuring quality, durability, and consistency from start to finish.
