Australian made

At Cunneen Signs, we proudly manufacture all our signage in-house right here in Australia

From initial concept to final installation, our end-to-end production process is handled under one roof at our Sydney facility giving us complete control over quality, lead times, and customisation.

With decades of experience and a commitment to continual investment in cutting-edge equipment and technology, our team of skilled tradespeople, craftsmen, designers, and project managers deliver premium results across every project. Whether it’s fabricated metal signage, precision engraving, large-format digital prints, or LED illuminated signs, we have the tools and talent to bring it all to life, without outsourcing overseas.

We also operate our own bronze foundry on-site, giving us full control over the casting process for commemorative plaques, custom lettering, and heritage signage. Our workshop is equipped to work with a wide range of materials including brass, timber, and aluminium, and our capabilities extend to traditional techniques like handpainting, gilding, and detailed finishing—skills that are increasingly rare in the industry.

Being Australian made isn’t just a badge of honour for us, it means faster turnarounds, stronger communication, and reliable quality you can count on. By keeping our manufacturing local, we’re also supporting Australian jobs, suppliers, and the future of our industry.

When you work with Cunneen Signs, you’re working with a partner that values craftsmanship, accountability, and delivering signage that truly stands the test of time.
