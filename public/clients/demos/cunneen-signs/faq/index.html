Questions

Questions buyers ask before they brief a signmaker

Every answer below comes from Cunneen's own pages, with the source linked.

Do you make the signs yourselves, or subcontract?

We proudly manufacture all our signage in-house right here in Australia. From initial concept to final installation, our end-to-end production process is handled under one roof at our Sydney facility.

Source: Australian Made

Can you cast bronze plaques, and how large?

Yes. We specialise in traditional sand casting and can cast in aluminium, bronze and brass. Our largest single pour to date was a plaque measuring 525 x 2187 x 8mm thick. We are the only sign company in Australia with an in-house foundry.

Source: Plaques and Coat of Arms

Do cast bronze plaques come with slip resistance certificates?

For public installations, safety matters as much as aesthetics. That is why we can provide slip resistance certificates for our cast bronze plaques.

Source: Plaques and Coat of Arms

Can you install signs at height, such as sky signs on towers?

Yes. Working at height demands absolute attention to safety and detail. We have teams trained and experienced in rope access, crane lifts and other specialised installation methods.

Source: Sky Signs

Can the sky signs be lit and change colour?

Our capabilities range from large-format illuminated lettering to advanced RGBW systems that can be programmed to change colour for events, brand campaigns or seasonal displays.

Source: Sky Signs

Do you handle council approvals, engineering and civil works?

Our services go beyond just installing a sign: we can provide local authority approvals, engineering, civil works, environment controls and electrical connections, as a complete turnkey solution.

Source: Capabilities, Installation

Do you make braille, tactile and fire safety signs?

Yes. From fire safety signs and safety strip decals to braille and tactile signage, each piece is designed to meet regulatory requirements while complementing your environment.

Source: Statutory Signs

Can you work from our brand guidelines, or do you design as well?

Whether we are interpreting established brand guidelines, enhancing supplied designs, or crafting something entirely new, we bring every project to life. In most cases we place the sign into a photo montage to show how it will look on site.

Source: Capabilities, Design

What does a job look like from first call to handover?

Five steps: site surveys, design, manufacture, installation and maintenance. Each project begins with a comprehensive site survey and client engagement.

Source: Our Process

Do you look after signs after they are installed?

We provide ongoing maintenance programs and comprehensive warranties. Where specified, we use an anti-graffiti/UV protectant clear coat that makes most graffiti easy to wipe off.

Source: Capabilities, Maintenance

Can you handle a very large rollout?

For the UNSW Health Translation Hub our scope was over 2000 internal and external signage elements, including three large-scale illuminated sky signs, delivered to a time-sensitive programme.

Source: UNSW Health project

Can you match heritage buildings?

For Capella Sydney, in a historic 1912 sandstone building, our team protected delicate sandstone and marble surfaces and crafted bronze-finished and brass signs to strict conservation standards.

Source: Capella Hotel project

Source: Contact
