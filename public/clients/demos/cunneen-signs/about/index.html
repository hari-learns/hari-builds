About Cunneen Signs

A family business, three generations on

At Cunneen Signs, our mission is to create high-quality, innovative signage that helps our clients stand out and communicate with impact. We are committed to delivering exceptional value through flexibility, reliability, and decades of industry expertise. By empowering our people, fostering strong customer and supplier relationships, and living our core values of CARE – Conscientious, Accountable, Reliable, and Extraordinary. We aim to exceed expectations and build lasting results for our clients, our team, and our business.

People and culture

At Cunneen Signs, people are at the heart of everything we do. For over 55 years, we’ve built more than just signage, we’ve built a culture of trust, craftsmanship, and care.

Founded as a family business in 1968, our story has always been shaped by the people behind the work. From the early days in Meadowbank to today’s hands-on workshop where quality is crafted, not outsourced, our team has grown but our core values remain the same: respect, reliability, and pride in a job well done.

We believe in creating a workplace where people feel supported, challenged, and empowered to grow. Many of our team members have been with us for over a decade, and some proudly for more than 30 years, testament to the strong relationships, shared knowledge, and mutual respect that define our culture.

A Legacy of Leadership and Learning

Across three generations of leadership, we’ve continued to invest in our people, whether through skills development, safety, or technology. From hand-poured bronze plaques to cutting-edge digital prints, our team combines traditional craftsmanship with modern capability.

We foster a collaborative environment where

Experience is valued, and emerging talent is nurtured

Ideas are welcomed, and continuous improvement is part of everyday thinking

Safety, inclusion, and respect are non-negotiable

Built on Family Values—Driven by a Shared Vision

Today, under the guidance of Managing Director Nicholas March, Cunneen Signs blends its heritage with a forward-thinking approach. Our people are central to this progress bringing energy, expertise, and care to every project, big or small.

Whether in the factory, on-site, or in the office, our team shares a commitment to quality, service, and doing things the right way.

We don’t just make signs. We build careers, relationships, and a culture we’re proud of.
