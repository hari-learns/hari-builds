Gunyama Park Aquatic and Recreation Centre

Cunneen Signs was proud to deliver a diverse and high-quality signage package for the award-winning Gunyama Park Aquatic and Recreation Centre in Zetland. As part of this major City of Sydney project, we worked alongside CPB Contractors and ExtraBlack to help bring the design vision to life across the expansive community facility.

Our scope included the fabrication and installation of custom totems with integrated LED screens, a cast bronze plaque, bespoke fabricated lettering, hand-applied signwriting, and direct printing on stone for a truly distinctive finish. Every element was produced with care to withstand the unique conditions of an aquatic environment while contributing to the centre’s contemporary and welcoming aesthetic.

This project demonstrates Cunneen Signs’ capability to deliver complex, detail-driven signage across multiple mediums—combining traditional craftsmanship with advanced fabrication techniques. From bold statement pieces to refined branding elements, our work helps unify the space and enhance the visitor experience throughout the precinct.
