Campbelltown Catholic Club

As part of an extensive refurbishment of Campbelltown Catholic Club, Cunneen Signs was engaged to deliver a high-quality signage package in collaboration with Meridian Construction.

This project highlighted our ability to combine craftsmanship with creativity, incorporating over 100 unique signs including custom brass elements, engraved timber panels, hand-painted details, and precision-fabricated signage. Each piece was tailored to complement the architectural finishes and elevate the guest experience within the newly transformed venue.

Delivered during a period of significant industry disruption, the project is a testament to the resilience of our team and our commitment to excellence regardless of external challenges.

From elegant wayfinding solutions to impactful brand signage, this project reflects our dedication to delivering signage that not only informs, but enhances the spaces it lives in.
