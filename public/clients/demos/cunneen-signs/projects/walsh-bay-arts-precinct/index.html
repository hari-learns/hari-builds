Walsh Bay Arts Precinct

Cunneen Signs proudly contributed to the revitalisation of Sydney’s award-winning Walsh Bay Arts Precinct by delivering a comprehensive signage package for this landmark cultural project.

Our signage solutions include wayfinding and interpretive signs carefully crafted to complement the precinct’s heritage architecture and vibrant public spaces. Using custom materials and precise fabrication techniques, we created signage that offers clear navigation while respecting the design vision of TZG, Minale Gerber and Collider.

Working closely with Richard Crookes Constructions, our team ensured the signage integrated seamlessly into the precinct’s landscape and architecture, enhancing the visitor experience across theatres, waterfront promenades, and gathering areas.

This project highlights Cunneen Signs’ expertise in delivering tailored, high-quality signage for complex heritage and cultural environments.
