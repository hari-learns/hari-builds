Bankstown Airport Precinct

As part of ongoing upgrades to the Bankstown Airport precinct, Cunneen Signs was engaged to design, manufacture, and install a comprehensive signage package that improved visibility and navigation across the site.

The project featured seven freestanding pylons up to 5 metres high, each supported by 2-metre-deep engineered footings to ensure long-term structural integrity. Complementing these were large-format building signs — each 6 metres wide by 2 metres high — showcasing tenant-specific graphics that reinforced brand presence throughout the precinct.

Further works included signwritten graphics on both concrete and corrugated surfaces to display street addresses, along with a series of low-level aluminium directional and tenant identification signs that provided clear and consistent wayfinding.

We coordinated closely with tenants to schedule works and ensure our installation activities did not interfere with ongoing airport operations.

From heavy engineered structures to fine graphic applications, this project demonstrates our capability to deliver cohesive, high-quality signage solutions across a complex and active industrial environment.
