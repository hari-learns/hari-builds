UNSW Signage

Cunneen Signs has been a trusted signage partner to UNSW for over 30 years. As the University’s preferred supplier, we’ve delivered consistent, high-quality signage across all campuses—from everyday wayfinding to iconic placemaking elements.

Our team works closely with UNSW, project architects, designers, and contractors to ensure every sign aligns with brand, accessibility, and quality standards. A dedicated Cunneen Signs Project Manager oversees all UNSW projects, ensuring smooth coordination from design through to installation and ongoing maintenance.

With an in-depth understanding of UNSW’s signage guidelines and environments, we provide end-to-end solutions that are functional, compliant, and visually integrated.

Our Work Includes:

External signage: Directional signs, wayfinding totems, help points, interpretive elements, and high-level sky signs

Internal signage: Room IDs, amenities signage, privacy film, directional systems, and refurbishments

Placemaking: Transforming spaces into vibrant, engaging environments—such as Basser Steps and Sunswift

Event signage: Temporary signage and installations for launches, activations, and special events including graduation

Whether planning a new build, refurbishing a facility, or activating a public space, Cunneen Signs brings experience, quality, and reliability to every UNSW project.
