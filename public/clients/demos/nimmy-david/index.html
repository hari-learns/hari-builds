About Nimmy

Kerala's first real estate vlogger

I am Nimmy David, a Kochi based realtor who helps buyers and sellers in the premium real estate market. I bring over 18 years of experience with the best-known builders in the Kerala real estate industry. I am also the founder and partner of the firm EZED Realty with a vast client base that includes entrepreneurs, politicians, celebrities and NRIs.

As a client, you will benefit from my in-depth knowledge of the premium real estate market, ability to adapt to market fluctuations and my “people-first” approach to business.

In 2019, I became Kerala’s first real estate Video blogger (Vlogger). My video blogs are hugely popular with clients outside India as it gives them access to great properties from the comfort of their home.
