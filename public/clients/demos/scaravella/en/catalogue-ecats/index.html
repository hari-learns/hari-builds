How eCat.S works

“eCat.S” is the easy and intuitive software that allows you to identify the screw you need from our complete production range. Only for Microsoft Windows (requires the Microsoft .NET libraries). Rel. 23.03, 4 May 2023.

  1. Download and installAfter downloading the installer from the site, launch it and follow the instructions. The installation creates the icon on the desktop and in the start-up menu.
  2. Enter the search dataThe search data input section is on the left, the data of the screws in the database on the right. Every time you enter a value, the screws with a corresponding parameter are selected at once.
  3. Open the data sheetBy double clicking on the line of a screw you open its file; you can open more than one to compare similar screws.
  4. PrintWith the button “Stampa” you choose whether to display a print preview and then print, or print directly.
  5. Updated databaseThe database is constantly updated: it always uses the most recent available version.

Note: we do not have screws with a diameter and pitch different from those indicated in eCat.S.
