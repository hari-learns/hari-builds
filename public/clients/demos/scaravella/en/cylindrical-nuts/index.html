Cylindrical ball screw nuts

Single, internally preloaded and double preloaded cylindrical nuts from the Scaravella catalogue.

Cd = dynamic load, Cs = static load, in daN, as in the catalogue tables. System speed limit: n × Dn ≤ 90000 (n = rpm, Dn = nominal diameter).

Find in this familySee also the cylindrical nuts

Single cylindrical nut MC

A single body with the helical thread for the balls and the housings for the ball recirculation deflectors. Supplied without preload (medium axial backlash 0.01 to 0.02 mm); zero backlash on request. Also available with threaded end (MCTF).

Cylindrical nut, internal preload MCPI

A single body like the single nut; to increase the stiffness of the screw/nut coupling the thread is made with a spacing S between the two series of recirculations: the amount of offset determines the preload required.
