Production of ground ball screws

Ground precision ball screws have long been established in the field of movement of machine axes, thanks to their characteristics of:

  • High reliability
  • Extreme precision
  • Maximum performance
  • Long life

Every component of our ball screws is designed and manufactured in-house, taking into account the ever-increasing demands of the market in terms of precision of movement and speed of work.

The screws can be made with

Accuracy class
3-5-7, according to ISO 3408 Standard
Diameter
from 16 mm up to 63 mm
Pitch
from 5 mm up to 40 mm
Length
up to 5600 mm

Silver

Silver series

The Silver Series ball screws have established themselves in the field of movement of machine axes thanks to their requirements of reliability, precision, efficiency and durability.
