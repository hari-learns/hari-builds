Ground ball screws overhaul service

Repair, don't replace: a sustainable way of living.

This service, managed with the utmost speed, allows you to reuse your ball screws within a few days, limiting your operational difficulties as much as possible. The experience gained by Scaravella F.lli s.r.l in overhauling precision ball screws allows us to provide a service that is increasingly appreciated by all our Customers, whatever their industrial size.

How it works

  1. Recognise the signsJamming, discontinuity in movement and noise during work show the decay of the screw and nut system which, over time, leads to machine downtime.
  2. Send us the screwShipping address: Scaravella F.lli, Via Bentelli 25, 29121 Piacenza, Italy. Phone +39 0523 480192 · 480121.
  3. Check and evaluationThe testing technicians check screws produced by any manufacturer and accurately evaluate their conditions, with precision testing instruments.
  4. Restoration, when possibleThe Overhaul Department restores, whenever possible, the previous operational features of the screw.
  5. Back on the machineThe service allows you to reuse the screw within a few days.

Why a screw wears

Scarce lubrication, the contact of the ball screw with machining residues as well as particularly severe operating conditions often worsen the wear of the parts and reduce the life of a screw. When facing such a situation, the technician appointed to the repairs can only replace the screw-nut system.

It isn't always necessary to replace the ball screw with a new one: the experience of our testing technicians, the processing quality and the precision of our testing instruments allow our Overhaul Department to check screws produced by any manufacturer, to accurately evaluate their conditions and to restore, whenever possible, their previous operational features.
