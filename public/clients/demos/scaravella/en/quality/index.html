Quality

Constantly diligent and attentive to the quality of its products, in April 1999 Scaravella F.lli S.r.l. obtained ISO 9001:2015 Quality System Certification, thanks to which it has implemented an internal reorganization process that allows to ensure the supply of products matching the Customer's requirements through constant improvement of the company performance.

Quality policy

Revision April 2020

To pursue full customer satisfaction and to consolidate and increase competitiveness, Scaravella defines and promotes the Quality Policy through the commitment of everyone who works in and for the company. The Management's commitments concern:

  • Customer satisfaction
  • Customer interface and speed of response
  • Continuous improvement with interest in market evolution
  • Suppliers
  • Internal resources
  • Safety in the workplace
  • Quality and improvement
  • Interested parties
