Flanged ball screw nuts

Single, internally preloaded and double preloaded flanged nuts. Flanges may have a DIN hole pattern (6 holes up to ø32, 8 holes from ø40) or STD (standard); particular shapes and hole patterns on request.

Cd = dynamic load, Cs = static load, in daN, as in the catalogue tables. System speed limit: n × Dn ≤ 90000 (n = rpm, Dn = nominal diameter).

Find in this familySee also the flanged nuts

Single flanged nut MF

A single body with flange, with DIN or STD (standard) hole pattern. Supplied without preload (medium axial backlash 0.01 to 0.02 mm); zero backlash on request. Also available with central flange (FC). Flanges with particular shape and holes on request.
