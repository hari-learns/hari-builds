About

Atelier Ana Oliveira

Atelier Ana Oliveira designs residential and commercial interiors and is a reference in the field in the Algarve.

Ana Oliveira studied Interior Design at the Lisbon School of Design and became a Certified Home Stager through the Escuela Madrileña de la Decoración, accredited by IAHSP, and through the Portuguese Association of Home Stagers.

Every project is built on her knowledge of Feng Shui. Each room is designed to respect the flow of energy in the space. Because true design is not only about visual beauty. It should bring body, mind and spirit into harmony.

Fully aware of the influence our surroundings have on us, the aim is a single one: to create an emotional bond between the client and the space.

As Creative Director, I strive to turn walls into stories, houses into homes, spaces into experiences.

I am here for you. Thank you for being there!
