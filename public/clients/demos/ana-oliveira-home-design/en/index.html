What is Home Staging?

Home Staging is a property-marketing tool: preparing and presenting a property for sale or rent with specific, proven techniques.

Born and practised in the United States for 50 years, started by Barb Schwarz, it is now used all over the world.

Selling a property, or letting it as a holiday rental? The goal of Home Staging is clear: sell faster, at the best price.

Because there is no second chance to make a good first impression! We Love the Details!
