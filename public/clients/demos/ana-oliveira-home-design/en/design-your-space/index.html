New · Room Studio

See your room in our designs

Try it before you book: your own room, redesigned from a photo you take.

  1. 1Upload a photo of the room
  2. 2Confirm the room and pick a style
  3. 3Write what you would like to change
  4. 4See the before and after and send it to the studio

A concept image to show the idea. The real project is always designed with you, with Feng Shui and every detail considered by Ana.

Is it free?

Yes. You can try a few styles a day at no cost and without an account.

Is the image the final design?

No. It is a concept that helps you show what you want. Ana designs the real project with you: measurements, Feng Shui, materials, ordering and installation.

What happens to my photo?

It is only used to create the image. It reaches the studio only if you press “Send”.

Does it work for home staging or holiday lets?

Yes. Photograph the room empty or furnished and ask, for example, for “ready to sell” or “bright holiday apartment”.
