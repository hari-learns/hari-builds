Home Staging

Sell faster, at the best price: your property prepared and presented at its best.

Interior design projectsCommercial designPrestige ConsultancyHome Staging

Home Staging is a property-marketing tool: preparing and presenting a property for sale or rent with specific, proven techniques.

Selling a property, or letting it as a holiday rental? The goal is clear: sell faster, at the best price.

Holiday-let refresh, Arriba

For empty properties

Home Staging project

Putting an empty property on the market is NOT a good idea! 90% of people cannot picture an empty home, so if you are selling an empty property, you are speaking to a minority of 10%.

  1. Visit to the property, photographic and measured survey.
  2. Briefing on layout, function, target buyers, decorative style, materials and everything else the project needs.
  3. Project presented as a moodboard and a dimensioned 2D plan, with a suggested layout and the pieces to buy.
  4. Installation of the project on site.
  5. Furniture hire possible, subject to availability.
Get in touch

For furnished or lived-in homes

Refresh HS consultation

The way you live is not the way you sell. Selling faster and at the best price takes a precise, decisive preparation of the property.

  1. Visit to the property.
  2. Diagnosis of what to improve.
  3. A written report with the actions to take, plus a shopping list of pieces and where to buy them.
  4. The client carries out the work. If the list seems too long, you can choose the Home Staging project instead.
Get in touch

Turnkey

Home Staging for holiday lets (AL)

The turnkey service with everything your holiday let (Alojamento Local) needs.

  1. Visit to the property, to understand its architecture and surroundings.
  2. Briefing on layout, function, zones, target guests, decorative style and materials.
  3. Project presented as a moodboard with suggested layout and pieces.
  4. Installation on site, delivery and assembly.
