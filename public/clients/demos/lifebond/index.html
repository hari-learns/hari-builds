Since 1980

Surat's weaving, since 1948

Lifebond Machines Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted name in textile machinery, backed by decades of experience, engineering expertise and a deep understanding of the evolving textile industry. Established in 1980 by the Dhamanwala Group, Lifebond carries forward a legacy rooted in Surat's weaving and textile processing industry since 1948.

Development Mindset

From market demand to machinery solutions

Lifebond initially introduced shuttle looms and than as per market demand started manufacture twisting machines, winding machines Two For One twisting machines, Cop Winding Machines, Fabric inspection cum Rolling Machines and Rapier Looms.
