Product overview

Designed for speed, control & flexibility.

The WT9100 combines direct drive, electronic control and advanced weft insertion with multiple shedding options for diverse weaving requirements.

Technical specification

Machine specifications

Reed Space Nominal
190, 210, 230, 250, 280, 340, 360 cm
Useful Range
Reduction from nominal 0-60 cm (190-250 cm); 0-80 cm (280-360 cm)
Yarn Range
Spun Ne100 ~ Ne2.2; Filament 56 dtex ~ 1350 dtex
Weft Selection
2-color, 4-color, 6-color, 8-color
Drive
Direct drive main motor; magnetic brake; programmable start; built-in inverter. Speed can change during weaving
Power
2.2 kW crank; 3.0 kW cam; 3.7 kW dobby; 5.5-7.5 kW Jacquard
Weft Accumulator
Electronic FDP weft accumulator. ABS control, air ballon breaker
Weft Insertion
Main nozzle + sub nozzle combined system; automatic insertion control; 1st pick control. Stretch nozzle; independent timing auxiliary nozzle; energy-saving double nozzle valve
Shedding
Crank 4 shafts; negative cam 8 shafts max; positive cam 10 shafts max; dobby 16 shafts max; Jacquard shedding
Let-Off
Electronic let-off by servo motor; double roller electronic let-off with auto reverse. Up & down beam / twin beam; flange Φ914 mm, Φ1000 mm; flange Φ800 mm
Take-Up
Electronic take-up by servo motor; pick density standard 15-300 picks; weaving length display. Batching motion 720 mm; max take-up Φ600 / Φ520 mm by shedding type
Beating
Crank type beating, multiple sley sword beating. 4 Link narrow / 6 Links wide
Selvage
Planetary gear leno selvage; center leno device. Electronic planetary gear leno
Lubrication
Oil bath system for main driving parts; centralized lubrication. Automatic centralized lubrication
Auto-Stop Motion
Photoelectric double weft sensor; electronic 6-row warp contact bar; automatic stop for leno/waste yarn break. Bobbin yarn break sensor; anti-hand beating sensor
Control & Automatic
Computer control for data setting, monitoring, self-diagnosis and interface; automatic pick finding. AFC, AJC, centralized networked monitor; memory card; jet automatic control
