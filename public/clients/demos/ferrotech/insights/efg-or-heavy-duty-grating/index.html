Electro-forged or heavy duty grating: which one?

What is the difference between electro-forged and heavy duty welded grating?Electro-forged grating is a resistance-welded grid of load bars and cross bars used for walkways, platforms, trenches and floors. Heavy duty welded grating is built for rolling and static loads such as plant floors, loading docks and airports, with deeper and thicker load bars.

The short answer

Electro-forged steel grating (EFG) is a steel grid formed from load bearing bars and cross bars through resistance welding. It is the usual choice for pedestrian and light industrial access: walkways, platforms, trenches, stair treads, mezzanines and covers.

Heavy duty welded grating is designed for gratings under heavy rolling and static loads: highways, plant floors, loading docks, inlet covers and airports. It is made in a wider range of bar sizes and spacings, because conditions run from light-duty forklifts to semi-trucks and trailers.

How the load decides the bar

Once the bar spacing is chosen, the bearing bar size must be specified from the expected load and the clear, unsupported span. A longer span or a heavier load means a deeper or thicker bearing bar.

Serrated bearing bars can be used on either type to improve skid resistance. For pedestrian traffic, BS 4592 part one describes three loading categories: light duty (one person), normal duty (regular two-way traffic) and heavy duty (high density traffic).

Before you choose

Send the clear span, the load type (pedestrian, forklift, truck), the finish and the standard you work to. Ferrotech publishes a load table of safe uniformly distributed loads and deflections for simply supported panels, based on a maximum permissible stress of 180 N/mm², and the engineering team can confirm the selection.

Frequently asked

What is the difference between electro-forged and heavy duty grating?

Electro-forged grating is resistance-welded and typically used for walkways and platforms; heavy duty welded grating uses deeper, thicker load bars for rolling and static loads such as plant floors and loading docks.

What bar depth can heavy duty grating have?

Ferrotech makes heavy duty gratings with load bar depths from 25 mm to 150 mm and thicknesses of 5, 6, 8, 10 and 12 mm.
