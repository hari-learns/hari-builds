Service outline

Peak performance, sustained

Whether you need on-site maintenance or off-line machine overhauls and rebuilds, our team of experienced engineers has the expertise to ensure your plant and equipment operate at peak performance, all while minimising downtime and disruptions to your production.

Specific services
  • On site maintenance & servicing
  • Plant/equipment refurbishment
  • Modifications/upgrades to existing machinery

Maximize Uptime

Our expert engineers provide on-site maintenance and servicing to keep your equipment running smoothly with minimal disruption.

Machinery Upgrades & Overhauls

From refurbishments to performance-boosting modifications, we enhance and extend the life of your machinery, on-site or at our facility.

Our approach

Four steps, with you involved at the points that matter.

01

Needs Assessment

We begin by engaging with you to understand your maintenance requirements and get a clear picture of your equipment's current condition.

02

Evaluation & Pricing

Our team conducts a detailed assessment of your machinery and provides competitive, straightforward pricing to address your maintenance needs.

03

Sourcing & Production

Leveraging our in-house capabilities and trusted partners, we manufacture or source high-quality replacement components tailored to your specifications.

04

Maintenance Support

Finally, we perform expert maintenance and service work around your production schedule, ensuring minimal downtime and peak operational performance.
