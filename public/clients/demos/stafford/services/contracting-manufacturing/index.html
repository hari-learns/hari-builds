Service outline

Your vision, engineered to perfection

We collaborate closely with you to bring your designs to life. Whether you have fully developed, ready-to-manufacture drawings or require design adjustments for production, we ensure the consistent delivery of high-quality components and equipment tailored to your specifications. Additionally, through our trusted manufacturing partners, we offer a comprehensive solution for complete machine production and automation, providing you with a seamless, one-stop service.

Specific services
  • Laser cutting
  • Sheet metal folding & forming
  • Manual & CNC machining
  • Fabrication & polishing
  • Assembly & factory commissioning
  • Stainless steel drive component manufacture

One-stop shop

We can handle the project in entirety, from mechanical components through to electrical and automation.

Value added

We look at your designs through a manufacturing lens and work with you to get the best outcome.

Our approach

Four steps, with you involved at the points that matter.

01

Consultation & Scope

We kick off by connecting with you to fully understand your project needs and define the scope of supply, ensuring your vision is clearly set from the outset.

02

Pricing & Proposal

We provide a clear, competitive quote tailored to your requirements, setting the stage for a confident and collaborative partnership.

03

Review & Processing

When needed, our experts offer targeted input to optimize your customer-supplied designs for efficient production, enhancing manufacturability while preserving your original vision.

04

Manufacture & Commission

Finally, we bring your project to life with meticulous manufacturing and thorough commissioning, ensuring each component meets our exacting quality standards.
