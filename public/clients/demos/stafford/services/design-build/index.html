Service outline

Concept to completion

We provide tailored engineering solutions, from modifying existing equipment to creating fully bespoke systems. Using SolidWorks, we model, evaluate, and produce detailed designs, followed by in-house manufacturing and a full turnkey service. Our process includes customer involvement at key stages to ensure optimal results for projects of any size, from single units to large-scale production. Additional services include FEA, prototyping, and documentation.

Specific services
  • Custom machine design
  • Equipment modification & upgrades
  • Prototyping and validation

Our approach

Four steps, with you involved at the points that matter.

01

Kick-off

We start by engaging with you to define the project's vision, establish key objectives, and clearly outline the scope of work.

02

Development & Design

Our experts transform your ideas into tailored concepts with initial budget estimates, evolving them into comprehensive designs using advanced SolidWorks modeling.

03

Approval & Finalized Pricing

We present detailed designs and transparent pricing for your review, ensuring every element meets your expectations before moving forward.

04

Manufacture & Commissioning

Finally, we bring your project to life with meticulous manufacturing and thorough commissioning, delivering a turnkey solution that exceeds expectations.
