Stafford Engineering acts as a one-stop engineering partner for a leading healthcare product provider, transforming detailed drawings into expertly manufactured and assembled components and machines for producing products that service the healthcare market. Leveraging a relationship built over many years, we work in a collaborative manner, providing manufacturing feedback and advise where practical to produce the best possible outcome. We ensure precision, quality and efficiency are present throughout, meeting the exacting standards of the healthcare sector.

20+Years that Stafford Engineering has been servicing the healthcare sector.
240+Significant projects completed.
7000+Number of people employed internationally by our customer.
01

Background

A leading global designer, manufacturer, and marketer of innovative healthcare solutions, specializing in respiratory care, acute care, and the treatment of obstructive sleep apnoea required a manufacturing partner to turn their designs and ideas into reality.

02

Challenge

Ensuring the highest quality standards that are expected in the healthcare industry are maintained through the manufacture and assembly of intricate designs. Additionally, providing actionable feedback where required meant balancing strict adherence to client specifications with our insights to optimize manufacturability.

03

Solution

We combine technical expertise with a collaborative approach to deliver outstanding results. We meticulously review drawings through the processing stage of the project, utilize state of the art machinery, highly skilled tradespeople and rigorous quality control in order to manufacture and assemble the mechanical systems to a high standard. Working with our client to deliver a superior product whilst meeting the required schedules, we further strengthen our reputation as a trusted partner in the healthcare manufacturing sector.
