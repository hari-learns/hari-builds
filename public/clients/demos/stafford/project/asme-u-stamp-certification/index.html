01

Background

Stafford Engineering is the only manufacturer in New Zealand certified with the ASME U-stamp, a globally recognized accreditation we have proudly held since 2016. This certification enables us to design, build, and certify pressure vessels to ASME Section VIII Division 1 standards, ensuring they meet strict international safety and quality requirements. Serving both local and international markets, we produce vessels for a range of industries, with significant expertise in the food processing sector. Our certification allows us to provide clients with pressure vessels that are fully compliant and ready for use in the most demanding applications worldwide.

02

Challenge

Adhering to ASME standards for pressure vessel production is a complex process that requires expertise in design, material selection, manufacturing, and certification. From rigorous stress analysis, design verification and precise welding to material traceability and non-destructive testing, each step ensures the vessel meets stringent safety and quality requirements. Challenges include managing specialized materials, complying with inspection protocols, and maintaining detailed documentation, all while balancing cost and efficiency.

03

Solution

At Stafford Engineering, we specialize in designing ASME-compliant pressure vessels or manufacturing them from your provided designs. Our process includes design validation to meet ASME Section VIII standards, certified material sourcing, qualified welding, and rigorous non-destructive and hydrostatic testing. We work closely with third-party inspectors to ensure all requirements are met, providing reliable, fully certified vessels, built to a world class quality.
