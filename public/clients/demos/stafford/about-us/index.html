Who we are

Since 1986, Stafford Engineering has evolved from a local precision workshop into a multidisciplinary force in engineering. What began as a service for local industries has grown into a trusted partner for clients across food processing, packaging, and pharmaceuticals, both here in New Zealand and around the globe.

Our expertise spans a range of industries where precision, reliability, and innovation are essential. Over the years, we've built lasting relationships by solving complex challenges, streamlining processes, and consistently delivering engineering solutions that meet the highest standards. Whether it's supporting advanced manufacturing or designing equipment from the ground up, we bring decades of experience and a mindset that values simplicity in solving the most intricate problems.

A history of excellence

Since 1986, Stafford Engineering has been competing on the world stage, offering a service that consistently exceeds expectations.

  1. 1986Roger Evans alongside brother Don found Stafford Industries
  2. 1995Stafford Industries outgrows facility and moves to Tasman Road site and starts exporting to Australia
  3. 1996Starts to invest in CNC machining and Laser Cutting
  4. 1999Stafford Industries and Stafford Sheetmetal combine and move to Aztec Place site, starts exporting to the USA
  5. 2002Stafford Industries is rebranded as Stafford Engineering
  6. 2006Succession planning begins with shareholder group increasing
  7. 2011ISO 9000 accreditation
  8. 2015Roger leaves the day-to-day operation with Kaleb James stepping into the role of MD
  9. 2016Stafford Engineering gains ASME U-Stamp certification
  10. 2018Stafford Engineering Australia established
  11. 2020Stafford Engineering works through the challenges of COVID to continue to supply to the healthcare sector
  12. 2025Stafford Engineering brand refresh and website launch
