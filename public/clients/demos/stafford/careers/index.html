Careers

Roles we hire for

Current openings are listed on Seek and Trade Me.

Polishers

Through the operation of drills, handheld polishers and grinders, Polishers aid in the manufacture of high quality products and maintain a high level of accuracy, appearance and attention to detail.

Machinists

With a high level of attention to detail, accuracy, appearance and machining skills, machinists use both conventional and CNC lathes and milling machines along with other workshop machinery to produce high quality parts that meet requirements specified through engineering drawings.

Fabricators

Fabricators apply strong attention to detail, precision, aesthetics, and fabrication expertise to produce high-quality parts that meet the specifications outlined in engineering drawings. They utilize TIG, MIG, arc, and gas welding techniques, along with equipment such as gas and plasma cutters, brake presses, guillotines, rollers, drills, handheld grinders, and various other workshop tools.

Fitters

By demonstrating a high level of fitting & machining skills, with attention to detail, accuracy and appearance, fitters test, align, modify and assemble equipment within the workshop and aid with installations on site where required.

Project & Design Engineers

Project & Design Engineers communicate effectively with customers both internal & external and through a high level of design skill, proficiency and practicality produce designs, engineering drawings and manage projects.

Other key roles

Other roles which are pivotal to the business include: Sales & Business Development, Support Engineers, Logistics & Quality Assurance, Workshop Assistants, Pre-Fabrication and Laser Cutting, Administration Support.

Our culture

What we're about

We believe the best solutions come from a team that works together and values doing things properly. We foster a culture built on collaboration, continuous learning, and practical problem-solving. Whether you're on the workshop floor or in the design room, everyone plays a part, and fresh ideas are always welcome.

Here, it's about having each other's backs, taking pride in our work, and enjoying the process along the way.

We offer flexible working hours, an active social club, competitive rates and an enjoyable working environment alongside a supportive and like minded team.

Apprenticeships & Cadetships

We maintain a number of apprentices / cadets throughout the various manufacturing departments in the business, keeping up the flow of new talent into the business.

Internships

Each year we offer summer work placements to Engineering undergrads as part of their course requirements, both in the workshop and engineering office.

Professional Development

Through both internal and external training, our staff continue to grow and develop professionally. We have had a relationship with the Icehouse since 2005 with a number of staff having gone through their programmes.

We believe in building the future engineering workforce requires investment into people from an early age. We have been involved in WECA (Waikato Engineering Careers Association), and SSEP (Secondary School Employer Partnerships) from inception and regularly have school or Polytechnic tours through our facility with the aim in giving young people an insight into industry.
