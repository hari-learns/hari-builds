Shearwater R90, Premium Performance

Shearwater R90

Length x width9 x 2.8 m
PeopleUp to 10 incl. driver
Outboard300 – 400 hp
Fuel tank200 L
Top speed45 – 50 knots

The R90 offers the functionality and comfort of a RIB, but enjoys the refinement of a luxury sports boat. Its sleek looks, speed and seaworthiness are second to none. It will glide you and your guests along the coast in comfort and style.

Make the Shearwater R90 your own by customising the hull and tubes, two tone console and the sumptuous Silvertex seating. We offer the option of two or four helm seats, and three rear end designs to best match your needs.

Up front is a large V bow cushion with internal storage; it also houses the anchor windlass. The large command console has been designed with huge dash space to house all the electronics and NAV gear you expect on a RIB this capable. The dash has been carefully designed so the engine management is at your fingertips, it also has the option for a discreet toilet. The helm seating has shock dampening suspension. The seat base drops away to form a standing helm, the wrap-around lumbar support adding security from sideway movement.

The generous aft bench seat and sun pad leads down to the bathing area, with easy access to the water by the telescopic ladder. We recommend 300-400 hp propelling the R90 to 45-50 kn with ease.

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding purchasing or renting a Shearwater RIB, we are here to assist you. Reach out to us by email, phone or in person at our workshop.
