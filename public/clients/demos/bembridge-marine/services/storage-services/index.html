Bembridge Marine

Bembridge Marine storage services

Bembridge Marine has purpose built boat handling equipment to transfer your boat from the water to storage. You don't need your own trailer in most cases, weight dependant.

60spaces

Outside storage

We have 60 outside rack spaces for RIBs and sports boats up to 9m.

85spaces

Inside storage

We have 85 inside storage spaces with racks for RIBs and sports boats up to 7.5m and ground spaces up to 9m.

Winter storage

Winter storage runs 1st October to 31st March and has to be booked as a full 6 month space.

Summer storage

Summer storage is charged on a monthly basis.

Packages

We offer several storage packages: outside floor storage away from the yard, dry stack outside storage, dry stack indoor storage, and winter storage in a secure dry barn away from the yard.
