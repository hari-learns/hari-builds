Bembridge Marine

Services

Dedicated to all aspects of your boating needs.

Yard services

Our waterside premises have direct access to the water. We have our own pontoon and slip, along with launch and recovery equipment. We can carry out general maintenance, pressure washing, antifoul etc.

Read more

Marine engineering and servicing

We have a team of skilled marine outboard engine technicians working around the Solent area. From service and diagnostics, set up and upgrades, in house or by our mobile unit, we are able to keep your boat in top working order.

Read more

Engine sales

We are very proud to have been one of the south coast's leading authorised Mercury / Mariner and Mercruiser inboard main dealers for 45 years.

Read more

RIB tube repairs and replacement

Our in-house tube technicians learnt their skills over many years, able to carry out damage repair, upgrades and tube replacements.

Read more

Boat building and repairs

Bembridge Marine has a dedicated boat building shop, we can produce and repair GRP components, hull and deck damage and produce our own brand of boats.

Read more

Fit-out and maintenance

We cover boat maintenance, including wear and tear, damage repair, upgrades, and electronics. We use contractors for stainless steel, upholstery, and specialist cleaning if needed.

Read more

Boat storage and drystack

We have 60 outside rack spaces for RIBs and sports boats up to 9m. We have 85 inside storage spaces with racks for RIBs and sports boats up to 7.5m and ground spaces up to 9m.

Read more

Spares and accessories

We carry a large amount of general chandlery, lubricants, electrical fit out items and outboard spare parts.
