Bembridge Marine yard services

Bembridge Marine is situated beside the water at Bembridge Harbour.

We have our own slipway, pontoon and specialised equipment, allowing us to pull out most motorboats up to 8.5m for general yard services such as pressure washing, cleaning, antifouling etc. We also work with trusted local specialist contractors who can supply a range of occasional trades we don't offer in-house.

If your boat is too large to pull out in our yard, we work with another yard just across the water to lift out and we will carry out the engineering work.

We hold a range of paints and anodes in stock at competitive rates. If we don't have the items you need, we hold accounts with specialised chandlery supply companies, who supply overnight in most cases.

We offer several storage packages: outside floor storage away from the yard, dry stack outside storage, dry stack indoor storage, and winter storage in a secure dry barn away from the yard.

We have very competitive rates, please contact us for pricing.
