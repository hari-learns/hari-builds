A family boatyard

Family owned and run on Bembridge Harbour since the 1960s

Based on the Isle of Wight, Bembridge Marine LTD is family owned and run to this day. Our large waterside boat yard has a slipway, a pontoon and specialised recovery and storage equipment.

Our large team of fulltime and summer staff allow us to cover general yard duties. We have skilled marine engineering technicians, specialising in the current outboard motor models, with a mobile unit. Our in-house boat building team produces new RIBs, along with GRP repairs and upgrades. Our tube shop / repair technicians replace RIB tubes year round.
