Inside Ayodhya Vistar

A gated township planned around daily life

RERA-approved and Government approved, 22.88 acres, more than 400 residential units and more than 50 amenities, on Main Tonk Road, Chaksu Bypass, Jaipur.

01

Entrance Gate

A grand entrance inspired by timeless Indian architecture, designed to create a powerful first impression. It reflects heritage, peace, and cultural pride, welcoming residents into a spiritually enriched living environment.

02

Ram Darbar Temple

A serene temple dedicated to Lord Ram at the heart of the township. Designed for daily prayers, meditation, and spiritual gatherings, it creates a peaceful atmosphere filled with positivity and devotion.

03

Dashrath Palace: Community Hall

A thoughtfully designed community space for social, cultural, and religious events. Ideal for celebrations, gatherings, and meetings, bringing residents together in harmony.

04

Water Storage System (CWR & OHSR)

Efficient underground and overhead water storage systems ensuring uninterrupted water supply and modern infrastructure support.
