VETO electric stove

An exceptionally long-lived electric stove whose structure has stayed the same since 1965. The open frame “breathes” and spreads the heat evenly.

Buy the electric stove

The law of löyly

The stove is not too tall. The top of the stones sits at footbench height, so the bather sits in the löyly pocket down to the toes.

Why it lasts

  • Elements apart from the stones

    Elements of domestic stainless steel sit outside the stone basket. They very rarely burn out.

  • Stainless steel

    The frame is entirely stainless steel, the only durable material against temperature swings and water.

  • Built-in humidifier

    Gives the sauna a suitable base humidity throughout. The löyly never feels dry.

  • 10-year warranty

    The warranty covers the whole stove, including the elements. The control unit warranty is set by its manufacturer.

Nine colours

Black, grey, white, blue, dark red, green, light pink, light blue and stainless steel.
