Under 30min

In the sauna in under half an hour.

When a stove heats quickly and you can bathe after half an hour instead of an hour, less wood or electricity is used in total. Preheating always uses the most.

Wood stove

Double firebox: a tall firebox and an afterburner. Flue gases travel twice as far and more heat is taken from the wood.

Electric stove

Open structure: cool air rises through the stove from floor level. A moderate amount of stone heats quickly.

A structure where air moves.

A double firebox that aids combustion, air channels that recover heat and a humidifier together make an unusual combination.

  1. Afterburner

    Primary air enters under the grate, secondary air through the door edges. The flue gases finish burning in the upper firebox, flues and afterburner.

  2. Open air channels

    Room air rises in vertical channels and through the stone space. Temperature differences even out.

  3. Humidifier

    An enamelled basin at the back of the stone space steams water and softens the löyly throughout.

From one man's curiosity to a third generation

VETO began in 1949, when Arvo Oksanen noticed that boiler technology could be applied to a wood stove.

  • 1949Arvo Oksanen designs the wood stove prototype, the first afterburner in a wood stove.
  • 1952Muko Oy is founded. Wood stove production begins.
  • 1965The electric stove is finished. Continuous production begins in 1966 as Veto Elektro.
  • 2003The VETO wood stove gets the best overall grade in a Tekniikan Maailma comparison.

I greatly value my grandfather's life's work, his uncompromising standards and foresight.

Henri Salokangas, third generation of the family company, managing director
