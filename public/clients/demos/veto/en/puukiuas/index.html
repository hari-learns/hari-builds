VETO wood stove

A wood-burning stove designed with thought for people who love good löyly. The sauna heats in under 30 minutes at best and uses less wood.

Built to last

  • Strong steel

    The back wall of the firebox is 6 mm steel, other key walls 4 mm. The seams are welded on both sides.

  • Four outer shells

    Polished stainless steel, enamelled black, grey or white. The shell can be replaced if needed.

  • Four flue connections

    From below on both sides and from behind, on separate order also from the top.

  • Adjustable legs

    Stainless steel. The stove stays level and off a damp floor.

Made in Finland, by many small firms

Making one wood stove employs, beyond our own company, more than ten small firms across Finland as subcontractors. Finally we finish every stove in Helsinki.
