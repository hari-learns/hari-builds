Products

Press tools & dies, designed and built to your specification

We are engaged in designing and manufacturing tools, press tools and dies as per custom specification. We manufacture a wide range of machine tools and dies that are available in a variety of shapes and sizes.

Basically, a press tool is an assembly of a tool and a die, a few other parts, and accessories.

The designs created in the tool room provide the foundation for high-quality stamping, cutting and tool forming of any geometry and complexity.
