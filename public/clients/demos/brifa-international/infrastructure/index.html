The plant at GIDC Vatva

We have mother machineries like CNC machines as well as conventional machineries. Sheet metal components are manufactured using power press machines.

We manufacture a wide range of precision sheet metal components, available across capacity ranges, to the specifications our clients ask for.

As described on Brifa's current site, each of the precision sheet metal components has a high degree of durability, performance and price competitiveness.
