CNC tool room facilities

We have an in-house design and development center with simulation and tool design facilities, manned by design engineers with proven experience and expertise in creating designs that are extremely crucial for the tool room.

The designs created here provide the foundation for high-quality stamping, cutting and tool forming of any geometry and complexity.

Brifa's Engitech Expo listing, published by the organiser, also names CNC wire-cut job work, power press stamping and press tools and dies among its services.
