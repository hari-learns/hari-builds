Customized sheet metal components

We meet the requirement of our clients by offering a large array of sheet metal components. These components are used in automotive, automobile and other engineering industries.

By following industry defined guidelines, our team of professionals manufactures the offered components using optimum quality raw material with the aid of the latest manufacturing techniques. In tune with our clients' diverse requirements, we offer these sheet metal components in varied specifications.

Sheet metal components are manufactured using power press machines. As described on Brifa's current site, the precision sheet metal components are abrasion and rust proof, and offered with a high degree of durability, performance and price competitiveness.
