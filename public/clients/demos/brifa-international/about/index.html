About

Sheet metal dies and components since 1984

Established as a sole proprietorship company in the year 1984 at Ahmedabad (Gujarat, India), we, Brifa International, are devoted towards manufacturing the finest quality range of sheet metal dies and sheet metal components.

We have become the preferred choice of our clients owing to our genuine quality, nominal price range and strict quality control.

Owing to the widespread experience and excellent strategies of our mentor, Mr. Brijesh Acharya, we have been able to gain a huge client base and accomplish the diverse demands of our valuable clients.
