Timber joinery and cabinetry in Blenheim

From kitchens to wardrobes, windows to stairs, we don’t believe in one-size-fits-all. We build joinery that fits you.

Our skills can be adapted to create anything

Our team has a diverse knowledge base of all aspects of the building and manufacturing industry. We specialise in both residential and commercial joinery for Marlborough and beyond, but from our showcase of work our skills can be adapted to create anything.

We manufacture custom joinery and kitchens in our timber department, alongside the aluminium windows and doors made on the same floor.

What we build

  • Stairs, wardrobes, and storage
  • Bathrooms, laundries, and specialty units
  • Timber & aluminium joinery
  • Windows & doors
  • Commercial joinery and fit-outs
