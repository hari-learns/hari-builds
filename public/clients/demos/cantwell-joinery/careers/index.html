We are looking for an experienced aluminium fabricator to join our manufacturing team.

Experienced aluminium fabricator

Experienced aluminium fabricator

This is a hands-on role for a skilled tradesperson who takes pride in producing aluminium windows and doors in both the residential and light commercial markets. The successful applicant must have a minimum of 8 years’ experience in an Altus or APL aluminium factory and be confident working with a wide range of extrusion, hardware and components.

If you can

  • Fabricate aluminium windows and doors
  • Understand how to measure and fabricate sashes for retrofits
  • Install aluminium joinery on site
  • Read plans and ensure accurate, high-quality builds that go out correct and compliant, the first time
  • Problem-solve and make sure jobs are completed to a high standard
  • Demonstrate attention to detail, work flow and take pride in your work
  • Work independently and as part of a team
  • Pass D&A testing every time
  • Hold a full NZ driver’s licence
  • LocationBlenheim, Marlborough
  • Job typeFull time, wages or contract options
  • BenefitsTool allowance, 1pm finish on a Friday

We can offer

  • Wages or contract options
  • Competitive rates based on experience
  • Full-time work
  • Variety of projects, and a variety of work within our diverse factory: we manufacture custom joinery and kitchens in our timber department
  • Long-term opportunity with an established business
