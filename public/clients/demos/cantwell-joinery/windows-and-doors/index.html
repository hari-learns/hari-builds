How to measure a window or door

How to measure a window or door

To be able to give you an accurate price, we need some help from you: some information about the doors and windows, where they will be going and what they might look like.

  1. 1

    Draw it

    It is helpful to indicate how many sections and opening sashes you would like so the quote is more accurate. An awning is hung from the top; a casement is hung from the side and can open left or right.

  2. 2

    Name it and measure it

    Label your window or door with a name so they can easily be identified later. Then measure the distance horizontally, then vertically, then the sections (if applicable).

  3. 3

    Over the whole frame

    Ideally measurements are taken over all the frame. If an architrave covers it, go with the bigger measurement, so you know the price won’t be any more expensive when it comes time to do a full site measure.
