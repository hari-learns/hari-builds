About us

A Marlborough family business since 1973

Since 1973, Cantwell Joinery has been part of Marlborough’s story, hand-building kitchens, windows, doors, and bespoke joinery that stand the test of time.

Where craft meets care

A family business at heart

We were taught that what you build matters but how you build it matters even more.

That’s why everything we design, manufacture, and install is crafted locally by a team of highly skilled joiners and aluminium specialists who take pride in every detail.

From kitchens to wardrobes, windows to stairs, we don’t believe in one-size-fits-all. We build joinery that fits you.
