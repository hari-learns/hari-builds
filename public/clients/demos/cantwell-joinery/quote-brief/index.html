Measuring tips from our guide: kitchens

  • If you have house plans you could include those rather than re-measuring.
  • The measurements do not have to be exact, as we would always do a final site measure ourselves before placing the job into production.
  • Draw or list the whole space available, not just where the current kitchen is.
  • When measuring windows or doors include the architraves, as cabinets don’t cover these.
  • Please write your dimensions in cm or mm, not inches and feet.
  • It is useful to understand the area surrounding the kitchen, so include some photos when you email.

Measuring tips from our guide: windows and doors

  • Ideally measurements are taken over all the frame.
  • If an architrave covers the frame, go with the bigger measurement, so you know the price won’t be any more expensive when it comes time to do a full site measure.
  • Measure the distance horizontally, then vertically, then the sections (if applicable).
  • An awning is hung from the top; a casement is hung from the side and can open left or right.
