Our History

Interior Design That’s Timeless

Esteban Interiors is a commercial and residential San Diego interior design firm focused on creating beautiful, functional spaces for a modern lifestyle. We understand that every project and client is different — which is why we get involved at the architectural level and always work collaboratively with our clients to incorporate their individual needs and develop their personal style. With a long history in interior design, we can help you create a space that is truly unique, timeless, and of course, exudes quality.

Leadership

Esteban Lopez

President of Design, CCO

Esteban balances creativity and experience and is a well-respected “Style Maker” within the design community. He continues to bring a fresh perspective to the San Diego landscape that is distinct from other firms in the area. His bespoke and cosmopolitan design style draws high-end clients to his work around the country. Esteban's role is to ensure every detail is thoughtfully considered to achieve the client's goals and expose them to new ways of envisioning their home.

Carl Pallini

Designer

Carl earned a BFA in Fashion Design and Retail Merchandising from Columbus College of Art & Design. He has 18 years of experience working in the Residential Interior Design field. Carl adheres to the philosophy that your surroundings significantly impact your well-being. His design challenge is to create a completely new environment or integrate new pieces into an existing decor. Carl can create an atmosphere with a unique style that reflects the owner's individuality. He believes that a person's distinct story should be reflected in their home.
