Full-Service Interior Design

Crafted to Inspire

Esteban Interiors specializes in full-service commercial and residential interior design that is unique, functional, and timeless. By taking the time to listen to clients’ needs and preferences, our top interior designers are able to create cohesive and personalized homes and businesses that engage and inspire. We guide clients through every step to make the process as enjoyable, stress-free, and hands-off for them as possible.

Initial Consultation

At the start of each project, our interior designers will hold an initial consultation with you to discuss your specific needs and preferences. This step is crucial in determining space planning, lighting design and layout, color palette direction, styling, and even custom furniture design.

Design Proposal

After the initial consultation, our interior designers will draft a design proposal and design contract for you to review. The complete design concept can include photo-realistic renderings, design sheet sets, a paint color palette or samples, lighting suggestions, fabric samples, and furniture suggestions. Additional meetings or shopping trips may be required to create a space that best reflects your personality and style.

Budget & Plan Finalization

Our interior designers will prepare a preliminary budget tailored to meet your specific financial goals. Once approved, a finalized plan will be presented, and our designers will work with you to determine time frames. The finalized plan will include color palette direction, window treatments, wall coverings, furniture design, and styling.

Procurement & Implementation

After plans have been finalized and agreed upon, our team will move forward with securing contractors and procuring product and art. Our designers will then implement the design plan to create your unique dream space. We’ll take care of everything to make the process as easy as possible for you.

Design Integrity & Communication

In order to ensure a smooth, stress-free process for you, we’ll continuously follow up with vendors and contractors to maintain design integrity and project deadlines. We will also maintain clear and frequent communication with you to ensure that your expectations are being met.
