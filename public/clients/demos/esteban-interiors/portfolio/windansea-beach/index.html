Interior Design Portfolio

Windansea Beach

  • Location: La Jolla, San Diego
  • Design style: Modern
  • Category: Residential
  • 11 photos

The historically designated home was purchased by our client in Windansea Beach. The brief was to design a home that reflects modern living, with a timeless sensibility, all while combining the client’s personal style. Our biggest achievement was decompartmentalization of the home’s existing flow, to create a home that flows in unison with the beautiful courtyard enclave we created. The home lives very large and open and lends itself to ample entertaining with the cleverly designed wine vault. The added “Casita” or what we labeled as the recreation room which features a linear bar, shuffleboard, and ample seating as a counterbalance to the courtyard.

Unique features

Custom entryway stone flooring, wine vault, large-format steel pocket doors, custom splash pool with waterfall feature, and custom built-ins throughout.

“I have worked with Esteban and James for a variety of interior design projects and installations. I am pleased to say the quality of work from their firm was exemplary. Their creative ideas were inspiring and helped bring my home to a new level that I had not anticipated. In addition, their attention to detail with regard to quality of work, timeliness, and managing expenses within budget were not only on target, but have far exceeded my expectations.”

Brian Ahern
