Casa de Manana

2 photos

Esteban Interiors undertook the reimagining of a home, transforming it into an ultra-modern retreat that reflected the vibrancy and legacy of the clients’ lives. Inspired by European Modernism, our designers incorporated sleek lines, metallic touches, and pops of red and orange that would stand out against the cool grays used throughout the house. Unexpected elements were also incorporated, such as a wood-carved Aztec calendar mounted on the wall and clear tables with a whimsical, Moroccan-inspired design. The result was a blend of modern and eclectic elements that created a sense of Hollywood glam with cosmopolitan chic.

Unique features

Roche Bobois’ Bowling Chandelier, Ligne Roset’s cherry red Ploum Sofa, and a tech lighting monorail system from Urban Lighting.
