J. Public Relations – New York

  • Location: Manhattan, New York, NY
  • Design style: Modern Eclectic
  • Category: Commercial
  • 5 photos

With a brand identity already established, Esteban Interiors needed to create a design that would reinforce the look and feel of J. Public Relations. With a focus on rose gold complemented by accents of pink and gold, our team created a compelling and dynamic communal workspace that has the nuances and flexibility for the modern workforce.

Unique features

Established a clear and focused brand identity within a commercial high-rise for a well-established PR firm.

“Esteban Interiors has been successful in all aspects of the design process and has far exceeded our expectations in creativity, communication, and execution. From the beginning, Esteban and James are professional and always show attention to detail with regard to design, budget, and the follow-through process.”

Jamie Lynn Sigler
