Our Aesthetic

Of Origin was founded on the idea of truth to materials.

Whenever possible, we focus on raw, organic pieces that point to where they came from. Maybe it's a live-edge custom wood table, elements in concrete, worn leather, wool, or hot-rolled steel, or that perfect velvet chair you’ll sink into at the end of every day.

Always, our mission is to create spaces for life and work that feel authentic, deeply grounded, and inspired.
