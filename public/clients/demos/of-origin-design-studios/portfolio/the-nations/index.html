THE NATIONS

This interior design project in The Nations was a light renovation and furnishings package for newly married clients looking to upgrade their home to fit their lifestyle as young professionals. Of Origin Design Studios created a moody, layered primary bedroom and an elevated living space designed for both everyday life and entertaining. It's a good representation of our approach to interior design in Nashville's newer neighborhoods: distinct, personal spaces that still feel timeless, built around where our clients are in life right now.
