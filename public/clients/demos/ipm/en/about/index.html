IPM about us

The IPM company was founded in 1992 as a partner of renowned European machine manufacturers and suppliers of the latest technologies for processing plastics, rubber and silicone. IPM provides innovative technologies and machines for extrusion, compounding and recycling. Usually, the supply of these machines and complete plants is associated with technology transfer, because in innovative projects the dominant element is the use of modern and unique processing method. The highest quality of machines is a necessary element but not sufficient to obtain innovative products. It is necessary to have a constantly developed special innovative technologies for specific processes.

BG PLAST belongs to the world leaders in the production of lines for the extrusion of plastic films and plastic sheets. In addition to the extrusion lines, the company also produces coating and laminating lines for the production of a wide range of laminates, which are increasingly used in various sectors. The company was founded in 1987 and has extensive experience thanks to the implementation of numerous special projects around the world. www.bgplast.it

Represented companies

The companies represented by IPM are the result of careful selection and long cooperation. We demand the highest technical, commercial and ethical qualifications from the companies we represent. Each of these companies is not only one of the world leaders in their field in technical and technological terms, but is also known for using the highest standards of customer service, where the basis of relationships is full professionalism and high trust.

BG PLAST

BG PLAST belongs to the world leaders in the production of lines for the extrusion of plastic films and plastic sheets. In addition to the extrusion lines, the company also produces coating and laminating lines for the production of a wide range of laminates, which are increasingly used in various sectors. The company was founded in 1987 and has extensive experience thanks to the implementation of numerous special projects around the world. www.bgplast.it

COLMEC SPA

During 45 years of activity, COLMEC has gained a leading position in the world market in the construction of rubber and silicone compounding and extrusion lines. The company specializes in complete lines from the production of profiles for the automotive and construction industries, to industrial high and low pressure hoses, fuel hoses, cooling and turbocharging TCI hoses, hoses for air conditioning, hoses for steering and braking systems and unique lines for rubber compounding and silicone. www.colmec.it

COMERIO ERCOLE SPA

A world leader, producing rubber calendering lines as well as machines and equipment for producing rubber compounds. The first rubber mill was constructed by COMERIO ERCOLE in 1894, the first internal mixer in 1900 and the first rubber calender in 1919. Over 100 years of continuous development in the calendering industry, over 1.000 implementations around the world, 14 international patents and a team of specialists always available to their clients, confirming that the company is always synonymous with quality and technology guarantees. A family business founded in 1885. www.comercole.it

FRIUL FILIERE SPA

The company is one of the leaders in the field of extrusion and decoration of thermoplastics. This is one of the few companies in the world supplying complete projects for extruding plastic profiles and pipes and dealing with the implementation of complete turnkey production lines for the production of special profiles. A family business founded in 1977. www.friulfiliere.it

GAMMA MECCANICA SPA

The company is one of the world leaders in the production of machinery and equipment for recycling plastics. Each regranulation line is designed and tailored to the individual needs of the customer, so as to ensure the best performance and quality of granules while minimizing energy consumption. GAMMA MECCANICA also designs and manufactures machines and equipment for the production of mineral wool and XPS panels. The company was founded in 1977. www.gamma-meccanica.it/

FRATELLI MARIS SPA

The company belongs to the world leaders in the field of production of complete lines for compounding plastics and rubber. The family company was founded in 1962 and from the very beginning specialized in the production of twin screw extruders, which are the heart of the compounding line. Currently, the company produces a wide range of co-rotating twin screw extruders. The company, along with a complete installation for compounding, also provides technology developed specifically for specific recipes, which guarantees the achievement of the highest quality of produced compounds. MARIS machines are also widely used in the recycling of plastics and rubber and in the so-called re-compounding process, i.e. a technology that is a combination of regranulation and compounding. www.mariscorp.com

PLAXTECH SRL

The owner of the ROTEAX brand - the most innovative and unique technology for the production of transport pallets from unsorted (mixed) polyolefin waste (PE, HDPE, LDPE, LLDPE, PP, etc.). A family business, founded in 1997.

PREVIERO

PREVIERO is one of the world's leading producers of equipment for crushing, shreddinh, grinding, agglomeration and pulverization of plastics. The company specializes in devices used in plastic recycling lines under the SOREMA brand, thanks to which these lines can work with high efficiency and in a completely automatic way. A family company founded in 1922. www.previero.it

PREVIERO RUBBER SRL

The PREVIERO RUBBER company is a branch of the PREVIERO company and is one of the world's leading producers offering equipment for grinding and granulating machines for the rubber industry. The company specializes in complete installations enabling the grinding and grinding of natural and synthetic rubber so that their transport, weighing and dosing can take place in a fully automatic way. www.rubber.previero.it

FRATELLI SACCHI S.N.C.

SACCHI was established in 1926 and specializes in complete systems for transporting, dosing, storing, packaging and palletizing bulk materials. The company has a wide range of patented solutions for robotic and semi-automatic systems that have been developed specifically for use in the plastics and rubber industry. www.cmsacchi.com

SOREMA SRL

SOREMA is one of the world leaders in building recycling lines. The family business, founded in 1922, has been managed by the Previero family for 4 generations. Designs and implements personalized recycling solutions based on the properties of the starting material, customer requirements and end-use applications, optimizing investments and costs. The goal of SOREMA is not only to sell lines or individual machines, but also to provide its clients with specific technical solutions in accordance with environmental conditions, information about the process and economic needs. www.sorema.it
