Who We Are

About Hakeem Glass

Established on 10 September 2010 in the Salmabad Industrial Area, Kingdom of Bahrain, Hakeem Glass & Mirror & Aluminium W.L.L. has grown into one of Bahrain's trusted names in architectural glazing and aluminium contracting. Led by Managing Director Abdul Hakeem Abu Hajeer, who brings over 35 years of hands-on experience in the glass and aluminium industry, the company combines technical expertise with a client-first approach.

Our skilled and experienced team is committed to delivering high-quality, innovative glazing solutions on time and on budget, from intricate residential glasswork to large-scale commercial and hospitality projects across the Kingdom.

  • In-house measuring, fabrication & installation
  • Tempered safety glass & certified aluminium systems on every project
  • From boutique villas to hotel towers and national landmarks
  • Trusted by leading contractors and developers across Bahrain
