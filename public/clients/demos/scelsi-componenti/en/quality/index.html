Quality and certifications

Quality control: every measurement confirmed.

Thanks to our experience and professionalism in precision turning and milling, we can machine a wide range of materials: stainless steel, iron, plastic, cast iron, copper, brass, bronze and castings of various sizes.

Our machining, small-medium-large series and high-precision samples, is the result of careful work by highly qualified staff. Through collaboration with reliable companies in the field of treatments and special processes we can guarantee the finished product. We provide certification on the materials and a finished product sheet.

We offer

  • Material certificateChemical composition and mechanical properties, according to standard EN 10204:2004.
  • Measurement certificateTo confirm the measurements of machined parts with certainty: issued directly by the measuring machines.
  • Certificate of conformityGuarantees that the entire production process was carried out in accordance with ISO 9001:2015.
