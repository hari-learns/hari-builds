Materials

To each project its own material.

We offer tailored machining solutions for a wide range of materials. Thanks to the experience and high technology of our milling and turning departments, we can carry out precise and customised machining on steels, special alloys, aluminium, plastics and more.

Whether it is complex machining on hard metals or high-precision operations on lighter materials, our team of experts ensures quality results, meeting the specific needs of each customer.

Steel alloys are specially formulated blends of iron and other elements designed to provide increased strength and durability. These alloys offer superior resistance to wear, corrosion and heat, making them ideal for precision parts and components that require high performance and reliability.
