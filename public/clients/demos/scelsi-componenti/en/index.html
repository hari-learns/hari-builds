The complete service

From drawing to finished part, with one point of contact.

Scelsi Componenti gives you a complete service: from the analysis of the drawing to the accompanying certificates.

  1. 01

    Feasibility study and quotation

    We analyse the drawing and answer with a proposal.

  2. 02

    Procurement of raw or semi-finished material

    Bars, plates, tubes: the right material, already certified.

  3. 03

    Machining on horizontal machining centres

    CNC milling for flat, contoured or complex surfaces.

  4. 04

    Machining on vertical machining centres

    Fifteen milling machines, with strokes from 500 to 2000 mm.

  5. 05

    Machining on CNC lathes

    With driven tools and Y axis, some with a bar feeder.

  6. 06

    Heat treatments

    Hardening, stress relieving, tempering and others.

  7. 07

    Surface treatments

    Anodizing, electroplating, painting, nickel plating and others.

  8. 08

    Certifications

    Material, measurement and conformity certificates.

Quality

Every step checked, from raw material to finished part.

Strict control plans guarantee the quality of every single step, from the choice of raw material to the semi-finished material to the finished part. That is why the company is DIN EN ISO 9001:2015 certified.

  • Material certificateChemical composition and mechanical properties, according to standard EN 10204:2004.
  • Measurement certificateTo confirm the measurements of machined parts with certainty: issued directly by the measuring machines.
  • Certificate of conformityGuarantees that the entire production process was carried out in accordance with ISO 9001:2015.

A family workshop

The address changes, the mindset does not.
