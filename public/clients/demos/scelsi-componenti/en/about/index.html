About us

Constant commitment has consolidated our success.

Scelsi Componenti S.r.l., founded in 1987, has grown through continuous investment in high-precision CNC technology and machinery.

Who we are today

Our experience and professionalism in precision turning and milling allow us to machine any kind of material, from stainless steel to plastic, and have meant that our clients are national and multinational companies.

Strict control plans guarantee the quality of every single step, from the choice of raw material to the semi-finished material to the finished part. That is why the company is DIN EN ISO 9001:2015 certified.

Our contract production of small-medium series and high-precision samples requires accurate machining with CNC machining centres and lathes.

Our story

  1. 1987

    The farmhouse

    It was 1987 when Nicola Scelsi and his wife Tiziana Brambilla decided to take on a new adventure by opening a small machine shop inside a farmhouse.

  2. The years after

    Growth

    With passion, determination and tenacity they grew the business, offering precision turning and milling services. The results soon began to show and, within a few years, the workshop received a growing number of requests, establishing itself as a reliable name in the sector.

  3. Then

    The address changes, the mindset does not

    The company grows and, meanwhile, the three children Serena, Claudio and Gabriele grow too, gradually starting to work alongside mum and dad. Their arrival brings new energy and new ideas.

  4. Today

    A company with great goals

    Keeping up a constant commitment and a mindset built on fairness, respect and transparency in every collaboration, Scelsi Componenti is a workshop that has turned from a family-run business into a company with strong capabilities, able to meet market needs.

The heart of our company is not just the machines, but the people who make them work at their best.

Our philosophy

  • Precision and quality

    We guarantee accurate machining in both plastic and metal materials, adhering to strict technical standards and controls to deliver finished work that fully meets the specifications required by the customer.

  • Professionalism

    Technical expertise, precision in machining, on-time delivery and reliability in every job.

  • Customer focus

    We listen to needs, offer customised solutions, ensure quality, punctuality and support during every stage of the job order.
