010.5 g to 4 kgsingle pieces

Pressure die casting

The pressure die casting process has evolved to permit design of products to reduce metal content, lessening weight and cost. Through this process, molten metal is injected into a steel mold or die with a die casting machine accompanied with pressure.

We have set a new standard of quality in the pressure die casting process, through our own understanding, knowledge and perfection in this specific process. Today, we are considered as one of the best manufacturers and suppliers of pressure die casting of single pieces weighing from 0.5 g to 4 kg.

0220 yearsof gravity die casting

Gravity die casting

If you want to go for mass production and that also for fully mechanized casting, then gravity die casting is a good casting process. For producing smooth and textured, well defined, accurately dimensioned metal parts, the gravity die casting method is also a very good process.

We have 20 years of experience in extracting quality gravity die casting components for mass production and for fully mechanized casting.

03up to 1 tonnesingle piece

Sand casting

For hundreds of years, among all other castings, sand casting continues to be one of the most popular casting methods. Even today, it has a great significance in the area of large metal forms production.

We are manufacturing quality sand castings up to 1 tonne single piece using our processing facilities. With a team of talented professionals we have been capable to merge quality with customization.

04all primaryzinc casting alloys

Zinc casting

Kevin Metal Cast is a well established manufacturer and exporter of high precision zinc castings. The zinc castings offered are best for high volume, net-shaped, tight tolerance metal component manufacturing.

Zinc, being the easiest alloy to cast, offers high ductility and good impact strength. It can also be easily plated, making zinc castings economical for small parts like building hardware, buttons and luggage fittings, electrical switchgear parts and decorative artware. Making the best utilization of our facility we cast all the primary zinc alloys used in casting.

05CNCvertical machining centre

Precision machined components

Our company specializes in manufacturing precision machined components and executes custom orders. The CNC machining center backs us to manufacture components using various grades of aluminium and zinc. These machined components feature extremely close tolerance, smooth finish and dimensional stability.

We are equipped with a CNC vertical machining center and a turning center, along with facility for milling and lathe machines. This enables us to meet the specifications of the clients with greater accuracy.
