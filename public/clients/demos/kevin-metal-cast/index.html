About

Own foundries, guided by a metallurgical engineer.

The company operates under the guidance of one of its partners, Shri D.C. Firake, a metallurgical engineer with over 35 years of experience in non-ferrous sand castings and pressure and gravity die castings.

At Kevin Metal Cast we have our own foundries along with state-of-the-art sand casting, pressure die casting and gravity die casting machinery.
