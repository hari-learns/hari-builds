About

Own foundries, guided by a metallurgical engineer.

Kevin Metal Cast is a manufacturer of aluminium pressure and gravity die castings and non-ferrous sand castings.

We have adequate technical expertise to provide the industry with excellent castings according to the client's specifications.

The company operates under the guidance of one of its partners, Shri D.C. Firake, a metallurgical engineer with over 35 years of experience in non-ferrous sand castings and pressure and gravity die castings.

At Kevin Metal Cast we have our own foundries along with state-of-the-art sand casting, pressure die casting and gravity die casting machinery.

With our expertise and in-time delivery of finished products, we have hundreds of satisfied industrial clients to whom we have delivered thousands of tonnes of aluminium die-cast products, including export-oriented job work.
