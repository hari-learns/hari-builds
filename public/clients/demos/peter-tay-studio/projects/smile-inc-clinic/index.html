Smile Inc. Clinic

2009 · Commercial & public · 3 photos

The project

Smile Inc. is a boutique dental practice located within a shopping mall in downtown Singapore. The conceptualization of a patient-friendly setup was to allay any fears a patient has of visiting the dentist.  The raised platform and the use of mirrors and high-gloss stainless steel at the entrance heighten the experience of the client as he/she enters the clinic and walks toward the reception and the surgery room.
