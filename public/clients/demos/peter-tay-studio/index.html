About Peter Tay

Best known for his sleek yet luxurious approach to design, Peter Tay's designs are contemporary and timeless.

  • 2001Peter Tay starts his own practice after the Architectural Association, London (2000)
  • 2014Designer of the Year, President's Design Award Singapore
  • 2021Lifetime Achievement Award, Society of Interior Designers Singapore (SIDS)
  • 2026Asia Outstanding Design Personality Award, Asian Biennial Award

Selected Projects

All selected projects (23)
01

Good Class Bungalow at Swettenham Road (2024)

The spiral staircase is the key feature of this design, serving not just as a piece of art but as a vital connection between spaces, guiding one through the journey of this bungalow house.

02

House at Sixth Avenue (2020)

Surrounded by lush greenery and the sounds of nature, this house is designed as a modern tropical sanctuary where the edges between the interior and exterior spaces are neatly defined and are detailed in a way to allow the two zones to flow seamlessly into each other.

Read more
03

Penthouse at Ocean Drive, Sentosa (2015)

Situated along Ocean Drive at Sentosa Cove, this penthouse at one of Sentosa island's premium condominium features beautiful panoramic views of both the South China Sea as well as the marina at Sentosa Cove.

Read more
04

House at Holland Park (2010)

Not only is the entrance courtyard the central axis of the house, it also serves to organize the house both horizontally and vertically.  The play of textures, reflection and transparency provides sensorial experience to the space.  The use of glass separates the different areas physically, as well as provides visual continuity throughout the space.

Read more
05

House at Belmont Park (2019)

The architectural expressions of the old and the new, the classic and the modern are mediated by the glass bridge, which serves to connect and separate the two parts of the house.

House at Sentosa Cove (2012)

Located on Sentosa Cove, this house is situated barely 30 metres from the shoreline.
