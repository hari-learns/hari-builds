Design your space

Upload a photo of your own room, choose a mood and say what you want. In about 30 seconds you see your room redesigned, then send the before, the after and your wishes to Peter Tay Studio.

  1. 01Upload a photo of your room.
  2. 02Check the room type, found for you.
  3. 03Choose a style and write what you want.
  4. 04Send the before and after to the studio.

A first impression made by an image model, not a design. The studio replies by email.

What photo works best?

A wide photo taken in daylight from a corner of the room, with the windows in view. Plans and sketches work too.

Is this the studio's design?

No. It is a quick impression in a chosen mood, so the studio can see your room and what you want before a first conversation.

What happens after I send it?

Your before and after and your wishes reach projects@petertaystudio.com, and the studio replies by email.
