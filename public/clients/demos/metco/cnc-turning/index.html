CNC turning: precision components, from prototypes to full production runs

Using state-of-the-art CNC lathes, Metco’s skilled machinists produce precision components to your exact specifications.

Driven by integrated CAD/CAM software, the 3 and 4-axis CNC lathe capabilities are almost endless. The machines are equipped with fully automated load/unload systems, allowing them to run more efficiently and reducing the cost of parts.

Metco can run anything from prototype work to full production runs. A current vacancy notice refers to a brand new suite of automated DMG MORI NLX 2500 and DMU 50 machines “allowing true lights out production”.
