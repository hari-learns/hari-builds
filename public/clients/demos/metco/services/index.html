Precision engineering, sheet metal fabrication and contract manufacturing

Metco offers all its capabilities as individual services, including sheet metal services, but its specialty is end-to-end design and engineering, adding value from initial consultation and concept design through to finished products. Its Wellington-based team offers full engineering and sheet metal services including delivery to Auckland, Christchurch and across New Zealand.

  • Free consultation

    Share your ideas with Metco’s designers and engineers. Bounce your thoughts off the team and make the most of its experience to look at possibilities for your component or product.

  • Concept development

    Using 3D CAD/CAM software, ideas are converted into defined form. You can review and alter the details until your vision is fulfilled.

  • Prototype creation

    From digital concept to a physical prototype you can hold in your hands, modified or improved until you are ready for manufacture.

  • Onsite collaboration

    Your own fully-serviced office space at Metco, for on-the-spot communication, experimentation and issue resolution.

  • Made to your specifications

    If you know exactly what you want, Metco manufactures it in whatever quantities you require, whenever you need it.

  • Full end-to-end service

    Project managed from start to finish: Metco takes care of the design and manufacturing issues.

Incorporating safety in design and manufacture

Metco understands the obligations on designers and manufacturers of plant and structures to ensure every part is safe for use in the workplace. Its proactive approach to health and safety is meant to give clients comfort that they also meet their obligations under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015.

Quality assurance

Ongoing quality control standards have been a key pillar of Metco’s success. It says it is currently adapting and standardising its QA system toward ISO 9000 accreditation.
