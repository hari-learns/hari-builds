CNC milling: market leading machining

With cutting-edge machining centre technology, Metco’s engineers manufacture products or components to your precise specifications, even to consumer-grade finish if required, economically.

Driven by integrated CAD/CAM software, the 5-axis mill produces highly complex 2D or 3D components that would be awkward or impossible with less advanced machines.

Ultra-precision is assured by an integral rotary/tilt table (providing 30% more rigidity), thermal displacement compensation and double-contact tool holding. All components are Renishaw-probe-monitored and rejected if 6 demanding criteria are not met.

Powerful production capability means bigger volumes, shorter lead times and lower costs. A high speed 42-position automatic tool changer and just 2 setups per item (compared to 6 commonly required in the industry) save operator intervention time and give sharper dimensional referencing.

Metco also designs and manufactures special jigs and fixtures for holding raw materials. Just about any material is workable, including stainless steel, steel alloys, aluminium, brass and plastics.
