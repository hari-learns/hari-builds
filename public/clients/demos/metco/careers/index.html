Jobs at Metco

Metco Engineering 2002 Limited is a privately owned company based in Lower Hutt. Owners Paul Jessup and Brent Greer began their business partnership in 2002, and since then Metco has grown from employing six staff to a multi-million dollar business employing over fifty.

Listed on the Metco site

  • Robot Welder Operator

    The welding department is expanding with 2 additional robot weld machines. Suits someone with previous CNC experience, MIG welding experience, robot experience or a genuine desire to learn.

  • CNC Machinist / Programmer

    The CNC machine shop is expanding with 2 further 4-axis DMG MORI mill turns on order. Ideally 3+ years’ experience on CNC mill turn lathes; GibbsCAM experience is an advantage.

  • Toolmaker

    Supporting manufacturing and maintenance of tooling for the busy press shop, plus engineering support to the fabrication team. Ideally 2+ years in toolmaking.

To have your details on record, or to ask questions, email recruitment@metco.co.nz or call +64 274262419, as listed on Metco’s vacancies page.
