Eight steps to a great product

This is the design process Metco goes through when working with you. From your initial consultation through to delivery of your finished product or component, the process is meant to ensure the product meets or exceeds your expectations.

  1. 01

    Meet

    Metco meets to discuss your needs, usually in person at its premises in Lower Hutt. If you are in the Wellington area, the team can go off site to meet you. If you are outside Wellington, a video call works: there is video conferencing in the boardroom.

  2. 02

    Imagine

    Tell Metco about your product or component requirements. How does it need to look or function, or be presented and packaged? How many do you need produced? What’s your budget? “You don’t need to have all the answers or be the expert here. Tell us the parameters you’re working with and we’ll work out the rest.”

  3. 03

    Design

    Several product options are likely to be developed during the design stage, to address manufacturing or functional challenges, or to keep costs within your budget. Metco discusses the options and the reasons for them so you can make an informed decision.

  4. 04

    Sign off best solution

    You see your design come to life in 2D or 3D. Final refinements can be made before manufacturing begins. The design is approved when you are 100% satisfied your requirements have been met.

  5. 05

    Create a prototype

    One prototype is created and thoroughly checked before production begins.

  6. 06

    Test and evaluate

    Metco carries out extensive testing to make sure your product meets all requirements, and says it is in the process of getting ISO 9001 accreditation.

  7. 07

    Manufacture

    Components or products are manufactured in-house by specialist teams, including premium powder coating finishes if required.

  8. 08

    Deliver

    Assembly, packaging and transport are managed in-house, anywhere within New Zealand or the world, including the design of pallets, crates and boxes.

Source: metco.co.nz/our-process/
