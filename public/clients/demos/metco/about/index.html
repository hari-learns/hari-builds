A 100% Kiwi company, with an international reputation

From Antarctica to China and across New Zealand, Metco provides a complete five-star engineering and manufacturing service.

Metco says it is the only Wellington engineering manufacturing company to offer a full end-to-end solution from initial consultation and concept design, through to finished products. Everything is managed in-house, from raw materials, prototype design and sheet metal manufacturing through to the finished product.

“We have a great ‘co-makership’ relationship with Metco that has become part of our process to deliver cost-effective solutions for our clients.”

Pertronic Industries

Supplying over 600 clients

Metco supplies over 600 clients with engineering services ranging from one-off commissions to contract runs of tens of thousands of units. A portion of its production is destined for international markets.

Product range

Stainless steel, steel alloys, aluminium, brass and plastics, including window stays; sheet metal components for office furniture, shelving and storage; and components for lighting, foam cushioning, fire engines, fire safety, data centres, defence and transport servicing, and other end uses.
