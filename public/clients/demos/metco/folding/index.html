Folding: an extensive range of folds, small to large runs

At the forefront of sheet metal bending and forming, Metco offers quality precision bending using the latest technology.

Using the latest sheet metal folding and forming methods, expert operators can produce items to precise dimensions. With an extensive library of tools, Metco can perform a large range of folds.

The Salvagnini automated folding cell can fold a large variety of items with an enormous reduction in setup and production times, saving unit costs. It also minimises the human handling normally required on standard press brakes, making it suitable for both small and large production volumes.

Metco’s press brakes complement its advanced laser cutting and turret punching, providing a versatile end-to-end sheet metal production solution.
