Design and engineering by Metco’s Wellington team

Let us take your ideas for product creation, modification, or improvement, and turn them into viable cost-effective designs.

With state-of-the-art 2D and 3D CAD software, Metco can bring your ideas to life in a digital prototype. From there, functionality or aesthetics can be adjusted easily, getting it right before manufacturing and custom sheet metal fabrication starts.

Using its design, engineering and manufacturing expertise, the team will find the smartest means possible to achieve your goals, in ways you may not have thought of, and consult with you right through the design and engineering process, resolving any potential manufacturing issues.
