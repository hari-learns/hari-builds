Powder coating: an almost endless supply of colours and finishes

Metco’s state-of-the-art paint line produces a uniform, high-quality, durable and attractive finish that is highly resistant to scratches, cracking, peeling, UV and rust.

Powder coating capabilities

  • Product up to 24000 mm length, 1500 mm height and 800 mm width
  • Almost endless supply of colours and decorative finishes using Dulux powders
  • Colour matching for high volume runs
  • Nordson booth and Nordson Encore XT spray system
  • 70-metre conveyor system setup
