Welding and fabrication: you dream it, we make it

Complementing laser cutting, turret punching and folding, Metco provides high-end fabrication of components and products.

Advanced stainless steel sheet metal fabrication facilities and skilled staff give Metco the versatility to do a wide range of projects to a premium standard for clients in Wellington, Auckland and across New Zealand.

Fabricating capabilities include

  • Robot welder
  • Mig, Tig and spot welding
  • Nut and stud insertion
  • Tube cutting
  • Grinding
  • Drilling, countersinking and tapping
  • Sanding, rumbling and buffing
  • Rolling
  • Shearing (max. width 2.4 m)
