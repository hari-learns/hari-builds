Precision laser cutting, competitive pricing

With state-of-the-art laser cutting machines, a high-capacity storage tower and automatic material loading, Metco cuts complex-profile components with ultra-precision at record throughput.

The automated 24/7 unmanned production system can process about 100 tonnes of material over a weekend, using a Trumpf TruStore 3030 facility holding up to 51 pallets of 3 tonnes each, coupled with a Lift Master Compact that moves sheets between the storage tower and twin lasers.

Metco says normal industry costs can be cut by 60% by the reduced setup time of 22 seconds per sheet (versus 1.5 minutes without automation) and the 85 to 90% time-utilisation of its lasers.

The Trumpf TruLaser 3030 offers high flexibility, including processing of non-ferrous metals. Its one-cutting-head strategy and automatic nozzle changer raise efficiency with minimised downtime. A Trumpf TruLaser 2525 works in tandem with the 3030 to double production capacity.

Metco can design, manufacture and ship laser cut blanks, components or finished products for clients in Wellington, Auckland, Christchurch and throughout New Zealand and overseas.
