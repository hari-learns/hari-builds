The people behind the quote

Metco’s key people, from Metco’s own profiles. Directors are hands-on with customers: “Being available to customers and involved in their product solutions is rare personal service from a company’s director.”

  • Paul Jessup

    Director and CEO

    Responsible for purchase ordering, quoting and company growth, and just as likely to be involved with customers one-to-one in design and R&D. Walks through the departments three to four times a week checking the quality of work going out the door.

  • Brent Greer

    Director and Production Manager

    In charge of production and scheduling. When a customer’s own design raises questions, you will hear from Brent: often there is a way to produce it a little differently, simpler and more cost-effective. He also keeps customers informed with a due date.

  • Jayden Jessup

    Manager of CNC

    Responsible for the CNC shop. Plans and schedules tool making projects, individually quality checks the wide range of parts the CNC shop produces before mass production begins, and generates drawings for products. Apprenticed at Metco and full time since 2008.

  • Peter Rhoades

    Production Manager

    With Metco since 2010; responsible for production of light press assembly and other press components, plus dispatching, material forecasting, supply chain and product development.

  • Lloyd Still

    Sales and New Business

    A trade qualified fitter and turner with more than 30 years of manufacturing management experience. Researches new markets and explains how Metco’s capabilities can better meet a customer’s manufacturing needs.

  • Tanya Williams

    Accounts and Customer Service

    Metco’s longest serving office staff member, with the company since 2007: accounts, orders, invoicing and customer service, and the primary go-to person for staff and often for customers.

Source: Metco: Our People
