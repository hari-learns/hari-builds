FF&E and Procurement

Creating a comfortable and coherent roomscape can be harder to achieve than some might imagine. With lara et al’s combined years of experience, we have an exceptional eye for detail and can source the most unique and exciting furniture, lighting, artwork and accessories for their clients, ensuring the space is comfortable, textured and highly personalised.

We offer a full ff&e or turn-key solution that ensures peace of mind to our clients that we will make all purchases on their behalf managing any supply, delay and availability issues. We will then carefully coordinate storage, delivery and installation, all within the time and budget expectations for the project.
