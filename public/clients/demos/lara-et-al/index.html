The studio

UK based Interior Design studio lara et al have been creating warm, welcoming and beautiful homes since 2017

From the initial concept idea, through the design process, then final installation and dressing, Lara and her team’s attention to detail is un-faltering. This specialised comprehensive service results in timeless interiors that are highly personal to each one of their clients.

Considerable time, thought and consideration are given to each one of their client’s requests, creating truly unique homes built around them.

Services

From the first idea to the final dressing

01

Interior Architecture & Design

With our experienced and trained studio team, lara et al is well placed to re-imagine existing layouts or re-design outdated interior details. We understand the importance of light, volume of space and flow to create intelligent solutions for how to use interior spaces.

More on Interior Architecture
02

FF&E and Procurement

Creating a comfortable and coherent roomscape can be harder to achieve than some might imagine. With lara et al’s combined years of experience, we have an exceptional eye for detail and can source the most unique and exciting furniture, lighting, artwork and accessories for their clients, ensuring the space is comfortable, textured and highly personalised.

More on FF&E and Procurement
03

Bespoke Furniture & Joinery Design

Often, the best and only option is bespoke. Whether you have a challenging space or simply the desire for something totally unique, specially crafted joinery and beautifully designed furniture can not only solve clients problems but elevate a room to the next level.

More on Bespoke Furniture & Joinery
04

Concept & Developed Design

By far the most exciting part of our work is to develop and create the blue print for your dream home! We LOVE listening to your ideas and practical requirements that feed into the internal layouts and design language. We will work closely with you to imagine and mold your home with floor plans, hand sketches, visuals and carefully selected finishes, furniture and lighting. Combined, these elements will bring to life our collective vision and give you the confidence to progress

More on Concept & Developed Design
