Metalfond production cycle

Die design

The design of dies and trimming dies takes place after a careful analysis of the customer's request.

Based on our experience we can offer:

What we offer

  • Co-design to support the customers' designers in optimising the industrialisation of the castings
  • The making of prototypes
  • The definition of products and tooling with 2D and 3D CAD systems
  • Die-filling analysis with dedicated software, which makes it possible to identify in advance any defects in the casting and so to optimise the die design

The dies are built in partnership with a pool of specialised, carefully selected suppliers, always in close collaboration with and under the careful supervision of Metalfond.
