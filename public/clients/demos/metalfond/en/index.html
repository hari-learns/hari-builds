The production cycle

A single point of contact, from design to shipping

Metalfond S.r.l. is a foundry in Brescia that carries out aluminium pressure die casting: by selecting and coordinating suppliers on the customer's behalf, it also carries out die design like a true project leader. A single point of contact with a single objective: the customer's satisfaction and security, in every phase of production.

    History
    Since 1967, with the Donati family since 1992
    1. 1967
      The Metalfond S.r.l. foundry is founded and from the start works in the die casting of non-ferrous alloys, mainly brass.
    2. 1992
      It is bought by the Donati family and aluminium pressure die casting is introduced.
    3. 2012
      From September the new IP presses of 1,100 t and 1,350 t are in operation.
    4. 2013
      New Striko Westofen melting furnace installed and started up. Complete overhaul and replacement of accessories of the 850 t press.
    5. Today
      Over the last 20 years production has grown by 50%, up to the current 300 tonnes/month of processed aluminium.
