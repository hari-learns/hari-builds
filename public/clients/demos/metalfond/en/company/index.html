Metalfond S.r.l.

Company and history

Operates with state-of-the-art systems in aluminium pressure die casting.

Metalfond S.r.l. operates with state-of-the-art systems in aluminium pressure die casting.

The foundry is served by 3 melting furnaces which guarantee, after careful degassing, the quality of the material sent to the machine-side melting furnaces.

Metalfond S.r.l. has fully automatic presses from a minimum of 300 t to a maximum of 1,350 t, which make it possible to produce parts of various sizes, with the possibility of applying vacuum and temperature control to the dies.

The customer base has gradually diversified by sector of activity, with an important concentration in automotive.

Turnover is generated 40% in Italy and the remaining 60% in Europe, with a high concentration towards the German market. The drive towards export is strong and constant.

The workforce is made up of 50 employees and the production halls cover an area of about 8,000 sq m on a site of about 14,000 sq m.

History

From 1967 to today

  1. 1967

    The Metalfond S.r.l. foundry is founded and from the start works in the die casting of non-ferrous alloys, mainly brass.

  2. 1992

    It is bought by the Donati family and aluminium pressure die casting is introduced.

  3. 2012

    From September the new IP presses of 1,100 t and 1,350 t are in operation.

  4. 2013

    New Striko Westofen melting furnace installed and started up. Complete overhaul and replacement of accessories of the 850 t press.

  5. Today

    Over the last 20 years production has grown by 50%, up to the current 300 tonnes/month of processed aluminium.

Environment

Metalfond is committed to protecting the environment and operates with care and in compliance with current regulations with:

  • 5 different fume extraction systems
  • 1 system for separating water from the oily lubrication residues; the water is then put back into circulation, while the oil is disposed of in specialised plants

Production scrap is remelted, therefore without generating environmental impact.
