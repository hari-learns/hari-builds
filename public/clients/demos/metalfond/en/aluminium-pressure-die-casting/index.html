Aluminium pressure die casting

  • 300-1,350 tautomatic presses
  • 15 kgmaximum casting weight
  • 2vacuum systems

Moulding is the central phase of the pressure die casting process.

Metalfond S.r.l. has presses from 300 to 1,350 tonnes, fully automated, most of them working under process control.

We can make castings of the most varied sizes, from a few grams up to 15 kg in weight.

The presses are fitted with temperature controllers, which keep the die temperature constant, reducing thermal shock, extending die life and ensuring steadier production.

We also have two vacuum systems: one centralised and one portable.

Vacuum moulding is applied where the function of the casting calls for particular requirements of structural integrity.
