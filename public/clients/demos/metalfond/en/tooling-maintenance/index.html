Metalfond production cycle

Tooling maintenance

Metalfond S.r.l. has a tool room with lathes, numerically controlled milling machines and electrical discharge machining used for routine and extraordinary maintenance and for repairs.

For every production batch, the person in charge cleans and services the tooling and then stores it in the dedicated warehouse.
