UNI EN ISO 9001:2015

Certifications and quality policy

Metalfond S.r.l. has invested in human, technical and production resources to respond as well as possible to the quality needs of the market.

It has therefore obtained certification of its Quality System to the UNI EN ISO 9001:2015 standard from the well-known institute DET NORSKE VERITAS. The validity of the certificate can be checked on the DNV website.

Quality policy

Metalfond s.r.l., a company working in the die casting of aluminium alloy products, has as its main objective customer satisfaction, aiming to exceed customers' expectations.

Customer orientation, the analysis of customers' technical and quality needs, the definition of the characteristics of the required product and our experience in the sector allow us to identify and propose the best solution for achieving high levels of quality, productivity and competitiveness.

Our commitment to achieving this objective is the continuous improvement of the services, processes and products offered, from both a technical and a quality point of view, and the optimisation of costs, consumption, plants and machinery, which together with the monitoring of company processes guarantee their efficiency and reliability.

The training, instruction and involvement of staff, aimed at strengthening their responsibility, competence, awareness and growth, as well as the qualification of our suppliers, whom we regard as a company resource, are essential elements for achieving a high quality standard of product and for business continuity.

Compliance with current environmental and safety regulations, keeping the working environment orderly and clean with regard to the product made, and respect for people are basic components of safe working environments and reliable equipment.
