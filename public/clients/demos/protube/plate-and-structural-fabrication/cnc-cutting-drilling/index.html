Plate and structural fabrication

CNC drilling and thermal cutting machines

  • Machine

Protube supplies a wide range of advanced CNC machines and concepts equipped with plasma, laser, oxyfuel, waterjet and 3D mill technologies. Besides machine mechanics, MicroStep develops and supplies advanced control systems for machine tools and CAM software with custom features.

Automated lines of the DS type are an effective means to achieve high productivity in flanged parts production, through automation of loading input material and automated removal of finished parts.
