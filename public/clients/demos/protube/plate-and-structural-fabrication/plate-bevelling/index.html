Plate and structural fabrication

Plate bevelling machines and chamfering tools

  • Machine
  • Consumables and wear items
  • Spares

Protube Engineering supplies a range of high-quality machines, consumables and wear items for all types of bevelling and chamfering, including plate, structural and pipe.

PRO Art series: mechanical bevelling with a high-speed rotating milling cutter, so the mirror finish achieved is ready to be welded without any additional weld prep work. Near and far side bevelling can be achieved simultaneously with dedicated machines for each position.

MicroStep: up to 50% of parts produced in the CNC cutting industry worldwide need to have bevelled edges, yet few machines are installed with bevel stations. MicroStep bevel heads cut the bevel directly into the raw material (DBP), or add bevels to parts that have already been cut with a straight tool (ABP, including top-Y bevels on mild steel parts thicker than 50 mm by plasma, or up to 300 mm with an oxyfuel rotator). Auto-calibration of tool geometry (ACTG) reduces machine setup time from several hours to a couple of minutes.
