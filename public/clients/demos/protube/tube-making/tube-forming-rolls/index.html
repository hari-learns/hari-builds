Tube forming rolls and accessories

  • Consumables and wear items
  • Spares

Founded in 1987, Eurolls is an Italian company recognized worldwide as one of the best suppliers of tooling. Their entire process from design to heat treatment is manufactured 100% in house. Eurolls produce tube forming rolls either in accordance with drawings or by design: solid and split rolls, rolls with TC inserts, floating flanges, clamps, always manufactured to strict tolerances and finishes.

Forming rolls can be made of special steels, sintered materials or various other alloys. For high quality products, such as automotive or furniture applications, and whenever tight tolerances are required, Eurolls offers combined materials, including solid carbide rolls in the most critical applications. Eurolls can produce roll tooling for tube/pipe up to 24″.
