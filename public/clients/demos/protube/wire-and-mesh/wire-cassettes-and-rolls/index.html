Wire, reinforcing and mesh

Wire rolling cassettes, rolls and accessories

  • Consumables and wear items
  • Spares

For the wire industry, Eurolls was the first company to convert the traditional die process in wire drawing to wire rolling, by means of its unique quality microcassettes. Eurolls was the first to produce micro, modular, traditional and custom made cassettes.

Protube supplies high quality equipment and wear parts suitable for the production of smooth or ribbed, low, medium and high carbon steel wire, as well as copper, aluminium and titanium wire. Many items already in stock or can supply on short notice.

Partner Eurolls, Vitari, Teurema www.eurolls.com

Includes

  • Reinforcing wire cassettes – simple and practical
  • Special wire micro cassettes – easily replace traditional dies
  • Maintenance bench for cassettes
  • Sizing, forming, reducing and profiling rolls – tungsten carbide or steel
  • Roll holders – steel
  • Stress relieving rolls – tungsten carbide
  • Roughing rolls and descaling rolls
  • Flattening rolls for metal strips for electrical contacts
  • Motorized rolls for wire pulling
  • Profiling rolls with ribs to follow different market standards
  • Tungsten carbide inserts
  • Rolls for hot rolling of copper or aluminium wire rod
  • Regrinding of used and damaged rolls
