    About Protube

    Your success is our business

    With many years of experience in the metal processing and niche manufacturing industries, Protube Engineering – with its engineering department – is a partner you can always rely on when innovation, high performance and cost efficiency are critical factors to your business. We advise on, supply, and install the world’s most technologically advanced manufacturing machines and accessories, along with a full range of consumables and wear items to ensure smooth and efficient operation. Project engineering, installation and commissioning is all done in strict accordance with Australian Standards. Our philosophy is to treat our customers like genuine partners and add value to their business.
