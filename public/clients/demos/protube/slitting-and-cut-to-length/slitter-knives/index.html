Slitting and cut-to-length

Rotary slitter knives and machine elements

  • Consumables and wear items
  • Spares

TKM Rotary Slitter Knives epitomise precision and durability for demanding slitting applications in the metal industry. Engineered with ultra-tight thickness tolerances, they ensure exceptional accuracy and repeatability. Available in four tolerance classes and four surface finish options, from ground to mirror-polished, they deliver outstanding wear resistance and burr-free cutting. Each knife is tailored with a finely tuned material and heat-treatment recipe to optimise toughness and longevity.

Paired with Protube’s rubber stripper rings, which provide reliable strip control and protect material surfaces, they form a high-performance solution that improves efficiency, reduces scrap and extends tooling life.

Partner TKM www.tkmgroup.com

Includes

  • Steel service centres: rotary slitter knives and accessories, tooling for over-arm separators, cut-to-length knives (straight and chevron blades, NC-ground), scrap chopper knives, KSF microplan polishing machine, light-weight spacers, DYNABLOC quill assemblies, CASKA software for computer assisted tooling set ups
  • Rolling mills: bar mill and structural mill knives, slap shears, crop shears, side trimmer blades, profile shear blades, high precision knives for laser welding lines
  • Recycling industry: granulator knives, pressure lips, guillotine shear knives, bimetal knives for agglomerators, scrap chopper blades
