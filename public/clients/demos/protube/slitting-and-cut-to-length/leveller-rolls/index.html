Slitting and cut-to-length

Leveller rolls, back-up rolls and bearings

  • Consumables and wear items
  • Spares

B.S.A. s.r.l. is an Italian company specialized in the production of rolls, backup bearings and other components for leveling machines. With the support of induction heat treating facilities, TERMOSTAHL and TTL can supply all types of cylindrical induction hardened and ground rolls. All work rolls are made of chromium, molybdenum and vanadium steel grades to match all customer requests for hot and cold working applications and for all the different configurations of leveling equipment.

The long experience of over 50 years of mechanical operations and heat treatments allows special materials and proper hardening profiles to improve the lifetime of rolls and backup bearings in accordance with the different mill working conditions.

Includes

  • Work and back-up rolls for hot and cold plate levellers
  • Intermediate rolls, including grooved intermediate rolls for aluminium
  • Rolls for bar and profile straighteners and bending machines
  • Arbors and mandrels for slitters, presses and forming rolls
  • Refurbishment of levelling and straightening rolls, refurbishing of cassettes
  • Bearings for optimal performance
