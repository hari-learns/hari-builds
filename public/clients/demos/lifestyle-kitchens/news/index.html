News posts from the current website, newest first. The latest post on the live site is from December 2016.

9 December 2016

Latest Commercial Fitout

Our latest commercial fitout is very different! While in the factory it didn’t look that much, now installed on site, it looks fantastic. So many different finishes including powder coated steel, mesh, telegraph poles (yes telegraph poles), recycled timber as well as custom upholstery.

20 September 2016

Blackmores Fitout

The fitout consisted of lockers, kitchen and a breakout area. Our favourite part is the kitchen island bench top which was made from a 6mm porcelain tile with a black powder coat steel frame and water fall ends. A stylish cost effective finish.

30 March 2016

Coast Cafe opening, Sydney International Airport

We have been working on the “Coast Cafe” at Sydney International Airport. The job and installation is now complete and we couldn’t be happier with the final result.

9 February 2016

Sydney International Airport Coast Cafe: the table

This specially made table is from American oak timber and will have a European oak veneer inlay on the top. The inlay will be machined into a pattern and stained in four different colours.

8 December 2015

Stainless steel benchtops

Stainless steel is a great alternative for bench tops. This stainless steel vanity bench top has the bowl welded in to give a flush seamless look.

16 September 2015

If we can draw it, we can build it

In-progress pictures from the commercial fitout for Deloitte at Canberra’s Brindabella Park. A pretty intricate design and construction. This particular feature is the front of house concierge counter. Generally if you can visualise it, and we can draw it, we can build it.

25 July 2015

IMED before and after

Before and after photographs of the IMED building in Canberra. The curved Corian reception counter was custom made for the project.
