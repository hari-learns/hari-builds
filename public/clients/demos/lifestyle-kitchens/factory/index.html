The factory

A fully automated, computerised facility at 5 Cooper Place, Queanbeyan. Showroom and factory are on the same site.

Spray, glass, metal, upholstery

With the large growth of both our residential and commercial joinery, we soon outgrew our small rented premises. And in 2005, we constructed our own 2000 m² purpose-built factory at 5 Cooper Place, Queanbeyan.

With the opening of our new factory we also upgraded all of our machinery to run a fully automated and computerised facility which is equipped with the latest in computerised machinery.

We are self sufficient as the majority of our joinery is processed in house. And as such, we don’t need to rely on outside resources, eliminating time delays in our manufacturing process. Therefore we can manufacture joinery in a very fast time frame.

What happens inside the factory.

    CNC routing

    We run an Accord “Morbidelli” flat bed router with a 4 metre bed that precisely cuts and drills all of our joinery to the millimetre.

  • 02

    Spray painting

    Our own in house spray painting facility producing both gloss and satin polyurethane finishes in colour and clear, the clear being for our veneers and solid timbers.

  • 03

    Colour-back glass

    We produce our own colour back glass splash backs, made to measure on toughened glass.

    Read more
  • 04

    Metal fabrication

    We manage our own in house metal fabricating facility which produces high quality fixtures, fittings and components. Unique and bespoke metal elements in a variety of finishes.

  • 05

    Upholstery

    We can also provide a full range of upholstery services.

  • 06

    Solid surface

    Solid surfaces such as Corian are no problem as we are licensed and accredited to fabricate and manufacture this product. Corian can also be heated and bent into three-dimensional shapes using thermoforming.

    Read more
  • 07

    3D shop drawings

    We run a powerful 3D CAD software package, Pytha. Pytha allows us to produce a high end presentation of shop drawings for our project managers, architects, clients and tradespeople, and a 3D data exchange in common file formats (ie. dxf, dwg etc).
