Residential

Residential kitchens & joinery

Concept, design, manufacture, completion. Custom built to suit your requirements and space.

Getting a brand new kitchen is a once in a lifetime experience for many of us, and can also be a daunting and stressful time. So many choices and decisions to make. We believe the kitchen is also the hub of the home, and we have been making our clients’ dream kitchens since 1998.

Whether it be a kitchen in a new home or a total kitchen renovation in your existing home we custom build to suit your requirements and space. A lot of our clients come to us with a design already in hand. We can work with your plans too.

How much does a new kitchen cost? From approximately $5,000 to $50,000, depending on size, finishes, fittings, bench top, splash back and appliances. Read the FAQ.

Kitchen designer

Meet Katie.

Katie Finlayson heads up our kitchen design team. She has been designing kitchens for over 15 years, being trained by one of Sydney’s renowned kitchen designers Colin Hirnich-Brown. Katie works alongside our clients to design their dream kitchen from concept through to completion.

Our showroom (including kitchen displays) and factory are both at 5 Cooper Place. Showroom Monday to Friday 9am to 4pm, and, by appointment.
