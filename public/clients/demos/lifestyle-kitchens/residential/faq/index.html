Residential joinery FAQ

Design, appliances, materials, cost, licences and trades.

Can I bring my own kitchen design?

Yes, many of our clients come to us with their kitchen design already in hand, as they have had their kitchen already designed as part of the planning stage of a new home or home renovation.

Does Lifestyle Kitchens & Joinery design kitchens?

Yes we have a highly experienced in house design team.

Do you supply 3D computer designs?

Yes once you have engaged us to manufacture your new kitchen and we have completed a check measure on site, we will supply you with 3D & 2D computer drawings of your brand new kitchen.

Do you only sell kitchens?

We design and manufacture loads of other joinery! Including laundries, custom made vanities, outdoor kitchens, walk in robes, studies, tv units, bespoke joinery etc.

Do you supply appliances and sinks?

Yes we do, we can purchase your selection of appliances for you, we can also help with the selection! We recommend selecting sinks from the Oliveri range. If you buy your own appliances, provide the model number and specifications so we can ensure your kitchen fits perfectly around them.

How long does the stone bench top take?

Stone bench tops are not delivered with your kitchen: a check measure by the bench top supplier is taken once the kitchen cabinets are installed. It then takes approximately 15 working days from check measure to have your bench top installed.

What is the best bench top material?

Surfaces include: Natural Stone, Caesarstone, Corian, Stainless Steel, Natural Timber, Laminate etc. Discuss with our kitchen designer what surface best suits your lifestyle, needs and purpose.

How much does a new kitchen cost?

From approximately $5,000 to $50,000. Factors include the size of your kitchen, the finish on the cupboards, the number of cupboards and drawers, fittings, bench top, splash back and appliances.

What are the payment terms?

5-10% deposit, 80% progress payment, balance due on completion. Full payment is required prior to ordering of glass for glass splashbacks.

Is Lifestyle Kitchens & Joinery licensed?

Yes we hold a builders licence in NSW & ACT. NSW Builders Licence Number 221224C. ACT Builders Licence Number 200427165.

Do you organise other trades?

Yes, we have licensed trades people that we will organise and coordinate for the kitchen removal and installation, listed as an option on the quotation. You can also organise your own: we will not supervise trades people you hire.
