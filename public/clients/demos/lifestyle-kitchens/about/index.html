About Lifestyle Kitchens & Joinery

Family owned since 1998 and operated from our state-of-the-art manufacturing plant in Queanbeyan NSW.

Lifestyle Kitchens & Joinery Australia Pty Ltd commenced business in 1998. We are a residential and commercial joinery business based in Queanbeyan NSW, and while we work predominantly in the Queanbeyan and Canberra areas, we also travel for projects in many other locations including Sydney, Melbourne, Newcastle and Brisbane.

Originally Lifestyle Kitchens operated solely as a kitchen manufacturing company producing custom-made kitchens for residential clients in new homes and renovations. However, after achieving great success in this market, we diversified into commercial joinery. These days we work with project management companies to deliver detailed and bespoke joinery into large corporate and government buildings. The commercial side of the business has well exceeded our expectations.

Our mission: quality and innovative design in joinery solutions with outstanding customer service. We value repeat business and have built our business around it. We have many clients getting their second and third kitchens custom made by us. We are also held in high regard by our commercial project managers and clients for both our quality of work and our ability to deliver on fast-paced projects.

The factory

Management team

Meet the people behind the factory.

Scott Lee

Managing Director

Scott started Lifestyle Kitchens & Joinery in 1998 in a small rented premises in Queanbeyan. Today we operate from our purpose built 2000 square meter computerized factory at 5 Cooper Place. Scott is our head estimator for commercial projects and is also hands-on within the business, helping hand over large commercial projects. Scott thrives on fast paced projects and completing jobs in a short time frame to the highest of quality and standard.

Jason Lee

Managing Director

Jason is a licensed builder and started out his working life building brand new homes, roof conversions and home extensions. Jason now plays an active role at lifestyle overseeing the day to day running of the factory and site works. Jason’s knowledge and expertise in the building & construction industry brings much value to the business.

Kieran Brown

Drafting Manager

Kieran Brown leads our drafting team with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of commercial joinery.
