Corian solid surface

Licensed, accredited fabricators and installers of solid surface products for commercial and residential projects.

Solid surface products, such as Corian, offer homeowners, architects and designers a wide range of design choices and possibilities for aesthetic expression.

Manufactured by DuPont, Corian is a solid, virtually seamless, non-porous material made from natural minerals and acrylic resin. Pieces of Corian can be glued together inconspicuously to create a seamless look, giving amazing design flexibility for a range of horizontal and vertical surface applications.

Kitchen bench tops, outdoor kitchens, vanities and beyond, throughout homes and hospitals, restaurants, laboratories and public spaces, Corian solid surfaces deliver high performance and outstanding aesthetics.

Corian can also be heated and bent into three-dimensional shapes using a process called thermoforming, which adds to the versatility of the product.

Lifestyle Kitchens & Joinery Australia are licensed certified fabricators and installers of all solid surface products for both residential and commercial projects.
