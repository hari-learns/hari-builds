Painted glass

Lifestyle Glass, a division of Lifestyle Kitchens & Joinery Australia: made to measure colour-back glass.

Glass is the simplest and most stylish way to give a kitchen, bathroom, hotel or office entrance or wall a facelift, creating more depth, light and feeling of space.

Our splashbacks are manufactured using 6mm grade A toughened safety glass as per Australian Standards. Toughened glass is about 5 times stronger than normal float glass and is heat resistant. Toughened mirror splash backs are also available.

We prefer to use colours from the Dulux range, and can also provide a colour matching service. Clear glass has a green tint that shows through on light colours, so for white or light colours we recommend low-iron glass, which gives the true colour.

Splashbacks are made to order after the installation of your bench top. Turn around time for glass splashbacks is around 15 working days from final measure.
