CBM · Butterers

Collator Butterer

A hygienic, automated conveyor system with dual feed lanes for precise timing control of production runs, with a heated hopper that stops the butter solidifying in the chilled environment.

120/minPublished top speed (2 lanes at 60 per minute (CBMD: 4 lanes))
60 mmMax product height
130 mmMax product width

Efficient

Runs up to 60 operations per minute per lane. High speed 2, 3 or 4 slice modes available.

Dynamic

The machine releases product at timed intervals, determined by the rate entered; by varying the speed of the subsequent production conveyor, product spacing can be altered.

Production monitoring

Fully controlled and monitored locally, or remotely on a PC network. Stand-alone or synchronised/integrated.

Collator Butterer Duo (CBMD)

Two heated butter boxes and four lanes with conveyors that lift the slices for precise buttering. Lanes can be run singly, as a pair, with three lanes or all four at once.
