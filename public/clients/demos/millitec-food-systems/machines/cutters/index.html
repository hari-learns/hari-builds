Slicers & cutters

Ultrasonic cutters and manual cutting solutions

Millitec's slicers and cutters are engineered to deliver precise, clean cuts for sandwiches, wraps, toasties, and more. Our hygienic cutting equipment includes both ultrasonic machines and manual jigs, ideal for food to go, bakery, and ready meal sectors.
