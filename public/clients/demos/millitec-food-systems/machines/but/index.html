BUT · Butterers

Butterer

An ergonomically designed machine for buttering bread and rolls in an integrated sandwich production line. Spreads whipped and aerated butter, margarine, or other spreads.

Consistent

The ability to refill during operation and spread across the entire width ensures thorough coverage and consistency.

Super hygienic

The machine's simple strip-down cleaning process facilitates easy maintenance and sanitation, ensuring optimal hygiene and minimising downtime.

Quiet

Noise level below 79 dB(A) LEAV criterion.

Precise buttering

Spread thickness and product thickness are adjustable. Suitable for bread slices, wraps, and other products.

Published as 'minimum height 40 mm, maximum width 130 mm'. Confirm the height range with Millitec.
