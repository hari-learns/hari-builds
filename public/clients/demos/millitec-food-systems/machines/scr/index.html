SCR · Sanitising

Sanitising Conveyor

Safely and hygienically transfers ingredients, tools and other items from low care to high care, minimising the risk of cross-contamination.

Versatile

Available in various sizes to cope with items from small tubs to complete baskets and trays. Options for chemical storage and roller conveyor.

Automated

Once activated it performs all functions automatically. The operator simply feeds items into the machine.

Sustainable

Water recirculation system, extremely low water consumption.
