Revolutionising Manufacturing: Millitec’s Rapid Production Process

Follow the journey from order to delivery at Millitec and see how we ensure quality and efficiency every step of the way in food machinery manufacturing.

Millitec, where innovation meets efficiency, is set to redefine the food processing industry by delivering top-quality machines with record-breaking turnaround times.

In this blog post, we will reveal how Millitec produces your product before delivery to your site.

A journey through production: the small bespoke conveyor

Let's take a closer look at the impressive production journey of a small bespoke conveyor:

01. order taken

A customer requested a new process to be set up within the week. Despite it being an impossible delivery timeframe for most, we committed to delivering a small, customised conveyor within one day. Challenges are always welcome here at Millitec!

02. design

The conveyor's design comes to life in 3D CAD. During this phase, we carefully incorporate any special requests from the customer.

Meet one of our designers, who's busy adding side rails and adjusting the conveyor's length to align with the customer's specifications.

03. production planning & programming

Transitioning from the design stage, we export files directly to our machine shop and fabrication department, kickstarting production. Here are some essential tasks during this stage:

1. machine shop: program the cnc machines

2. laser team: set up the laser cutter for precision steel sheet cutting

2. stores: select the parts & update the bom

With these tasks completed, the conveyor is primed for the production stage.

04. laser cutting

Our brand-new, cutting-edge flatbed laser cutter takes centre stage, precisely cutting sheet materials to a tolerance of 0.1mm.

The machine's legs and framework are also expertly cut using our state-of-the-art tube laser cutter.

05. machine shop

Our CNC machine shop manufactures parts that are not readily available in stock.

06. folding

The flat laser-cut components move to our folding section, where our new 7-axis CNC press efficiently shapes them.

07. fabrication

All main fabrication components enter production, meticulously welded together by our skilled and hygienic team of fabricators. Witness our expert fabricators working hard on the conveyor's bed and legs.

08. panel build & finishing

Simultaneously, the control panel is crafted on a precision laser-cut gear plate, ready to complement the other components upon exiting the fabrication department.

The fabrication undergoes thorough cleaning and polishing. Any parts requiring bead blasting are meticulously treated in our dedicated stainless-steel blast room on-site.

09. assembly

All component parts fabricated, machined, and carefully selected are ready for the final assembly stage.

Electronic drawings are shared with our technicians through touchscreen stations in our assembly area, ensuring a seamless and precise assembly process.

10. quality control & certification

Once fully assembled, the final machine undergoes rigorous quality control checks. After passing the test, the machine is thoroughly cleaned, CE-marked and carefully packed for shipping.
