Hygienic, High-Speed Solutions for Ready Meal Manufacturers

In the fast-paced world of ready meal manufacturing, speed, hygiene, and precision are crucial.

In the competitive world of ready meal manufacturing, speed, hygiene, and precision are essential. Whether you're handling pasta, sauces, or complete meal assemblies, Millitec’s advanced food automation machinery is engineered for high-care environments and seamless integration into your existing production lines.

Our innovative systems help you:

  • Reduce food waste through accurate portion control
  • Increase outp with fast, reliable automation
  • Maintain the highest hygiene standards with easy-to-clean designs

Hygienic Pasta Depositor: Consistent, Clean Portioning

Engineered for high-viscosity foods, the Hygienic Pasta Depositor ensures every portion is delivered with accuracy and speed. With its twin-head, servo-driven system, it achieves up to 60 deposits per minute — making it ideal for busy ready meal lines.

Key Benefits

  • Integrated recipe handling for batch consistency
  • Dressing and sauce balance system to prevent overdosing and maintain flavour accuracy
  • Sauce and Dressing Capture technology for accurate back-of-pack (BOP) nutritional data
  • Adjustable deposit range from 1.0g to 2.0g, perfect for small pot portions
  • Minimal waste thanks to high-accuracy operation
  • Perfect for pasta salads, ready-to-eat meals, and portion-sensitive products

Perfect for pasta salads, ready-to-eat meals, and other portion-sensitive products.

Multi-Head Sauce Depositor: Flexible, High-Speed Saucing

The Multi-Head Sauce Depositor gives you total control over deposit weight, density, delay, and suck-back to prevent drips and mess.

Capable of up to 100 deposits per minute, it’s the ideal solution for fast-paced, high-volume production lines.

Ideal for

  • Varying product types and packaging sizes
  • Preventing drips or mess with suck-back control
  • Fast-paced, high-volume production lines

Volumetric Servo Depositor: Energy-Efficient Precision

The Volumetric Servo Depositor (VSD) enables direct depositing or sheeting at speeds of up to 100 operations per minute. It’s fully servo-driven, offering:

Advantages

  • Up to £4,000 in annual energy savings compared to pneumatic systems
  • Enhanced speed, cleanliness, and control Ideal for manufacturers looking to cut costs without compromising quality

HPL Indexing Conveyor: Smooth, Reliable Product Handling

Our HPL Indexing Conveyor ensures steady product flow with continuous or indexed motion.

Optional punched belts keep trays securely in place, working seamlessly with Millitec’s depositors and cutters to maintain production consistency.

Key benefits:

  • Continuous or indexed operation for maximum flexibility
  • Secure product handling with punched belts Smooth integration with depositors and cutting systems

Ultrasonic Sandwich Cutter: Clean, Accurate Slicing

Cut sandwiches and layered meals precisely with the Ultrasonic Sandwich Cutter.

Using ultrasonic vibrations, it prevents smearing and sticking — ensuring every slice is accurate and consistent.

Key benefits:

  • Perfectly clean cuts without smearing or tearing
  • Consistent results for sandwiches and layered products
  • Fast, hygienic, and production-efficient slicing

Cutter Exit Conveyor (CEC): Maintain Alignment After Cutting

The Cutter Exit Conveyor (CEC) follows the SKF Cutter to maintain spacing and alignment of finished products.

This ensures smooth handover to packaging lines and further automation processes.

Advanced Hygiene Solutions for Food-Safe Automation

Millitec’s Clean-in-Place (CIP) Units and Sanitising Tunnels are portable, effective, and built for continuous production environments. These systems help you meet hygiene standards without downtime.

Main benefits:

  • Easy to maintain
  • Fast sanitation cycles
  • Supports BRC and food safety compliance
