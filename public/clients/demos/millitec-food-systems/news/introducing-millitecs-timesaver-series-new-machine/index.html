Introducing the Ultimate Production Equipment Timesaver 22 RB Series

We’ve recently incorporated the new Timesaver 22 RB Series into our shop floor operations — helping us optimise production time and ensure we never waste yours.

At Millitec, we're thrilled to introduce our latest game-changing addition to the manufacturing process - the versatile Timesavers 22 RB Series. This cutting-edge production equipment is set to revolutionise our deburring and finishing operations, utilising a variety of brush and disc heads to enhance the quality of our products.

Enhanced effciency & quality

With the ability to deburr and finish a wide range of metal widths in a single operation, this production equipment allows us to improve the quality and consistency of our fabrication process significantly.

Streamlined production

Previously, our deburring process was a labour-intensive manual task. The introduction of this production equipment has not only accelerated our production speed but has also elevated the overall excellence of our products.

This efficient machine not only saves time but also proves to be cost-effective in the long run, liberating our skilled engineers to focus on other critical aspects of our operations. At Millitec, our dedication to maintaining the highest standards in everything we do remains unwavering.

Your time matters

Incorporating this new addition into our process allows us to optimise our time, ensuring that we don't waste yours. We value our customers and are committed to delivering excellence in every facet of our work. With our new production equipment, we're ready to take your experience to the next level.
