Millitec Featured in Sandwich & Food To Go News May/June 2025

Millitec is featured in the May/June 2025 issue of Sandwich & Food To Go News, showcasing our innovative solutions for automated production lines.

We’re excited to announce that Millitec has been featured in the May/June 2024 issue of Sandwich & Food To Go News!

The article focuses on the rising demand for pasta salads in the food-to-go market — and how our Hygienic Pasta Depositor (HPD) is helping manufacturers keep up with portion control, production efficiency and sustainability goals.

Designed to handle delicate ingredients like pasta, vegetables and sauces, the HPD is a fast, precise solution for large-scale food production. With features like sauce distribution control, electric actuators and easy-clean design, it’s built for both hygiene and performance.

We’re proud to be recognised for the work we’re doing to support evolving food trends with smart automation technology.
