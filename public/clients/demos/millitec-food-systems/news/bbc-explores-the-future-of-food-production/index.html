Exploring Automation in Labour Markets: A BBC Visit to Millitec

The BBC explores the future of food production with Millitec, highlighting how automation and innovation are transforming the way food is manufactured.

We were excited to have a visit from the BBC East Midlands Today to take a closer look at our cutting-edge automation solutions this week!

Visit from bbc

The BBC recently paid a visit to Millitec, engaging in a compelling discussion regarding the potential impact of robotics on job opportunities and its role as a possible solution to the labour shortage triggered by new immigration regulations.

Meet the millitec hdr lidding robot

During the visit, Richard took the opportunity to introduce our cutting-edge HDR Delta Robot. This innovative technology is designed to revolutionise the sandwich-making process by replacing manual pick-and-place operations. The HDR Delta Robot is not only remarkably precise and high-speed but also boasts an estimated return on investment that surpasses that of manual labour wages.

As we continue to explore the evolving landscape of technology and its impact on employment, it becomes increasingly clear that innovative solutions like the HDR Delta Robot offer promising opportunities for businesses and workers.
