Millitec Welcomes Erewash MP Maggie Throup

Maggie Throup MP visits Millitec to explore our latest advancements in robotics and automation, supporting innovation and growth in UK food manufacturing.

Millitec, a pioneering company in food automation machinery and robotics, has significantly impacted Draycott since moving to the area in late 2019. The latest milestone in their journey saw them proudly hosting Maggie Throup, the Member of Parliament for Erewash.

A £1.2 million investment in innovation

Millitec is continuing to invest in Research and Development, with around 25% of turnover and 40% of staff dedicated to innovation projects, robotics and Artificial Intelligence within the food processing industry. This commitment to innovation places them at the forefront of their industry, constantly driving progress and efficiency in food automation.

Millitec's impact on the community

Maggie Throup, MP for Erewash, recently paid a visit to Millitec, and her interaction with the company’s Managing Director, Richard Ledger, was nothing short of inspiring. Their discussions centred around their shared passion for science, community involvement, engaging young people in STEM, and their deep-rooted affection for the local area.

Maggie Throup was clearly impressed by Millitec’s contributions to the Erewash community. She remarked,

It’s fantastic to see Millitec integrating into Erewash, becoming such a key part of the community and looking to engage more young people in STEM. It is truly incredible that nestled between the old mills of Draycott, lies such a forward-thinking and innovative organisation, designing and developing robots being shipped across the world.

Maggie Throup

Millitec Food Systems specialises in designing, developing, and manufacturing next-generation food automation machinery and robotics. Since our move to Derbyshire, we have generated nine new job opportunities, contributing positively to the local economy and fostering growth in the region

We were thrilled to welcome Maggie to Millitec. We always take pride in showcasing the innovation we have spent so long perfecting whilst also discussing our core values including STEM engagement. We look forward to becoming an integral part of the Erewash Business Community.

Richard Ledger

In conclusion, Millitec Food Systems Ltd is not just a company; it’s a driving force behind innovation in the food processing industry. Our commitment to R&D, cutting-edge technology investment, and community dedication make them a valuable asset to Erewash and the broader business community. As we continue to expand and develop groundbreaking solutions, Millitec is undoubtedly a company to watch in the world of food automation.
