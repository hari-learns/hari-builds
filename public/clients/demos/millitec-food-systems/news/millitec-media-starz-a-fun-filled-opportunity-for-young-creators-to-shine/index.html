Millitec Media Starz: A Fun-Filled Opportunity for Young Creators to Shine!

Millitec Media Starz gives local students a chance to shine by creating their own food radio shows. A fun, educational initiative supporting young creators.

At Millitec, we’re proud to be a local business that manufactures cutting-edge food automation machinery. But beyond that, we care deeply about the community that has supported us over the years. That’s why we’re excited to launch Millitec Media Starz, an initiative in collaboration with Erewash Sound that gives young people a chance to shine, be creative, and win up to £1,000 for their school!

What's millitec media starz about?

Millitec Media Starz invites year five and year six students to create their very own 15-minute radio show on any topic related to food. Whether they explore the world of culinary creations, talk about sustainable eating, or come up with a fun food quiz, students can let their creativity soar.

Supporting local talent

As a business rooted in this community, we believe in giving back and fostering talent. By providing professional guidance and hands-on experience, we hope to inspire the next generation of creative minds. Schools will receive comprehensive online tutorials, and industry experts will visit to help students bring their shows to life, which will then be broadcast on Erewash Sound.

Prizes that support creativity

  • Overall Winner: The listener's votes will decide which school wins £1,000.
  • Best Idea: A prize of £500 for the most innovative concept.
  • Best Performance: Another £500 for the most entertaining performance.

Schools can use the prize money to fund any creative project of their choice, making this competition a fantastic opportunity to support young talent in our community.

Inspired by a love for radio

Millitec’s Managing Director, Richard Ledger, shared his personal inspiration for the project:

Growing up, I was passionate about radio and even built my own transmitter. We’re offering kids the same chance to have fun and gain new skills while being broadcast on real airwaves. We can’t wait to hear their ideas!

Richard Ledger

How can schools get involved?

It's easy to get involved! If you're a teacher or a parent of year five or six students, just visit mediastarz.co.uk to register. Record a short video or voice note on your phone, sharing the food topic you'd love to talk about in your show and upload the file to the website.

This is an amazing opportunity for schools to foster creativity and give their students an unforgettable experience. Let’s celebrate our community and help young people shine on the airwaves— sign up today!

For more information, visit mediastarz.co.uk or email us at contact@mediastarz.co.uk.
