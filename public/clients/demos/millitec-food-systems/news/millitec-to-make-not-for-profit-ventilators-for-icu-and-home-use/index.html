Millitec Takes Action: Developing ICU and Home-Use Ventilators

Millitec develops not-for-profit ventilators for ICU and home use, supporting healthcare efforts with innovative engineering during times of urgent need.

Millitec, as a Midlands-based leader in high-care robotics and manufacturing, is harnessing its 15 years of expertise to contribute to the global battle against COVID-19.

We developed ICU (Intensive Care Unit) ventilators and home-use ventilators that generate oxygen. Remarkably, these life-saving devices will be made available to the NHS on a not-for-profit basis.

Revolutionsing home-use ventilators

With a rich history of high-care manufacturing and robotics, Millitec is committed to significantly impacting the fight against the pandemic. Leveraging our experience and innovation, we have embarked on developing a range of ventilators for both ICU and non-ICU settings. Importantly, these ventilators will be provided to the NHS at cost, emphasising our commitment to supporting healthcare professionals during this critical time.

One standout feature of Millitec's non-ICU ventilator is its built-in oxygen concentrator. This innovation eliminates the reliance on heavy oxygen tanks, making it feasible for individuals to self-isolate in the comfort of their homes. This advancement reduces the burden on healthcare facilities and enhances the safety and care options for patients recovering from COVID-19.

Proactive approach to ventilator production

Recognising the situation's urgency, Millitec immediately began seeking government support. They have proactively begun work on ventilators to speed up production, demonstrating their commitment to saving lives. Richard Ledger, the Managing Director of Millitec, emphasised the company's determination to make a difference, saying,

We can't just sit around and do nothing; every day, there are thousands of people dying worldwide, and each of those lives lost is a tragedy. As a company, we are committed to supporting our fabulous NHS in any way that we can, and these units will be produced for the NHS on a not-for-profit basis.

Richard Ledger

Millitec's proactive and compassionate approach exemplifies the spirit of unity and innovation needed to combat the pandemic in these challenging times. Our ventilators represent a ray of hope for those affected by COVID-19 and are a testament to the power of expertise, determination, and collaboration in the face of adversity.
