Step 1

Pick your product

Sandwich or roll, wrap, pot or ready meal, round fruit or veg, toastie or pastry.

Step 2

Type its size and speed

Height or thickness and width in millimetres, and how many a minute you want.

Step 3

Read the line

Each stage shows the Millitec machines that take it, the speed Millitec publishes, and the exact reason when one does not fit.

What the check uses

Only what Millitec already publishes on each machine page: the product size range (minimum and maximum height, maximum width), the published speed per lane and in total, and the belt widths of the conveyors. Where a machine's page gives no product size, the check says so and asks you to confirm with Millitec. Results are indicative; Millitec confirms every line against your actual product.
