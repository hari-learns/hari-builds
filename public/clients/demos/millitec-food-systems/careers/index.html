Shape the future of food automation

At Millitec, we design and build high-performance food automation systems used by manufacturers worldwide. From design to assembly, installation, and spares, our team is involved at every stage of the process.

We value practical thinking, precision, and a proactive mindset. Whether you're experienced or just starting your career, you'll have the chance to learn, solve real problems, and make a meaningful impact.

We are proud to be recognised as a Disability Confident Committed employer and are committed to encouraging diversity and eliminating discrimination.
