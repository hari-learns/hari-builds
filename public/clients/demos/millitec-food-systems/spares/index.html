Ordering spare parts

Have these two details ready

  • Machine serial number
  • Part number (as listed in your machine manual)

This information allows us to identify the correct component without delay and reduce the risk of ordering errors. If you're unsure or cannot locate these details, our team is happy to assist.

Obsolete and custom parts

For discontinued or hard-to-source components, Millitec can provide bespoke solutions, including reverse engineering and custom manufacturing. This ensures your equipment continues to operate even when original parts are no longer available.
