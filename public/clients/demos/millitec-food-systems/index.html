How we started

A machine to butter bread

Richard grew up around engineering, helping out in his dad's engineering business as a kid. He used to go in and fix the machines in the old lace factories in and around Nottingham.

Richard studied Physics at Cambridge. On graduation, he spent a lot of time building electronic devices and came up with some incredible inventions.

In 2005, when chatting with a friend who worked in a food factory, he asked Richard if he could build a machine to butter bread, and so Millitec was born!
