Distributors

Distributors come complete with controls for easy operation from ground level (control cable extra). Each set of controls is manufactured from galvanized steel, with mounting brackets to fit any size leg trunk manufactured by Lambton Conveyor, so controls can be mounted at any desired height.

Flat back distributor (turn head)

  • Galvanized weather tight enclosure
  • Large inspection door from top to bottom
  • Heavy-duty long-lasting construction; positive position locking
  • Schedule 40 pipe for inner spout; inner spout brush ensures product control
  • Cable or electric actuated; easy turning with UHMW guide
  • Round flange inlet and outlets; short throat for limited loss of discharge height

Dual inlet pendulum distributor

  • Large inspection door at each end
  • Heavy-duty long-lasting construction; positive position locking
  • Cable or electric actuated; easy turning with sprocket and UHMW guide
  • Round flange inlet and outlets; short throat for limited loss of discharge height
  • Standard abrasion-resistant steel liners in pendulum spouts
  • Standard urethane liners in all pendulum discharge transitions

Electric distributor controller

The SG-383 combines touch screen technology and a durable DC drive control. The entire process is continuously monitored by the onboard CPU to ensure accurate positioning at all times.

  • One-touch control, bright display, easy set up, auto tuning, self-test feature, high reliability
  • Options: customizable controls, cold climate package, catastrophic failure over-ride

Manual distributor controller

The swing spout contains a dense bristle brush to reduce dust and buildup. Positive pendulum positioning is achieved through Lambton’s high grade roller chain. The mechanism is unlatched by depressing a foot pedal, and turning the spout is as easy as turning a steering wheel.
