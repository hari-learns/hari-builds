Elevator towers and support structures

Lambton manufactures a complete range of structural solutions to support your material handling systems including catwalks, towers, and custom support frames. All structures are engineered for strength, longevity, and compatibility with Lambton conveyors, elevators, grain loops, and grain cannons.

Elevator support towers

  • Standard sizes: 4', 6', 8', 10', 12'; custom sizes also available
  • Available in 4-leg configurations
  • Modular section construction for flexible design and transport
  • Integrated platforms, ladders, stairs and safety cages optional
  • Designed for catwalk connection, head service access, or equipment mounting

Two-leg tower applications

  • Mount directly to grain bins or other buildings for compact installation
  • Ideal for catwalk, grain cannon, or small conveyor support
  • Engineered to utilize existing structure for lateral stability

Catwalks and bridge spans

Lambton FEA design ensures safe, durable structures for any wind speed or ice requirement.

  • Modular bolt-together, full galvanized construction
  • Fully welded side truss
  • Compatible with all Lambton conveyors and other major brands
  • Designed in accordance with the AISC Manual of Steel Construction using RISA Technologies analysis software
  • ASTM A-325 galvanized bolts, nuts and locking devices for all structural connections; structural steel hot dipped galvanized to ASTM A-123 and A-153
  • Types shown in the catalogue: walk-thru bridge, truss catwalk, under bridge catwalk, cantilever catwalks (no roof loads)
