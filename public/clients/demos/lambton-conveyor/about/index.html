About us

We’re celebrating 60 years of service

Isn’t it time you got to know us better? Family owned since 1965, Lambton is Canada’s leading manufacturer of grain storage, handling and conditioning systems.

Our 200,000 sq. ft. headquarters in Wallaceburg, Ontario, is home to production and support resources serving international farms, grain marketers, food processing and feed manufacturing industries. We pride ourselves on quality manufacturing for the industry’s most complete range of grain system structures, components and accessories.

Lambton is rooted in the rich southern Ontario farmland of the Great Lakes region. Today, with international manufacturing facilities and a global network of field representatives, we continue to value our close personal connections with customers and associates around the world.

One source. One solution.

There is more to a grain storage system than bins and conveyors. You need know-how behind the equipment. Lambton is the one source that brings together all the components your system will need, plus the engineering and service support to make sure it all works together, your way.

Our modular design principles give us the flexibility to custom-fit every assembly to suit your process. That way, our global network of factory-trained field consultants can work with you onsite to develop, build and support the grain system that works best for your operation.

The same principles also help to simplify the installation process, where all the components work together seamlessly and integrate smoothly with any equipment you are already operating.

Service support

Lambton is committed to delivering the best in local expertise and factory support.

We work closely with our worldwide network of qualified dealers and installers to understand your local needs and practices. Our regional sales specialists provide expert product knowledge on grain systems and components to advise dealers and customers on the best solution for efficient, practical and affordable results tailored to individual needs.

Regional managers and certified dealers receive extensive factory training to ensure seamless, responsive communication from the factory to the field, and from the field to the factory.
