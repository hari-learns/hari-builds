Three grain bin families

Choose the family that matches how hard the bin will work.

  • 4" Corrugated Un-Stiffened Grain Bins: recommended for most farm-based grain storage systems
  • 4" Corrugated Stiffened Grain Bins: recommended for larger and more demanding grain storage systems
  • 4" Corrugated Commercial Grain Bins: recommended for high volume, heavy duty applications for grain processing and distribution

All Lambton-built grain bins include

  • Standard construction provides G115 (350 grams/m² of zinc) galvanized steel with 28% more zinc protection than traditional bins
  • Larger 44" sidewall sheets with 4" corrugation improves grain flow, increases vertical strength and reduces build time
  • Access doors with 44" or 66" opening with bin step as standard
  • A 32.5 degree roof angle is standard for both structured and non-structured models, with peak load capacities engineered as needed
  • Farm-stiffened grain bins feature an innovative swedged joint that eliminates stiffener splices and reduces build costs
  • Commercial bins use Lambton’s robust stiffener for additional strength and stability on taller and larger bin structures
  • All designs stress tested with accurate calculations for wind, rooftop equipment loads and snow loads

Features and options

  • Choice of roof ladders, handrails, stairs and platforms
  • Upgraded roofs and wind rings to suit your climate
  • Side draw systems, aeration systems, unloading systems
  • Aeration: centrifugal and axial fans with high and low speeds; full or partial aeration floors
  • Unloading: power sweep systems, conventional tube unloads, sweep augers and chain conveyors
  • Hardware: JS1000 plated Grade 8.2 with over 1,000 hours of salt spray testing, 20-25 times the corrosion resistance of regular electro-zinc products
  • Large 18" roof vents, pre-punched into roof sheets on request
  • New product: square feed bins, galvanized, bolted modular design with raised inspection door on the lid
