Built to last

Lambton solutions are built to last

Our FEA testing systems and robotic manufacturing maintain the highest consistency for our renowned quality standards.

  • G115 galvanized steel

    350 grams/m² of zinc specified for all Lambton grain bins: 28% more zinc protection than traditional structures.

  • 4" corrugation

    Less resistance to downward flow, prevents grain hang-up and bridging. Larger 44" sidewall sheets reduce build time.

  • Stress-tested design

    Bin construction is optimized with calculations for wind stresses, roof-mounted equipment loads and evenly distributed and unbalanced snow loads.

  • CAD designed, CNC produced

    Lambton bucket elevators are CAD designed and CNC produced to ensure end-to-end consistency and precision every time.

Celebrating 60 years in business

Our 200,000 sq. ft. headquarters in Wallaceburg, Ontario, is home to production and support resources serving international farms, grain marketers, food processing and feed manufacturing industries.

Lambton is rooted in the rich southern Ontario farmland of the Great Lakes region. Today, with international manufacturing facilities and a global network of field representatives, we continue to value our close personal connections with customers and associates around the world.
