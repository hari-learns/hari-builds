Grain handling

Grain loops

A grain loop is an automated solution that ensures a continuous flow of grain within your facility. Receiving, conveying, bin unloading and load-out are combined in one cost-effective system, with galvanized steel, bolted construction.

10,000BPH maximum (254 MTPH)
6"–12"tube sizes
½"UHMW paddles

Features

  • Robust construction built for heavy-duty grain handling
  • Continuous loop design for efficient, seamless transport with no manual intervention
  • Dust containment system may be included
  • Compatible with spouts, bin inlets, chutes and diverters; expandable and modular design
  • Two intake options: receiving hopper or inlet hopper
  • Unique split transitions at the corners allow quick replacement without breaking the chain

Rotary gates

  • Full galvanized construction
  • Hot-dipped rotating downspout tube with dual chain drive and UHMW chain tensioners
  • UHMW wrapper with adjustable holding brackets for a tight seal around the downspout
  • Top inspection panels at entry and exit points
