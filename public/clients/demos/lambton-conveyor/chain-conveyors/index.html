Chain conveyors

The en masse chain conveyor moves large volumes of material horizontally, vertically or at any angle, carrying the material in a solid, compact stream that reduces product degradation and minimizes dust emission.

30,000BPH en masse maximum
762MTPH
10-8-5 gagalvanized construction
81X / 81XHchain, 24,000 / 44,000 lb

Lambton brings over 60 years of experience to the design and manufacture of chain conveyor systems custom engineered for your specific requirements. With capacities up to 30,000 BPH (762 MTPH), Lambton is continually developing and expanding its grain-handling line.

Standard features

  • Computer-aided modular bolt-together design, CNC manufactured
  • 10-8-5 gauge standard galvanized construction
  • Heavy-duty pillow block bearings and drive sprocket
  • Removable inspection cover, dust seals, UHMW return rollers
  • Optional abrasion-resistant steel liners; AR liner is standard in round bottom conveyors
  • Paddles are ½" thick UHMW, CNC manufactured: no metal-to-metal contact, lower HP and longer conveyor life
  • Drag hoppers with adjustable flow gates in 4, 8 and 12 foot lengths
  • Optional auto grease on head and boot bearings

Partition chain conveyor

The Partition Chain Conveyor features individual compartments or partitions along the chain, allowing the controlled transportation of materials, even those with different characteristics or sizes, and reducing the risk of contamination. Capacities up to 20,000 BPH.

Manway and conveyor catwalks

  • Designed to fit all conveyors; 24" standard walkway width
  • Support towers produced with the same precision as the catwalks they support
  • X-brace trussing and a truss and guy wire kit available
