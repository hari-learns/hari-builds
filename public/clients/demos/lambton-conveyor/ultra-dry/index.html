Ultra Dry in-bin grain drying system

The Ultra Dry In-Bin Grain-Drying System blends proven drying technology with innovative Lambton features for optimum drying performance and uniformity. It can handle most types of grain, with a potential grain drying capacity of over 1,000 bushels per hour.

How the system works

  • Wet grain is loaded into the Ultra Dry overhead drying chamber. The drying unit forces moisture from the grain up and out of the bin through the roof vent system.
  • Grain is released to the storage and cooling area after drying to a pre-determined moisture level. The drying chamber is then refilled with wet grain.
  • The cooling fan operates continuously to cool the dried grain. The Ultra Dry system recycles the heat from the cooling grain, improving dryer efficiency.
