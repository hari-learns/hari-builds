Grain handling

Grain cannons

A grain cannon, also known as a grain pump or a double run, is a versatile chain conveyor that uses galvanized tube housing to convey material at angles up to 60 degrees. Grain cannons are often used where an auger or an incline drag conveyor would traditionally have been required.

20,000BPH maximum (508 MTPH)
60°maximum angle
10,000BPH, incline cannon curve

They span long distances without requiring catwalks, making them a more economical alternative to a drag conveyor.
