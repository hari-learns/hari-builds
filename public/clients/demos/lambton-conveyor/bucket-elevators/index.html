Grain handling

Bucket elevators

A bucket elevator is a versatile and efficient material handling system used to vertically transport bulk materials, such as grains, powders and aggregates. Lambton made its first mark on the global grain-handling industry as a top manufacturer of dependable, long-serving elevator and conveyor equipment.

Lambton bucket-type grain elevators

  • Gentle seed elevators: recommended to prevent damage to delicate crops like lentils, peas and pulse crops
  • Standard grain elevators: efficient grain handling in most farm applications
  • Commercial grain elevators: durability and maximum volume in commercial applications

Head, trunking and boot

  • Head: G90 galvanized bolt-together design; 10, 8 and 5 GA construction; internal ¼" thick UHMW curved head liner; slide lagged and crowned head pulley; inspection door on both sides of head
  • Trunking: G90 galvanized bolt-together design in 14, 12 and 10 GA; removable side panel for inspection; flanges welded with silicon-bronze MIG wire to prevent corrosion
  • Boot: vertical stiffening angles; heavy-duty pillow block ball bearings; die springs on the belt take-up for proper tensioning; bolted clean out doors; cleanout drawer and self cleaning options

Standard features and options

  • Double row spherical roller bearings as standard for head shaft sizes 1-15/16" and larger
  • Durable, non-sparking polyethylene buckets as standard; steel, nylon or urethane buckets for rough and abrasive materials
  • UHMW liners as standard; abrasion resistant steel, ceramic chip and urethane on request
  • Standard platform and ladder packages with optional safety cage, rest platforms, work platforms and head platforms
  • Discharge transitions, boot inlet hoppers, electrical monitoring equipment
