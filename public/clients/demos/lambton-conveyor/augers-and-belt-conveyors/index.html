Size my system

Screw conveyors

  • Sizes from 6" to 24" available; heavy duty helical style auger
  • Heavy duty galvanized steel construction
  • Formed galvanized steel U-trough with easily removable lid; round bottom self cleaning design
  • Bolted coupler shaft for easy assembly and customization

Utility augers

  • Available in 6", 8", 10", 12" and 14" tube sizes
  • Can be operated on angles of up to 45 degrees
  • Self-cleaning; portable version available
  • Slip fit tube on boot hopper adjustable to the required length

Grainway belt conveyors

  • Compact: fits under bin floors, minimum height 12"
  • 12" belt unloads up to 5,000 BPH, 18" belt up to 7,500 BPH and 24" belt up to 10,000 BPH
  • Gentle handling with slow speeds, rubber belting and a plastic deflector inside the discharge
  • Bolted construction, 10 gauge steel frame; galvanized and powder coated options
  • Hydraulic drive kit, wheel assemblies, bin accessories and rubber skirting as options
