National Veterans Monument, Akershus Fortress

On assignment from Forsvarsbygg, Varia produced and installed three elements in aluminium bronze: a plaque with the names of those killed in international operations, a torch with an eternal flame and a sign that says what the place is. Designed by the architects Manthey Kula.
