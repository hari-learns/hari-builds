Skytta department

The department is a sheet-metal and mechanical workshop with machines and equipment to produce most things in sheet-metal and steel structures. We work with most metals, mainly black steel, stainless / acid-proof steel and aluminium.

The field of work is production equipment, conveyors, special machines, walkways and work platforms. We also do mechanical maintenance, service and dismantling of machines and surrounding equipment.

The workshop also does traditional work in building and contracting: installation of structural steel, stairs, railings and similar. The department is EN 1090-1, EXC 2, approved. We also hire out mechanics and welders for various assignments.

Furuset department

The Furuset department is a welding, repair and installation workshop with versatile welders and mechanics.

The workshop mainly works with welding and repair of various types of containers, swap bodies, compactors and general mechanical equipment in industry, building and construction.

The work takes place in our own workshop and out at our customers', where we carry out jobs with our own service vehicles and the necessary tools.
