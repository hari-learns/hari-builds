Varia Mek Verksted

Contract manufacturing

Details and components from drawing, sample or template, from plasma cutting to complete products.

Varia has a well-equipped sheet-metal and mechanical workshop where we make almost any detail and component.

Technical help and problem solving are among our strengths. Send us a sketch or drawing of what you want and we will find the best production solution.

  1. Welded steel and sheet-metal structures
  2. Plasma cutting of details in steel, stainless and aluminium
  3. Components from drawing or sample / template
  4. Production of prototypes and complete products
  5. Machine building and rebuilding of machines
  6. Lifting equipment, cranes and lifting yokes
  7. Corten steel, planters, edge steel and gutters
  8. Pipe racks / piping in stainless or steel
  9. Conveyors and roller tracks
