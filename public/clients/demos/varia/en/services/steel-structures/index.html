Steel structures

The workshop is approved for production and installation of structural steel to NS-EN 1090-1.

Besides structural steel we have long experience producing sheet-metal and steel structures for industry and contractors. We build in steel, stainless / acid-proof or aluminium as you wish.

Typical examples of our earlier deliveries

  • Work platforms, walkways and gangways
  • Steel mezzanines
  • Stairs and crossings, railings
  • Steel guards for floors and walls
  • Machine foundations and machine frames
  • Crane tracks and lifting yokes, certified
  • Silos, tanks and vessels in steel, acid-proof and aluminium, complete pipe racks
  • Lift shafts and steel structures for buildings
