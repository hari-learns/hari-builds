Community sponsorship

Supporting the Mackay community for over 30 years

Every quarter, the team at Consolidated take the time to review applications from local community groups and charities as per the dates below. We’re proud to support a wide range of events, programs, and activities through sponsorships that help make a difference in the community we serve.

To apply, please fill out the form below or download the printable version to prepare your details. Once all details are completed, submit your application.

Your application covers

  • Organisation name
  • Website
  • Not-for-profit? (yes/no)
  • ABN
  • Contact name
  • Role
  • Phone
  • Email
  • Sponsored by Consolidated previously? If so, when?
  • Sponsorship amount requested ($)
  • Date requested by
  • Event date
  • Event location
  • Short description
