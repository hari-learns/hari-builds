About Consolidated

Knowledge from the past, innovating for the future.

Consolidated Engineering Plastics Products (formerly ‘Consolidated Plastics & Epoxy’) was first established in 1988 and is known for providing the highest level of innovative thinking and industry-leading solutions.

The knowledge and experience gained over previous decades has led us to having the highest level of innovative thinking and industry-leading solutions which we develop at our purpose-built facility in Mackay. Our in-house capabilities are second to none, with the ability to take a client’s project from concept to completion.
