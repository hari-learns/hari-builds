Safety – Quality – Efficiency

The on-site services team at Consolidated facilitates our manufacturing capabilities, with quality site-based support and installations. Throughout Australia, the site-services team can deliver epoxy and grout installations, Diacon guarding, flow promotion lining packages, and engineered solutions.

Our on-site services team are trained and qualified to install our products across Australia. From lining packages and guard installations, to flooring remunerations and heavy grout injections, our team is equipped to deliver on-site results with a key focus on safety and efficiency.

  • Epoxy and grout installations
  • Diacon guarding
  • Flow promotion lining packages
  • Engineered solutions
  • Heavy grout injections
