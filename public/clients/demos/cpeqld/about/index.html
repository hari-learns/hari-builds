About Consolidated

Knowledge from the past, innovating for the future.

At Consolidated we work with a broad range of materials, with a primary focus and unique expert knowledge of plastic, epoxy and rubber to create exceptional solutions for our clients.

Consolidated was first established in 1988 and formerly operated under the banner of ‘Consolidated Plastics & Epoxy’. In earlier times, Consolidated’s main focus was to provide high quality plastic, rubber and epoxy products, as well as offering technical support to developing industries.

After more than 30 years of operation, Consolidated is still achieving its original goals, however our capabilities have grown and evolved into a driving force for innovation and engineering.

The knowledge and experience gained over previous decades has led us to having the highest level of innovative thinking and industry-leading solutions which we develop at our purpose-built facility in Mackay. Our in-house capabilities are second to none, with the ability to take a client’s project from concept to completion.

It’s possible.

Who are we

“Consolidated can’t help my business… right?”
Wrong.

At Consolidated we understand that no two businesses or challenges are the same. Our diverse team of industry experts have the cumulative skills, experiences and backgrounds to provide solutions to a huge range of clients regardless of industry, size or location.

Our engineering, production and site services teams work together to provide an all-encompassing service including ongoing support to our clients across Australia. This is why our clients trust the team at Consolidated to keep their business moving.

When dealing with our team, you will only need one point of contact to move your project from start to finish. We keep things simple, to keep things moving.

Owner / Director

Brendan Refalo

Graduated as a mechanical engineer in 1993. Worked within the sugar industry in Mackay until 2001 as a mechanical engineer performing both maintenance management and over seeing capital projects for sugar factory processing improvements. After 2001 became part of Consolidated applying plastics, rubber, and epoxy materials to solve the reliability issues not only in the sugar industry but in coal mines, ports & other minerals processing industries of regional Queensland. Extensive industry experience has led to many successful cost reduction initiatives in these industries.
