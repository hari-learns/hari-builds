Elite AT43 layout and features

Three cabins, one bathroom with a separate shower, a full-size fridge freezer and a cockpit built for fishing, diving and dining.

Elite AT43 features and options: an overview

  • Powerful, reliable and efficient world-leading engine(s)
  • Elite Marine's renowned silent chines: tranquil sleep with no slap
  • Contemporary, comfortable interior with lounge seating covered in your choice of leather, hard wearing vinyl or fabric upholstery and substantial storage drawers below
  • Stylish galley featuring a laminate or solid surface bench top, modern tap ware and appliances
  • Full-size household fridge/freezer with vegetable drawer
  • Modern bathroom with laminate or solid surface bench top, vessel bowl, quality mixer tap, mirror and ample storage
  • High quality, hard wearing flooring
  • Thoughtfully positioned LED lighting throughout
  • Ample storage cabinets and drawers with easy lock hardware
  • Premium carpet and underlay
  • Soft furnishings and Manchester package options available
  • Entertainment system in the lounge including a flat screen TV and sound system
  • Separate shower stall with quality shower head as well as a hot shower on the aft deck
  • Toilet with fresh and salt water flush
  • Optional foredeck sunbathing area

Helm

  • Windscreen in tempered glass with wipers and fresh water washers
  • Electronic engine instrumentation and Multi-Function Display(s)
  • Comfortable, high quality leather (or your choice of upholstery) helm seat
  • Anchor winch and chain counter
  • Safety monitoring panel
  • A range of electronic packages are available

Who sleeps where

  • Master cabinisland berth
  • Starboard guest cabinking, converts to twin singles
  • Port cabinfull-length bunks

Cabin use as described by Power Boat Magazine (Dec 2025): owners in the island berth master, adult children in the starboard guest cabin, grandkids in the port cabin with full-length bunks.
