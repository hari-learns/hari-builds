The Elite AT43

A New Zealand made 43-foot motor yacht. Single or twin engines, three cabins, custom interior finishes.

Designed by Bill Upfold

Feels more like a custom build than a production boat.

We are proudly exclusively building The Elite AT43. This production (GRP Moulded) initiative is a 43-foot model designed to fit a 13.5m or 14m marina berth. With options for single or twin engines, a three cabin layout and a range of interior finish options, it will feel more like a custom build than a production boat.

The large versatile cockpit will suit the keen fisherman or diver, but is also spacious enough for outdoor dining or sunset cocktails. Uniquely designed silent chines make for a quiet nights sleep, no slapping even in a bit of chop.

You will no doubt recognise the sleek lines and intelligent design of Elite Marine. When paired with the established quality and distinction of Allan Tongs Boatbuilders, the result is a boat designed and built in New Zealand with the care and precision of a custom, bespoke motor yacht but with a production boat price tag.

Buyers ask

Questions buyers ask

Will the AT43 fit my marina berth?

It is a 43-foot model designed to fit a 13.5 m or 14 m marina berth. Length overall is 13.5 m to 13.8 m, beam 4.5 m, draft 1.05 m. The swim platform can be extended during the build to make full use of a 14 m berth (Power Boat Magazine, December 2025).

Single or twin engine?

Single Volvo D8 600 hp or twin 440 hp D6. Singles cruise at 20 kts and top out at 26 to 27; twins cruise at 26 kts and top out at 31 to 32. At cruise speed the fuel consumption per nautical mile is the same, says Nigel (Boating NZ, August 2026). Both options are shaft driven.

How big is the fuel tank?

1300 L is the published capacity on this site. Boating NZ (August 2026) reports 1,300 litres as standard, with 1,500 and 1,800 litre options for longer range. Water capacity is 720 L.

How many people can sleep aboard?

Six, in three cabins: an island-berth master, a starboard guest cabin with a king that converts to twin singles, and a port cabin with full-length bunks. Boating NZ also reports a two-cabin layout is possible, trading the third cabin for a second bathroom or laundry.

How long does a build take, and what does it cost?

Power Boat Magazine (December 2025) puts delivery at around six to eight months against more than a year for a similar custom boat, and the price around 20 percent below a like-for-like custom build. Boating NZ (August 2026) notes engines come from offshore and typically take about six months. Price and the next build slot are by enquiry: ask Nigel.

Can I change the interior?

Yes. Interior finishes, upholstery, benchtops and colours are chosen with Ester, and being custom boat builders the yard can modify elements to suit each customer, for example longer bunks or a reclining cockpit seat instead of the BBQ.

Is the cockpit set up for fishing and diving?

The large versatile cockpit suits the keen fisherman or diver and is also spacious enough for outdoor dining. A wide boarding platform keeps fishing mess outside, and the sliding transom doors open wide enough to pull the tender through.

Is it quiet at anchor?

The silent chines are designed to stop the slapping at anchor. Owners of Kokomea: 'one thing we are super impressed with is how quiet the boat is on anchor at night, such great sleeps!'

Can I buy an AT43 in Australia?

Yes: d'Albora Yacht Brokers is the Exclusive Authorised Dealer across Australia.
