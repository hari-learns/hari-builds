Elite AT43 specification

BuilderAllan Tongs Boatbuilders
Length overall13.5 m to 13.8 m

Fuel capacity1300 L
Water capacity720 L

Source: allantongsboatbuilders.co.nz/the-elite-at43.

Designed to fit a 13.5 m or 14 m marina berth.

Check my berth

Engines

Twin Volvo D6 440 hp

Twins cruise at 26 kts and top out at 31 to 32 (Boating NZ, Aug 2026). Sea-trial figures for a twin D6 440 hp boat are on the speed and fuel page.

Speed and fuel

Single Volvo D8 600 hp

Singles cruise at 20 kts and top out at 26 to 27 (Boating NZ, Aug 2026). Both options are shaft driven; bow and stern thrusters are standard.
