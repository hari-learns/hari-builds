Elite AT43 owners and reviews

Five owners in their own words, and the independent reviews of the boat.

Ikō Let's Go!

We took our brand new AT43 for its first proper trip away last week. Leaving our home base at Sandspit we headed down to the Coromandel area for five great days. This boat certainly lived up to its name Iko (Japanese for 'lets go'). Handling was a dream through all the sea conditions we experienced. We were very stable and comfortable even when well past the advised cruise speed. This will be a boat well suited to our Hauraki Gulf playground.

Prior to launch we went through a fun build process. Nigel, Ester and their crew are a well balanced and experienced team. Nothing seemed to phase them - I guess after 10 boats from this mould they have done it all before.

Darryl and Julia

Kokomea Sunset Glow

Before our beautiful new Elite AT43 we had a Rayglass 3500, this was our first launch after many years of small boating. We loved the open plan sedan style of the Rayglass but wanted a bit more space to accommodate family.

The AT43 ticked all our boxes; being NZ made in Auckland, so close to our Northland marine playground, still offering the open plan sedan, but with three cabins, and a practical but stylish design. Right from our first conversations we felt an immediate connection with Nigel and Ester. As owners of a family business, we have huge respect for them as the second generation at Allan Tongs Boat Builders.

The AT43 handles beautifully in all conditions so far and for the size of the boat, with twin Cummins 425hp engines it is surprisingly economical. There are so many great features, too many to list, but one thing we are super impressed with is how quiet the boat is on anchor at night, such great sleeps! Having the option to semi customise, choosing interior finishes and extras, allowed us to add our own touches and fine-tune the boat to meet our needs and priorities.

Alan and Annie

Kōkako

Nigel and Ester and the Alan Tongs team are simply outstanding. From the exceptional build quality, to their passion to ensure we were consistently delighted, we could not ask for more.

We love our boat and loved the whole build experience.

Ross and Jan

Amamus Latin for: We Love

We quite simply love our beautiful AT43, so we thought we would give you a list of all the things we really enjoy.

  • The boat is very stable.
  • The space is fantastic. Lots of room. We had the work crew on board on a not so good night and still managed to fit 15 inside. It was cosy.
  • The twin seat steering station - we use it all the time.
  • The nice wide boarding platform keeps fishing mess on the outside of the boat so really easy to keep clean.
  • Level walk through with only three steps forward - what a difference from being constantly up and down.
  • Having the enclosed back area - it gives us a dry outdoor space especially now its winter and when we have had rain all weekend. Wet gear is no longer a problem.
  • Having the aft helm station for berthing. Makes it super easy.
  • Having solar panels. In the middle of the day last Sunday when it was pretty gloomy, we were still getting 11 Amps.
  • The heater makes winter boating feasible, and in the winter you get lots of nice calm days.
  • The seating is really comfortable, and we are so glad we didn't go leather.
  • The electronics are great. A mate who is an Air NZ pilot said 'there is more technology on Amamus than on the ATR that he flies'.
  • Everyone comments how beautifully finished it is and the decor - thank you Ester.

One of our good boating friends said 'I've got boat envy'. And at the end of each weekend away we don't want to go home.

Paul and Jenny

Awatea Dawn of a New Day

We really do love everything about our AT43. The 3 cabin design is definitely a winner and is greatly admired by everyone who comes onboard.

Over each summer we take her out for about sixty consecutive days travelling from Tauranga to Great Mercury, Coromandel and Te Kouma harbour, Waiheke and Ponui, and to Great Barrier. GB is 115 nautical miles from Tauranga. In winter we took her out for a month making good use of her lovely heater, a well worthwhile extra as is the zen water maker! Priceless extra.

Wayne and Janet
