Who builds it

A family boatyard that stopped building one-offs and now builds one boat, very well.

Allan Tongs Boat builders has been building custom composite and traditional luxury boats in Auckland since 1983. Allan retired in 2022; his son Nigel and wife Ester are now at the helm, with a team of qualified boat builders and marine cabinet makers on Auckland's North Shore.

We are proudly exclusively building The Elite AT43: a production (GRP moulded) 43-foot model with the care and precision of a custom, bespoke motor yacht but with a production boat price tag.

The boat

The Elite AT43, room by room

Cockpit

The cockpit does everything

The large versatile cockpit will suit the keen fisherman or diver, but is also spacious enough for outdoor dining or sunset cocktails. The sliding transom doors open wide enough to pull the tender through without removing the outboard.

Galley

Galley and cockpit become one room

An electric galley window slides down and the glass door slides across, creating great indoor outdoor flow. The cook stays part of the party.

Sleep

Silent chines: no slap at anchor

Uniquely designed silent chines make for a quiet nights sleep, no slapping even in a bit of chop. Three cabins sleep six, with a choice of interior finishes chosen with Ester.

Helm

A helm with outstanding visibility

Modern, easy to use and with outstanding visibility. Bow and stern thrusters are standard, so berthing a 43-footer stays calm.
