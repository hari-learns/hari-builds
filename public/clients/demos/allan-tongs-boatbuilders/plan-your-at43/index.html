    What the planner uses
    Your berth
    The AT43 is a 43-foot model designed to fit a 13.5 m or 14 m marina berth. Length overall is 13.5 to 13.8 m, beam 4.5 m, draft 1.05 m.
    Your crew
    Three cabins sleep six: island-berth master, a guest cabin with a king that converts to twin singles, and a port cabin with full-length bunks.
    Your trip
    Knots and litres per nautical mile from the sea-trial table, a published tank size, and the reserve you choose.
