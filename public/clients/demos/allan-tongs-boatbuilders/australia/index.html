The Elite AT43 in Australia

d'Albora Yacht Brokers is the Exclusive Authorised Dealer across Australia for the Elite AT43. Email or call the broker, or use the planner and send the numbers.

The Elite AT43 is a 43-foot model designed to fit a 13.5 m or 14 m marina berth, with single or twin engine configurations, three cabins sleeping six and custom interior finishes.

Allan Tongs Boatbuilders builds every AT43 in Auckland, New Zealand. d'Albora Yacht Brokers lists the boat for Australian buyers (2026, price on application).
