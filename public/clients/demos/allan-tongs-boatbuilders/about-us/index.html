A family boatyard on Auckland's North Shore

Allan Tongs Boat builders has been in business building custom composite and traditional luxury boats in Auckland, New Zealand since 1983.

We are a family owned business with Allan retiring in 2022 and son Nigel and wife Ester now at the helm. We have a great team of specialist, qualified boat builders and marine cabinet makers working from a purpose built premises on Auckland North Shore. Members of the team have been involved in high profile composite builds, such as Volvo round-the-world race yachts and America's Cup yachts, bringing a broad skill-base and expertise.

We are proudly exclusively building The Elite AT43. This production (GRP Moulded) initiative is a new 43-foot model designed to fit a 13.5m or 14m marina berth.

So get in touch and lets talk about how we can get you on the water in the beautiful yet practical Elite AT43.

The craft

How an AT43 is built

  • Female moulds

    The yard builds in female GRP moulds, down to the dashboard, armrest, sunroofs and cupboard doors. Engine bearers and structural bulkheads go in while the hull is still in the mould.

  • Foam core, vinylester skin

    A vinylester outer skin and engineered laminates either side of structured PVC foam cores: a stiff, light build that saves weight and therefore fuel.

  • CNC cabinetry

    Interior cabinetry is ply-foam-ply, vacuum pressed and cut by CNC, then fitted by the yard's cabinet makers.

  • Your finishes

    Customers visit as their boat takes shape and choose upholstery, benchtops and colours with Ester.
