Automation that frees up people

Finmekanik has automated parts of production and invested in new machines. In the Svensk Verkstad interview the CEO explains that it is no longer one person who can run a given part: several can step in when needed. A new automation cell was ordered to be in place before Christmas 2025.

One new machine almost every year

'It is not every day but (almost) every year in recent years that we buy a new machine for the factory. Demand for our machining keeps increasing.' (news, 15 April 2025)
