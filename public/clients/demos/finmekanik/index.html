Process

How a part travels through our shop

Our working process for contract machining, step by step.

  1. 01

    Technical review

    We analyse the customer's documentation and identify critical dimensions and specifications together with the customer.

  2. 02

    Fixtures and tooling

    Our toolmakers and grinders develop optimal fixture solutions and tooling. For simpler machining we make our own fixtures in-house.

  3. 03

    Production and optimisation

    We produce samples and optimise the production process for maximum precision and efficiency.

  4. 04

    Delivery and quality assurance

    The product is delivered for evaluation and measurement. We ensure documented quality and repeatability ahead of series production.

Quality you can check

Certified since 1996, named supplier of the year in 2022 and 100% on delivery and quality for a full year.

ISO 9001ISO 9001 certified, continuously

We are certified in ISO 9001, a standard for the quality management system of a company. The quality management system describes how we continuously improve and adjust the business to meet our customers' needs. We consistently deliver products and services that meet customer and legal requirements and strive to increase customer satisfaction through efficient processes.

  • Mar 2023
