    About Richter Fertigungstechnik

    Owner-managed in Rankweil since 1994

    As a versatile production partner with a passion for innovation, we support you from development through prototyping to series production and ensure smooth supply of your products over the entire life cycle.

    1. 1994

      Richter Fertigungstechnik GmbH is founded by Herbert Richter in Rankweil: production and machining of sheet-metal parts and plates and machining of aluminium and plastic parts.

    2. 2015

      The company has been based in Rankweil since 2015; DI (FH) Alexander Richter takes over as managing director.

    3. Today

      The company currently employs 11 people.

    We deliver to

    • Vorarlberg
    • Liechtenstein
    • Ost-Schweiz
    • Bayern
    • Baden-Württemberg

    Own sheet and plate stock, large (redundant) machine park

    FAQs

    Frequently asked questions

    Are small series / small-scale production possible?

    There is no minimum order quantity. We make prototypes of just one piece as well as small quantities.

    How quickly can you start production?

    Depending on how well the specifications are already developed, within 3 working days.

    Which materials can you process?

    Sheet-metal parts, plates made of aluminium, copper and steel as well as plastic parts are no problem for us.

    How large can the workpieces be?

    Turned parts from 4 to 200 mm diameter

    Milling from 5 to 1200 mm thickness

    Eroding from 5 to 520 mm thickness

    Grinding from 5 to 600 mm thickness
