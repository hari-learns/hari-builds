About Richter Fertigungstechnik

Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication

History & background

Owner-managed in Rankweil since 1994

As a versatile production partner with a passion for innovation, we support you from development through prototyping to series production and ensure smooth supply of your products over the entire life cycle.

From our headquarters in Rankweil we can supply Vorarlberg, Liechtenstein, eastern Switzerland, Bavaria and Baden-Württemberg flexibly and quickly.

  1. 1994

    Richter Fertigungstechnik GmbH is founded by Herbert Richter in Rankweil: production and machining of sheet-metal parts and plates and machining of aluminium and plastic parts.

  2. 2015

    The company has been based in Rankweil since 2015; DI (FH) Alexander Richter takes over as managing director.

  3. Today

    The company currently employs 11 people.

We deliver to

  • Vorarlberg
  • Liechtenstein
  • Ost-Schweiz
  • Bayern
  • Baden-Württemberg

Philosophy & way of working

Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication

Our processes are designed to make things as simple as possible for our customers.

  • Early consulting

    Already in the development phase we offer comprehensive support to identify and avoid possible production problems early.

  • One-stop shop

    Because we cover everything from prototyping to series production, customers do not have to coordinate several suppliers.

  • Flexible lead times

    We adapt to the customer's needs, to deliver on time even with short-notice changes or tight schedules.

  • Design optimisation

    We help to design products for manufacturing, to reduce cost and production time.

  • Transparent and clear communication

    We value simple, clear communication and keep the customer regularly informed about project status, to avoid misunderstandings.

  • Logistics and procurement

    We take over the complete procurement process and ensure efficient, on-time supply of the materials needed.

  • Fast response times

    Thanks to short response times we can react flexibly to enquiries, changes or problems, so projects stay on schedule.
