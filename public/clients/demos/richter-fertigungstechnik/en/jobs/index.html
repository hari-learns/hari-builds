Build your career at Richter Fertigungstechnik in Rankweil, Vorarlberg!

Join our team! We are currently looking for:

Zerspanungstechniker (m/f/d), machinist

You are responsible for

  • Production of milled parts on CNC-controlled machines (samples and series)
  • Quality control
  • Independent creation of CNC programs (CamWorks, SolidWorks, Shopmill)
  • Process improvements and optimisation
  • Maintenance and care of the machines
  • Ensuring efficient machine use

We offer

  • Varied products and tasks
  • 4-day week / 38.5 h
  • Attractive working environment
  • Modern machine park
  • Young, motivated team
  • Pay according to collective agreement

You have

  • Completed vocational training (apprenticeship as machinist or equivalent)
  • Completed military or civilian service (Austria)

You are

  • Flexible and resilient
  • Curious and an optimiser
  • Independent
  • Thorough and consistent
  • Enthusiastic about technology

You want

  • Work-life balance
  • Performance-based pay
  • Great colleagues
