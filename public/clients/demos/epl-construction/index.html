What we do

Roads, drains, walls and wharves, built by one team

EPL has been providing civil construction since 1997 and understands planning and consent requirements, soil types, weather, and seasonal traffic patterns across the Coromandel Peninsula and central North Island.

  • Building site worksEPL has been servicing the residential and commercial building sectors for over 20 years.
  • Land developmentResidential and commercial land development, greenfield and brownfield, delivered on time and within budget.
  • DrainageThe construction of stormwater and drainage infrastructure is a core part of EPL's business.
  • WaterInstallation, repair and replacement of water supply and distribution infrastructure.
  • SewerResidential drainage systems and heavy civil projects: gravity sewers, rising mains and pump stations.
  • Road constructionSealed and unsealed urban and rural roads, access-ways, driveways, car parks and hardstands.
  • Retaining wallsTimber, concrete, block, tilt panel, gabion, rock and reinforced earth retaining walls.
  • Civil structuresHead-walls, outfalls, manholes, pump station chambers, culverts and fords, formed and poured by our own team.
  • FootpathsFootpaths, cycleways and trails in concrete, asphalt, aggregate and timber.
  • Coastal protection and marine workBoat ramps, pontoons and soldier walls built inside the tidal zone.
