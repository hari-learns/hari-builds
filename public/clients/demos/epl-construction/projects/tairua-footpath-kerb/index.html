Project

Tairua footpath and kerb and channel improvements

Thames-Coromandel District Council · Tairua

Construction of 590m of kerb & channel, 250m of 1.5m wide concrete footpath, 210m of new 225mm uPVC stormwater including catchpits and manholes, and 1100m2 of road pavement was undertaken to provide parallel parking and improve drainage adjacent the Tairua Sports Field and Rugby Ground. In addition new kerb and channel and footpath was also installed along a 160m section of Paku Drive.
