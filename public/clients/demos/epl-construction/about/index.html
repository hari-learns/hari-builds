About EPL Construction

Founded in 1997 and based in Whangamata, serving clients in building, land development, government and utilities infrastructure.

Founded in 1997, EPL Construction Ltd (EPL) has established itself as a valued contractor to clients in the Building, Land Development, Government and Utilities Infrastructure sectors. Delivering a diverse range of civil construction solutions to clients throughout the Coromandel Peninsula and central North Island, our specialties include building site works, earthworks, concrete works, land development, drainage, pipe laying, roads, pump stations, retaining walls, footpaths, coastal protection and marine work.

EPL has a strong reputation for being a resourceful and responsive civil engineering contractor, that clients can rely on to deliver. Our ability to self-perform the majority of the scope enables us to optimise project cost, quality and schedule to ensure we deliver the best possible outcomes for our clients. Over the last five years, we have significantly diversified our business and increased our capability and capacity in line with client needs. With significant in-house capability, a large inventory of owned plant and equipment and a strong management team, EPL has the resources and capability to ensure project success and quality outcomes.

EPL operates from a modern well-equipped office and depot facility located at 316 Aickin Road, Whangamata. Our fleet of well-maintained plant and equipment allows us to respond to and deliver a wider variety of complex and technically challenging projects. The fleet is regularly maintained and consistently updated to ensure that the plant and equipment is of sound, safe and reliable condition. All plant and equipment is fully owned by EPL and free of encumbrance.

We have reliable associations with local contractors including Barry Blake Construction, Brownmores, HEB, Hamish Stewart Construction to name a few, enabling us to provide efficient correlation on extensive projects.

Certified and approved

EPL Construction are certified members of Civil Contractors New Zealand and approved contractors with ISNetworld and SHE Health & Safety.

The team

People you will deal with

  • Bruce Connell

    Managing Director

    Bruce is a certified plumber and drainlayer and he has more than 36 years of experience in the civil construction industry working through-out New Zealand and Australia. Bruce's relevant project experience includes drain-laying, building site works, pile driving, bulk earthworks, road construction, tunneling, hard rock mining and the construction of water, sewer and stormwater pipe systems, pump stations, outfalls, intakes, retention basins, and pollutant traps.

  • Sheridan Fathers

    BE Civil (Hons), CPEng

    Sheridan has more than 15 years' experience in the civil construction industry both here in New Zealand and Australia. Sheridan is a member of the Engineering New Zealand Waikato Branch Committee. Being a Chartered Professional Engineer (CPEng) she has a strong knowledge of civil engineering principles and concepts. She is dedicated to ensuring health and safety, quality and environmental requirements are being implemented.

  • Alan Roberts

    Site Representative
