Company

A mechanical engineer's workshop in Bapunagar.

Vishvakarma Industries was established in 2004 by Mr. Tarun Panchal and makes timing pulleys, gears and other engineering components.

Vishvakarma Industries is a leading concern engaged in manufacturing and supplying mechanical power transmission products, situated in Ahmedabad, the commercial capital of the state of Gujarat.

The company makes a wide range of timing pulleys, used across industries such as textile machinery and the automobile industry.

Quality products at economic rates are the final interest of each customer, and Vishvakarma Industries serves power transmission products at a very competitive price.

We can prepare any type of timing pulley as per your specification and requirement. Please contact us for more details.
