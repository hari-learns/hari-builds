Medical-device components

Instruments and implants, from certified material.

Medical equipment is manufactured to international standards and procedure as per ISO 13485 for medical devices.

Raw material recommended for instruments and implants: mainly SS 316L meeting ISO 5932-1, and titanium alloys as per ISO 5932-2 and 3. Materials are tested to the standard before use in manufacturing.

Each product is made to quality standards with the required validation and a quality assurance plan.
