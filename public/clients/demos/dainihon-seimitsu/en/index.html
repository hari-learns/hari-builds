Dainihon Seimitsu Y.K.

From mould to finished part, under one roof

Draft English summary translated from the company's Japanese pages, to be confirmed by the company.

Dainihon Seimitsu Y.K. is in Toyohashi, Aichi. Founded in November 1999 with mould and press departments, it added plastic moulding in 2001 and can now take a customer's drawing through design, mould making, moulding, assembly and shipping.

Equipment: 53 injection moulding machines from 10 t to 300 t (40 horizontal, 13 vertical for insert moulding), 27 FANUC robots, 3 ultrasonic welders, 7 NC EDM machines, 3 wire EDM machines, 6 machining centres, and measuring equipment (CMM, 3D scanners, image measuring machines, digital microscope).

Materials: ABS, PA, PBT, PC, PE, POM, PP, PPS, PPE, TPE and others. For resins not tried before the company asks for the MSDS. Super engineering plastics such as PAR, PPS and PSF are also moulded.

Certification: ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 (certified by G-CERTI JAPAN, valid to 8 May 2028). 93 employees as of March 2025.

Contact: TEL +81-532-43-0208 / FAX +81-532-43-0207 / info@dainihon-s.co.jp. Address: 249-1 Kamonoyama, Kumoya-cho, Toyohashi, Aichi 441-3103, Japan.
