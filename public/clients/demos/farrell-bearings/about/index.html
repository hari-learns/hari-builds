An independent bearing specialist, 100% New Zealand owned

Farrell Bearings Ltd supplies industrial and recreational bearing and power transmission users.

Based in Hamilton New Zealand, Farrell Bearings Ltd supplies industrial and recreational bearing and power transmission users with quality products.

We are a long-established independent bearing specialist and an authorised distributor for Schaeffler Group (FAG, INA), Timken, NSK, and Renold.

We currently supply bearings and related products to many major industrial sites in New Zealand such as pulp and paper, timber, heavy transport, agriculture, automotive, power generation, and machine manufacturers, as well as many recreational applications.

Farrell Bearings is proudly 100% New Zealand owned and operated! If you can't identify what you require, please contact our experienced sales team for assistance.
