Find a bearing by its size

Type what you measured with a caliper. The finder looks through every metric ball bearing size on the shop.

Measure the bore, the outside and the width with a caliper, in that order. Your shop lists sizes as ID X OD X W. Try or .

Sizes are matched to the metric ball bearing list on the Farrell Bearings shop (443 sizes). Other bearing types, imperial sizes, housings and belts: call or email the sales team with your measurements. Always confirm fit with the sales team before you buy.

How to measure

ID
Inside diameter, or bore
OD
Outside diameter
W
Width

Where no part number is available, measure the dimensions in this order: inside diameter (bore), outside diameter, width. Enter them as, for example, 30X62X16.

More on identifying a bearing

If you can’t identify what you require, call the sales team.
