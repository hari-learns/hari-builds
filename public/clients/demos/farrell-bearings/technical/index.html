Identify a bearing

Bearings can usually be identified easily. If you cannot, we are able to assist.

Identify it

  1. Equipment manuals may clearly identify the bearings required with a part number.
  2. Most bearings have a clearly marked designation on the side face of the inner or outer ring. For sealed bearings it is often on the seal.
  3. Where no part number is available, measure the dimensions in this order: inside diameter (bore), outside diameter, width. Enter them as, for example, 30X62X16.
  4. If you cannot identify your bearing, call in to see us, send the bearing with as much information about the application as possible, or email us a photo.

Bearing types

  • Ball bearings
  • Cylindrical roller bearings
  • Needle roller bearings
  • Tapered roller bearings
  • Spherical roller bearings

Stop counterfeit bearings

Farrell Bearings Ltd ensures all product is genuine. Counterfeit product is a serious issue in the bearing market and it is not always easy to identify by end users. Products can look very similar to the genuine item and have similar markings and packaging. Some will even have certificates of authenticity to convince you of their integrity.

Source from authorised distributors

This is the best way to ensure that your product has come directly from the manufacturer. Farrell Bearings is an authorised distributor for FAG, INA, NSK and TIMKEN, and ensures its other products are sourced from manufacturers directly or their authorised distributors.

Be wary of a fantastic deal

Counterfeit product is often significantly cheaper than original product. If the brand you are buying is considered a quality brand and it is being offered at a budget price, make sure you can trace its authenticity.
