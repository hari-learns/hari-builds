Genuine, or nothing

Farrell Bearings Ltd ensures all product is genuine.

Counterfeit product is a serious issue in the bearing market and it is not always easy to identify by end users. Products can look very similar to the genuine item and have similar markings and packaging. Some will even have certificates of authenticity to convince you of their integrity.

Source from authorised distributors

This is the best way to ensure that your product has come directly from the manufacturer. Farrell Bearings is an authorised distributor for FAG, INA, NSK and TIMKEN, and ensures its other products are sourced from manufacturers directly or their authorised distributors.

Be wary of a fantastic deal

Counterfeit product is often significantly cheaper than original product. If the brand you are buying is considered a quality brand and it is being offered at a budget price, make sure you can trace its authenticity.
