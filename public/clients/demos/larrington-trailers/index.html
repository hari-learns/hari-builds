Engineered, not assembled

Proper engineering beats cost-cutting every time.

No shortcuts. Our patented folded and formed chassis isn't just stronger than conventional box section: it's proof that proper engineering beats cost-cutting every time. When we designed the single tipping ram in 1995, everyone said twin rams were necessary. We proved them wrong.

1995

First single tipping ram

Uses less oil and pressure per tonne than a twin-ram system, reducing wear on every part, including your tractor's oil pump.

60°

First to tip to 60°

The Majestic was the first beet-heaping trailer. Larrington's single ram lifts the loaded body to 60°; Larrington says its competitors only lift to 50°.

2006

Patented 'M' chassis

Designed and patented by Larrington, the 'M' style chassis gives your trailer added strength while being lighter than a conventional box section chassis.

1990

First air suspension

First air suspension on agricultural trailers, one of the Larrington firsts since the first hydraulic taildoor in 1978.

Since 1973

From muddy fields to industry revolution.

Ray Larrington had a problem. Running the largest agricultural contracting company in the UK, he was tired of wooden trailers breaking under the brutal demands of British farming. So he built something better.

The full story

  1. 1962Ray Larrington establishes the largest UK agricultural contracting company
  2. 1965First custom steel-bodied trailers with commercial axles
  3. 1973RL Trailers founded: dedicated manufacturing
  4. 1978First hydraulic taildoor
  5. 1990First air suspension on agricultural trailers
  6. 1994Richard Larrington takes over and moves the company from Brothertoft to Boston
  7. 1995First single tipping ram
  8. 1996Majestic beet-heaping trailer wins a LAMMA award; first to tip to 60°
  9. 2006Patented 'M' style folded and formed chassis
  10. 2011UK-wide dealer network established
  11. 2023Hire fleet starts working on farms
