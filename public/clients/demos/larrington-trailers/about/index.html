About us

From muddy fields to industry revolution

Ray Larrington had a problem. Running the largest agricultural contracting company in the UK, he was tired of wooden trailers breaking under the brutal demands of British farming. So he built something better. When Richard took over the business in 1994, he didn't just continue the legacy: he transformed it through engineering excellence and relentless innovation.

Two innovators, one vision.

  1. 1962Ray Larrington establishes the largest UK agricultural contracting company
  2. 1965First custom steel-bodied trailers with commercial axles
  3. 1973RL Trailers founded: dedicated manufacturing
  4. 1978First hydraulic taildoor
  5. 1990First air suspension on agricultural trailers
  6. 1994Richard Larrington takes over and moves the company from Brothertoft to Boston
  7. 1995First single tipping ram
  8. 1996Majestic beet-heaping trailer wins a LAMMA award; first to tip to 60°
  9. 2006Patented 'M' style folded and formed chassis
  10. 2011UK-wide dealer network established
  11. 2023Hire fleet starts working on farms

The Larrington standard

Four principles that have driven decades of innovation.

01

Engineer it right

No shortcuts. Our patented folded and formed chassis isn't just stronger than conventional box section: it's proof that proper engineering beats cost-cutting every time. When we designed the single tipping ram in 1995, everyone said twin rams were necessary. We proved them wrong.

02

Build it to order

Your farm isn't a template, so why should your trailer be? Every Larrington is custom-built in Boston to your exact specifications. Chassis length, suspension type, brake system, paintwork.

03

Innovate relentlessly

First hydraulic taildoor (1978). First air suspension on agricultural trailers (1990). First to tip to 60° (1996).

04

Stand behind it

2-pack marine grade paint. Commercial-spec axles. British manufacturing. These aren't marketing terms: they're commitments.

Worldwide

Used more overseas than at home

Although Larrington Trailers is a UK manufacturer, our Potato Box Loaders are now used more overseas than at home. Growers in Slovakia, Germany, Poland, Australia, Tasmania, Japan, Ireland, Canada and the USA trust our box loaders to protect their crops season after season.
