Tipping trailers

Larrington tipping trailers are engineered for strength, stability and fast unloading. Whether moving soil, stone, grain or general farm materials, our tipping trailers deliver dependable performance day after day.

Our range includes single-axle tipping trailers, tandem-axle tipping trailers, high-capacity bulk tippers, silage and grain tippers, and bespoke tipping bodies. Every tipping trailer is manufactured in the UK using high-quality materials and proven engineering techniques.

Our tipping trailers are designed with reinforced bodies, powerful hydraulic rams and stable chassis geometry to ensure safe operation even under heavy loads. Key features include high-strength steel bodies, robust tipping cylinders, hydraulic tailgates, grain chutes and silage sides, LED lighting and safety systems, plus air or hydraulic brakes.

Benefits include fast unloading times, reduced operator effort, long-term reliability, and suitability for agriculture, construction and industrial use.

Discover the Larrington Majestic range of tipping trailers—engineered in the UK for heavy-duty performance and reliability. The Majestic combines robust construction with powerful hydraulic tipping, making it the go-to choice for demanding agricultural and industrial applications. With custom specifications, advanced hydraulics, and exceptional build quality, Larrington Trailers delivers tipping solutions that exceed expectations across farms, construction sites, and commercial operations throughout the UK and beyond.

Engineering Excellence

Every Larrington tipping trailer is built to the highest standards using premium materials and cutting-edge manufacturing techniques. Our trailers feature reinforced chassis construction, hydraulic tipping systems that deliver smooth and reliable operation, and corrosion-resistant finishes that protect your investment for years to come.

Backed by decades of trailer manufacturing expertise since 1973, we know what it takes to build equipment that performs reliably day in, day out. From initial design through to final assembly, every detail is carefully considered to maximise durability, safety, and efficiency.

  • Heavy-duty steel construction with reinforced loadbeds
  • Advanced hydraulic systems for smooth tipping
  • Corrosion-resistant coatings and finishes
  • Precision-engineered components throughout

Versatile Applications

Our tipping trailer range is designed to handle a diverse array of applications across multiple industries. Whether you’re transporting grain, root crops, aggregates, or general agricultural produce, our trailers provide the capacity, stability, and reliability you need to get the job done efficiently.

Each model in our range offers unique features tailored to specific applications. The Harvester excels in general agricultural use, the Majestic handles heavy-duty commercial operations, the Rootking is optimized for root crop harvesting, and the Tri-Axle Majestic delivers maximum capacity for the most demanding tasks.
