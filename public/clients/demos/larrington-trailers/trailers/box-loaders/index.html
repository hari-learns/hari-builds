Box and bag loaders

Our box loader range brings together the Guardian Potato Box Loader, the new Guardian HCS Box Loader and the Winglift Carrot Box Loader to give growers a complete solution for gentle, controlled box loading.

From cushioned tops and patented Cushion Lock technology to angled loading for root crops, each model is designed to reduce damage, protect crop quality and keep loading efficient in both field and store.

Potato growers know that the real profit is not just in lifting the crop—it’s in protecting its quality all the way into store. The Larrington Potato Box Loader range, including the Guardian chute system and the original Cushion-Lock loader, is designed for one purpose: to move potatoes from the ground straight into boxes without skin damage or bruising.

By combining controlled cushioning with a carefully engineered loading path, our box loaders gently place potatoes into the box instead of dropping or throwing them. The result is a cleaner, brighter sample that holds its value in store and gives growers a clear edge in a competitive market.

With much of the world transporting potatoes long distance using big bags, a Big Bag Loader is a natural progression from our proven box loading technology.

This category highlights our approach to big bag loading: simple, easily serviceable hydraulics, gentle crop handling and components that customers can easily source locally wherever they are in the world.

Guardian HCS Box Loader Overview

The Guardian HCS Box Loader is the natural progression from the original Guardian Potato Box Loader by Larrington Trailers. While the concept came about when developing a system to handle cabbages, the HCS platform is capable of handling multiple crops with ease, allowing you to load directly from the harvester into boxes.

  • Soft cushioned top: A thick layer of protective foam across the top surface helps eliminate the excess damage usually caused when loading into a conventional trailer.
  • Patented Cushion Lock system: Cushion locks, originally built with cabbages in mind but developed to handle a wide range of crops, ensure the crop is distributed in a controlled and gentle way into the boxes below.
  • Easy unloading: Once the full length of the top is filled, the box loader is taken back to the farm where the top frame is lifted to fill the boxes. An electric hydraulic system in the tractor cab gives the driver full control over the lift speed and loading of the boxes.

Winglift & Guardian Box Loader Range

Our Winglift Box Loader is designed to load carrot crops while reducing tuber damage and stem cracking. By angling the boxes, the trailer allows the crop to glide down the inside of the box, with each box loaded individually so it can be filled to maximum capacity.

Although this trailer was originally designed with carrots in mind, its effectiveness is not limited to carrot crops and it works just as well with potato crops. The Winglift Box Loader has proved very popular in Poland and other overseas markets, where an additional operator on the harvester often controls the elevator from the side of the machine to maximise box fill and minimise waste. The top and side of this loader are covered in 50 mm of foam, reducing damage to the crop.

The Guardian Box Loader by Larrington Trailers perfectly completes the chain of tasks carried out by high-value harvesting and storage equipment. Instead of dropping your crop into the bottom of a wooden box, the Guardian Loader with its 50 mm cushioned top receives the crop gently and allows it to transfer into the waiting box with ease.

  • The transfer chute stays closed until around 25 kg, then gently pushes the cushioned rollers open so the crop slides down into the box.
  • Loaded like a standard trailer, helping to avoid elevator damage when an elevator catches on a box.
  • Safer for the driver and harvester, reducing the risk of costly elevator repairs.
  • Once back in the store, the top section can be raised slowly and gently to finish loading the boxes.

Why Gentle Loading Matters

Impact damage and skinning during lifting are some of the biggest causes of lost value in potato crops. Every bruise or scuff is a potential downgrade later in the season. Traditional loading systems rely on gravity and speed, which can be harsh on the crop—especially premium varieties and long-term storage potatoes.

Both the Guardian and Cushion-Lock systems use controlled cushioning to slow and support potatoes as they enter the box, reducing impact. Potatoes are guided into position, not thrown, which protects skin finish.

  • Controlled cushioning slows and supports potatoes as they enter the box
  • Gentle placement, not dropping—protects skin finish
  • Direct loading from field to box without running through a grader
  • More of your crop reaches the end customer in top condition

Reduced Labour, Smarter Grading

During lifting, labour is expensive and graders are often under-used. With the Larrington Potato Box Loader, you can load directly into boxes in the field—no need to run the crop across an expensive grader during lifting. The grader can be reserved for when you’re actually preparing crop for market.

Although Larrington Trailers is a UK manufacturer, our Potato Box Loaders are now used more overseas than at home. Growers in Slovakia, Germany, Poland, Australia, Tasmania, Japan, Ireland, Canada and the USA trust our box loaders to protect their crops season after season.

A box loader is not a short-term purchase. Larrington Potato Box Loaders are engineered for a working life of around 20 years, giving growers long-term protection for their most valuable asset, their crop.

  • Seed potato growers needing perfect skin finish
  • Table potato producers supplying supermarkets and premium markets
  • Export-focused farms where quality claims are costly
  • Growers lifting directly into long-term storage

Bag Loader Overview

With much of the world transporting potatoes long distance using ‘Big Bags’, a Big Bag Loader was a natural progression from the Guardian Box Loader.

Simplicity and ease of use have always been at the forefront of our designs, and our Big Bag Loader is no different. Unlike alternative brands, we have kept our hydraulics simple, and reduced mechanical parts while using components which our customers can easily source wherever they are.

  • Cushioned Top: Just like our Guardian Box Loaders, the Big Bag Loader is fitted with a cushioned top to gently receive the crop from the elevator.
  • Camera System: To ensure the driver can keep an eye on what’s going, the Big Bag Loader is fitted with a camera system that feeds to a monitor (supplied with the trailer) in the tractor cab.
  • Simple Bag Release System: The bags are loaded by opening the cushioned floor section, allowing potatoes to gently flow down, before raising the top frame/hopper to complete the load. When done, the bags are lowered on to the floor, the articulated hooks release the bags and the driver can simply pull forward leaving the bags in place, ready to be loaded for transport.

Advanced Features & Maintenance

Our Big Bag Loader is engineered for optimal performance and user convenience, integrating smart design choices to enhance efficiency and ensure longevity in demanding agricultural environments.

  • Safety First: When pulling forward to release the bags, a prop is automatically engaged to ensure it is safe for the next lot of bags to be hooked up for loading. Closing the gates, automatically releases the prop so you can begin the next load.
  • Easily Serviceable with Minimal Downtime: The heavy duty build quality and simplicity of the design mean our customers aren’t likely to ever face any issues, but the use of easily sourced and commonly stocked parts ensure minimal down-time no matter where you are.
