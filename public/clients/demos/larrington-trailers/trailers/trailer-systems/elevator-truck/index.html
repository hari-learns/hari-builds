Elevator Truck

Price on request

The Larrington Elevator Truck is engineered to the same high standards as every Larrington machine and built to handle a wide variety of crops with ease. Designed for efficiency and stability, it features front hydraulic support legs that create an exceptionally steady platform while an agricultural trailer tips into the rear hopper.

From there, the powerful elevator system transfers the crop smoothly into even the tallest of commercial lorries, making high‑level loading effortless. The result is faster turnaround times, reduced handling, and a cleaner, more controlled loading process—ideal for farms, contractors, and large‑scale crop movements.

When prolonged heavy rain hits, it doesn’t just make harvesting more difficult—it dramatically increases the amount of mud carried onto public roads as trailers leave the field. Farmers are legally responsible for any mud their machinery deposits on the highway, and if the road becomes hazardous, this can lead to prosecution. In many cases, it also results in costly, time‑consuming road‑cleaning operations to stay compliant and keep other road users safe.

Using the Larrington Elevator Truck keeps the agricultural trailer in the field and loads directly into commercial lorries, massively reducing the amount of mud transferred onto the highway. Less road contamination means fewer clean‑ups, lower operating costs, and a safer, more efficient harvest operation.
