Trailer systems

An evolution of Larrington’s renowned easy rollover sheet system — now hydraulically operated. Developed in direct response to customer demand, our hydraulic sheeting system is unique to Larrington Trailers and engineered to work seamlessly alongside your trailer’s tipping and taildoor operation, keeping you focused on the job.

Our tyre inflation and deflation system lets you reduce pressures in the field to protect soil structure, then quickly return to safe road pressures, controlling both tractor and trailer tyres together from a simple tractor-mounted unit.

Discover our premium range of tipping trailers, engineered in the UK for superior performance and reliability. From the robust Elevator Truck to the heavy-duty Tri-Axle Majestic, each trailer is built to withstand the toughest agricultural and industrial applications. With custom specifications, advanced hydraulic systems, and exceptional build quality, Larrington Trailers delivers solutions that exceed expectations across farms, construction sites, and industrial operations throughout the UK and beyond.

System Highlights

Key benefits of the system at a glance.

  • A Complete System: Fit the inflation/deflation kit to both tractor and trailer so the whole outfit can run at the correct pressures.
  • Fast Inflation / Deflation: Move from field to road pressures in around 90 seconds, keeping you working instead of waiting.

In the Field and On the Road

How the system supports day-to-day operations.

  • Easily Swap Between Tractors: Front-bumper-mounted hardware on the 3-point linkage makes it straightforward to move the system between suitable tractors.
  • Easy Operation: Control tractor and trailer (or other appliance) tyres together from a single in-cab switch, making pressure changes quick and repeatable.
