Robust Construction & Durability

Every Larrington flat deck trailer is engineered for exceptional durability and long-lasting performance, built to withstand the toughest agricultural and commercial applications. Our chassis construction utilises 250x150x6mm RHS box section, cross-braced with 60×40 box section and finished with a 4mm steel plate floor covering. Commercial-grade components are standard throughout, including full commercial ten stud axles with 406 x 120 brakes set at 1900 track for enhanced stability and reliability. A 2ft (60cm) headboard is fitted as standard along with twin rear LED lights, rear towing eyes, and a full-width bumper, ensuring your trailer is ready for immediate deployment in demanding operational environments.

Backed by decades of manufacturing experience since 1973.

  • Heavy-duty 250x150x6mm RHS box section chassis construction
  • 4mm steel plate floor covering for maximum durability
  • Commercial ten stud axles with 406 x 120 brakes as standard
  • Standard 2ft headboard with twin rear LED lights and full-width bumper
  • Backed by decades of manufacturing experience since 1973.

Versatile Applications & Customisation

Our comprehensive flat deck trailer range delivers exceptional versatility across diverse industries and applications, providing reliable transport solutions for bales, boxes, machinery, vehicles, and personnel. From standard Flat Deck and Dolly Flat Deck configurations for general agricultural use, to specialised Low Loaders for vehicle transport, Hydraulic Beavertails for equipment loading, Curtain-Siders for weather-protected goods, and People Carriers for personnel transport, we offer a solution for every requirement. Extensive customisation options are available including ladder bale racks, hydraulic box pushers, pull-over sheets, side access ladders, and our innovative Raise and Lower Drawbar system. Single line hydraulic brakes or twin line air brake systems can be specified to suit your towing vehicle, ensuring optimal compatibility and performance.

  • Agricultural bale and box transport with optional ladder racks
  • Vehicle and machinery transport with low loader and beavertail options
  • Weather-protected goods transport with curtain-sider configuration
  • Personnel transport solutions for agricultural and commercial operations
  • Comprehensive range of optional extras and customisation options

Adaptable by Design

The Transporters range is engineered around real-world flexibility. Seating and canopy assemblies remove quickly, converting a people carrier into a flat-deck trailer when the need arises. The Cattle Transporter’s hinged dividing walls allow the body to be reconfigured instantly — or removed altogether for conventional flat-deck bale and box work. Whatever the season demands, a Larrington transporter adapts to meet it.
