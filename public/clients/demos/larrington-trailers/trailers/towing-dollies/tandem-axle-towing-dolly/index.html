Tandem Axle Towing Dolly

Price on request

Designed to be coupled to long commercial trailers, the Larrington Towing Dolly (also known as a artic/articulated dolly) can allow a tractor to tow commercial trailers across fields so that they can be loaded directly by a harvester.

Built to order in Boston, Lincolnshire. Price is given on request, as on larringtontrailers.com.

This has become quite a popular unit for customers both here in the UK and across Eastern Europe and Africa.

The unit features a central rocking suspension system (Bogie suspension), and can come with a variety of options to ensure you get the towing dolly you need for the job. From powered axles to self-tipping systems, read on to find out more.

Central Rocking Suspension

Fitted as standard to the Larrington Towing Dolly, this system provides maximum oscillation for the suspension over rough and uneven terrain while keeping the weight of the load firmly on the back of the tractor; helping the tractor pull the trailer more easily.

JOST Fifth Wheel Coupling

A standard feature of the Larrington Towing Dolly, the Jost fifth wheel coupling plate is fitted at a height of 1200mm from the ground. This allows the unit to easily revers under a commercial trailer for coupling. Fitted with heavy duty rear chassis member, LED Lights and air couplings so the air to the trailer can connected and braking can happen.

Wide Range Of Suitable Tyres

The Unit can be supplied with a wide range of 45 profile tyres that help to keep the height of the unit down, making coupling on to a JOST fifth wheel plate easy.

Powered ‘Driven’ Axles Available

Power drive axles can be fitted to greatly increase the pulling ability of the towing dolly in wet conditions. Operated by the PTO shaft of the tractor, you can achieve 8 wheel drive by fitting power drive axles to your Towing Dolly.

Self-contained Tipping System

Yes, you read correctly. The Towing Dolly can be fitted with a self-contained tipping system enabling it to tip a commercial trailer. This includes a pump, motor, and oil tank that is driven by the spool system from the tractor.
