Low loaders

Larrington low loader trailers are built for safe, stable transport of agricultural and industrial machinery. Designed for strength and ease of loading, our low loaders are ideal for tractors, telehandlers, diggers and specialist equipment.

Our low loaders feature strong chassis construction, secure tie-down points and low-angle ramps for easy loading of wheeled or tracked machines. Key features include heavy-duty ramps, reinforced deck, multiple lashing points, hydraulic or spring-assisted ramp options, LED lighting and safety systems, and air or hydraulic braking.

All low loaders are built at our Boston, Lincolnshire facility and can be customised to suit your machinery and transport requirements.

Built for Versatility

Larrington low loader trailers are built to the highest standards for heavy-duty transport of vehicles and machinery. The range is designed to give you the best of both worlds: load and transport plant and equipment with ease, while retaining the ability to use the trailer as a conventional flat-deck for bales or boxes.

Our low loaders use underslung suspension to keep the deck as low as possible, reducing overall height and keeping the centre of gravity low for stability and access. We offer a choice of ramp options—from standard and spring-assisted ramps to hydraulic ramps or a hydraulic beavertail—so you can choose the solution that best suits your needs.

  • Dual use: machinery transport and flat-deck bale/box trailer
  • Underslung suspension for low height and low centre of gravity
  • Ramp options: standard, spring-assisted, hydraulic ramps or beavertail
  • Tailored to your requirements

Versatile Applications

Our low loader range is built for the transport of vehicles and machinery over short and long distances. Whether you’re moving tractors, diggers, or other plant, the low profile and stable load bed make loading and transport straightforward and secure.

When you’re not carrying machinery, the same trailer works as a conventional flat-deck for bales or boxes. With different ramp options available—standard, spring-assisted, hydraulic ramps, or hydraulic beavertail—you can specify the setup that fits your operation.

  • Vehicle and machinery transport over short or long distances
  • Conventional flat-deck use for bales and boxes
  • Choice of ramp types to suit your loading needs
  • Low height and low centre of gravity for stability
