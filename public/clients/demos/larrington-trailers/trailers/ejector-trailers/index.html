Advanced Ejector Technology

Every Larrington ejector trailer incorporates sophisticated ejector technology engineered for precision material discharge. Our advanced ejector systems utilise powerful hydraulic mechanisms that push materials forward with controlled force, ensuring complete and efficient unloading without manual intervention.

Backed by decades of manufacturing expertise since 1973, our ejector trailers feature robust steel construction, precision-engineered ejector plates, and advanced hydraulic controls. Each component is designed to work seamlessly together, delivering dependable performance that maximises productivity while minimising downtime.

  • Heavy-duty ejector plate systems with reinforced construction
  • Advanced hydraulic ejector mechanisms for smooth material discharge
  • Corrosion-resistant finishes protecting against harsh operating conditions
  • Precision-engineered components ensuring consistent ejector performance

Efficient Unloading Solutions

Our ejector trailer range delivers exceptional unloading performance across diverse industries and applications. The ejector mechanism eliminates the need for tipping, making these trailers ideal for operations where controlled material discharge is essential, whether you’re handling grain, root crops, aggregates, or bulk agricultural materials.

Each model in our ejector range is purpose-built for specific operational requirements. The standard Ejector provides reliable bulk material discharge, the Ejector Blower combines ejector technology with air-assisted distribution, the Ejector With Side Elevator offers enhanced loading flexibility, the Ejector Chaser Trailer excels in material transfer operations, and the Beet Chaser is specifically optimised for root crop harvesting with precision ejector control.

  • Agricultural grain and crop unloading with minimal material loss
  • Construction site material discharge in confined spaces
  • Industrial bulk material handling requiring controlled unloading
  • Commercial operations demanding efficient material transfer
