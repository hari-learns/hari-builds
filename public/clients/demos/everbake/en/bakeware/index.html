Bakeware

Bread tins and baking trays for the bakery

EverBake has its own production of bread tins, baking trays and other types of bakeware. Our engineers design all kinds of baking trays, bread tins, peelboards and coatings for the bakery.

EverBake Bakeware & Coatings produces and supplies a wide range of folded and deep-drawn bread tins, solid trays, perforated trays and baguette trays in various types and sizes for artisanal and industrial bakeries. After production the tins can be given a non-stick coating.

The range

Bread tins

  • Deep-drawn or folded tins
  • Aluminised steel (alusteel)
  • Various sizes possible
  • Options such as ribs, holes, stackable
  • Custom made, suitable for automation
  • Non-stick coating possible

Baking trays

  • Available in different materials
  • Various sizes available
  • Perforated or non-perforated
  • Custom made, suitable for automation
  • Non-stick coating possible

Baguette trays

  • Number and length of troughs made to measure
  • Perforated aluminium or steel
  • Non-stick coating possible

Peelboards

ABS plastic with a 2 mm aluminium frame, lightweight but scratch and dent resistant.

Coating and recoating

EverBake helps bakeries worldwide with coating or recoating bread tins and baking trays. The coatings have excellent non-stick properties, so greasing is almost never needed. High heat resistance and very good wear resistance give a long life. EverBake also offers a quick recoating service, using a modern and environmentally friendly cleaning technique to remove the old coating.
