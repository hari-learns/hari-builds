ZELA

ZELA greasing machines and spray solutions for the bakery

ZELA is the brand name for greasing machines, and also for spraying machines for, for example, tiger paste. The unique spray technology applies release agent, tiger paste, jelly or chocolate accurately, at low pressure and in an even pattern over the baking tins or products.

ZELA machines are produced and supplied exclusively by Zela Technical Solutions, part of EverBake Group, which also provides maintenance and service with its own service engineers across the Netherlands.
