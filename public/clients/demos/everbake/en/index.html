EverBake Group offers bakery solutions for every bakery. From bakeware and coatings, spray solutions to ovens, refrigerators and machines.

Family business

A family business with vision

Under the leadership of founder Peter Eversdijk, who comes from a family of bakers, EverBake grew into a leading player with a broad and innovative range of bakeware, coatings, machines, ovens, and cooling and proofing solutions.

With the arrival of the second Eversdijk generation, EverBake is taking the next step in the family business's development.

Experience Center

Headquarters and Experience Center in Breda

On Monday 6 October the new EverBake Experience Center in Breda was all about oliebollen. Together with Zeelandia, EverBake organised a demonstration afternoon with about 60 visitors from bakeries, ice cream parlours and catering.

On 13 October the demo was at Bakkerij Hendrik Schaap in Marknesse, with about 50 visitors.
