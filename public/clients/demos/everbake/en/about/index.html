About EverBake Group

A family business with vision

EverBake Group is an internationally operating supplier and manufacturer for artisanal and industrial bakeries. Under the leadership of founder Peter Eversdijk, who comes from a family of bakers, EverBake grew into a leading player with a broad and innovative range of bakeware, coatings, machines, ovens, and cooling and proofing solutions.

With the arrival of the second Eversdijk generation, EverBake is taking the next step in the development of the family business.

Thirty years of business, in years

Years from the family page and the interview with Peter Eversdijk on everbake.com (translated).

  1. 1986

    Peter Eversdijk joins the family business of his father and brothers, a bakery and raw materials firm.

  2. 1992

    Peter founds EverBake, with a clear vision: supplying high-quality Teflon coatings for bakeries.

  3. 2001

    Takes over customer Faber Metaalindustrie; renamed EverBake in 2006.

  4. 2011 – 2013

    Takes over Becker-Bongard ovens (importer of Bongard in the Netherlands) and Capway Pan.

  5. 2016

    Zela Technical Solutions joins the group.

  6. 2019

    EverBake becomes dealer of the HEUFT-ICECOOL group.

  7. Now

    The second generation steps up: Thom Eversdijk for Finance, HR, Purchasing and Operations; Nick Eversdijk for sales and business development.

We deliberately do not choose forced innovation. We ask our customers where improvements lie and try to respond.

Nick Eversdijk

The second generation

With their shared commitment and fresh outlook, Thom and Nick build on the foundation laid over the past decades, with respect for the values and way of working that made EverBake big.
