Frying lines

CREARE frying line, for oliebollen, apple fritters and other fried products

Frying a good oliebol (Dutch doughnut) is not only about fresh oil, quality ingredients and the right frying time. A constant temperature in the fryer matters too, and this is where the CREARE excels. Its heating elements deliver constant heat transfer, and the first element is more powerful so cold dough barely affects the oil temperature. The fryer is insulated, so less heat is lost and less electricity is used.

Automatic pushing of the heavy baskets

The CREARE is available with an automatic basket pusher that can even be added to an existing line later. With a fixed frying time it keeps quality constant. The Unidos depositor controls size, speed and number of doughnuts per minute (about 120 per minute). The line is made of high-quality stainless steel, and with a cover plate it can serve as a worktable.

Automatic oil refill

With the automatic refill system you can keep frying continuously. Oil is added drop by drop, so level and temperature stay right, and the baker sets how many litres per hour are added.
