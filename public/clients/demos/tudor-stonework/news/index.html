News

From Tudor's own news page. Dates are shown only where Tudor printed them.

Approved members of FORS

Tudor Stonework have achieved Bronze Level accreditation for the Fleet Operator Recognition Scheme (FORS), which allows us to measure and monitor the performance of our operations and allows for best practice.

Schluter Approved Installers

Tudor Stonework are the first Approved Installers of the industry-leading Schluter waterproofing system. We are very proud to be able to offer our clients a wider range of services.

December 2016

Stone Federation Awards ceremony

Tudor Stonework were invited to the Stone Federation Awards ceremony and had a thoroughly enjoyable time viewing all the high calibre projects entered into this year's competition. Big congratulations to the winners and runners-up.

CHAS accredited and Constructionline members

We have achieved CHAS accreditation status. The CHAS scheme assesses suppliers' health and safety competence. Being CHAS approved means we are compliant with the relevant health and safety guidelines.

We are also members of Constructionline, the UK's leading procurement and supply chain management service that collects, assesses and monitors standard company information.

2016

Proud finalists at the Construction News Awards

Tudor Stonework attended the Construction News Awards ceremony hoping to win the Specialists category for our work at the Burlington Arcade. We had a fantastic night, and despite coming in as runners-up, we've been congratulated on our work by our peers and are feeling really very chuffed with ourselves.

2016

Shortlisted for Project of the Year

We are very proud to have been shortlisted for the Construction News Project of the Year award for our work at the Burlington Arcade, an iconic London landmark. Architect: Jamie Fobert Architects. Completed July 2015.

The Burlington Arcade project

Mingling with celebs at the Burlington Arcade launch

It's not nice being walked over, but we made an exception for the elite of British society and celebrities like Rachel Stevens, who walked all over the new iconic walkway we installed at the Burlington Arcade.

The event was a culmination of 4 months' work to select the stone, fabricate the intricate pattern and install the 64,000 pieces by night, avoiding disruption to shoppers and traders.

June 2014

Training day at our factory

Some of our staff attended a training event at our very own factory, to be updated on the new techniques and advances in stonework fabrication. It was an educational and inspiring day for all.

Should you need inspiration or advice for your project, you are more than welcome to arrange a factory visit with us.

Investing in training our workforce

Thank you to Schluter Systems who came along to our factory and demonstrated to some of our lads. The afternoon was very informative. We continually invest in all our staff to keep their skills updated and allow for professional development.

7 June 2015

Supporting Great Ormond Street Hospital

One of our team was due to run in the Race for the Kids charity event on behalf of Tudor Stonework, held in Battersea Park, London.

2013

Sponsoring the OLSG Golf Day

For the second year running Tudor Stonework sponsored the OLSG Golf Day, which was again a great success. Our aim is to raise money for a variety of charities throughout the year.
