Stone

Semi-precious stone

Semi-precious stones are naturally found in small pieces and are bonded together with resin, and then cut and polished into slabs and tiles. Onyx is a gem stone found in various regions of the world.

Both are very beautiful but delicate materials, usually used for feature walls and decorative panels under back-lit conditions, or for bespoke furniture such as vanity units and tables. They vary dramatically in colour and texture depending on the quantities of different minerals within them.
