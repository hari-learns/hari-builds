The company

Importers, designers, fabricators and installers of natural stone and composite material, from a factory in Enfield.

The factory

From quarry block to finished stone

We have national and international trade partnerships with quarries all over Europe and the rest of the world. Whether it's a kitchen worktop, a back-lit museum feature wall or a complete external façade, each raw material starts its life as a quarry block, produced into slabs or selected sizes, and from here it is transferred to our factory where the finishing processes take place.

Our factory is staffed by trained skilled stonemasons and is equipped with bridge saws, a CNC machine, an edge polisher and a separate banker workshop.

Should you need inspiration or advice for your project, you are more than welcome to arrange a factory visit with us.

The people

The people who fit it

One of Tudor Stonework's strongest areas is the high quality of labour we employ and our knowledgeable managers. Prior to Tudor Stonework our head of labour was a labour-only contractor for 25 years, employing up to 150 stone fixers for some of the largest natural stone projects in the UK and worldwide. Now a director at Tudor Stonework, we have access to what we regard as the most skilled stone fixers and masons available.

Standards

Accreditations and standards

Accreditations are described as on tudorstonework.com; confirm current status and dates with Tudor.

Stone Federation Great Britain

Members of the Stone Federation Great Britain: we work to technical standards and procedures laid out to ensure specialist contractors keep up to date with installation techniques and implement them to the correct specification.

CHAS and Constructionline

CHAS accredited: the CHAS scheme assesses suppliers' health and safety competence. Members of Constructionline, the UK's leading procurement and supply chain management service.

Schluter Approved Installers

The first Approved Installers of the industry-leading Schluter waterproofing system, offering clients a wider range of services.

FORS

Bronze level accreditation of the Fleet Operator Recognition Scheme, which allows Tudor to measure and monitor the performance of its operations.

On site

All operatives hold valid CSCS cards and wear a hard hat, high vis, safety boots, goggles and gloves as a minimum.
