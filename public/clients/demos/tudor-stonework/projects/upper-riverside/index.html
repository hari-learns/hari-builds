Upper Riverside, Greenwich Peninsula

London · Phase 1 summer 2018; phase 2 scheduled for October 2019

324kitchens, phase 1
198bathrooms, phase 1
467kitchens, phase 2
213bathrooms, phase 2

Greenwich Peninsula is currently the largest new build development in Europe. Tudor were instructed to install stone, quartz and tiling to kitchens, bathrooms and floors over the five high-rise buildings.

The first phase was completed in summer 2018 and included the installation of 324 bespoke kitchens and 198 bathrooms. The second phase covers 467 kitchens and 213 bathrooms, scheduled to complete in early October 2019.

Figures and dates as printed on the live site, which still lists this job as ongoing.
