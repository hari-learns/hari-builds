Regent's Crescent

London · July 2021

76kitchens
2021newest job on the portfolio

Regent's Crescent, originally built in the 19th century and a Grade I listed building, has been redeveloped, creating a business suite, cinema and spa facilities, and 70+ residential apartments.

Tudor supplied, fabricated and installed the stone worktops and islands to the 76 stylish kitchens within these luxury residences. The interior scheme responds to the heritage character of the architecture, offering elegant interpretations of traditional details.

Values and dates are as published on tudorstonework.com. Indicative only.
