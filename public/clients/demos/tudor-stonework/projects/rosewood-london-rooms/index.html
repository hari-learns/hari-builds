Rosewood London, rooms

London · March 2012 (Phase 1) and December 2013 (Phase 2)

2phases, 2012 and 2013

Featured as one of the top 5 star luxury hotels of the world, Tudor have been proud to undertake full installation to this historic landmark building. The results were achieved using natural stone complemented by mirror faced porcelain, producing the highest standard of deluxe accommodation.

Phase 2 of the project included the completion, to impeccable standards and finish, of the bespoke 'Manor House', the hotel's exclusive and prestigious Presidential Suite.

Their site calls the hotel Rosewood (formerly Chancery Court).

Values and dates are as published on tudorstonework.com. Indicative only.
