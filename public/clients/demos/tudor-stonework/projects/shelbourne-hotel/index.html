The Shelbourne

Dublin, Ireland · September 2019

265rooms and suites in the hotel
Approx. £785kvalue

An iconic luxury hotel in Dublin city centre. The hotel features 265 rooms and suites and an exclusive health club and spa.

Tudor undertook the supply, fabrication and installation of marble to a number of the rooms and to public areas including lobbies, stairways, WCs, barber shop, fireplaces and external entrance steps. Beautiful Irish Connemara marble was locally sourced and delicately handled to fulfil the needs of the project.

Values and dates are as published on tudorstonework.com. Indicative only.
