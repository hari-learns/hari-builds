The people who fit it

Stone installation

One of Tudor Stonework's strongest areas is the high quality of labour we employ and our knowledgeable managers. Prior to Tudor Stonework our head of labour was a labour-only contractor for 25 years, employing up to 150 stone fixers for some of the largest natural stone projects in the UK and worldwide. Now a director at Tudor Stonework, we have access to what we regard as the most skilled stone fixers and masons available.

Reading the drawings

At installation stage our site managers have the knowledge and experience to interpret what's on the drawings, order the specified associated materials and highlight any changes that may have occurred since construction stage. Expansion joints, slab curing times, underfloor heating and dead loads are just some of the issues we have to be able to understand to ensure the installation method is correct for the building construction.

Stone Federation

As members of the Stone Federation Great Britain we work to technical standards and procedures which have been laid out to ensure specialist contractors such as ourselves keep up to date with installation techniques and are implementing them to the correct specification.
