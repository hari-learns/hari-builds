What we do

A complete interior and exterior stonework service, from design through to procurement, installation and aftercare.

Before the stone is ordered

Consultancy

Specifying stone, mechanical fixings, installation methods and design, even procuring the stone before construction.

In-house design team

Design

Elevations, plans and section details for the whole stone package, including fixings and expansion joints.

Quarry block to finished stone

Supply and fabrication

Trade partnerships with quarries across Europe and beyond, and a factory with bridge saws, a CNC machine and an edge polisher.

The people who fit it

Stone installation

A head of labour who ran a labour-only contractor for 25 years, site managers who read the drawings.

Durable modern concepts

Composite installation

Ceramic, porcelain, mosaic, quartz, terrazzo tiles, metallic finish, glass and riverstone, from one tile to a full retail floor.

Aftercare

Maintenance

An Operation and Maintenance manual on completion, and repair and cleaning visits on any schedule.
