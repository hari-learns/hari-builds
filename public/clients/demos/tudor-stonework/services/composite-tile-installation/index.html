Durable modern concepts

Composite installation

As an alternative to natural stone, composite materials offer quality permanent solutions which can be suited to any design scheme. Modern day composite materials have higher densities, greater durability and are easier to maintain, and offer a wide range of finishes suitable for today's contemporary design concepts.

Accredited fabricators

Tudor Stonework has good working relationships with the country's major suppliers and is accredited to fabricate from a number of the largest composite slab providers. From a single tile replacement to a complete retail floor, Tudor Stonework offer all services required from design stage through to installation and handover.

Materials

Ceramic · Porcelain · Mosaic · Quartz · Terrazzo tiles · Metallic finish · Glass · Riverstone
