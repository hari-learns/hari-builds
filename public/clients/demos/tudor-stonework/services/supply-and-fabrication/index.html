Quarry block to finished stone

Supply and fabrication

We have national and international trade partnerships with quarries all over Europe and the rest of the world. Whether it's a kitchen worktop, a back-lit museum feature wall or a complete external façade, each raw material starts its life as a quarry block, produced into slabs or selected sizes, and from here it is transferred to our factory where the finishing processes take place.

The factory

Our factory is staffed by trained skilled stonemasons and is equipped with bridge saws, a CNC machine, an edge polisher and a separate banker workshop.

Health and safety

Tudor Stonework continues its positive approach to health and safety, ensuring we undertake our own in-house training as well as embracing the health and safety procedures of other contractors we work for. All our operatives hold valid CSCS cards and have agreed to wear a hard hat, high vis, safety boots, goggles and gloves as a minimum when on site. The majority of our staff have also attended abrasive wheel and PASMA courses held at our factory. Please contact us should you need any of the following: information from our H&S consultant, health and safety policy, health and safety operations manual, Constructionline certificate.
