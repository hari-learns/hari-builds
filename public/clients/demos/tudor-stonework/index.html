Signature job

Burlington Arcade: 64,000 pieces, installed by night

The Burlington Arcade is a true iconic London landmark, steeped in almost 200 years of British history. Its 200 m covered walkway now consists of a 64,000 piece geometric pattern of exquisite British stone.

The intricate design took 8 weeks to schedule, programme and fabricate using water-jet, and 4 months to install by night, avoiding disruption to shoppers and traders.

  • 64,000pieces of stone
  • 200 mcovered walkway
  • 8 weeksto schedule, programme and fabricate by water-jet
  • 4 monthsto install, by night

Architect: Jamie Fobert Architects · Completed July 2015 · Shortlisted for the Construction News Project of the Year and runners-up in the Specialists category, 2016

Factory and people

Their own factory, their own fixers

Each raw material starts its life as a quarry block, produced into slabs or selected sizes, and from there is transferred to the Tudor factory where the finishing processes take place.

The factory is staffed by trained skilled stonemasons and is equipped with bridge saws, a CNC machine, an edge polisher and a separate banker workshop.

Prior to Tudor Stonework our head of labour was a labour-only contractor for 25 years, employing up to 150 stone fixers for some of the largest natural stone projects in the UK and worldwide.

Should you need inspiration or advice for your project, you are more than welcome to arrange a factory visit with us.
