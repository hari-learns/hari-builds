Yatsu Onsen, Kawazu, Izu

Hanareya Ishidaya

A traditional Japanese inn founded in 1873. Eleven detached single-storey rooms stand in the garden, each with its own open-air hot-spring bath.

Good to know

  • Breakfast: a simple breakfast is offered free of charge on a trial basis (for guests on the bedding-only plan), served at 8:00 in the dining room. Dinner is not offered; restaurants are around Kawazu station.
  • Children are welcome. The hot-spring garden pool is open from early July to the end of September (9:00-17:00).
  • Non-smoking throughout the inn (outdoor smoking area). Rooms are single-storey; some have a few steps at the entrance or on the way to the room, and no room has a bed (a simple bed can be arranged on request).
  • Check-in questions, specific rooms and allergies: please call or e-mail first.

Getting here

  • From Tokyo Station to Kawazu Station: Odoriko limited express, about 2 h 30 min. From Atami Station: about 1 h 30 min.
  • From Kawazu Station on foot about 650 m (about 10 minutes; 2-3 minutes by taxi). No pick-up service.
  • By car: about 63 km from Numazu IC via the Izu Jukan Expressway and R414 (Amagi Pass); about 120 km from Atsugi IC via Odawara-Atsugi Road and R135 through Atami.
  • The Kawazu-zakura trees along the river bank are about 5 minutes on foot; Kawazu beach about 12 minutes on foot.
