Jachtwerf Heeg/Maintenance

Maintenance & refit: your boat, our passion

At Jachtwerf Heeg we believe good maintenance is the basis of carefree sailing. Whether it is a glossy hull, a clean underwater hull, installing navigation equipment or an inboard electric motor. Even a complete refit is possible: our team of professionals is ready to keep your boat in top condition.

Craftsmanship for every detail

Your boat deserves the best care. We specialise in a wide range of work:

Hull and finish
Spraying, polishing and applying new striping.
Technology and equipment
Replacing fittings, installing an inboard electric motor in your Randmeer, new standing rigging or mast work.
Underwater hull
Thorough treatment of the underwater hull and applying antifouling.
Repair and refit
From small repairs to large-scale renovations.

Maintenance during winter storage

Most boat owners choose maintenance during winter storage. That makes sense: while your boat is safely with us in Heeg, we make sure it is ready again for the new sailing season in spring.

Tip: tell us your wishes straight away when you deliver the boat for winter storage. That way we can plan the work tightly and your boat is guaranteed ready on time in spring.

Maintenance outside winter storage

Want maximum attention for your boat and possibly shorter turnaround times? Consider having (major) maintenance done in the summer months. While most sailors are on the water, we have all the time and space at the yard.

Undivided attention
We focus entirely on your project.
Quick turnaround
No queues like in the busy winter months.
Best result
Some jobs, such as spray work, benefit from stable summer temperatures.
Financially attractive
For big jobs in the low season we are happy to look together at a sharp, favourable proposal.

Setting the course together

Whether you choose winter or summer: at Jachtwerf Heeg your boat is in the hands of enthusiasts with years of experience. We are happy to think along about the best approach for your boat.
