Jachtwerf Heeg/Winter storage

Winter storage at Jachtwerf Heeg: safe and trusted through the winter

When the sailing season ends, our care for your boat begins. At Jachtwerf Heeg we offer more than a place ashore. We offer a complete service in which your boat gets the attention it deserves, so that in spring all you have to do is cast off.

Winter storage 2026/2027

The sailing season is still in full swing, but at Jachtwerf Heeg we are already preparing winter storage 2026/2027. Register your boat in good time.

Our winter storage service

We take the heavy work off your hands. Our standard winter storage procedure is thorough and professional:

Safely out of the water
We crane your boat out and place it on the correct, type-specific cradles for optimal support of the hull.
Cleaning
Your boat is thoroughly washed down and cleared of fouling.
Unrigging & storage
We unrig your boat expertly and provide safe, dry storage for your sails and rigging. Batteries, cushions and other parts are clearly registered and labelled so you have them again in the new season.
Engine maintenance
For the technical heart of your boat we work closely with the specialists of Scheepsmotoren Heech, so you can be sure of expert maintenance on your inboard or outboard engine.

The intake: no surprises in spring

Every boat that enters our storage gets a thorough intake. We inspect your boat for defects, wear on fittings or necessary maintenance to the underwater hull.

Planned maintenance
Did you already request maintenance when registering? Then we schedule it straight away and place your boat in the most logical spot in our storage.
Unforeseen defects
Do we find anything that needs attention during the intake? Then we contact you straight away. You receive a clear quote from us, so we can fix the defects during the winter period.

Planning is essential

For an efficient winter period your input is crucial. We organise our storage based on the planned work.

Please note: be in good time. The space available for winter storage and maintenance is limited. By registering your boat in time, we can plan your wishes optimally.
