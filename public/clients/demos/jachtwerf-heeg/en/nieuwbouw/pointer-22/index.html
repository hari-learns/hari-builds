Pointer 22

Modern daysailer with lifting keel and twin rudders, designed by Bosgraaf Yacht Design.

Request a quoteWhich boat?

What defines this model

  • Modern, sporty daysailer that combines elegance, speed and comfort.
  • Twin rudders and a lifting keel give surprising stability and sailing pleasure, on inland and coastal waters.
  • Known for high-quality finish and excellent solo characteristics: all lines can be reached from the helm, ideal for short-handed or solo sailing.
  • At 6.50 m long, 0.30 to 1.10 m draught and about 750 kg, easy to trailer.
  • Spacious cockpit and the option to stay aboard comfortably: suited to day trips and sporty weekends.
