Randmeer

The familiar open sailing boat: shallow draught, easy to trailer.

Request a quoteWhich boat?

What defines this model

  • Open sailing boat with a spacious, safe cockpit; our adverts call it well suited to families and recreational sailors.
  • Keel-centreboard: stable, yet suitable for shallow water, ideal for the Frisian lakes and other inland waters.
  • Our used boats are mostly offered with a tilting trailer, so transport and launching are easy.
  • Can be equipped for sport: trapeze, spinnaker, downhaul and hiking straps appear in our adverts.
  • New: a Randmeer with a fully integrated electric pod motor with folding propeller.
  • Built to order: four new Randmeer Tourings for Curaçao.
