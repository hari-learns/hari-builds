Jachtwerf Heeg/New boats/G2

G2

Light, stiff and direct on the helm: for solo, double-handed and racing.

Request a quoteWhich boat?

What defines this model

  • Known for its light, stiff hull, excellent sailing characteristics and direct helm feel.
  • Planes quickly and responds sharply to trim; real adrenaline in a fresh breeze, and surprisingly good in light air too.
  • Light and agile, ideal for both solo and double-handed sailing.
  • Suited to racing and to sporty recreational use: our adverts mention both race and touring sails.
  • Our showroom has G2s in Classic Design and New Design; the web shop has race and standard sails, gennaker and carbon boom.
  • Two G2s in our showroom have an electric outboard.
