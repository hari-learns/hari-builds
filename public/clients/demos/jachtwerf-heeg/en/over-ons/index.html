Jachtwerf Heeg/About us

Where craftsmanship, passion and sailing pleasure meet

“Craftsmanship, personal service and a passion for watersports.”

For more than 75 years Jachtwerf Heeg has been active in watersports. From our yard in Heeg we work daily on new builds, maintenance, repair and winter storage of sailing boats. We combine our years of experience with a practical approach and personal service.

With an experienced and enthusiastic team we take care of everything around your boat. From building a new Randmeer, G2 or Pointer to maintenance, repairs, winter storage and the sale of used boats. For parts and specific work on your boat you can also come to us.

One yard, everything for your boat.

New buildsYour dream yacht, realised to perfection.
Maintenance & RefitPreserve the value and beauty of your boat.
Winter storageYour boat in trusted hands, all year round.
Buying and sellingExpert guidance at every change of course.
Web shopParts and accessories for Randmeer, G2 and Pointer, among others.

For more than 75 years Jachtwerf Heeg has stood for craftsmanship and a passion for watersports. From new boats and maintenance to winter storage and parts: one yard, everything for your boat.
