Our story

One family, one bakery tradition, since 1861

The first American commercial pretzel bakery was founded by Julius Sturgis in Lititz, PA in 1861, and it was there that the crispy, hard pretzel was developed. That small shop, at the rear of a stone house, became America’s first commercial pretzel bakery.

From 1861 to the present, the Sturgis family has operated in continuous pretzel production, with the exception of two years during World War II when bakery employees were drafted into military service.

Read the full story
  • 1861Julius Sturgis opens America's first commercial pretzel bakery behind a stone house in Lititz, PA, and develops the crisp, hard pretzel.
  • 1946Julius's grandson “Tom” M.D. Sturgis establishes Tom Sturgis Pretzels.
  • 1958Dutch pretzels join the line. Back in the 1950s they were called “Beers”.
  • 1970The bakery and pretzel store open at the Shillington factory on Lancaster Pike, where the family has baked ever since.
  • 1974Little Ones and Little Cheesers arrive; Pretzel Gems follow in 1975.
  • 1998Crunchzels, the extra-crunchy pretzel, join the range.
  • 2004Many of the pretzels become Kosher certified (OU-D) by the Orthodox Union.
  • TodayThe fifth generation of Julius Sturgis's direct descendants bakes at Shillington.
