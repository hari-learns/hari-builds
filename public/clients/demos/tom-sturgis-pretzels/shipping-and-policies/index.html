Shipping, chocolate and returns

All our prices include standard ground shipping to street addresses in the continental USA.

Shipping methods and costs

All our prices include standard ground shipping costs to street addresses in the continental USA.

PO boxes, Alaska and Hawaii

Packages shipping to US Post Office boxes, Alaska and Hawaii are subject to additional charges. USPS is used for these locations and additional charges are added at checkout.

Expedited shipping

Some products cannot be shipped expedited, and expedited options do not include Saturday or Sunday delivery. See individual items, or call 1-800-777-3314 between 8:30 am and 5:00 pm Eastern.

International shipping

International shipping is not offered at this time. Corporate customers should call 1-800-777-3314 for assistance.

Future ship dates

Enter a future shipment date at checkout. The ship date is the day the package leaves the facility (Monday to Friday). Standard shipping dates cannot be guaranteed, so allow adequate time. All packages ship from the Reading, PA facility.

Check your address

House number, suite or apartment, street, city, state and zip code must be correct. Packages returned because of an incorrect address are re-shipped at the customer’s expense, and an address correction fee per package may be charged.

Substitutions

Tin and decorative carton designs are sometimes limited due to demand. The bakery reserves the right to substitute designs of equal or greater value.

Chocolate

Because of the nature of the chocolate, items containing chocolate ship only between October 1 and March 31. Chocolate can still melt in transit if temperatures are too warm, and no dry ice or cooling packs are used. A white “bloom” on chocolate from a temperature change is normal and does not affect taste or quality.

Breakage

The bakery does its best to minimise breakage; pretzels will experience some in shipping. Cheese, Crunchzels and Little Cheesers pretzels may break completely. Replacements and refunds are decided case by case.

Returns

Because of food safety, returns are not accepted, except when the wrong item was shipped or the item arrived damaged. E-mail csr@tomsturgispretzels.com or call 1-800-777-3314.
