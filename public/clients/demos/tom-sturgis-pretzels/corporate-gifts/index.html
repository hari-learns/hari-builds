Questions buyers ask

Before you order

Can I send gifts to many addresses in one order?

Yes. Add everything to the cart, then at checkout use “Set destinations” to split items between saved addresses and write a gift note for each one. You get an e-mail with tracking numbers once everything has shipped.

Is shipping included in the price?

All prices include standard ground shipping to street addresses in the continental USA. PO boxes, Alaska and Hawaii carry additional charges, and expedited shipping costs extra.

When can chocolate be shipped?

Items containing chocolate ship only between October 1 and March 31, and no dry ice or cooling packs are used. Chocolate items are always available in the Shillington store.

Do you ship outside the United States?

Not at this time. Corporate customers should call 1-800-777-3314 for help.

What is the smallest custom corporate order?

Customized gift items for corporate customers start at 100 units. The bakery can include insert cards you provide in each package.

What if pretzels break in shipping?

Some breakage is normal. Cheese, Crunchzels and Little Cheesers pretzels may break completely. Replacements and refunds are decided case by case.
