Five generations of pretzel baking

The Sturgis family has operated in continuous pretzel production since 1861, apart from two years in World War II.

1861

America’s first commercial pretzel bakery

The tasty baked snack food that we know as the pretzel had its origin in southern Europe about 610 AD. The first pretzels were soft pretzels, given to children as rewards for learning their prayers. The twisted shape represents arms crossed over the chest in prayer, and Pretiola means “little rewards” in Latin.

The first American commercial pretzel bakery was founded by Julius Sturgis in Lititz, PA in 1861, and it was there that the crispy, hard pretzel was developed. That small shop, at the rear of a stone house, became America’s first commercial pretzel bakery.

14

A family of bakers

From 1861 to the present, the Sturgis family has operated in continuous pretzel production, with the exception of two years during World War II when bakery employees were drafted into military service.

Julius Sturgis and most of his 14 children operated the family business for many years. Several of Julius’ sons branched out and established their own pretzel bakeries, and their sons have continued the Sturgis family tradition.

1946

Tom Sturgis Pretzels

Today, the fifth generation of direct descendants of Julius Sturgis is producing pretzels at Tom Sturgis Pretzels. Our company was established by Julius Sturgis’ grandson “Tom” M.D. Sturgis in 1946. Great-grandson Tom Sturgis, Jr., and great-great grandson Bruce Sturgis provide hands-on, daily guidance for the bakery. Five generations of pretzel baking have made the Sturgis family the “First Family of Pretzels.”

1970

Shillington, then and now

This is our bakery and pretzel store in Shillington, PA, near Reading. We have been at this location since 1970. Many modifications and additions have been made to our building and baking equipment over the years in order to continue to bake using “old fashioned” techniques in a modern world.

The Sturgis family and their dedicated team of pretzel bakers devote their skills and attention to detail to bring you the quality pretzels you’ve grown to love. Same quality ingredients; same recipes; same great flavors!

From hand twisting to machines

Thousands a minute, where it once was forty

Pretzels were originally made by hand from start to finish. Through all the changes, from hand twisting at a top speed of 40 pretzels per minute to extrusion of thousands of pretzels per minute, pretzels remain a favorite snack for young and old.

Although pretzel machinery has changed a great deal over the years, Tom Sturgis Pretzels still bakes some of our pretzels on a soapstone hearth surface which produces the highest quality pretzels available.
