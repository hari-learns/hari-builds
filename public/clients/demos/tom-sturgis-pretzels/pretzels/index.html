Our pretzels, one by one

Below is a list of all the pretzels the bakery makes. Tap one for the facts, and the nutrition fact sheet is one tap further.

Chocolate covered pretzels are available for shipment during the cooler months, October through March, and are always available in the retail store.

Cheese Gems

Just like our Pretzel Gems®, our Cheese Gems® are precious little delicious bites of goodness. Real gems made with real cheddar cheese baked right in give our pretzels the best cheese flavor you can find.

Cheese Pretzels

Our Cheese pretzels have been around almost as long as we have. We still use the best cheddar cheese we can find to make these exceptional pretzels. The cheese is baked into the pretzel because the real cheese is added to the dough during mixing. Try these and you won't be disappointed.

Chocolate Cookie Stiks

These are not a chocolate coated pretzel but rather a pretzel with the chocolate bake right into the pretzel. An intense chocolate flavor is the first thing you notice and the crisp, crunchy pretzel texture comes next. There’s no melting during hot weather and no sticky fingers. It’s a whole new way to look at pretzels.

Dutch

Our Dutch style is a medium hard pretzel. Sourdough contributes to the fine taste. Our Dutch pretzels were called "Beers" back in the 1950's. This pretzel goes well with a cold drink and a smile. We have been making Dutch pretzels since 1958 and the flavor never goes out of style.
