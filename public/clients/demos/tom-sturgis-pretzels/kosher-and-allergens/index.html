Kosher, allergens and ingredients

Kosher certification

Many of our pretzels became Kosher certified (OU-D) by the Orthodox Union in early 2004. Our manufacturing practices are in compliance with the Orthodox Union’s requirements. Look for OU-D in the description when shopping. When Kosher certified products are packaged with non-Kosher certified items, the entire package is no longer Kosher certified.

Allergen information

All our products contain wheat. In addition, some products contain milk, soy and sesame. While every effort is made to keep allergens separate, there is always the possibility of cross-contamination on the lines.

Bioengineered ingredients

Since 2022, products containing ingredients on the federal Bioengineered List must disclose it on the packaging, and that list includes corn. The bakery continues to use the same quality ingredients it always has, so the bags now bear the Bioengineered symbol. New bag, same great ingredients.
