In the sail loft

How a sail is made

Your sails will be made in the UK at our purpose-built loft with quality and attention to detail at every stage.

  1. 01

    Measuring

    We have one of the most extensive sail libraries in the world, with over 60,000 sail designs on record. If we don't have your sail plans we will be happy to talk to you about our measuring service.

  2. 02

    Choosing the cloth

    We only use the best cloth manufacturers, including Bainbridge, Challenge, Contender and Dimension.

  3. 03

    Panel configuration

    Our in-house CAD design team will generate the panel layout for your sails, simulating the aerodynamics of how your sails will react to different wind strengths, sheeting angles and trim.

  4. 04

    Laser cutting

    We are one of the few lofts with their own in-house laser cutter. This heat-seals the edges ensuring they will not fray. Your design is passed electronically to the laser cutter.

  5. 05

    Bonding panels

    Each panel is glued together before stitching, using the very best high bond adhesive tapes. The adhesive is designed specifically for bonding sailcloth.

  6. 06

    Sewing the panels

    The panels are sewn in a variety of stitch patterns using zigzag and 3-step zigzag stitching. The thread is UV, chafe and abrasion resistant and braided rather than twisted, so it lies flatter and won't creep or run when damaged.

  7. 07

    Fittings

    All our fittings are of the highest quality, from the stainless steel rings and specialised pressed holes in the corners, to the webbing on reefing headsails and the shackles used to attach the luff slides.

  8. 08

    Inspection

    We carefully inspect your new sails before they leave the sail loft: every sail is pulled out on our test rig for final inspection and quality control before despatch. We also give you our unique 5-year Cruising Sail Guarantee.

  9. 09

    Delivery

    Your sail is sent in a heat sealed, thick polythene package to ensure it arrives in perfect condition wherever you are in the world. All packages are also fully insured for transit.
