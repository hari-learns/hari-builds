Shade sails and canopies

Shade Sails and Canopies from Jeckells the Sailmakers offer an almost unlimited degree of versatility in design options, colours and material choice.

Shade Sails and Canopies from Jeckells the Sailmakers offer an almost unlimited degree of versatility in design options, colours and material choice. Apart from UV block, we can make sails that offer complete protection in most weather conditions and our materials are available in either plain or fire retardant specifications depending on their end use.

We are happy to work closely with Architects or directly with the end user to ensure the perfect results. We have our own riggers who can work on site to erect the shade and supply all the necessary wire and rope work.

We can make any type of fabric structure and are delighted to quote for all projects no matter how small, large or even how strange they are. We have supplied retractable smoking shades for pubs all over the country, shade sails for schools, gardens, leisure parks and various other interior and exterior structures including four innovative Tensile Shade Sails to create a hospitality area for one of the Ferrari F1 Team sponsors at the first ever Abu Dhabi Grand Prix.

Some of the stranger jobs we have done in recent years have included fake acoustic insulation for a World War 2 observatory dome in North Norfolk and PVC 'caterpillars' for a scout jamboree at the Norfolk showground. However crazy your project just ask if it's something we can handle: you will be surprised by what we can do and how affordable it can be.

What we make, in their words

Fabric options

For a shade sail that is purely for UV protection we use mesh a lot as it looks stunning and can be left up outdoors for long periods of time more easily than most solid fabrics. If you require a showerproof sail, fabric options include our marine acrylic canvas, which looks natural and comes in lots of colours. The only fully waterproof fabric is PVC, but it is a membrane and can be sticky and claustrophobic in the summer; it needs a steep angle of fall (15 degrees or more is ideal). We also have fire retardant options for indoor and outdoor specifications.

Posts and rigging

We can supply mild steel, stainless steel or timber posts with suitable diameter and specification to ensure they are strong enough for the job in hand. We have also engineered our own removable posts, and if you have a suitably strong tree in the area we can make special tree harnesses that we have designed.

Retractable options

We can engineer a retractable option should you wish, using either bespoke steel roller blinds, or the type of furling gear that is usually found on the front of a yacht. We can even offer automated options if required. Pubs particularly like retractable options for outdoor smoking areas where cover is only needed when it rains.

Design, installation and maintenance

We can provide a full service from design through to installation or we will work with you to provide the elements you need. We have an in-house designer who can simulate your environment and superimpose onto it what we're proposing, and a team of experienced riggers in-house. A rolling maintenance contract takes your sails down in the winter, inspects them, and puts them back up in the Spring.

Schools

We do a lot of work for schools as young children need protection from the elements and we're quite used to working around the curriculum and meeting health & safety requirements. We will do our best to match up your school colours.

Canvas panels

We not only make shade sails, but also canvas panels that are fixed on with turnbuttons and can have plastic 'windows' or zip doors. We can make pretty much anything that can be sewn using the materials and components we have access to.
