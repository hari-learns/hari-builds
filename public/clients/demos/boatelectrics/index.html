Family-owned and operated

Founded in 1974 to support the Clyde fishing fleet

Boat Electrics & Electronics Ltd was founded in 1974 by Julian Coleman and remains a family-owned and operated business. It was established to support the Clyde fishing fleet in Scotland, and quickly expanded to serve both the leisure and commercial sectors.

Under the leadership of Managing Director Simon Coleman, the company has experienced significant international growth, particularly in the commercial sector.
