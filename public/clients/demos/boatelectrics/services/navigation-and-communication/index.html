Marine Navigation & Communication

Boat Electrics and Electronics (BEE) is a trusted leader in the supply, installation, and maintenance of advanced marine navigation and communication electronics. With over 50 years of experience, we proudly serve a diverse range of industries, including workboats, fishing, commercial shipping, the MoD, and leisure vessels.

Operating from strategic locations across the UK, we are well-positioned to provide comprehensive services to customers nationwide and internationally. As authorized dealers and distributors for all major manufacturers, BEE offers an extensive portfolio of cutting-edge navigation and communication equipment, tailored to meet the unique demands of each client.

Our team of highly skilled engineers undergoes regular training with leading manufacturers to stay at the forefront of technological advancements. This commitment to continuous improvement ensures that every installation, repair, or maintenance project is completed to the highest industry standards.

Technical Expertise & Exceptional Service

Whether you're outfitting a new vessel or upgrading existing systems, BEE combines technical expertise, premium products, and exceptional service to deliver solutions you can trust. From the first consultation to ongoing support, we are your reliable partner for marine navigation and communication needs.
