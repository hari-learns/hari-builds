Projects

Recent projects

BEE Ltd continue to provide service and support to vessels across Europe in all sectors of the marine industry from Ferries, tugs, crew transfer vessels and MoD.

MCS Swath 1

BEE Ltd. have recently completed a full electrical, navigation and communication package.

The system was designed and manufactured in the UK before being shipped to Taiwan for the Shipyard to install. We provided technical assistance throughout the build and commissioned the vessel in Taiwan.

The vessel is now operating in European waters and benefiting from our support and service network.

Seacat Enterprise

BEE Ltd. have recently completed a fully integrated bridge package including navigation / communication and control and monitoring.

The bridge console was fully fitted out off the vessel at our facility allowing more efficient installation to the vessel. The Control and monitoring system by Boning brings together all engine data along with vital vessel services allowing the entire vessel to be controlled and monitored by one person.

The full system was designed and tested to DNV HSC OSC class rules and the customers requirements.
