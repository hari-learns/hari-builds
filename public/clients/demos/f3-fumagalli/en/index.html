News

Carpet washing: Quick Washing business branch acquired

F3 Fumagalli is proud to announce its recent acquisition of the business branch of QUICK WASHING from Forni Varese, carpet treatment and washing.

The wet-cleaning and finishing line Quick Washing is a completely automated system and allows the performance of different processes, in sequence and controlled.

The company

“F3 Fumagalli is born from the long time experience gained in the ironing industry by its founders, and by the profound passion for this craft.”

Enrico Fumagalli, CEO and Founder

The high level of know-how acquired during the long experience in this field, the constant search for innovation and a healthy spirit of competition allowed F3 Fumagalli to become a reference point in the several areas in which it operates:

  • Industrial finishing
  • Steam generators for the pharmaceutical, food, rubber and chemicals industry
  • Packing and carrying of hanging clothes
  • Laundry industrial drying
  • Carpet washing systems

Our policy is focused on the satisfaction of our vast clientele. That's why we pay special attention to requests and suggestions to improve the technical performance of our products.
