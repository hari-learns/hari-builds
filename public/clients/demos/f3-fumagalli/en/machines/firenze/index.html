In detail

Production up to 70 shirts/hour.

Lateral tensioners for long and short sleeves.

All the shirtfinishers of our “Firenze” range match with a Collar&Cuff press of Firenze Range, for one or two shirts at the same time, with or without built-in boiler (up to 60 kW).

Firenze 70

Unit for shirts finishing of wet and dry garments, long and/or short sleeves. Production up to 70 shirts/hour. The plates for the finishing of bodies, yokes, shoulders and sleeves plackets are steam heated. Heating, vacuuming, steaming and blowing dummy. Touch Screen Monitor and joystick for the easiest usage. Impeccable quality and low maintenance costs.

Firenze 40

Unit for blowing finishing of wet and dry shirts.

Production up to 35/40 shirts/hour. Lateral tensioners for long and/or short sleeves. Hidden frontal clamp, electrical heating. Automatic presses for the finishing of yokes and sleeves plackets also available. Controls and adjustments of maximum simplicity, thanks to the large size Touch Screen display. Steamed integrated superheater for an optimal quality of the finishing. 100% electrical version also available (installed power 28 kW)

Firenze 30

Multipurpose dummy for finishing of shirts (production up to 30/35 shirts/hour) and jackets. The functions of two different dummies have been joined in one dummy only, optimizing the investment where the volumes of work aren’t too high. Maximum versatility and usage facility due to a 9 programs computer, easy and adaptable. Standard steamed superheater, for an optimal vaporization either on the shirts and on the jackets.
