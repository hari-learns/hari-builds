Steam generators

Vulcano

25-340 kg/h of steam

The Vulcano steam generators cover a steam production that goes from 25 to 340 kg/h.

The overlapped versions “x2” have two 100% independent generators , in order to guarantee a continuous production of steam even in case of failure. The generators can work individually or at the same time.

  • Inox PED boiler
  • Carbon steel PED boiler
  • Inox frame

In detail

PED approved

Double independent electronic circuit of probes for the maximum guarantee of the electric heating elements.

Stainless steel AISI probe tank built outside the boiler shell.

Double thermal insulation of the boiler, to save on the thermal losses.

Independent heating elements, selectable by the operator.

Safety

Double safety valve, led and buzzer for alarms.

Minimum encumbrance

Minimum encumbrance, thanks to the vertical frame.

Hhorizontal boiler

The horizontal boiler, for the maximum steaming surface, to avoid dragging typical of the vertical boilers.

“X2 versions”

The overlapped version “x2” have two 100% independent generators, in order to guarantee a continuous production of steam even in case of failure. The generators can work individually or at the same time.

Optional

  • Second pump kit, to work even in case of failure.
  • Stainless steel economizer, to save 10-15% of electricity
  • Stainless steel condensate tank, thermoregulated, electronic level and antismoke device.
  • Stainless steel boiler and external frame, for food, pharmaceutical and medical industry.
  • Increased pressure 7,5 bar
  • Third safety probe
