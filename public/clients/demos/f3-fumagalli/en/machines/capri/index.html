Drying and washing

Capri dryers

Load from 10 to 65 kg

Tumbler dryers, available with electrical, gas or steam heating. Made with AISI stainless steel drum and filter, reversing drum action, self-tensioning flat belt and wide door of charge with 180° opening angle. Available in 3 versions.

  • Eco Electrical
  • Electrical
  • Gas
  • Eco Steamed
  • Steamed

Versions and models

  • Lab

    Laboratories and laundry

    With microprocessor control panel with 10 programs and inverter on the drum motor (potentiometer controlled) for laboratories and specialized laundries.With drawer filter for a quick cleaning.

  • Self

    Self-service

    With microprocessor control panel for a connection to a centralized unit of payment or coin selector for self-service laundries.

    Simple use

    With electromechanical timer and thermostat, for a simple use and maintenance.

In detail

Economized “SOLE” version

This version recover the heat from the exhaust air, allowing a 30% of power saving.

Is the friendly version of the environment and of the wallet.

Safety

The Capri dryers are equipped with all the safety devices prescribed by the regulations both the porthole and on the filter panel.

Wet-cleaning basket

This innovative basket is ideal for drying water-washed clothes, instead of dry wash. Its special design avoids the felting of fabrics and the shrinking of the garments.
