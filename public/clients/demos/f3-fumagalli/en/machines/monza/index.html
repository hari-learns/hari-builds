Monza tables

In detail

Adjustable height tables, designed to answer to legal obligation in terms of ergonomics on job role.

Available with steaming, stainless steel board and electronic control (F1 and F2 with built-in boiler, F3 and Curtains).

With or without boiler

Equipped with standard or maxi size boards, can be supplied with or without boiler.

Adjustable on 6 levels

Vacuuming and blowing adjustable on 6 levels. Energy saving thanks to the stand-by function.

Adjustable height

AR range are height adjustable.

Safety and diagnostic

Simple and intuitive alarm and maintenance diagnostic on a large display.
