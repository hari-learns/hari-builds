Ironing and finishing

Manual press

Built-in boiler up to 60 kW

“Manual” is an ironing manual press. Sturdiness and absence of ordinary maintenance are the main characteristics of this machine. It offers height regulation as a standard feature, while it doesn’t have any rivals when it comes about the optionals.

In detail

Built-in boiler up to 60 kW, vacuum, ironing or spotting arm, light, iron and more. For the most demanding customers, on request, you can have it with hand lever opening or inverted pedals.

Available with 10, 26, 55lt. boiler, anti-gloss teflon sole for all shapes, plus balanced and articulated head

for an optimal alignment of the boards.
