About us

We do innovation at Workumer Jachtservice together

We do innovation at Workumer Jachtservice together with our partners. “Standing still is going backwards.” With our partners we strive for continuous renewal and improvement. It gives us energy to turn innovations into new and existing ships or other products.

New developments

Partly thanks to the VIA subsidy from SNN, Workumer Jachtservice B.V. and Marinminds develop the following activities as a project: Automatic Tacking System, Electric Water Taxi, Mixed Reality Field Service.

Blue Efficiency Shop

To achieve significant sustainability of both existing and newly built vessels, a technological step is necessary. As a project, Workumer Jachtservice B.V. and Van Oossanen Naval Architects B.V. are jointly developing a one-stop-shop service concept in which a tuned underwater foil, the patented Hull Vane®, can be designed, built and fitted under an existing motor yacht within 5 days. A fuel reduction of 15 to 25% is aimed for.

Mediterranean Sea Aak

At this moment Workumer Jachtservice B.V., in combination with Van Oossanen Naval Architects B.V., is developing a Lemsteraak suitable for the Mediterranean. The growing demand from the market means we apply this innovation to an originally Dutch product.

15 – 25%aimed-for fuel reduction with the Hull Vane®
5days to design, build and fit a Hull Vane®

SNN is a partnership of the three northern provinces for granting subsidies and representing their interests in The Hague and Brussels.
