About us

Our story: taking care

We enjoy it when you enjoy your ship with your family, friends and/or racing team. Taking care of your sailing pleasure runs like a thread through our company. Customers often tell us this too. It is second nature to us and we gladly do it for you with craftsmanship and dedication.

Our origins

Our origins

Our founder, father Herman Douma, had the philosophy that you must always want to serve the customer in every respect. He saw how important service is in a market where people want to enjoy their ship a few weeks a year. He made that the trademark of our family business.

This philosophy only works if the whole family wants to go for it. And that succeeded. Mother Janneke Douma still looks in almost every day on her boys Jelle and Daan. Because the spark really jumped to them. The Douma brothers have grown the company into a full-service business, remain true to taking care of customers and believe in progress.

Direction from A to Z

To keep your ship in good condition direction is needed; on planning, on purchasing, on deployment of skilled people, but also on the demand. We gladly think along with you, even if you want to make changes during the process, despite the tension that may arise. We want to oblige and take care of all our customers as much as possible. If this tension has consequences for delivery, we discuss it with you.

Craftsmanship

The beauty of working in our trade is the high craft level. Only with skilled craftsmen can we keep realising the beautiful custom work for you. That is why we make sure the different tradespeople work together on your ship at the right moment. The craftsmen can then focus on the product.

Innovation

Continuous improvement of the ships also requires knowledge and science in our designs and the build process. Together with our naval architects we listen to wishes from the market and try to combine these with possibilities in technology.
