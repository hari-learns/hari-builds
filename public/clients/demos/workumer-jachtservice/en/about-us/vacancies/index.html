(Junior) yacht interior builder (1 fte)

To expand our team we are looking for a (junior) yacht interior builder. Together with the team you are responsible for making luxury joinery, decks and parts for interior and exterior of ships.

What we ask

  • Vocational or university-of-applied-sciences level of work and/or thinking, with experience in the field;
  • Flexible, proactive and collegial attitude;
  • Creative problem-solving thinker;
