Yacht building

Build process

Building a ship is often a wonderful life experience; a dream that comes true after a special process.

01 / 13

  1. Step 01

    Design

    Every building process starts with the design and tuning it to the client's wishes. Within a ship class there is often so much variation possible that a good design and lines plan is needed to give the process the right direction and steering.

  2. Step 02

    Innovation

    The development of our clients' wishes does not stand still, nor does technology. That is why we actively develop along. With our partners we play a leading role in applied science in optimisations and technology. Where wind tunnels and towing tanks used to be used to measure and test optimisations, naval architects can now develop and show full computer simulations. This greatly shortens the cycle from design to build and test.

  3. Step 03

    Preparation

    All design agreements come together in a 3D model. As the client you already get a good picture of your dream ship; for us as builders it gives a hold on our process and planning.

  4. Step 04

    Hull construction

    Skilled hull builders assemble the hull from a fully calculated cutting package. However modern the hull parts are prepared, in the building of the hull you see centuries-old craftsmanship.

  5. Step 05

    Paint systems

    The hull is the foundation of the ship. The skipper must ultimately be able to rely on it for many years. For that, the most modern paint systems are used, applied to a blasted hull.

  6. Step 06

    Installations

    Our modern ships contain a world of control technology, navigation and communication equipment. We like calm and order in the installation work, because we like to deliver beautiful work. But above all to always be able to maintain the systems with confidence because of their clear and orderly layout.

  7. Step 07

    Interior

    To use the build time as efficiently as possible we build our interiors in a so-called ‘mock-up’. In the mock-up all frames are set up in the joinery workshop so that they effectively form exactly the ship to be built. In this frame construction the joiners then build a removable interior that can later be fitted in the hull. That way the building of interior and exterior runs in parallel.

  8. Step 08

    Engine system

    There is a lot to choose from in the engine of your ship. We work with various brands from diesel to hybrid.

  9. Step 09

    Teak deck

    The teak deck can also be prepared in the mock-up so that it can be laid immediately when needed. Here again the old skill in a modern coat.

  10. Step 10

    Spars, leeboards & rudder

    Spars, leeboards and rudders are not only beautiful to look at, they are actually a piece of science. After all the calculation to reach the optimal size and the lowest air and water resistance, the joiners build these beautiful wooden elements from carefully chosen woods and grains.

  11. Step 11

    Sail plan & rigging

    Depending on preferred use we deliver your ship with touring sails and/or racing sails. The rigging plans are fully calculated for all weather conditions and uses.

  12. Step 12

    Sea trial

    And then it is time, the first sail with the ship. Testing all equipment, installations and sailing characteristics. Whatever is not yet as desired is solved or optimised.

  13. Step 13

    Delivery

    The ship is ready and hangs in the crane for the christening. Often an emotional moment for both you as client and for us. We wish you a safe voyage and plenty of sailing pleasure.
