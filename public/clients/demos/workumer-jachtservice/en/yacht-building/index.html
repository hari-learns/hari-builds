Yacht building

Builders of Lemsteraken, aluminium yachts and custom built ships

From our origins we are yacht builders; we love boats, we love sailing, maintenance and service, and so we have always experienced what can be better and more beautiful in a ship. Our clients therefore let us build new ships, or improve their ship.

New Lemsteraken

Giving shape to your dream, building a new Lemsteraak, is what we like to do together with you. With your wishes and ideas, our experience and the craftsmanship of our people, we arrive at a design, build and finish that meets the most modern standards, with your sailing comfort and ease of use as important values for us.

See all our new Lemsteraken

Custom built

We are glad that together with our customers we can look back on a history in which we built everything from small motorboats to very well-equipped racing aken and luxury motor yachts. In doing so we gained a lot of knowledge and experience: of possibilities in technology, in design and sailing behaviour, and also in interior building and ship installation work.

Discover our custom built projects

Sichterman

As yacht builders we have introduced a beautiful product with the Sichterman, combining comfortable and sporty sailing. These seagoing aluminium ships are completed fully optioned for a large cruising area. To that end the building process of the ship is monitored so that a CE-A certificate can be issued on delivery.

Design partners

Design partners

To make sure our ships meet the most modern standards and requirements for use, we work with various partners in design and naval architecture. That way we make sure the ships we deliver integrate beauty, high-quality technical possibilities and sailing characteristics. Proof of this are the designs of various architect bureaus that we have completed for our clients.
