Pride of the Zuiderzee

Lemsteraak

Lemsteraken are beautiful, stately ships used as family ship, as racing ship or of course both. Once designed in the Netherlands for use in fishing or pleasure sailing, the users of these ships have always been busy adapting them as well as possible to their purposes. We continue this in the Lemsteraken we build new or refit for you. Classic ships develop quickly without doing violence to the classic character. That way we combine modern demands with old values and aesthetics.

That is why we sponsor the VA/VB class organisations and SSRP.

Lemsteraak new builds

Lemsteraak new builds

With a new Lemsteraak you first make choices, such as:

  • A visserman with a large open cockpit;
  • A yacht version with spacious interior living space;
  • The desired look and detailing
  • The degree of seaworthiness
  • Racing and/or leisure sailing
  • Cruising area

Based on your package of requirements and wishes we design your ship with you and the naval architect in form and technical specification. We also guide you through the choices and design of the interior and the composition of the technical installations on board. How involved you want to be in the process of design and build from A to Z depends on your interests and availability. It is up to you. We tune it together with you.

Refits

Refits

There can be all kinds of reasons to refit your ship; its appearance, the state of the technical installations, changing use, but also damage. Either way a Lemsteraak is a high-quality ship and is worth maintaining thoroughly or refitting in due time.

In the photos you see various refits we have carried out: interior, engine room, technical installation and navigation or a complete refit.

Lemsteraak charter

Lemsteraak charter

If you want to enjoy sailing a Lemsteraak on the Frisian lakes, the IJsselmeer or the Wadden, you should definitely charter one. Lovely for a holiday, for running dry on the mudflats once, but also to experience which type of aak you like best. Many have gone before you by chartering from Sailcharter Friesland, an active, service-minded organisation where you will surely feel at home.

Lemsteraak brokerage

Lemsteraak brokerage

For years we have worked together with Lemsteraak brokers. Through this cooperation we can take care of new owners by taking over the service and maintenance of their ship.

Many ship owners keep their ship at our locations, which lets us combine extensive showroom capacity with service and maintenance. Your buying or selling advisers and your maintenance advisers are then always nearby.
