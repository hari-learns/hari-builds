Winter storage

Winter storage

During the sailing season you often see a lot of our work process, because you are there and see us at work. At the end of the season we prepare the ships for winter. A whole process in which we can service your ship in many places, in order to then store it safely. Through this process we can also inform you properly about necessary work and/or improvements.

Work included for winter storage in the hall or on land: craning the ship out and back in in spring; use of high-pressure pump to clean the underwater hull; washing the hull; placing the ship on stands; use of ship stand; transport to and from the winter storage hall.

Work we can carry out in consultation with you, charged afterwards: engine service (filters, oil, gearbox etc.), refuelling with diesel, winterising of propulsion and generator, winterising of sanitary and drinking water systems, rigging and de-rigging of mast(s) and leeboards, removing and bending on sails, making technical installation ready to sail, gas installation inspection.

Prices incl. VAT, season 2026-2027, from the Workumer Jachtservice price list. Changes in prices are reserved without prior notice. The relevant HISWA conditions apply to all our supplies of services and/or goods.

The risk for ships with the mast up stored outside on land is entirely for the owner's account. For insurance reasons it is not allowed to leave gas bottles and loose fuel tanks on board during winter storage. These are stored for you. Batteries must also be disconnected during storage.

The whole process in 13 steps

The whole process in 13 steps

  1. Step 01

    Registering for winter storage

    Via our site you can download and fill in a winter storage form. On this form you can indicate whether you want your ship to winter inside, outside or in the water, and which regular maintenance work you do or do not want carried out. You can also already pass on the points of attention that we should inspect or tackle.

  2. Step 02

    Delivering the ship

    When you have sailed out for the season, make an appointment for when you want to bring the ship to us, or when you want us to collect it. Our planners then make sure the necessary work is scheduled and, if necessary, a skipper is arranged.

  3. Step 03

    De-rigging

    Before your ship leaves the water we first de-rig it carefully. If you wish, we make a rigging report with a skilled rigger, giving you an overview of the state of your sail wardrobe as well as the standing and running rigging. Safety comes first.

  4. Step 04

    Craning out and cleaning

    Your ship is craned out and freed of algae and other growth with a powerful high-pressure cleaner. Then the first inspection of the underwater hull takes place. If necessary we take photos to inform you.

  5. Step 05

    Placing on stands

    After cleaning and inspecting we place your ship on a suitable stand and prepare it for transport to the winter storage or to the workshop.

  6. Step 06

    Winterising

    Winterising can take place in the water and on land, depending on the installation work on board. It is important that all water pipes, hull fittings and engine cooling are made frost-proof. Finally the batteries are fully charged and disconnected.

  7. Step 07

    Storing parts

    After de-rigging the sails are dried and then stored in the heated sail loft. The mast and spars are stored in the mast storage where they are ready for any maintenance work.

  8. Step 08

    Maintenance work

    We are the address for all possible maintenance work on your ship, such as: keeping up or renewing varnish and paint work, regular maintenance, overhaul or renewal of installations and machinery on board, optimisation of sailing characteristics and rigging, maintenance and optimisation of navigation and communication systems, maintenance or change of interior or exterior joinery, upholstery, inspection of the underwater hull.

  9. Step 09

    Craning in

    During and after craning in the ships we carry out various checks to rule out the risk of leaks.

  10. Step 10

    Rigging

    Rigging your ship is a precise matter. The often heavy parts must be fitted secured to guarantee use that is as safe as possible. For this we use as fixed a work order and inspection round as possible.

  11. Step 11

    Making ready to sail

    To make ready to sail, all the frost-proofed piping is vented, flushed and tested. The disconnected batteries and installations are also reconnected and the engines and equipment put into operation.

  12. Step 12

    Final clean

    When all the technical work is done we make your ship clean and fresh. And if you wish, we air the beds and make them with clean sheets.

  13. Step 13

    New season

    We do everything to let you start the sailing season without a care, and of course based on your wishes and needs. We wish you plenty of sailing pleasure again and look forward to seeing you again in the autumn.
