Yacht service

Yacht service & maintenance from A to Z

By Yacht Service & Maintenance from A to Z we really mean from A to Z. When you let us look after your ship we will always inform you about the work that is necessary or desirable for a carefree sailing season. Of course we start from your wishes and expectations.

One asks us only for the necessary maintenance, another wants us to check his ship before every trip. Yet another lets us make the ship race- or holiday-ready. We gladly do it all!

Service

Service

We are happy to be ready for you with, among others:

  • Interim inspections of engine and installations
  • Rigging your ship for a comfortable and safe trip
  • Rigging your ship race-ready and preparing it for race inspections
  • Transferring your ship by water
  • Equipment and installations
  • Insurance and claims handling

Maintenance

Maintenance

We keep your ship in optimal condition, with annual maintenance where needed;

  • Engines and cooling system
  • Installations and navigation and communication equipment
  • Mast, spars, rudder and leeboards
  • The paint system of steel, aluminium and wooden hulls
  • Teak decks and yacht joinery
  • Electrics, ICT, internet and TV
  • Welding of steel, stainless steel and aluminium
  • Preserving underwater hulls
  • Damage repair in wood, steel, aluminium and polyester

Winter storage

Winter storage

Safe wintering is the preservation of your ship. Thanks to our facilities we can do this for you inside and/or outside:

  • In heated halls
  • Storage outside on a closed yard
  • Wintering in the water
  • Wintering in boathouses

You discuss with us your wishes for winter storage and your needs for service and maintenance.
