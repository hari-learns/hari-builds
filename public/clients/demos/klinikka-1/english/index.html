Family-owned medical practice in Helsinki

Klinikka 1 Oy is a Finnish family-owned medical practice managed by doctors Esa and Pia Tahvanainen. We are committed to offering high-quality healthcare services at affordable prices, ensuring that our services remain accessible to all patients.

Book an appointment

Services

Our clinic is located in Myllypuro (Kivensilmänkuja 2, 3rd floor), near the metro station. We provide general healthcare, occupational health services, and specialized care in areas such as internal medicine, surgery, psychiatry, radiology, geriatrics and orthopedics.

Occupational health

We offer flexible, cost-effective occupational health services with no monthly fees. Services are priced based on actual needs. New company setup 400 €, annual updates 150 €, job surveys every five years from 300 €. A basic occupational lab package costs 100 €. Clients can claim Kela compensation for preventive work (60 %) and medical treatment (50 %).
