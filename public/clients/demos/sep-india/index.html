SEP India Pvt. Ltd.

If you are looking for precision-machined, high quality metal parts, you have arrived at your destination.

SEP India Private Limited is a world class, ISO 9001 and IATF 16949 certified manufacturer of machined metal components for Automotive, Medical, Fluid Delivery and Metering systems. Established in 1991, SEP was founded and continues to be led by Mr. Mohan and Mr. Ganesh.

We can deliver both high-volume as well as highly customized, low volume components.

Quality

At its basic core, quality in manufacturing means that we can deliver defect-free products to our customers.

At SEP, we see quality as a process driven function that enhances our operations to ensure reliability and consistency, increases trust and credibility, and also optimize price by reducing the cost of lost labor hours or scrap metal resulting from deficient quality.

The company

Founded and still led by two engineers.

SEP was founded and continues to be led by Mr. Mohan (Mechanical engineer) and Mr. Ganesh (Electrical and Electronics Engineer) to synergize their vast experience in manufacture and supply of automotive components and knowledge of machine tools.

Mr. K. Mohan

Managing Director

Mr. Mohan has held CEO positions in several leading automotive companies, notably as President of Sundaram Fasteners (Rs 3000 crore / $500 million) and Fenner India (Rs 600 crore, $150 million).

Mr. N. Ganesh

Joint Managing Director

Mr. Ganesh started his career with ABB Ltd (then known as Hindustan Brown Boveri) and became a Sales and Service Engineer for CNC Machines with Kirloskar Electric before founding SEP.
