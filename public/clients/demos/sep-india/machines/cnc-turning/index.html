CNC turning and turn-mill

22 CNC turning centres and 9 CNC turn-mill centres: the core of the floor.

The turning centres are 9-axis and 5-axis Citizen machines, described by SEP as high precision and suited to hard parts. The 9 CNC turn-mill centres are sliding head machines.

SEP can machine, mill or produce any metal part up to 3 kg of weight per unit, starting from bar stock, hot or cold forgings or aluminium die castings, in carbon steel, stainless steel, high cobalt / high chromium special steel, aluminium, brass and other non-ferrous metals.
