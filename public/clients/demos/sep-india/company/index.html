About SEP India

Established in 1991, SEP is a precision machining company in the Sipcot Industrial Area in Hosur, founded and still led by two engineers.

Over 25+ years, SEP has earned a distinction in the cost, quality, reliable and timely delivery of our products. Our focus on customer satisfaction has established many long standing, internationally discerning and quality customers.

Vision

To create a culture of excellence and inculcate such enduring values that they become ingrained in the daily lives of our employees to make SEP India a standard bearer amongst supplier companies.

Mission

To manufacture parts that meet or exceed customer needs every time and at an optimal cost that delivers an economic benefit to both companies so as to create a symbiotic relationship that enhances the capabilities and market share of both companies.

Management

SEP was founded and continues to be led by Mr. Mohan (Mechanical engineer) and Mr. Ganesh (Electrical and Electronics Engineer) to synergize their vast experience in manufacture and supply of automotive components and knowledge of machine tools.

Mr. K. Mohan

Managing Director

Mr. Mohan has held CEO positions in several leading automotive companies, notably as President of Sundaram Fasteners (Rs 3000 crore / $500 million) and Fenner India (Rs 600 crore, $150 million). He pioneered the entry of sintered, forged and machined components of Sundaram Fasteners into General Motors in 1994. He later joined Al Anwar Industrial and Trading Company in Muscat, Oman, where he was responsible for the performance of 6 subsidiary companies and set up 2 new companies, a ceramic tile factory and an investment casting foundry. Upon return to India, he set up Indo-US Mimtech, a metal injection molding company, before 4 years at Fenner. He took a more active role at SEP since 2004 and has remained current with the industry through leadership roles at the regional and national levels of ACMA and CII.

Mr. N. Ganesh

Joint Managing Director

Mr. Ganesh started his career with ABB Ltd (then known as Hindustan Brown Boveri), one of the world’s leading engineering companies in electrical power and industrial efficiency. He became a Sales and Service Engineer for CNC Machines with Kirloskar Electric before founding SEP.

Infrastructure

SEP owns two plants on 2.5 acres of land in the Sipcot Industrial Area in Hosur, Tamil Nadu, India. Two shop floors of 27,000 square feet house 67 machines.
