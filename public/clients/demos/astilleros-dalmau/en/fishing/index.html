Fishing

Drassanes Dalmau designs and manufactures customized to the shipowner any type of fishing boats of fiberglass up to 50m length type of catamaran or monohull:

- Trawling fishing boats
- Pelagic fishing boats
- Purse-seine fishing boats
- Tuna fishing boats
- Longline fishing boats
- Trammel boats
- Minor arts boats, mussel fishing boats, shell fishing boats, light boats, auxiliary boats,...
