The POPEYE LIFEANDSEA represents the evolution of our Fast440 range, a passenger vessel designed to offer maximum performance, efficiency, and comfort for demanding tourist operations.

The planing monohull of these vessels has been developed with state-of-the-art composite materials and structural lamination technologies implemented by DRASSANES DALMAU's R&D department in the successful SLIM series. The result is a lighter vessel with lower fuel consumption and excellent handling, with working speeds of 20 knots and the potential to reach speeds of up to 30 knots.

The POPEYE LIFEANDSEA has a capacity for 56 passengers and a length of 14.90 meters. This model combines a modern design with a lightweight and robust construction, providing fast, stable, and safe navigation even in demanding conditions.

Powered by two 510 HP Volvo Penta engines, the POPEYE LIFEANDSEA offers a perfect balance of performance, reliability, and operational efficiency.
