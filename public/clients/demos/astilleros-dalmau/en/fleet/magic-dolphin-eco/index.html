The MAGIC DOLPHIN ECO is a stylish hybrid catamaran and like all the boats built by Drassanes Dalmau, a tailor-made boat. The refined hull design ensures great stability. It is a boat with an elegant design that gives the feeling of sailing on a yacht, offering passengers the feeling of privacy. The feeling of turning off the engines and sailing only in hybrid is very relaxing, due to the absence of noise, silent navigation, and low emissions. It has a capacity of 150 passengers. Travelers will be delighted with the navigation and will enjoy the ride along the coast of Funchal, Madeira. The catamaran reaches a speed of approximately 17 knots, with four engines, two 12kW electric and two 350 hp. Owner: magicdolphinmadeira

The boat has:

  • A solarium area in the bow with nets from which you can access the water through an articulated ladder leading to the deck that is completely hidden during navigation. It has large perimeter corridors for dolphin watching.
  • Diaphanous upper deck with sliding roof.
  • Two large swim platforms.
  • Two toilets, one for disabled people.
  • Two bars and dance floor.
  • Cover closing system with side awnings.
  • Auxiliary control bridge.

Owner: magicdolphinmadeira
