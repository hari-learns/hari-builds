In the same way as the Fly Blue One and the Fly Blue Tres, it is a very elegant and extremely light passenger catamaran, designed to offer maximum comfort to passengers so that they have the feeling of sailing on their own yacht. It is a versatile and highly efficient boat capable of sailing at a speed of 20 knots with two 440 hp engines.

The boat has:

  • A solarium area in the bow with nets from which you can access the water by means of an articulated ladder brought to the deck that is completely hidden during navigation.
  • Diaphanous upper deck with sliding roof.
  • Two large bathing platforms.
  • Two toilets, one for the disabled.
  • Bar and dance floor.
  • Removable padded seats.
  • Roof closing system with innovative sliding windows.
  • Catering area with cold room.
  • Auxiliary control bridge on both sides.
  • Professional music equipment and ambient lights with color regulation.
