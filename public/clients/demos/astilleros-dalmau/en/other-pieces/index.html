Other pieces

As our main business is building fiberglass boats, we have a long EXPERIENCE, and physical and TECHNICAL RESOURCES enough to the design and manufacture of any project made with composites.

We have a technical office, mainly dedicated to the design of our products to meet the needs of the customer, where all 3D projects are developed. We calculate the projects to OPTIMIZE COST without losing strength or quality.

We manufacture the pieces using own, new or client's molds. We can build any mold with CNC. We use different systems of lamination depending on the characteristics of the piece: hand layup, projected, infusion, RTM,...

For these reasons our company also specialized in DESIGN and MANUFACTURE of pieces and molds with COMPOSITES with dimensions UP TO 50m for different sectors:
