SENSATION CUATRO, NEW CATAMARAN DAY CHARTER

"The Sensation Cuatro, catamaran" day charter "sailing has been designed for a professional and demanding work, also providing customers with high comfort and optimal performance to make each sailing in a memorable experience"

Drassanes Dalmau has built an innovative catamaran “day charter” with inverted bow. The catamaran currently working in the Port of Blanes. It is a boat that wants to become a revolutionary benchmark in the market of catamarans "day charter".

Speed ​​and comfort guaranteed for passengers

Our client wanted a comfortable and efficient passenger catamaran for sailing. For this, the technical team of Drassanes Dalmau has optimized in order to produce the catamaran in a comfortable, fast and very light boat. Passengers on board have the perception of sailing on their yacht. It is a catamaran that stands out for having elegant and smooth lines that ensure feelings of comfort, safety and exclusivity. All this has been achieved, offering customers a spacious solarium in the bow area with nets, a large bar with large aft barbecue and an area of banks protected from the sun with a design awning, getting a very versatile catamaran in which it can organize events, parties and receptions of all kinds in addition to daily departures. This "day charter" has two boarding options; by the stern sides and by bow in port, and with direct access to the beach sand by bow. It is really a highly recommended boat to spend some relaxing moments and it has all the necessary services to spend days on board and combine them with exclusive events and catering. It has the capacity to accommodate 150 people who can free to move at the deck of the boat.

Maneuverable and efficient catamaran

It should be noted on the ship the ease of maneuver. This catamaran of 22 meters in length and with inverted bow offers efficient navigation. The catamaran has a magnificent sail system with sailing is functional and safe for people on board. It is remarkable that this ship in sea trials reached 8 knots of speed with exclusive handling of sails at 13 knots of wind and with engines with a power of only 150hp came to sail at 14 knots. The sail system has been made using high quality materials designed for professional use of the boat. The system consists of a mainsail and Genova roller both with "vektron" fabric, the mast for its part is lacquered aluminum in black and is equipped with "full batten" rail. The winches for driving the mainsail, as well as the Genoa winder are of the electric type with the aim of improving safety and optimizing maneuvers during sailing, and all the fittings have been oversized considering the professional use of the boat.

It is undoubtedly a fast ship with a great hydrodynamics. Drassanes Dalmau has built a very light ship that achieves a reduction in emissions to the atmosphere and save on fuel compared to other similar ships. For the construction of this catamaran, state-of-the-art engines that comply with the most demanding anti-pollution standards have been used.

Safety and requirement

As usual in vessels built by Drassanes Dalmau, special attention is given to all the mechanisms required to provide safety vessels beyond the current regulations, complying with the criteria of stability in case of a failure. It has among others with a system of extinguishing machines by CO₂, fire detection system and motorized closing of ventilation gates ...

The Sensation Cuatro, catamaran" day charter " sailing has been designed for a professional and demanding work, also providing customers with high comfort and optimal performance to make each sailing in a memorable experience.

Catamarans and monohulls up to 50 m. A family shipyard in Arenys de Mar since 1963.
