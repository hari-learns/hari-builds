Since 1963, in Arenys de Mar

  1. 1963

    Josep Dalmau founds the family business.

  2. 1972

    Fibreglass is definitively adopted: pioneers in Spain.

  3. 1985

    Passenger vessels become the core business.

  4. 1990

    First fishing catamaran: a longliner.

  5. 1994

    First passenger catamaran for tourist use.

  6. 1998

    Second shed in the Arenys de Mar industrial area.

  7. 2002

    Third shed, in Arenys de Mar port with direct access to the sea.

  8. 2005

    Pioneers in infusion lamination.

  9. 2009

    The high-efficiency SLIM series is born, with ECO SLIM as its flagship.

  10. 2010

    ISO 9001 quality certification.
