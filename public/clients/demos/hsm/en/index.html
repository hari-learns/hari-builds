What we do

Iron work

A young and dynamic team qualified to carry out all projects in the metalworking sector.

Stainless steel

All kinds of railings, screens, gates, grilles and industrial metalwork.

Aluminium

Aluminium frames with or without thermal break, interior partitions, conservatories and more.

Cladding

Roof and façade cladding in aluminium, iron, lacquered sheet, sandwich panel, Alucobond and others.

Metal structures

Several types of steel structures, calculated and modelled in 3D to optimise fabrication and assembly.

Maintenance and assembly

Specialised teams for gutters, roof cleaning, replacing fibre-cement roofs, painting and restoring equipment.

Who is HSM?

With family origins going back to the 1980s, HSM started with iron work in premises of less than 200 m². At the turn of the century it began to explore international markets, first France, then Switzerland, where it is firmly established, and now the Caribbean.

Today HSM has more than 2,000 m² of covered factory area and a portfolio in stainless steel, corten steel, iron, aluminium, Alucobond and several types of metal structures.
