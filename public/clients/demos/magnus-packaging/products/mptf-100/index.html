MPTF 100 tube filler for laminated tubes

A user-friendly state-of-the-art machine with logical controls, most suitable for laminated tube filling and sealing operations.

110tubes per minute
Request a quoteCheck my tube output
Photo: Magnus single head tube filler (Magnus has no separate MPTF 100 photo)

Toothpaste, shaving creams, cold creams, ointments, colours, paints, adhesives, food pastes and syrups are some of the typical products for which this machine has proved very successful.

The machine can fill ointments, pastes, creams and liquids of different viscosities for the food, cosmetics and pharmaceutical industries with close accuracy. The product rat tail cut-off device is custom built for clean operation.
