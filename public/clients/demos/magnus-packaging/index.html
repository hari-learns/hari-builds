Safeguards built into the machines
Taken line by line from the Magnus product pages and brochure.
  • No tube, no fill
    Automatic disengaging of the filling mechanism when a tube is missing.
    MPTF 200/400
  • No product, no carton
    An outstanding feature of the MPC-120, alongside separate vacuum pumps for carton pick-up.
    MPC-120
  • No trapped air
    Progressive filling while the lifted tube retreats down from the filling nozzle. No air is trapped, filling weight is exact and no paste enters the closure folds.
    MPTF 200/400
  • Guards you can see through
    All guards in stainless steel sheet and all openable doors made of polycarbonate, with safety as per industrial guidelines.
    MVC-120
  • One operator
    Only one operator required, even at maximum output.
    MPTF 200/400
