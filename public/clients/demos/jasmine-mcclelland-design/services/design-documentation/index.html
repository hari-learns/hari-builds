Services / Stage 1

Concept Design to Final Documentation

Residential renovations and new builds: from the first brief to a documentation package ready for your builder.

Full Home Renovations including Apartments

We have a passion for repurposing spaces in your home to better function for your lifestyle. We know your house is one of your biggest investments, and will make the most of your existing space.

Small Extensions

We will redesign your existing floor plan to help you determine what you truly need and give new life to unused spaces. Choosing to extend can save you money and also reduce your environmental footprint.

We’re able to recommend trusted and experienced professional builders, suppliers and tradespeople, and have links to a community of local artisans. We collaborate closely with our clients to ensure every detail reflects their vision, aiming to elevate our clients’ designs to a place that exceeds their expectations and enhances daily life.

Our proven design process ensures successful project completion from the initial consultation to your final documentation package.

We will set you up with everything you need for construction.
