I have a major back problem and had been told by a specialist to get a kitchen that didn’t require me to bend too much, so it was really important for us to get a design that would work for us.

When Jasmine returned and showed us her design outline we wanted to say ‘Ahhh yes, of course’; Because it all looked so simple and so right… why hadn’t we thought of this earlier? It was modern, clean, and practical and worked around the other rooms in our home. It also featured so many of the things we love, natural wood, colour etc. She took a non functioning room and by simply rearranging a few things and moving a few doorways managed to create a massive pantry, let in loads of natural light and find room for masses of bench space. It was perfect!

We had our own builders and cabinet makers and Jasmine happily liaised with them over the renovation. Charlie, our cabinet maker, claims that Jasmine’s design is the best kitchen design he has ever made and that she was a pleasure to work with. We only had to talk to Jasmine a couple of times after we received the design plans as she had factored everything into her design package, suppliers, colours, products etc, everything we needed to move ahead with the job.

We will never be able to thank Jasmine enough for her amazing design. Our kitchen was once a room that used to really bring us down and that we hated using. Now this beautiful room has become the centre of our home and we spend hours sitting there, talking, drinking, cooking etc. So much so that we no longer want to go out as much in the evening, we’re now happy to just stay home and relax in our lovely new room.

We really want to thank Jasmine for her intuition, her skill and her wonderful design. We are so grateful for everything she did for us.

Ann & JonCoburg Kitchen
