Projects / Homes

Mount Martha Residence

  • LocationMount Martha VIC 3934
  • HIA Winner · Victorian Kitchen & Bathroom Awards 2019 Bathroom Design
  • HIA Winner · Victorian Kitchen & Bathroom Awards 2019 Kitchen Design
  • HIA Winner · Victorian Kitchen & Bathroom Awards 2019 Residential Interior Design

Set atop the beautiful cliffs of Mt Martha, this contemporary home offers a seamless blend of luxury, comfort, and functionality. The poolside garden entrance is just the beginning, as you step inside and are immediately greeted by breathtaking, sweeping ocean views that stretch across the open-plan kitchen, living, and dining area. This meticulously designed space celebrates the surrounding landscape, featuring clean layouts, stunning finishes, and a natural flow between multiple indoor and outdoor entertaining areas. Each room is thoughtfully positioned to maximize the ocean views, creating a true haven for relaxation and connection with nature.

The house renovation completely fulfilled the brief for a space where a family with multiple interests and pursuits could adapt and grow over the years. It was an absolute pleasure to design this home!

We originally engaged Jasmine to assist with interior design and finishes throughout our entire house. We had already started the building process. We soon discovered that Jasmine’s talents extend way beyond choosing finishes and tiles. Jasmine immediately identified areas of our design where the functionality could be improved. We took on board Jasmine’s design ideas and the result has been a significant improvement to the style and functionality of our house, in particular the kitchen/dining/loungeroom and Master Bedroom Retreat including a stunning Dressing Room and Ensuite. We were so impressed we continued our relationship with Jasmine right through the building phase to the final selection of soft furnishings for the final product.

From the initial meeting, Jasmine listened to us and was immediately in tune with the design outcomes that we were aiming for. Trusting Jasmine’s design ideas allowed us to be more daring with some of our choices and finishes, in particular the tile selections and kitchen design. Her knowledge of products and finishes kept us at the cutting edge of current and upcoming trends. Jasmine allowed us to be involved in as much of the design process as we wanted to be, some areas we left completely to Jasmine’s discretion and when we added our own input it was great to have Jasmine to bounce our own ideas off.

Jasmine and Barbra are an extremely efficient team, responding quickly to any queries we had along the way. Jasmine’s attention to detail is impeccable; she is very professional but maintains a relaxed and fun approach to her work which we really appreciated.

We were very happy with trades that were recommended to us, in particular for the extensive custom joinery used throughout the house. Overall, we would highly recommend Jasmine and her team, by engaging a team of professionals we were able to achieve an amazing result; we only wish we had engaged her earlier on in the project!

Helen & DamienMt Martha Residence
