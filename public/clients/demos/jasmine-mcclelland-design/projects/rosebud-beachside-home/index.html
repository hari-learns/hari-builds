Rosebud Beachside Home

  • LocationRosebud VIC 3939
  • HIA Winner Victorian Eastern Regional Housing Awards 2021 Residential Interior Design
  • HIA Winner Victorian Eastern Regional Housing Awards 2021 Kitchen Design
  • HIA Winner Victorian Kitchen & Bathroom Awards 2021 Residential Interior Design
  • HIA Finalist Victorian Kitchen & Bathroom Awards 2021 Finalist Kitchen Design

Tucked in the trees on the foreshore of the Mornington Peninsula, the existing family home was completely transformed to create a luxurious beachside oasis. The design nods back to Mid-century elements, the clients love of art and the blue hues of the waterfront. Each space has been designed with practicality and function at the forefront. Custom artworks and furniture were carefully curated and considered to finish off the home with warmth and personality.

My husband and I engaged Jasmine to design our internal spaces for our brand new house by the beach. She connected with us in a delightful and thoughtful manner to gauge the style we wanted and how we would use the spaces. Her initial design hit the brief! Jasmine came up with many ideas that we used to enhance the designs, her great eye for curating the finishes for all our cabinetry, benchtops and splashbacks was amazing. Her gift for combining the perfect colour palette for our beachside home exceeded my expectation! I was completely delighted.

We also engaged Jasmine to help decorate some of our spaces, she brought together the contemporary style of the architecture and our love of mid-century design, to create a home which is elegant and warm.

Jasmine managed to steer me away from of some of my disorganised thoughts but at the same time come up with viable alternatives! I’m sure I tried her patience, but she understood it was a big moment for us to get this right and was extremely thoughtful and nurturing.

My family is thrilled with the end result and we smile every-time we enter the house. It definitely ticks all the boxes for us! We would not hesitate to recommend Jasmine and her team.

Anne & GraemeRosebud Beachside Home
