Taking shape

In the studio now

Early renders from projects in the studio now.

Mornington Cinema

Early render

Born from Ian’s lifelong love of film, this boutique cinema in Mornington blends the glamour of 1950s Hollywood Art Deco with contemporary coastal touches. More than a cinema, it’s a passion project created for Mornington’s community of film lovers.

Collingwood Silo Apartment

Early render

Set within a converted silo in Collingwood, the renovation asked us to work with curves rather than corners. Custom joinery turns the building’s unusual nooks into practical storage, while allowing its original form and character to lead the design.

St Kilda Road Apartment

Early render

New York loft living, right here in Melbourne. Industrial elements and mid-century details bring warmth and personality to a St Kilda Road apartment, creating the perfect backdrop for our client’s collection of art and books.

Clifton Hill Townhouse

Early render

With strong bones and plenty of mid-century character, the home called for a renovation that would build on what was already there. Bold, unexpected details bring a fresh and youthful energy without losing the nostalgia our clients loved from the start.

Fitzroy North Residence

Early render

A Fitzroy North terrace filled with maximalist colour, Art Deco references and plenty of personality. Created for a client who wholeheartedly believes more is more, the design embraces bold patterns, playful details and an eclectic mix of finishes.

Collingwood Warehouse

Early render

Set within one of Collingwood’s notable warehouse buildings, the apartment lets the building’s raw character do much of the talking. Dark timber, Ceppo stone, painted panelling and industrial lighting add depth and texture without taking away from what made the space special in the first place.

Ivanhoe Residence

Early render

Art Deco influences are given a cleaner, more minimal feel in our Ivanhoe project. A carefully selected mix of colours and finishes brings character to the home without overdoing it.

Malvern East Residence

Early render

This full-house renovation of a Californian bungalow in Malvern East brings old and new together. The home’s original bones remain at the heart of the design, with a fresh new layer introduced for the way our clients live today.
