Projects / Bathrooms

Richmond Bathrooms

  • LocationRichmond VIC 3121

Two bathrooms and a powder room were given some new love. Neutral tones with a little surprise in the powder room. Each design was carefully thought through to meet our clients needs. Practicality and function is always at the forefront of our designs, and the spacial layouts and floor plans were essential to informing everything that follows. Our client is in love with her new bathrooms and that brings us so much joy!
