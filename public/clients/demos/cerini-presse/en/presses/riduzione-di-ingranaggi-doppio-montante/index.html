KN 2000-5000 2.M.

Single or double gear-reduction double-column presses

Double-column presses with two and/or four pressure points suit blanking, light and medium deep drawing, and progressive stamping.

The layout of the connecting rods, the rigidity of the structure and the length of the guides give the machine an excellent ability to resist off-centre loads left to right. The generous sizing of structures and mechanical parts makes these presses suited to the heaviest uses demanded by new needs.

The press drive is generously sized. Alloy steel shafts supported by self-aligning bearings (clutch shaft) or bronze bearings. A fixed or, as an option, variable stroke can be chosen.
