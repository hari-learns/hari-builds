KN 350-3000 I.R.

Single or double gear-reduction presses

The press drive gear is mounted on the eccentric shaft and is driven in turn by a smaller pinion; the flywheel and the brake-clutch unit are mounted on this pinion shaft, called the "fast shaft".

These machines run from 40 to 120 strokes per minute with strokes up to 300/350 mm. This solution suits blanking and medium deep drawing very well.

In larger presses with higher capacity and longer stroke, the drive can be a double transmission, with a gear on each end of the shaft.
