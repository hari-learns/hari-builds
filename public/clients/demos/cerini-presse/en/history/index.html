Cerini Presse

A story more than 70 years long

Carlo Cerini s.r.l. is an Italian metalworking company based in Rescaldina, in the province of Milan. It has made and sold its own products since 1948, the year it was founded.

A mechanical press is not a bare, empty piece of welded sheet metal, but a structure with character, born from an idea, from study and from the know-how of people who hand down from generation to generation the layout, the knowledge, the systems, the choice of materials… for Carlo Cerini s.r.l. all this translates into Made in Italy.
  1. 1948

    Carlo Cerini s.r.l. is founded: the company makes and sells mechanical presses.

  2. Anni '80

    The founder's death tests his son Claudio. With commitment and entrepreneurial spirit he revalues the company with new products, widens the range and opens foreign markets further.

  3. Oggi

    The experience is handed on to Claudio's son Andrea, who as a child worked alongside the staff, now part of the family: a new challenge with the market, always on the founder's foundations, Made in Italy.

This early and constant internationalisation is a big advantage over other Italian companies in the same sector. Listening to customers' needs, being present at trade fairs with exhibition stands and taking part in dedicated conferences play a fundamental role.

The company's pride and joy is complete control of production, from design to testing of the machine, carried out entirely inside the same plant. A significant array of machinery and specialised operators allow Carlo Cerini s.r.l. to build all the components of its presses itself, buying quality materials from certified suppliers.

Carlo Cerini is a "human-sized" company: it believes in work ethics and respect for the environment as synonyms of quality. It works in a deliberately simple, lean and effective setting, born on the idea of closeness between production, technical and administrative parts.
