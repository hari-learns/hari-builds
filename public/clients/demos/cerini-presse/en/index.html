A story more than 70 years long

  1. 1948

    Carlo Cerini s.r.l. is founded: the company makes and sells mechanical presses.

  2. Anni '80

    The founder's death tests his son Claudio. With commitment and entrepreneurial spirit he revalues the company with new products, widens the range and opens foreign markets further.

  3. Oggi

    The experience is handed on to Claudio's son Andrea, who as a child worked alongside the staff, now part of the family: a new challenge with the market, always on the founder's foundations, Made in Italy.
