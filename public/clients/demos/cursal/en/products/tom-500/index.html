TOM 500: Combination of vertical and horizontal cutting

TOM 500 – Industrial Power, Absolute Precision

Designed for the most extreme conditions of primary wood processing, the TOM 500 is the most powerful manual/semi-automatic crosscut saw ever built by CURSAL. With the largest cutting capacity in the range, it is the ultimate solution for high-volume rough wood processing — even if wet, twisted, or oversized. This machine is built to endure, produce, and optimize every second of the cycle.

Thanks to the combination of vertical and horizontal blade movement on ball bearing guides, the TOM 500 delivers smooth, precise, and repeatable cuts — even under heavy production stress. This dual motion maximizes cutting speed while maintaining top-quality results.

Available in two versions with 900 or 1100 mm work bench, the machine integrates seamlessly into automated lines with log saws, multi-blades, loading/unloading systems, transfer units, and board rotators. It can be configured with electro-pneumatic stops for repetitive cuts or CNC stops for variable production — all managed via an ergonomic and intuitive operator panel.

Its structure is robust and dust-protected; in particular, the radial bearing are pressurized by an exclusive CURSAL system that uses passive energy generated by the machine’s motion to ensure exceptional durability. The entire machine is designed to resist humidity and thermal stress, enabling continuous operation with minimal maintenance. The modular design and full compatibility with CURSAL accessories allow every line to be tailored to the customer’s exact needs.

TOM 500 is not just a crosscut saw — it’s an industrial partner, built for those in packaging, semi-finished goods, or exotic wood flooring sectors, where there is no room for error or downtime. When production gets serious, TOM 500 is the only credible answer. Powerful, reliable, and unstoppable, it’s made for markets where service support is limited. The TOM 500 is built with simplified and easily available components.
