TVM 600 and TVM 700 – Power and Versatility for Demanding, High-Volume Production

The TVM 600 and TVM 700 crosscut saws are the perfect combination of power, precision, and versatility, designed to meet the needs of industrial-scale production. These machines offer an exceptional cutting capacity thanks to their vertical reciprocating blade movement and large-diameter blade, ensuring fast, accurate cuts while minimizing downtime and boosting overall efficiency.

Key Features The modular structure of the TVM 600 and TVM 700, combined with a wide range of customizable accessories, allows seamless integration into different production lines. They can be configured with advanced systems such as electro-pneumatic stops and CNC-controlled end stops, making them highly adaptable to various production requirements. Simplified maintenance extends their lifespan and helps reduce operating costs.

Strengths

Flexible configuration: Easily adaptable to different applications and production environments through customizable setups.

Their cutting speed significantly reduces processing time, improving output and overall efficiency.

Robustness and reliability: Built for continuous operation with minimal maintenance, thanks to a durable and heavy-duty design.

Technology, Innovation, and Performance Their vertical blade movement optimizes cutting cycles.

Ergonomics and operator safety are a top priority, with, with design features aimed at minimizing fatigue and ensuring a safe working environment.

Application Sectors TVM 600 and TVM 700 are ideal for the packaging sector, semi-finished furniture components, structural timber processing with thick materials, and other industrial contexts requiring speed, accuracy, and high processing capacity.

If your production demands a crosscut saw that delivers power, flexibility, and consistent performance, these models are the perfect choice.

TVM 700: Strength and Innovation for Industrial

Production

The TVM 700 is the perfect solution for those who need to handle complex cuts and large-sized materials without compromising on precision. With a greater cutting capacity than the TVM 600, it is ideal for high-intensity industrial production requiring reliable and top-level performance. Its solid structure and vertical reciprocating blade movement allow it to process solid wood and thick panels with ease, ensuring fast and accurate cuts.

Designed for the construction, sawmill, and packaging industries, the TVM 700 is perfect for handling heavy materials and managing large loads without sacrificing cut quality. Its modular configuration allows integration with specific accessories that further optimize production processes, offering both flexibility and customization.

95% of TVM 700 units are equipped with CURSAL’s FCN electronic stop, highly valued not only for its precision and flexibility but also for its ability to absorb the impact generated by large boards.

In summary, the TVM 700 is a powerful and versatile machine designed to deliver maximum reliability and superior performance in demanding industrial environments.
