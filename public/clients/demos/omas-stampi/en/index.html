English summary

Dies and metal parts, from drawing to finished part

O.M.A.S. Stampi was founded in 1973 and today designs and builds dies and produces metal parts, managing the whole process from design to the finished article. 40 people, about 2,500 m² in Brugherio (MB), Italy.

Request a quote+39 039 832 264

Design

Blanking, progressive, block, transfer, bending and deep-drawing dies, designed in 2D and/or 3D with CAD/CAM links to the machines.

Die making

4 wire-EDM machines (Charmilles and Sodick), 2 CNC machining centres, 4 grinders; assembly and final test by specialised staff.

Metal parts

About 20 presses (Balconi, Bihler, Bruderer, Colombo, Invernizzi, IMV Galli). Steel, stainless steel, iron, aluminium, brass, copper, bronze and special alloys (Monel 400, Inconel 718).

Quality

Certified since 1997: ISO 9001, ISO 45001, IATF 16949:2016 (automotive). Lab with Smartscope OGP optical measuring, profile projector, hardness testers.

Die maintenance

Wear of every single die is tracked by dedicated software linked to the AS400 management system.
