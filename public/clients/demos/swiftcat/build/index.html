How a Swiftcat is built

We care about the small details.

The hull design

The patented hull design incorporates several innovative features:

  • Tunnel chinesTwo longitudinal steps on each side break up the solid water to form a two phase medium before it hits the tunnel roof (wet deck). This assures a comfortable, shock-free ride in rough water.
  • Asymmetrical sponsonsThe sponsons (demi-hulls) are asymmetrical with convex non-trips to guarantee banking to the inside in turns. This provides excellent straight-line stability, as well as safe and comfortable turning.
  • Symmetrical bowsThe symmetrical bows provide plenty of displacement up front to prevent any chance of stuffing or broaching, and prevent any see-sawing (veering from port to starboard) in quartering seas.
  • A high tunnelHead seas can be taken at speed. It allows fast backing-up without swamping the rear deck, and makes trolling and loitering in following seas dry and safe.
  • The swim platformThe swim platform at the transom protects the drive system and allows recovery at near water level in safety.
  • Centre of gravityCentre of gravity position is not critical on this hull design, which allows people to move around without upsetting stability or overall performance.

Where continual operation at high speed and in all weather conditions is required, the BOBKAT patented SKOKBREKER (Shock Breaker) can be fitted, which reduces slamming against the tunnel roof in high sea states.

Superstructures can be constructed out of GRP, sandwich, aluminium or even wood to suit the requirements of the end-user. Because of the high metacentric height of the basic hull, the deck structures can be high and wide to fully take advantage of the very large space available.

The wheelhouse

“A part of a boat or ship serving as a shelter for the person at the wheel.”

The wheelhouse is the power centre for your boat and so its design and finish are crucial to our customers. With such a varied and versatile number of ways of customising and configuring the layout of this internal space, the wheelhouse becomes a personal signature for the owner of the boat.

With plenty of room for seating, a galley, control panels and entertainment outlets, the wheelhouse can be made as comfortable as your living room at home, or be used to fill all of the requirements to use your vessel commercially.

The materials

Fibreglass

We use the latest and most tried and tested materials in our manufacturing process. Fibreglass is the key building block in our catamarans, as its durability, flexibility and versatility make it ideal in boat building. By laying multiple layers of fibre on top of one another, with each layer oriented in various preferred directions, the material's overall stiffness and strength can be efficiently controlled.

With very little wood being used, the boat is strong, lightweight and much less prone to rot and water damage. Instead an incredibly strong material known as Nida-core is used to stiffen laminates at a fraction of the weight of solid fibreglass.

The process

The gelling process

We use gelcoat to provide our vessels with a level of protection from UV rays and harmful chemicals. It is a preferred application due to its long-lasting durability. Gelcoat also protects the boat from leaks and cracks.

The infusion process

Our vacuum infusion process provides our vessels with a number of advantages: consistent resin use (and ultimately less resin wastage), high consistency and repeatability, alongside providing an overall cleaner process.

Ultimately, this provides our customers with a higher quality vessel, a better interior finish, and a faster cycle time.
