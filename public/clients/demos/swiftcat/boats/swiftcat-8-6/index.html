Swiftcat 8.6 power catamaran

8.6 m overall · 45 kn · 600 hp max

8M Series

About this boat

The 8m is a shortened version of the 9.6, typically reduced to this size for berth reasons. Although you get a similar ride to the 9.6, it is not quite as smooth and you get less space below deck for bunks etc.
