About Swiftcat

The vision

Our passion is to deliver our vessels to the highest standard, with expert craftsmanship and a bespoke service personalised to each buyer.

To achieve this industry leading service, we offer a customisable product, an efficient handcrafted process and a close relationship with our clients throughout the journey.

Our vessels are some of the best in their class: the building materials, design, manufacturing process and client experience have been painstakingly perfected since our conception in 2004.

We boast up to 25% more fuel efficiency than competitors, a 20% improvement in speed and the best possible value for money.

A leap from the building trade into boat building

After many years working in the building industry, Chris made a leap of faith into the boating world. In 2003, Chris purchased a boat which ultimately left him displeased and underwhelmed. He believed that boat building should be bespoke and customer led; thus setting him off on a journey to create his own vessel, which after one year of in-depth study and exploration resulted in the creation of his first build: a Swiftcat 9.6.

After meeting his business partner Seb in 2020, the company has strengthened not only in its work-force, but its efficiency, communication and meticulous attention to detail.

We strive for the best quality service through our vessels, our team and most importantly, for our customers.
